## Vignette

Researchers are investigating whether participation in different exercise programs affects systolic blood pressure among adults with hypertension. A total of 180 participants are randomly assigned to one of three interventions: aerobic exercise, resistance training, or yoga. After 12 weeks, the investigators calculate the mean systolic blood pressure for each group and wish to determine whether there is a statistically significant difference among the groups.

Which of the following statistical tests is most appropriate for analyzing these data?
