## Chief Complaint

Acute cyanosis, dyspnea, and irritability during play, relieved by squatting

## Vignette

A 4-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department after his mother witnessed him become acutely cyanotic, dyspneic, and irritable while playing in the backyard. She noted that he suddenly stopped running and squatted down on his haunches, after which his color and breathing rapidly improved. He has had several similar episodes over the past year. On examination, his lips and nail beds are mildly cyanotic, and a harsh systolic ejection murmur is heard at the left upper sternal border.
