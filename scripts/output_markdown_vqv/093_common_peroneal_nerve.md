A 46-year-old man presents to the clinic with progressive difficulty walking over the past several weeks. He recently lost 25 kg after bariatric surgery and reports that he frequently crosses his legs while sitting at work. He has noticed that his right foot “slaps” the ground when walking and that he must lift his knee higher than usual to avoid tripping. Physical examination reveals weakness of ankle dorsiflexion and foot eversion. Sensory testing demonstrates decreased sensation over the dorsum of the foot and lateral aspect of the lower leg. Plantarflexion is preserved.
