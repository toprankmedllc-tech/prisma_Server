## Chief Complaint

Recurrent ear infections and pneumonia

## Vignette

An 8-month-old boy is brought to the clinic because of recurrent ear infections and two episodes of pneumonia over the past 2 months. He was born at term and had no serious infections during the first few months of life. His mother reports that he recently completed antibiotics for otitis media but developed fever and cough again 10 days later. There is no history of chronic diarrhea, oral thrush, or opportunistic infections. Physical examination shows small tonsillar tissue and few palpable cervical lymph nodes. Laboratory studies show IgG 115 mg/dL, IgA 6 mg/dL, IgM 9 mg/dL, CD19+ B cells 0.4% of lymphocytes, and CD3+ T cells 72% of lymphocytes.

## Key Symptoms
