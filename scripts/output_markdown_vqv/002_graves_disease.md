## Chief Complaint

Weight loss, palpitations, and heat intolerance

## Vignette

A 29-year-old woman comes to the physician due to progressive weight loss, palpitations, anxiety, and heat intolerance for the past 4 months. She reports increased sweating, tremors, and difficulty sleeping. She also notes a family history of autoimmune diseases, including type 1 diabetes mellitus in her brother and pernicious anemia in her mother.

The patient states that her symptoms began several weeks after recovering from a gastrointestinal infection during a stressful period involving major surgery. Physical examination reveals a diffusely enlarged, smooth thyroid gland with an audible bruit. Bilateral exophthalmos and lid lag are present. Laboratory studies demonstrate suppressed TSH and elevated free T4 levels. Serum testing identifies thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulins. Genetic testing shows association with specific HLA alleles.

Which of the following best explains the pathogenesis of this patient’s condition?

A. Autoantibody-mediated destruction of thyroid follicles leading to hypothyroidism
B. IgG antibodies stimulating TSH receptors and activating adenylate cyclase
C. Viral-mediated granulomatous inflammation causing release of preformed thyroid hormone
D. Autonomous hyperfunctioning thyroid nodules producing excess thyroid hormone
E. Neoplastic proliferation of parafollicular C cells producing calcitonin
