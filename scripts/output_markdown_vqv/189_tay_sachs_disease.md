## Vignette

A 7-month-old infant of Ashkenazi Jewish descent is brought to the clinic for developmental regression and poor muscle tone. The parents report that the child had been meeting developmental milestones until about 5 months of age but has progressively lost motor skills. Physical examination reveals hypotonia, hyperreflexia, and exaggerated startle response to sound. Fundoscopic examination shows a cherry-red spot on the macula. No hepatosplenomegaly is present. Which of the following is the underlying cause of this condition?
