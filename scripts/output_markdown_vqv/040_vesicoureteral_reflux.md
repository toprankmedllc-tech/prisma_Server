## Vignette

A 4-year-old boy is brought to the pediatric clinic because of recurrent urinary tract infections and poor weight gain. His mother reports that he has had several episodes of fever, dysuria, and flank pain since infancy. Laboratory studies show elevated serum creatinine. Voiding cystourethrogram demonstrates retrograde flow of urine from the bladder into the ureters during urination. Renal ultrasound reveals unilateral hydronephrosis with cortical scarring.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying abnormality in this patient?
