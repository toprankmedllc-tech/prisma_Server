## Vignette

A 22-year-old woman comes to the emergency department due to progressively worsening lower abdominal pain and fever for the past 3 days. The pain is bilateral and associated with nausea and abnormal vaginal discharge. She is sexually active with multiple partners and uses condoms inconsistently. The patient reports pain during intercourse and spotting after sexual intercourse over the last month. Temperature is 38.8 C (101.8 F). Pelvic examination demonstrates cervical motion tenderness and mucopurulent endocervical discharge. Urine pregnancy test is negative. Nucleic acid amplification testing identifies an obligate intracellular organism that lacks muramic acid in its cell wall. If left untreated, this patient is at greatest risk for which of the following complications?
