## Vignette

A 19-year-old man is brought to the pulmonology clinic for worsening shortness of breath and recurrent respiratory infections since childhood. He was diagnosed with “asthma” at age 5 but has had multiple hospitalizations for pneumonia. His mother reports that he has always had bulky, foul-smelling stools. On examination, he is thin with a BMI at the 5th percentile. He has digital clubbing and bilateral crackles over the upper lung zones. Sputum culture grows mucoid colonies of Pseudomonas aeruginosa. Which of the following additional findings is most likely to be present in this patient?
