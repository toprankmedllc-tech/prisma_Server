## Vignette

Researchers are studying adherence to hand hygiene protocols among physicians in a large teaching hospital. For three months, electronic badge scanners are installed outside patient rooms to record whether physicians sanitize their hands before entering. Hospital staff are informed that compliance data will be collected as part of a quality-improvement initiative, but individual performance reports will not be shared with supervisors. At the end of the study, hand hygiene compliance is substantially higher than rates reported in previous years.

Which of the following best explains the increase in hand hygiene compliance observed during the study?
