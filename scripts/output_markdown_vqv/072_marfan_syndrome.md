## Vignette

A 19-year-old man is brought to the emergency department because of sudden severe tearing chest pain radiating to the back. He is tall and thin with disproportionately long extremities. Physical examination reveals hypermobile joints, arachnodactyly, pectus excavatum, and a high-arched palate. Ophthalmologic examination demonstrates upward displacement of the lens. His father died suddenly at age 34 from a “heart problem.” CT angiography shows dilation of the ascending aorta with an intimal flap.
