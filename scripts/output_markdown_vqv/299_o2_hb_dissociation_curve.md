## Chief Complaint

Respiratory distress requiring mechanical ventilation

## Vignette

A 4-day-old male neonate born at 32 weeks gestation is in the NICU for respiratory distress syndrome due to surfactant deficiency and requires mechanical ventilation with supplemental oxygen. Despite stable ventilator settings, an arterial blood gas shows a PaO₂ of 55 mmHg, while pulse oximetry reveals an unexpectedly high SpO₂ of 94%. In contrast, a term neonate with the same PaO₂ would typically have an oxygen saturation of about 85%. The discrepancy is attributed to developmental differences in hemoglobin composition affecting oxygen binding affinity. Which of the following best explains this finding?
