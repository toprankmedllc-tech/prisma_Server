## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 27-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after a motor vehicle accident. He was initially conscious but became rapidly more distressed and short of breath shortly after arrival. He is tachycardic, hypotensive, and using accessory muscles of respiration. On examination, there is absent breath sounds on the right side, hyperresonance to percussion, and tracheal deviation to the left. Jugular veins are distended. Chest X-ray is delayed due to hemodynamic instability. The patient suddenly deteriorates with worsening hypotension and hypoxia. The underlying pathophysiology of this condition is most likely due to which of the following?
