## Chief Complaint

Double vision and severe headache

## Vignette

A 58-year-old woman with a history of poorly controlled hypertension presents to the emergency department with sudden onset severe headache and double vision. She reports difficulty opening her left eye and states that bright light worsens the pain. Physical examination shows that the left eye is positioned “down and out” at rest. The left pupil is dilated and poorly reactive to light. The patient also has ptosis of the left eyelid. CT angiography reveals an aneurysm compressing a cranial nerve as it exits the midbrain.

Which of the following cranial nerves is most likely affected in this patient?
