## Vignette

A 16-year-old girl is brought to the clinic because of progressive hair loss over the past several months. Her mother reports noticing patchy bald areas on the scalp and missing eyelashes. The patient admits that she repeatedly pulls out her hair during periods of stress and feels temporarily relieved afterward. She has unsuccessfully attempted to stop the behavior multiple times. Physical examination reveals irregular patches of hair loss with hairs of varying lengths and no scalp inflammation or scarring.Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
