## Vignette

Researchers are studying the association between sleep duration and academic performance among medical students. A total of 240 students are enrolled in the study. Each student reports the average number of hours slept per night during the semester, and investigators record the student's final examination score. After the examinations are completed, students are classified into three performance categories based on their scores: low-performing, average-performing, and high-performing. The investigators then analyze the relationship between sleep duration and examination score using the original measurements collected from all participants.

Which of the following statistical tests is most appropriate for analyzing these data?
