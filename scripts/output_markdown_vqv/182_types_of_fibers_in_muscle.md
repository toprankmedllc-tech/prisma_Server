## Vignette

A 27-year-old man begins training for his first marathon after previously living a sedentary lifestyle. Several months into training, he notices that he can now perform prolonged exercise with much less fatigue. A muscle biopsy performed as part of a physiology research study demonstrates an increased proportion of muscle fibers containing abundant mitochondria, high myoglobin concentration, and extensive capillary networks. These fibers contract slowly but are highly resistant to fatigue.
Which of the following muscle fiber types is most likely predominant in this athlete’s trained muscles?
