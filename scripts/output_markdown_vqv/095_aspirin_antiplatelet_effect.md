## Chief Complaint

Prolonged gum bleeding after dental cleaning

## Vignette

A 61-year-old man comes to the clinic because of prolonged oozing from his gums after a dental cleaning earlier that morning. He has a history of ischemic heart disease and carotid artery stenosis. Six months ago, he began taking a daily over-the-counter medication to reduce his risk of recurrent ischemic events. He has no history of liver disease or inherited bleeding disorders. Physical examination shows mild gingival bleeding without petechiae or large ecchymoses. Laboratory studies show a platelet count of 245,000/mm3, prothrombin time of 12 seconds, activated partial thromboplastin time of 31 seconds, and bleeding time of 13 minutes.

## Key Symptoms
