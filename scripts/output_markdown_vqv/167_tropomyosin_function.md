## Vignette

A 22-year-old medical student participates in a physiology research study examining the molecular mechanisms of skeletal muscle contraction. During the experiment, an electrical stimulus is applied to an isolated skeletal muscle fiber. This triggers release of calcium ions. calcium binds to troponin C on the thin filament. Following this event, the myosin heads are able to attach to actin, initiating cross-bridge cycling and muscle contraction. The investigators note that, prior to calcium release, a specific regulatory protein normally prevents interaction between actin and myosin by blocking important binding sites on the thin filament.
Which of the following best describes the normal function of tropomyosin in skeletal muscle?
