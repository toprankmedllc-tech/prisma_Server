## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 24-year-old man presents with painless enlargement of lymph nodes in the neck for the past 2 months. He also reports intermittent fever, night sweats, and unintentional weight loss of 5 kg. Physical examination reveals firm, rubbery cervical lymphadenopathy. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 10.8 g/dL, WBC count of 12,400/mm³, and ESR of 72 mm/hr. Lymph node biopsy demonstrates large binucleated cells with prominent eosinophilic nucleoli in a reactive inflammatory background.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying pathological process?
