## Vignette

A large population study evaluates serum triglyceride levels in adults during routine health screening. Most individuals have values clustered within a narrow range around 1.0–2.0 mmol/L. However, a small subset of participants show extremely elevated levels, some exceeding 10 mmol/L. When investigators summarize the dataset, they find that the average triglyceride level is higher than the value that occurs most frequently in the sample.

Which of the following best describes the distribution of this dataset?
