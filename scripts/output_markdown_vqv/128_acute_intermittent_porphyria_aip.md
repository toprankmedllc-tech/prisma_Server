## Chief Complaint

Recurrent episodes of severe abdominal pain, anxiety, and confusion

## Vignette

A 28-year-old woman presents with recurrent episodes of severe abdominal pain, anxiety, and confusion. She reports that her symptoms worsen after starting a new anticonvulsant medication. Vital signs show temperature of 37.1°C, heart rate of 108/min, blood pressure of 134/86 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 18/min. Physical examination is unremarkable except for mild tachycardia. Laboratory studies show urinary porphobilinogen of 48 mg/24 hr (reference <2 mg/24 hr), urinary ALA of 22 mg/24 hr (reference <7 mg/24 hr), and normal fecal porphyrins. There are no skin lesions or photosensitivity.
