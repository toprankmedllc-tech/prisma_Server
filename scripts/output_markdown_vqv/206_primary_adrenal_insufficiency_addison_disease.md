## Vignette

A 38-year-old woman comes to the clinic because of progressive fatigue, weight loss, nausea, and dizziness for several months. She also reports craving salty foods. Her medical history is significant for autoimmune thyroid disease. Physical examination reveals diffuse hyperpigmentation of the skin, especially over the palmar creases and oral mucosa.
