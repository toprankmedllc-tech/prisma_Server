## Chief Complaint

Weight loss over 8 months (per mother); patient denies problem

## Vignette

A 17-year-old girl is brought to her pediatrician by her mother, who is concerned because her daughter has lost 14 kg over the past 8 months. The patient says she is “eating healthy” and denies any problem. She avoids carbohydrates and fats entirely and exercises 2 hours daily. She has not had a menstrual period in 5 months. She becomes visibly anxious when asked about weight gain and states she feels “fat” despite her BMI of 15.8 kg/m². On exam, she has lanugo hair on her arms, dry skin, and bradycardia. She denies binge eating or purging. Her mother reports she spends significant time weighing herself and researching calorie counts.

## Key Symptoms

Severe food restriction; intense fear of weight gain; distorted body image (feels “fat” despite low BMI); amenorrhea ×5 months; excessive exercise; denial of illness (poor insight); no bingeing/purging

## Labs

Hypokalemia; low estrogen; leukopenia; low T3; elevated BUN (dehydration)

## Physical Exam

BMI 15.8 kg/m²; lanugo hair on arms; dry skin; bradycardia; hypotension; no parotid enlargement; no dental erosion (no purging signs)

## Main Clue

Restrictive eating + distorted body image + significantly low BMI + denial of illness = anorexia nervosa, restricting type

## Supporting Clue

No bingeing/purging (rules out bulimia); lanugo and bradycardia confirm severe malnutrition; amenorrhea supports chronicity
