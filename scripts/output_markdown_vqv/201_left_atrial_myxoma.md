## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 52-year-old woman presents with 3 months of progressive dyspnea on exertion and two episodes of syncope. She notes her symptoms worsen when she lies on her left side. She has also had low-grade fevers and a 4-kg unintentional weight loss. On cardiac auscultation, an early diastolic low-pitched sound is heard at the apex, followed by a mid-diastolic rumble. Laboratory studies show a hemoglobin of 10.2 g/dL and an erythrocyte sedimentation rate of 78 mm/hr.Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
