## Vignette

A 67-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department due to sudden onset severe left eye pain, headache, nausea, and blurred vision that began while she was watching a movie in a dark theater. She reports seeing halos around lights. Examination shows conjunctival injection, a cloudy cornea, and a fixed mid-dilated pupil. Intraocular pressure is markedly elevated. After initial treatment to rapidly lower intraocular pressure, she is given a medication that contracts the ciliary muscle and opens the trabecular meshwork to improve aqueous humor drainage.
