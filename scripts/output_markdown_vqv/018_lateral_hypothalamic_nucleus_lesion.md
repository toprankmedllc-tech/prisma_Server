## Vignette

A 3-year-old boy is brought to the pediatric clinic because of progressive weight loss and refusal to eat for the past 2 months. His mother says he previously had a normal appetite but now “never seems hungry.” Two months ago, he underwent neurosurgery for removal of a suprasellar mass. Since the surgery, he has become increasingly thin and fatigued. Physical examination shows marked muscle wasting and decreased subcutaneous fat. Neurologic examination is otherwise normal. Damage to which of the following hypothalamic nuclei most likely explains this patient’s symptoms?
