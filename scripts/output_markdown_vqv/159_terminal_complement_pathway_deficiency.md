## Chief Complaint

Fever, headache, and neck stiffness

## Vignette

A 20-year-old college student is brought to the emergency department because of fever, headache, and neck stiffness for 1 day. He had a similar episode of bacterial meningitis at age 16 and was treated for disseminated urethral infection 8 months ago. Temperature is 39.2°C, blood pressure is 104/66 mm Hg, heart rate is 118/min, and pulse oximetry is 98% on room air. Physical examination shows nuchal rigidity and scattered petechiae on the lower extremities. Cerebrospinal fluid analysis shows leukocytes 1,850/mm³, protein 145 mg/dL, and glucose 24 mg/dL; serum glucose is 94 mg/dL. Gram stain of the cerebrospinal fluid shows gram-negative diplococci. Total hemolytic complement activity is not detected.
