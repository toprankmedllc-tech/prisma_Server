## Vignette

A 59-year-old man presents to the clinic because of worsening fatigue and decreased exercise tolerance over the past year. His medical history is significant for poorly controlled hypertension for more than 20 years. He admits to inconsistent medication use. Physical examination reveals bilateral retinal arteriolar narrowing with arteriovenous nicking on fundoscopic examination. Laboratory studies show serum creatinine of 2.4 mg/dL and mild proteinuria. Urinalysis demonstrates hyaline casts without hematuria. Renal ultrasound reveals bilaterally shrunken kidneys. Renal biopsy demonstrates hyaline thickening of small arterioles with narrowing of vascular lumens and patchy interstitial fibrosis.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
