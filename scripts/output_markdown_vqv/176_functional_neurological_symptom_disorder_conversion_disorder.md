## Chief Complaint

Sudden inability to move the right leg

## Vignette

A 24-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department after suddenly developing weakness in her right leg following a stressful argument with her partner. She states that she is unable to walk, but she appears calm and unconcerned about her symptoms. Neurological examination reveals normal deep tendon reflexes and inconsistent weakness during repeated testing. When asked to lift her unaffected leg against resistance, involuntary extension of the “weak” leg is observed. MRI of the brain and spine is unremarkable.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
