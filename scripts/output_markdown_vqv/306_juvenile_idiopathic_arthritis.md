## Vignette

An 8-year-old girl is brought to the pediatric clinic because of intermittent limping and morning stiffness for the past 5 months. Her mother reports that the child has increasing difficulty getting out of bed and often avoids running during school activities. Symptoms improve as the day progresses. The patient occasionally complains of left knee and right ankle pain but denies trauma.
Past medical history is unremarkable. Immunizations are up to date. Family history is notable for autoimmune thyroid disease in the mother.
Physical examination reveals mild swelling and warmth of the left knee and right ankle with decreased range of motion but no erythema. Temperature is normal. Ophthalmologic screening reveals asymptomatic anterior uveitis.
Laboratory studies show:
Test	Result
ESR	Mildly elevated
CRP	Mildly elevated
Rheumatoid factor	Negative
ANA	Positive
CBC	Mild normocytic anemia
Radiographs demonstrate periarticular soft tissue swelling without erosive destruction.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying mechanism responsible for this patient’s disease?
