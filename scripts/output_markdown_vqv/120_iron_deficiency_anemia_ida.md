## Chief Complaint

Fatigue, brittle nails, and difficulty concentrating

## Vignette

A 28-year-old woman presents with fatigue, brittle nails, and difficulty concentrating. She reports heavy menstrual bleeding for the past several months. Physical examination shows pale conjunctiva and brittle, spoon-shaped nails. Laboratory studies reveal hemoglobin of 9.1 g/dL, MCV of 72 fL, MCHC of 29 g/dL, serum iron of 22 µg/dL, serum ferritin of 8 ng/mL, TIBC of 480 µg/dL, and transferrin saturation of 6%.
