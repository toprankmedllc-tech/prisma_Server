## Vignette

A 65-year-old woman with a history of postmenopausal osteoporosis is started on weekly medication therapy to reduce her risk of vertebral and pelvic fractures. three months later, she presents to her primary care physician with painful swallowing and substernal chest discomfort. She notices that chest discomfort worsens after taking her medication in the morning. She admits that she usually takes the pill with only a small amount of water and often lies down shortly afterward because of fatigue. Physical examination is unremarkable ,vital signs are unremarkable ,she appears fatigued .
