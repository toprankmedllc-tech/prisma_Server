## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 16-year-old girl comes to the clinic requesting evaluation for dysuria and vaginal discharge. She states that she recently became sexually active and asks the physician not to tell her parents about the visit. Urine nucleic acid amplification testing is positive for Chlamydia trachomatis. The patient is otherwise healthy and denies abuse, suicidal ideation, or danger to others. Her parents are waiting outside the examination room and ask to be informed about the diagnosis.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?
