## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 64-year-old man with long-standing COPD is admitted for worsening shortness of breath and confusion. Arterial blood gas shows: pH 7.31, PaCO₂ 68 mmHg, PaO₂ 55 mmHg, HCO₃⁻ 33 mEq/L. The patient is placed on supplemental oxygen, after which his oxygen saturation improves but his mental status worsens and PaCO₂ further increases. Which of the following best explains the predominant mechanism of CO₂ transport in this patient?
