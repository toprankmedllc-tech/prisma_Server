## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 46-year-old man presents with fatigue, recurrent nosebleeds, and fever for the past several weeks. Physical examination reveals pallor and scattered petechiae over the lower extremities. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 8.2 g/dL, platelet count of 40,000/mm³, and elevated white blood cell count with numerous myeloblasts. Bone marrow biopsy demonstrates myeloblasts containing needle-shaped azurophilic granules.
Which of the following most accurately characterizes the fundamental pathological mechanism?
