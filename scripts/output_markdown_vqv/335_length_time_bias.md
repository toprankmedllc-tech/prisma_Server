## Vignette

Researchers evaluate a new annual screening program for pancreatic cancer in asymptomatic adults. After 10 years of follow-up, patients whose cancers were detected through the screening program appear to have substantially longer survival after diagnosis than patients whose cancers were diagnosed after developing symptoms. Investigators note that many of the cancers detected through screening were slow-growing tumors that remained asymptomatic for years, whereas rapidly progressive tumors often became symptomatic between screening visits and were diagnosed clinically.

Which of the following study modifications would best reduce the risk of this bias?
