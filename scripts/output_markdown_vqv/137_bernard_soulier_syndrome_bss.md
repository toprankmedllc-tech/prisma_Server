## Chief Complaint

Recurrent nosebleeds and easy bruising since infancy

## Vignette

A 5-year-old boy presents with recurrent nosebleeds and easy bruising since infancy. Physical examination reveals petechiae and prolonged bleeding from minor cuts. Laboratory studies show a prolonged bleeding time with normal PT and aPTT. Platelet count is decreased. Platelet aggregation studies show failure to aggregate in response to ristocetin.



## Key Symptoms

Recurrent nosebleeds; easy bruising since infancy; petechiae; prolonged bleeding from minor cuts; decreased platelet count; prolonged bleeding time; normal PT and aPTT; failure to aggregate in response to ristocetin



## Labs

Prolonged bleeding time; normal PT; normal aPTT; decreased platelet count; platelet aggregation shows failure to aggregate in response to ristocetin



## Physical Exam

Petechiae; prolonged bleeding from minor cuts

## Main Clue

Failure of ristocetin-induced aggregation + thrombocytopenia + giant platelets



## Supporting Clue

Childhood onset, recurrent epistaxis, mucosal bleeding, prolonged bleeding time, normal PT/aPTT, autosomal recessive
