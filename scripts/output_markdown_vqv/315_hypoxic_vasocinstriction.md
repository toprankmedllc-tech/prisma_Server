## Chief Complaint

Acute respiratory distress

## Vignette

A 34-year-old woman with no past medical history is admitted for acute respiratory distress after being placed on high inspired oxygen in the ICU for severe pneumonia. Despite improving oxygen saturation, she develops worsening pulmonary artery pressures on hemodynamic monitoring. She is noted to have increasing dyspnea and mild signs of right heart strain. Imaging shows patchy areas of pulmonary oligemia alternating with relatively normal perfusion. Arterial blood gas shows: pH 7.44, PaCO₂ 32 mmHg, PaO₂ 70 mmHg on oxygen therapy. Which of the following best explains the mechanism contributing to her elevated pulmonary artery pressure?

## Key Symptoms

Dyspnea; hypoxemia despite oxygen; worsening pulmonary artery pressure; right heart strain; heterogeneous lung perfusion

## Labs

ABG: pH 7.44; PaCO₂ 32 mmHg; PaO₂ 70 mmHg on oxygen

## Imaging Findings

Patchy pulmonary oligemia; uneven regional perfusion

## Physical Exam

Signs of right heart strain; increased work of breathing

## Main Clue

Patchy lung perfusion with increased pulmonary artery pressure in pneumonia/heterogeneous lung disease

## Supporting Clue

Right heart strain + V/Q mismatch pattern

## Question

Which of the following best explains the mechanism contributing to her elevated pulmonary artery pressure?
