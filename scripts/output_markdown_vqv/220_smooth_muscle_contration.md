## Vignette

A 46-year-old restaurant chef develops severe abdominal cramping after eating contaminated seafood at a local festival. While being evaluated in the emergency department, he also experiences repeated episodes of intestinal spasms and elevated blood pressure due to intense contraction of smooth muscle within the walls of his gastrointestinal tract and blood vessels.
A medical student observing the case asks how smooth muscle cells are able to contract despite lacking the troponin regulatory system found in skeletal muscle. The attending physician explains that smooth muscle contraction depends on formation of a specific intracellular complex that activates myosin light-chain kinase (MLCK), ultimately allowing interaction between actin and myosin.
Which of the following intracellular events is most directly responsible for initiating smooth muscle contraction in this patient?
