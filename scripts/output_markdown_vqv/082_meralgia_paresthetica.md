## Vignette

A 42-year-old obese man presents to the clinic with burning pain and numbness over the outer aspect of his right thigh for the past several months. He reports that the symptoms worsen after prolonged standing and while wearing a tight work belt. He denies back pain, muscle weakness, or difficulty walking. Physical examination reveals decreased sensation over the anterolateral thigh without motor deficits. Reflexes are normal.
