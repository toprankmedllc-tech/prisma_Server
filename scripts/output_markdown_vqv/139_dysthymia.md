## Chief Complaint

Feeling “down and empty” for over a decade

## Vignette

A 34-year-old woman presents to her primary care physician reporting that she has felt “down and empty” for as long as she can remember — at least since her early 20s. She says she has never really felt happy, struggles with low energy, poor concentration, and low self-esteem. She denies ever having a period of two or more weeks where her depressed mood was significantly worse than usual. She continues to go to work and maintain her relationships, though she describes it as “going through the motions.” She denies suicidal ideation, manic episodes, or substance use. She says, “I’ve just always been this way — I thought it was my personality.”

## Key Symptoms

Depressed mood for ≥2 years; low energy; poor concentration; low self-esteem; intact functioning; no discrete MDD episodes; no manic symptoms

## Labs

TSH normal; CBC normal; metabolic panel normal

## Physical Exam

No abnormalities; appropriately groomed; mood described as “flat but not tearful”

## Main Clue

Depressed mood for more than 2 years WITHOUT discrete major depressive episodes — hallmark of persistent depressive disorder

## Supporting Clue

Patient attributes chronic low mood to her “personality” — classic patient framing that delays diagnosis; functioning is preserved but chronically impaired

## Question

Which of the following is the most appropriate diagnosis?

## Correct Answer

B) Persistent depressive disorder (dysthymia)
