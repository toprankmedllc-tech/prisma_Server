He recently returned from a spelunking expedition in the Ohio River Valley, where he spent several days exploring caves. He has no significant past medical history and takes no medications. Physical examination reveals mild hepatosplenomegaly and several painless oral ulcerations. Laboratory studies show pancytopenia. Chest radiography demonstrates bilateral reticulonodular infiltrates. Bone marrow biopsy reveals numerous macrophages containing small oval yeast forms measuring 2–5 μm. Which of the following organisms is the most likely cause of this patient's illness?
