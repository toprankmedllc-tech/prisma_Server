## Chief Complaint

Request for pregnancy termination counseling

## Vignette

A 29-year-old woman comes to her obstetrician for counseling regarding termination of pregnancy after prenatal testing confirms a severe fetal anomaly incompatible with long-term survival after birth. The physician personally opposes abortion due to deeply held religious beliefs. The patient asks whether the physician can arrange the procedure because she would like care as soon as possible.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?
