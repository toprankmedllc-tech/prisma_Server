## Chief Complaint

Fatigue and pallor, progressive abdominal distension

## Vignette

A 5-year-old boy presents with fatigue and pallor. His parents report progressive abdominal distension. Physical examination reveals hepatosplenomegaly and frontal bossing. Laboratory studies show severe microcytic anemia. Peripheral blood smear reveals target cells. Hemoglobin electrophoresis shows decreased HbA and increased HbF and HbA2.
