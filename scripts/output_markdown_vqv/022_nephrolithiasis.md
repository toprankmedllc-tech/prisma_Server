## Vignette

A 46-year-old man presents to the emergency department with a sudden onset of severe left flank pain radiating to the groin for 6 hours. He reports associated nausea and one episode of vomiting but denies fever or dysuria. Temperature is 37.1°C, blood pressure is 148/92 mmHg, pulse is 104/min, and respiratory rate is 18/min. Physical examination reveals left costovertebral angle tenderness. Laboratory studies show serum calcium of 11.8 mg/dL, creatinine of 1.1 mg/dL, and urinalysis demonstrates hematuria with envelope-shaped crystals. Noncontrast CT scan of the abdomen reveals a 5-mm radiopaque stone within the left ureter. Further history reveals recurrent nephrolithiasis and chronic constipation.
Which of the following underlying conditions most likely predisposed this patient to his current disease?
