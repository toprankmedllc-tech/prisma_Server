## Chief Complaint

Progressive shortness of breath and chronic cough

## Vignette

A 38-year-old man presents with progressive shortness of breath and chronic cough. He has a 5-pack-year smoking history but developed symptoms much earlier than expected. Physical examination reveals diminished breath sounds and a barrel-shaped chest. CT scan of the chest shows lower-lobe predominant panacinar emphysema. Laboratory studies reveal decreased serum alpha-1 antitrypsin levels. His father died of cirrhosis in his 40s.
Which of the following best explains the pathophysiology of this patient’s condition?
