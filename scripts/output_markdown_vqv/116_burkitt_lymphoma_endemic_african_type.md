## Chief Complaint

Rapidly enlarging swelling of the jaw

## Vignette

An 8-year-old boy from equatorial Africa is brought to the clinic because of rapidly enlarging swelling of the jaw over the past several weeks. Vital signs show temperature of 37.8°C, heart rate of 98/min, blood pressure of 100/64 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 18/min. Physical examination reveals a large mandibular mass and mild cervical lymphadenopathy. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 9.4 g/dL, WBC count of 8,800/mm³, LDH of 920 U/L, and uric acid of 10.2 mg/dL. Biopsy demonstrates sheets of medium-sized lymphoid cells with numerous interspersed macrophages creating a "starry-sky" appearance.
