## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 68-year-old man presents with persistent lower back pain and fatigue for the past several months. He reports recent unintentional weight loss of 7 kg and increasing weakness. Physical examination reveals pallor and tenderness over the lumbar spine. Vital signs show temperature of 37.1°C, heart rate of 92/min, blood pressure of 128/74 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 16/min. Laboratory studies demonstrate hemoglobin of 9.2 g/dL, serum calcium of 11.8 mg/dL, total protein of 9.8 g/dL, and serum creatinine of 2.4 mg/dL. Skeletal survey shows multiple well-circumscribed radiolucent lesions in the vertebrae and skull.
