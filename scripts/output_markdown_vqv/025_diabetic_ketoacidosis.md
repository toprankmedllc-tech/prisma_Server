## Vignette

A 58-year-old man is brought to the emergency department because of progressive fatigue, confusion, and rapid breathing for 2 days. He has a history of poorly controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus and hypertension. Temperature is 37.0°C, blood pressure is 96/60 mmHg, pulse is 118/min, and respiratory rate is 30/min with deep respirations. Laboratory studies show serum sodium of 132 mEq/L, potassium of 5.6 mEq/L, chloride of 96 mEq/L, bicarbonate of 10 mEq/L, glucose of 520 mg/dL, and creatinine of 1.5 mg/dL. Arterial blood gas demonstrates pH 7.18 and PaCO₂ 24 mmHg. Urinalysis is positive for ketones and glucose.
Which of the following acid-base disturbances is most consistent with this patient’s presentation?
