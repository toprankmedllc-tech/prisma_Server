## Vignette

A 28-year-old man is brought to the emergency department with 5 days of fever, chills, and progressive shortness of breath. He has a long history of intravenous heroin use. On examination, his temperature is 39.2°C (102.6°F), blood pressure is 108/64 mm Hg, pulse is 118/min, respirations are 24/min, and oxygen saturation is 91% on room air. Multiple track marks are noted on his arms. Cardiac auscultation reveals a holosystolic murmur. Lung auscultation reveals scattered crackles bilaterally. Chest radiograph shows multiple peripheral nodular opacities, some with central cavitation.
