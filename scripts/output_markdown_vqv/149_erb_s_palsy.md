## Vignette

A full-term male newborn is delivered vaginally after a prolonged labor complicated by shoulder dystocia. During delivery, significant lateral traction is applied to the infant’s head in order to free the impacted anterior shoulder. Apgar scores are normal, and the infant appears otherwise healthy. However, shortly after birth, the mother notices that the baby is not moving the right arm normally.
Physical examination reveals that the right upper extremity is held adducted against the body with the shoulder internally rotated. The elbow is extended, the forearm is pronated, and spontaneous movement of the affected arm is decreased. The Moro reflex is absent on the right side, but the grasp reflex is intact bilaterally. Sensory testing demonstrates decreased sensation over the lateral aspect of the shoulder and arm. No facial asymmetry or lower-extremity abnormalities are noted.
Which of the following is the most likely cause of this infant’s condition?
