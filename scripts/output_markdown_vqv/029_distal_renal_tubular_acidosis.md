A 29-year-old woman presents to the clinic because of progressive fatigue, muscle weakness, and recurrent episodes of flank pain. She reports passing multiple kidney stones over the past 2 years. Temperature is 36.8°C, blood pressure is 118/72 mmHg, pulse is 80/min, and respiratory rate is 16/min. Laboratory studies show serum sodium of 139 mEq/L, potassium of 3.0 mEq/L, chloride of 112 mEq/L, bicarbonate of 15 mEq/L, and creatinine of 0.9 mg/dL. Arterial blood gas demonstrates pH 7.30 and PaCO₂ 31 mmHg. Urinalysis reveals persistently alkaline urine with calcium phosphate crystals. Renal ultrasound demonstrates bilateral nephrocalcinosis.
