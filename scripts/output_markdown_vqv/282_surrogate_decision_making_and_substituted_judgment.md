## Chief Complaint

Altered mental status following intracerebral hemorrhage

## Vignette

A 68-year-old man is admitted to the intensive care unit after a large intracerebral hemorrhage. He is intubated and lacks decision-making capacity. There is no advance directive on file. The patient’s prognosis is poor, with a high likelihood of severe long-term neurological impairment even if he survives. The medical team recommends transitioning to comfort-focused care.The patient’s wife states that her husband previously told her he would not want to remain on life support if there was little chance of meaningful recovery. However, the patient’s adult son and daughter insist that “everything possible” should be done and request continuation of full life-sustaining treatment. The patient’s parents agree with the children and request that the ICU team continue aggressive management. No clear consensus is reached among family members.

Which of the following is the most appropriate next step for the physician?
