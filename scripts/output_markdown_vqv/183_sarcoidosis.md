## Chief Complaint

Progressive shortness of breath and dry cough

## Vignette

A 32-year-old African American woman presents with progressive shortness of breath, dry cough, and fatigue for the past 3 months. She also reports painless red bumps on her shins and intermittent blurry vision with eye irritation. She denies fever, weight loss, or productive cough. On examination, there are bilateral crackles at the lung bases and several tender, erythematous nodules over the anterior tibia. Slit-lamp examination reveals anterior uveitis. Serum studies reveal elevated angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE) levels and hypercalcemia. What is the underlying mechanism of pathogenesis?

## Key Symptoms

Dry cough; dyspnea; fatigue; erythema nodosum; blurry vision; eye redness; no fever; no productive cough

## Labs

## Physical Exam

Bilateral basal crackles; tender erythematous nodules on shins (erythema nodosum); anterior uveitis on slit lamp exam

## Main Clue

Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy + erythema nodosum + uveitis + hypercalcemia

## Supporting Clue

African American female + chronic dry cough + elevated ACE levels
