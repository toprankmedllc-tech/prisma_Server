## Chief Complaint

Seizures and arrhythmia after spinal anesthesia

## Vignette

A 29-year-old woman undergoes an emergency cesarean delivery under spinal anesthesia. Shortly after administration of the anesthetic agent, she develops circumoral numbness, tinnitus, and agitation followed by generalized tonic-clonic seizures. Minutes later, she becomes hypotensive and develops a wide-complex ventricular arrhythmia. Resuscitative measures are initiated. The anesthesiologist suspects toxicity from the local anesthetic used during the procedure.
