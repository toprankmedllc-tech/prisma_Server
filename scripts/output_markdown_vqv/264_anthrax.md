A 54-year-old man presents to the emergency department with fever and a painless lesion on his right forearm. He works in a rural leather-processing facility and recently helped unload untreated imported animal hides.
Three days earlier he noticed a small pruritic papule that enlarged rapidly. He denies trauma.
Medical history is notable for type 2 diabetes and chronic alcohol use.
Vital signs:
* Temperature: 38.2°C
* Pulse: 108/min
