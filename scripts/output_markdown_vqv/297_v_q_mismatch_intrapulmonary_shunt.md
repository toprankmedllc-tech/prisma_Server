## Chief Complaint

Shortness of breath

## Vignette

A 72-year-old man is brought to the emergency department with a 4-day history of fever, productive cough, and worsening shortness of breath. His temperature is 39.1°C (102.4°F), respiratory rate is 30/min, and oxygen saturation is 82% on room air. Physical examination reveals bronchial breath sounds and dullness to percussion over the right lower lung field. Chest radiography shows dense right lower lobe consolidation. Arterial blood gas analysis confirms severe hypoxemia. The patient is placed on 100% oxygen via a non-rebreather mask. Thirty minutes later, repeat arterial blood gas analysis shows minimal improvement in arterial oxygen tension. Which of the following best explains this patient's hypoxemia?
