## Chief Complaint

Confusion and ringing in ears after aspirin ingestion

## Vignette

A 19-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department by her roommate 3 hours after ingesting an unknown number of aspirin tablets following an argument with her boyfriend. She complains of ringing in her ears, nausea, and feels restless and anxious. She has no prior psychiatric history and takes no regular medications. On arrival, she is alert but visibly agitated and diaphoretic. Her temperature is 38.2°C, blood pressure is 118/76 mmHg, heart rate is 104 bpm, and pulse oximetry is 98% on room air. She is breathing rapidly. Arterial blood gas shows pH 7.52, PaCO₂ 28 mmHg, and HCO₃⁻ 22 mEq/L. Serum salicylate level is elevated. Basic metabolic panel shows Na⁺ 138, K⁺ 3.1 mEq/L. Which of the following best explains the acid-base disturbance seen in this patient?
