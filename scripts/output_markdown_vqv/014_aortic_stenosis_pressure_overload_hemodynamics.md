## Chief Complaint

Exertional presyncope and angina

## Vignette

A 72-year-old man comes to the clinic due to episodes of dizziness and feeling like he might pass out, particularly when climbing the stairs to his apartment. He also reports occasional chest tightness during these exertional episodes, which resolves with rest. He has a history of hypertension and osteoarthritis. Physical examination reveals a late-peaking, harsh, crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur heard best at the right second intercostal space with radiation to the carotid arteries. Palpation of the carotid pulse demonstrates a delayed upstroke and decreased amplitude. Echocardiography is pending. Which of the following changes to the left ventricular pressure-volume loop is most likely expected in this patient?
