## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 34-year-old resident physician arrives to the hospital ward to start his morning shift in internal medicine. Several nursing staff members notice that he appears unusually drowsy, has slurred speech, and struggles to recall recent patient details. Over the past week, colleagues have also observed episodes of irritability and inconsistent clinical decision-making. During rounds, he nearly prescribes a contraindicated medication combination for a patient with renal impairment, but the error is intercepted by another team member. Later, a senior colleague notices a strong odor of alcohol on his breath.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?
