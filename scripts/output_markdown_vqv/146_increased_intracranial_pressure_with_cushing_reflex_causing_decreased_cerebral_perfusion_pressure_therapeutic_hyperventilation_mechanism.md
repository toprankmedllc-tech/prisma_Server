## Chief Complaint

Right-sided weakness and confusion

## Vignette

A 68-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after suddenly developing right-sided weakness and difficulty speaking. CT scan of the head reveals a large left middle cerebral artery infarction with surrounding cerebral edema. Over the next several hours, the patient becomes progressively somnolent. Repeat examination shows BP 210/110 mm Hg, HR 48/min and irregular respirations. Fundoscopy demonstrates papilledema. Due to concern for impending brain herniation, the patient is intubated and mechanically hyperventilated. Shortly afterward, intracranial pressure decreases and neurologic status transiently improves.

Which of the following best explains the mechanism by which hyperventilation reduced this patient’s intracranial pressure?
