## Vignette

A 67-year-old man presents to the emergency department with fever, productive cough, and pleuritic chest pain for 5 days. Chest radiograph demonstrates right lower lobe consolidation with a moderate pleural effusion. Thoracentesis reveals cloudy pleural fluid with low glucose, low pH, elevated lactate dehydrogenase, and numerous neutrophils. Gram stain shows gram-positive cocci in pairs. The patient is started on intravenous ceftriaxone and a second medication that inhibits bacterial protein synthesis by binding to the 50S ribosomal subunit. Which of the following adverse effects is most strongly associated with this second medication?
