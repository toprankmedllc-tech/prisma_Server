## Chief Complaint

Aborted sudden cardiac arrest during athletic practice

## Vignette

A 17-year-old high school basketball player collapses during practice and is found to have no pulse. He is resuscitated successfully after defibrillation. He reports two prior episodes of presyncope during sprinting drills over the past year. His father died suddenly at age 32 while jogging. On examination after stabilization, a harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur is heard at the left lower sternal border. The murmur becomes louder when he stands from a squatting position and softer when he grips a handle tightly. 
Which of the following is the most likely underlying cause of this patient's condition?
