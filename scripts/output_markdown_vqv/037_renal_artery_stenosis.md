## Vignette

A 68-year-old man presents to the clinic because of worsening hypertension despite adherence to multiple antihypertensive medications. He also reports intermittent headaches and increasing fatigue over the past several months. His medical history is significant for hyperlipidemia, peripheral arterial disease, and a 45-pack-year smoking history. Blood pressure is 186/102 mmHg, pulse is 84/min, and respiratory rate is 16/min. Physical examination reveals an abdominal bruit lateral to the umbilicus. Laboratory studies show serum creatinine of 1.1 mg/dL. Lisinopril therapy is initiated, and 1 week later his serum creatinine rises to 2.6 mg/dL. Duplex ultrasonography demonstrates marked narrowing of the proximal renal artery.
Which of the following best explains this patient’s rise in creatinine after initiation of therapy?
