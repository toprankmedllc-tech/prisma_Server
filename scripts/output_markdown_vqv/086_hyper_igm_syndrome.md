## Chief Complaint

Recurrent ear infections and pneumonia

## Vignette

A 10-month-old boy is brought to the clinic because of recurrent ear infections and two episodes of pneumonia since birth. His mother says he also had prolonged diarrhea after a daycare outbreak several weeks ago. He was born at term and had no complications during delivery. Physical examination shows mild cervical lymphadenopathy and scattered oral white plaques that can be scraped off. Laboratory studies show normal total lymphocyte count and normal numbers of circulating B cells. Serum immunoglobulin testing shows markedly increased IgM with decreased IgG, IgA, and IgE.
