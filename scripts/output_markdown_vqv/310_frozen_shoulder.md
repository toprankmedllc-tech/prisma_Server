## Chief Complaint

Shoulder pain and stiffness 

## Vignette

‏ 55-year-old woman presents to the outpatient clinic with progressive right shoulder stiffness and pain for the past 6 months. She reports that the pain initially started gradually and was worse at night, but over time the pain has slightly improved while stiffness has worsened significantly. She now struggles to comb her hair, reach overhead, or fasten her bra.

‏She has a medical history of type 2 diabetes mellitus (poorly controlled for 12 years), hypothyroidism, and hyperlipidemia. She denies trauma.

‏Physical examination shows:

* No erythema or swelling of the shoulder
* Markedly reduced active and passive range of motion in all directions
* External rotation is most limited
* Strength testing is normal but limited by pain and stiffness

‏Neer and Hawkins impingement tests are negative.

‏Plain radiograph of the shoulder is normal.

‏Which of the following is the most likely pathophysiologic mechanism underlying this condition?
