## Chief Complaint

Severe cyanosis

## Vignette

A full-term male newborn develops severe cyanosis and tachypnea within minutes of birth. Pulse oximetry remains low despite administration of 100% oxygen. Physical examination reveals a single loud S2 without a murmur. Chest radiograph demonstrates a narrow superior mediastinum with increased pulmonary vascular markings. Echocardiography shows that survival currently depends on a patent ductus arteriosus.

Which of the following embryologic abnormalities most likely caused this patient’s condition?
