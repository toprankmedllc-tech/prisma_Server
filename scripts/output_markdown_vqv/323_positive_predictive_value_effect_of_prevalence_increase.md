## Vignette

Researchers are evaluating a new rapid blood test for early detection of a rare autoimmune disease in a population screening program. The test has high sensitivity and specificity and is initially implemented in a region where the disease prevalence is low. After several years, due to environmental and demographic changes, the prevalence of the disease in the same population increases significantly.

Which of the following is the most likely consequence of this change?
