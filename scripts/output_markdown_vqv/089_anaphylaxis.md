## Chief Complaint

Sudden shortness of breath and lightheadedness

## Vignette

A 16-year-old girl is brought to the emergency department because of sudden shortness of breath and lightheadedness that began several minutes after eating a cookie at a school event. She has a history of eczema and seasonal allergies. On arrival, she is anxious and has diffuse itching, facial swelling, and scattered wheezes bilaterally. Physical examination shows generalized urticaria and mild swelling of the lips. Serum tryptase is increased.
