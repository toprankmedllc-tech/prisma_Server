## Vignette

A 19-year-old  employee becomes famous among his coworkers for consistently winning the “high striker” hammer game, where players use explosive force to ring a bell with a single powerful strike. He can also perform extremely rapid vertical jumps during a local athletic competition. However, during a charity 10-km run later that day, he becomes exhausted within the first few minutes and is unable to maintain his pace.
As part of a university exercise physiology project, a biopsy sample is taken from his calf muscles. The sample demonstrates large pale fibers with sparse capillary networks, low myoglobin concentration, and high glycogen stores. The fibers generate rapid, forceful contractions but fatigue quickly.
Which of the following muscle fiber types is most likely predominant in this individual?
