## Vignette

A 28-year-old man is enrolled in an exercise physiology study at a university research lab. He performs a graded treadmill exercise protocol while continuous hemodynamic monitoring is performed. At peak exercise, his heart rate has increased from 68 to 168/min, his stroke volume has increased from 70 mL to 110 mL, his systolic blood pressure has risen from 122 to 188 mm Hg, and his diastolic blood pressure has fallen from 78 to 64 mm Hg.
Which of the following substances released by exercising skeletal muscle most directly contributes to the decrease in his diastolic blood pressure?

## Physical Exam

Hemodynamic response to graded exercise: ↑ HR (68 → 168/min); ↑ SV (70 → 110 mL); ↑ systolic BP (122 → 188 mm Hg); ↓ diastolic BP (78 → 64 mm Hg)

## Main Clue

Healthy young adult performing exercise + rising stroke volume + ↑ systolic BP + ↓ diastolic BP indicating ↓ systemic vascular resistance from local skeletal muscle vasodilation

## Supporting Clue

The widening of the pulse pressure (systolic ↑, diastolic ↓) during exercise reflects two simultaneous mechanisms: increased stroke volume raising systolic pressure, and decreased SVR from skeletal muscle vasodilation lowering diastolic pressure
