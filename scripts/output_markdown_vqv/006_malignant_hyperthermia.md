## Vignette

A 20-year-old man is brought to the emergency department immediately after undergoing an emergency appendectomy. About 20 minutes after induction of anesthesia, he developed generalized muscle rigidity and a rapid rise in end-tidal CO₂ despite adequate ventilation. His vital signs during the episode were: temperature 40.2°C, blood pressure 160/95 mmHg, heart rate 145/min, respiratory rate 28/min, and oxygen saturation 96% on mechanical ventilation. The anesthesiologist immediately discontinued the inhaled anesthetic and administered an emergency medication, after which the patient’s condition began to improve. His father reportedly experienced a similar reaction during surgery many years ago.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying pathophysiologic mechanism responsible for this patient’s condition?
