## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 62-year-old woman presents to the clinic for increasing fatigue and recurrent infections over the past 6 months. She has a 25-year history of seropositive rheumatoid arthritis treated intermittently with methotrexate and low-dose prednisone. She reports frequent episodes of sinusitis and one recent hospitalization for pneumonia.
She also notes painless swelling in the left upper abdomen and early satiety. Over the past few months, she has unintentionally lost 4 kg (8.8 lbs).
Physical examination reveals:
* Temperature: 37.8°C (100.0°F)
* Blood pressure: 128/76 mmHg
* Pulse: 92/min
* Chronic deforming hand arthritis with ulnar deviation and swan-neck deformities
* Splenomegaly 5 cm below costal margin
* No lymphadenopathy
Laboratory findings:
Test	Result
WBC	2,200/μL
Neutrophils	800/μL
Hemoglobin	10.2 g/dL
Platelets	120,000/μL
ESR	Elevated
Peripheral smear shows no blasts. Rheumatoid factor is strongly positive.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying mechanism of her current condition?
