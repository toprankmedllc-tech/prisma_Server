## Chief Complaint

Excessive hand washing and intrusive contamination fears

## Vignette

A 26-year-old woman comes to the clinic because she spends several hours each day repeatedly washing her hands. She reports persistent thoughts that her hands are “contaminated with dangerous germs” after touching common objects such as door handles or money. Although she recognizes that these fears are excessive, she feels intense anxiety if she tries to resist washing her hands. Recently, she has been late to work multiple times because of prolonged cleaning rituals. She denies hallucinations, delusions, or substance use.

Which of the following is the most appropriate first-line treatment for this patient?

## Key Symptoms

Intrusive thoughts; contamination obsession; repetitive hand washing; anxiety when resisting compulsions; excessive cleaning rituals; preserved insight; functional impairment
