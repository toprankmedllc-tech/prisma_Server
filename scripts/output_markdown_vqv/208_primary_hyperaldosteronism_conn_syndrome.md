## Vignette

A 42-year-old woman comes to the clinic for evaluation of persistent hypertension despite treatment with hydrochlorothiazide, amlodipine, and lisinopril. She reports intermittent muscle cramps, fatigue, and increased urination. Physical examination is unremarkable except for elevated blood pressure. Laboratory studies reveal hypokalemia and metabolic alkalosis. Plasma aldosterone concentration is elevated, and plasma renin activity is suppressed. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
