## Chief Complaint

Nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain for 2 days

## Vignette

A 34-year-old female with a history of type 1 diabetes mellitus is brought to the emergency department by her roommate after two days of nausea, vomiting, and diffuse abdominal pain. She reports increased thirst and urination for the past week and admits to missing several insulin doses. She denies fever, diarrhea, or recent illness. Her blood pressure is 102/68 mmHg, heart rate is 112 bpm, pulse oximetry is 98% on room air, and temperature is 37.2°C. On examination, she is breathing deeply and rapidly, her mucous membranes are dry, skin turgor is decreased, and her breath has a fruity odor. Diffuse abdominal tenderness is noted without guarding or rebound. Laboratory studies reveal a pH of 7.22, bicarbonate of 10 mEq/L, serum potassium of 5.8 mEq/L, sodium of 132 mEq/L, glucose of 480 mg/dL, anion gap of 22, and large urine ketones. Which of the following best explains the elevated serum potassium in this patient despite total body potassium depletion?

## Supporting Clue
