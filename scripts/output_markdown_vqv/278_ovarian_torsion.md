## Chief Complaint

Sudden severe right lower abdominal pain

## Vignette

A 27-year-old nulliparous woman comes to the emergency department due to sudden onset severe right lower abdominal pain for the past 4 hours. The pain began abruptly while exercising and is associated with nausea and vomiting. She has a history of ovarian cysts but no prior surgeries. Her menstrual cycles are regular, and a urine pregnancy test is negative. Physical examination reveals right adnexal tenderness with guarding. Transvaginal Doppler ultrasound demonstrates an enlarged edematous right ovary with decreased blood flow. Which of the following is the most likely underlying mechanism of this patient’s condition?

## Main Clue

## Supporting Clue

History of ovarian cysts predisposing to twisting of ovarian ligaments
