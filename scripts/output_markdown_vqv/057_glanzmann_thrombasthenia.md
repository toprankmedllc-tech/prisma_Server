## Chief Complaint

Recurrent nosebleeds and easy bruising

## Vignette

A 5-year-old boy is brought to the clinic because of recurrent nosebleeds and easy bruising. His parents say he had prolonged bleeding after circumcision as a newborn and continues to bleed for a long time after minor cuts. There is no history of joint swelling or deep muscle bleeding. Physical examination shows scattered petechiae and several small ecchymoses on the lower extremities. Laboratory studies show a normal platelet count, normal PT, and normal PTT. Platelet aggregation studies show absent aggregation in response to ADP, epinephrine, and collagen.
