A 21-year-old college football player begins an intensive resistance-training program during the offseason. Over the next several months, he notices a marked increase in muscle size and strength, particularly in his arms and thighs. A muscle biopsy performed as part of a sports physiology study demonstrates enlargement of individual skeletal muscle fibers due to increased synthesis of contractile proteins and addition of new myofibrils within existing muscle cells.
A medical student asks how skeletal muscle fibers are able to increase their diameter in response to heavy weight training.
