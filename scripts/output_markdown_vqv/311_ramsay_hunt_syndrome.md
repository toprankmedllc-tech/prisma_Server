## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 68-year-old woman presents with severe right ear pain and progressive facial weakness for 2 days. She reports a history of poorly controlled diabetes mellitus, rheumatoid arthritis treated with chronic prednisone, and childhood chickenpox.
Three days earlier she developed burning pain deep inside the ear followed by a vesicular eruption.
She now reports difficulty closing her right eye and says “food tastes strange.”
Vital signs are normal.
Physical examination demonstrates:
* inability to wrinkle the right forehead
* inability to close the right eye
* flattening of the nasolabial fold
* drooping of the right mouth corner
Otoscopic examination reveals clusters of vesicles in the external auditory canal.
Taste sensation over the anterior tongue is diminished.
Hearing is mildly decreased on the right.
