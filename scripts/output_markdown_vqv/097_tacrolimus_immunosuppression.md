## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 45-year-old man comes to the clinic for follow-up 6 weeks after receiving a kidney transplant. He has been taking a multidrug immunosuppressive regimen since surgery. He reports new mild hand tremors and increased thirst over the past 2 weeks. Blood pressure is 152/92 mm Hg. Laboratory studies show serum creatinine of 1.8 mg/dL, fasting glucose of 178 mg/dL, hemoglobin A1c of 7.1%, and potassium of 4.9 mEq/L. The physician explains that one of his medications prevents graft rejection by reducing cytokine-driven T-cell activation.
