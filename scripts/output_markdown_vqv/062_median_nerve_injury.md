## Vignette

A 7-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department after falling from the monkey bars onto his outstretched hand. He immediately developed severe pain and swelling around the elbow and is unable to move his left arm. Physical examination reveals marked swelling and tenderness over the distal humerus. The forearm is held slightly flexed, and there is decreased sensation over the lateral palm and palmar aspect of the first three digits. When asked to make a fist, he has difficulty flexing the index and middle fingers. Radial pulse is present.
X-ray imaging demonstrates a displaced supracondylar fracture of the humerus.
Which of the following nerves was most likely injured in this patient?
