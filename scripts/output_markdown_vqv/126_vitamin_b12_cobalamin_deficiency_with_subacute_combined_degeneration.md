## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 56-year-old man presents with fatigue, difficulty walking, and numbness in both feet for the past several months. He reports a history of chronic alcohol use. Vital signs show temperature of 36.9°C, heart rate of 84/min, blood pressure of 118/72 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 14/min. Physical examination reveals decreased vibration and position sense in the lower limbs and a broad-based gait. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 9.8 g/dL, MCV of 112 fL, WBC count of 4,200/mm³, platelet count of 132,000/mm³, serum vitamin B12 of 148 pg/mL, methylmalonic acid of 1,240 nmol/L (reference range <370 nmol/L), and homocysteine of 28 µmol/L (reference range 5–15 µmol/L). Peripheral smear shows macro-ovalocytes and hypersegmented neutrophils.
