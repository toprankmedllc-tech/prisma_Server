## Chief Complaint

Discussion of biopsy results

## Vignette

A 52-year-old man comes to the outpatient oncology clinic for discussion of biopsy results confirming gastric adenocarcinoma. He recently immigrated from China and speaks limited English. When the physician enters the room, the patient’s adult son states, “My father understands some English, so you can just speak to me and I’ll explain things to him.” During the conversation, the son repeatedly answers questions before the patient can respond and asks the physician not to mention the word “cancer” because “it would destroy his hope.” The physician notices that the patient appears confused at several points and occasionally attempts to ask questions in Mandarin. A professionally trained video medical interpreter is immediately available through the clinic.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?
