## Chief Complaint

shortness of breath; chronic cough

## Vignette

A 58-year-old man presents with progressive shortness of breath and a chronic productive cough for the past 6 years. He reports producing large amounts of sputum every morning and has had multiple hospitalizations for respiratory infections during the winter months. He has smoked 2 packs of cigarettes daily for 35 years. His past medical history is insignificant. Physical examination shows cyanosis, bilateral wheezing, and pitting edema of the lower extremities. Pulse oximetry shows oxygen saturation of 88% at room air. His laboratory investigations has shown that hematocrit is 57%, PaO2 is 58% and PaCO2 is 62 mm Hg. Chest radiograph shows prominent bronchovascular markings and cardiomegaly. Histologic examination of the bronchi would most likely demonstrate which of the following changes?
