## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old woman presents with fatigue and brittle nails. Physical examination reveals spoon-shaped nails. Laboratory studies show low ferritin and low serum iron. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

28-year-old woman; fatigue; brittle nails; spoon-shaped nails; low ferritin; low serum iron

## Answer Options

A) Iron deficiency anemia
B) Thalassemia
C) Sideroblastic anemia
D) Hemolytic anemia

## Correct Answer

A. Iron deficiency anemia

## Explanation

Iron deficiency anemia is caused by depleted iron stores, most commonly from chronic blood loss such as menorrhagia. It presents with microcytic hypochromic anemia, low ferritin, and epithelial changes like koilonychia (spoon-shaped nails).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Low ferritin + koilonychia + microcytosis = Iron deficiency anemia

## Wrong Options

### B. Thalassemia

Thalassemia causes microcytic anemia due to reduced globin chain synthesis, typically with normal or increased ferritin and target cells. Koilonychia and low ferritin are not features.

**Buzzword Combo:** Target cells + normal ferritin + ↑HbA2 = Beta-thalassemia

### C. Sideroblastic anemia

Sideroblastic anemia involves defective heme synthesis leading to iron accumulation in mitochondria and ring sideroblasts in bone marrow. It is associated with high ferritin, not low ferritin.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ring sideroblasts + high ferritin + basophilic stippling = Sideroblastic anemia

### D. Hemolytic anemia

Hemolytic anemia is characterized by increased RBC destruction with elevated LDH, low haptoglobin, and reticulocytosis. Iron stores are typically normal or increased, not depleted.

**Buzzword Combo:** ↑LDH + ↓haptoglobin + reticulocytosis = Hemolytic anemia
