## Buzzword Question

A 12-year-old boy develops yellowing of the eyes and dark urine several days after a viral illness. Laboratory studies show increased unconjugated bilirubin and elevated LDH, and peripheral smear shows erythrocytes with irregular semicircular membrane defects. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Boy; yellowing of the eyes and dark urine after viral illness; increased unconjugated bilirubin and elevated LDH; erythrocytes with irregular semicircular membrane defects

## Answer Options

A) Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency
B) Hereditary spherocytosis
C) Pyruvate kinase deficiency
D) Sickle cell disease

## Correct Answer

A) Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency


## Explanation

Glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deficiency causes episodic hemolysis after oxidative stress. Decreased NADPH leads to decreased reduced glutathione, oxidative RBC damage, and splenic removal of damaged RBC portions.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Boy + hemolysis after viral illness + semicircular RBC membrane defects = G6PD deficiency

## Wrong Options

### B) Hereditary spherocytosis

Hereditary spherocytosis causes hemolysis due to RBC membrane protein defects. It is associated with spherocytes, splenomegaly, and increased MCHC rather than oxidative stress-triggered hemolysis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Spherocytes + splenomegaly + increased MCHC = hereditary spherocytosis

### C) Pyruvate kinase deficiency

Pyruvate kinase deficiency causes hemolytic anemia from impaired ATP production. It is not the classic cause of hemolysis after oxidative stress.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic hemolytic anemia + decreased ATP + echinocytes = pyruvate kinase deficiency

### D) Sickle cell disease

Sickle cell disease is caused by beta-globin polymerization. It presents with vaso-occlusive pain episodes and crescent-shaped erythrocytes rather than oxidative hemolysis after infection.

**Buzzword Combo:** Painful vaso-occlusive episodes + autosplenectomy + crescent-shaped RBCs = sickle cell disease
