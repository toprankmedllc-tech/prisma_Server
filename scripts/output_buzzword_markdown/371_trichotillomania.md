## Buzzword Question

An adolescent girl has patchy scalp hair loss with hairs of varying lengths and admits to repeatedly pulling out her hair during stressful situations. Which is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Patchy hair loss; hairs of varying lengths; hair pulling; stress-related behavior

## Answer Options

 A) Trichotillomania
 B) Alopecia areata
 C) Tinea capitis
 D) Obsessive-compulsive disorder


## Correct Answer

A. Trichotillomania


## Explanation

Trichotillomania presents with compulsive hair pulling leading to patchy alopecia with irregular hairs of different lengths, commonly triggered by stress.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hair pulling + patchy alopecia + hairs of different lengths = Trichotillomania

## Wrong Options

### B. Alopecia areata


Alopecia areata causes smooth circular patches of hair loss without evidence of broken hairs.


**Buzzword Combo:** Smooth bald patches + autoimmune disease + exclamation point hairs = Alopecia areata


### C. Tinea capitis


Tinea capitis is a fungal scalp infection associated with scaling and inflammation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Scalp scaling + fungal infection + cervical lymphadenopathy = Tinea capitis


### D. Obsessive-compulsive disorder


OCD involves intrusive thoughts and compulsions rather than isolated hair-pulling behavior.


**Buzzword Combo:** Intrusive thoughts + repetitive rituals + anxiety relief = OCD
