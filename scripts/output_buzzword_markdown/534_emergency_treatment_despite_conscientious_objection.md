## Buzzword Question

A patient arrives in the emergency department after a motor vehicle collision with severe blood loss and hypotension. The emergency physician personally objects to blood transfusions for religious reasons. No other physician is immediately available, and delaying treatment could place the patient at significant risk of death.

Which of the following is the most appropriate action by the physician?

## Buzzwords Used

Emergency care ; Hemorrhagic shock ; Physician religious objection ; Life-saving treatment

## Answer Options

A) Refuse transfusion because physicians should not violate their religious beliefs

B) Delay treatment until another physician becomes available

C) Provide life-saving transfusion

D) Ask the patient’s family to decide whether treatment should proceed

## Correct Answer

C) Provide life-saving transfusion

## Explanation

In emergency situations where delay threatens life and no alternative physician is immediately available, physicians must provide necessary stabilizing treatment regardless of personal moral objections.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Emergency situation + no alternative physician + life-saving care = treat immediately

## Wrong Options

### A) Refuse transfusion because physicians should not violate their religious beliefs

Personal beliefs do not justify withholding emergency life-saving treatment when no alternative provider is available.

**Buzzword Combo:** Religious objection + emergency care + refusal of treatment = unethical withholding

### B) Delay treatment until another physician becomes available

Delaying urgent treatment in a life-threatening emergency places the patient at unnecessary risk.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hemorrhagic shock + treatment delay + avoidable risk = incorrect management

### D) Ask the patient’s family to decide whether treatment should proceed

Immediate emergency stabilization should not be delayed for surrogate decision-making when urgent treatment is required.

**Buzzword Combo:** Life-threatening emergency + unnecessary delay + surrogate dependence = inappropriate action
