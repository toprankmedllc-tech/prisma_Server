## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old man presents to the orthopedic clinic with numbness and tingling in his right hand several weeks after falling while playing baseball. He reports persistent pain along the medial side of the wrist that worsens when gripping a bat. Over time, he has noticed weakness when spreading his fingers apart and difficulty holding objects firmly. Physical examination reveals decreased strength with finger abduction and adduction.Tenderness is present over the hypothenar eminence near the wrist.
Which of the following sensory distributions is most likely affected in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

persistent pain along the medial side of the wrist; weakness when spreading his fingers apart ;decreased strength with finger abduction and adduction; Tenderness is present over the hypothenar eminence

## Answer Options

A. Palmar and dorsal surfaces of the little finger and medial half of the ring finger
B. Palmar surface of the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of the ring finger
C. Dorsal surface of the lateral hand and posterior thumb web space
D. Skin over the lateral shoulder and upper arm

## Correct Answer

A. Palmar and dorsal surfaces of the little finger and medial half of the ring finger

## Explanation

This patient most likely has a fracture of the hook of the hamate causing compression or injury of the ulnar nerve within Guyon canal. This patient’s symptoms: Medial wrist pain after sports trauma ,Weakness of finger abduction/adduction ,Sensory loss in the medial digits suggest ulnar injury.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Baseball or racket injury + hypothenar pain + intrinsic hand weakness = hook of hamate fracture with ulnar nerve injury

## Wrong Options

### B. Palmar surface of the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of the ring finger

Incorrect — This sensory distribution is supplied by the median nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Nighttime paresthesias + thenar weakness + positive Tinel sign = carpal tunnel syndrome

### C. Dorsal surface of the lateral hand and posterior thumb web space

Incorrect — This area is primarily supplied by the radial nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Wrist drop + impaired extension + dorsal hand sensory loss = radial nerve injury

### D. Skin over the lateral shoulder and upper arm

Incorrect — This sensory region is supplied by the axillary nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Shoulder dislocation + difficulty abducting arm = axillary nerve injury
