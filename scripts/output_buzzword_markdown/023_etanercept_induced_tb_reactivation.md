## Buzzword Question

A 50-year-old woman with severe Rheumatoid arthritis is started on Etanercept after failing conventional therapy. Three months later, she develops fever, chronic cough, night sweats, and unintentional weight loss. Chest imaging reveals upper lobe cavitary lesions, and sputum analysis is positive for acid-fast bacilli.
Which of the following normal immune functions was most likely impaired by this medication?

## Buzzwords Used

 Etanercept; chronic cough; night sweats; unintentional weight loss; upper lobe cavitary lesions; acid-fast bacilli

## Answer Options

A. Maintenance of granuloma integrity by macrophage activation
B. Production of IgE antibodies against extracellular parasites
C. Neutrophil migration and adhesion to vascular endothelium
D. Complement-mediated lysis of encapsulated bacteria

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

TNF-α inhibitors such as etanercept impair granuloma formation and maintenance, increasing the risk of reactivation of latent Tuberculosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Etanercept + cavitary lung lesions + night sweats = TB reactivation

## Wrong Options

### B. Production of IgE antibodies against extracellular parasites

Incorrect — IgE-mediated immunity is primarily involved in allergic reactions and parasitic infections.

**Buzzword Combo:** Eosinophilia + elevated IgE = parasitic/allergic disease

### C. Neutrophil migration and adhesion to vascular endothelium

Incorrect — Defects in neutrophil adhesion are classically seen in Leukocyte adhesion deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Delayed umbilical cord separation + recurrent bacterial infections = leukocyte adhesion deficiency

### D. Complement-mediated lysis of encapsulated bacteria

Incorrect — Complement deficiency predisposes to infections such as Neisseria species.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recurrent Neisseria infections = complement deficiency
