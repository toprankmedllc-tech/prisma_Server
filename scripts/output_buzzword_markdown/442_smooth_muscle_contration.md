## Buzzword Question

A 34-year-old man is brought to the emergency department with severe renal colic caused by a ureteral stone. During evaluation, he develops intense spasmodic pain due to strong contraction of smooth muscle within the ureter. A medical student accompanying the team asks how smooth muscle contraction is regulated differently from skeletal muscle contraction. The attending physician explains that increased intracellular calcium activates a specific enzyme responsible for phosphorylating myosin and initiating contraction in smooth muscle cells.
Which of the following enzymes is most directly activated during smooth muscle contraction in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

increased intracellular calcium activates a specific enzyme responsible for phosphorylating myosin and initiating contraction in smooth muscle cells.

## Answer Options

A. Troponin kinase
B. Myosin light-chain kinase
C. Creatine kinase
D. Adenylate cyclase

## Correct Answer

B. Myosin light-chain kinase

## Explanation

Myosin light-chain kinase (MLCK) is the key enzyme responsible for initiating smooth muscle contraction. Increased intracellular calcium binds to calmodulin, forming a Ca2+–calmodulin complex that activates MLCK. Activated MLCK phosphorylates myosin light chains, allowing interaction between actin and myosin and producing contraction.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Smooth muscle + Ca2+–calmodulin + myosin phosphorylation = Myosin light-chain kinase

## Wrong Options

### A. Troponin kinase

Smooth muscle lacks troponin; contraction is regulated through calmodulin and MLCK instead.

**Buzzword Combo:** Troponin + thin filament regulation + skeletal/cardiac muscle = striated muscle contraction

### C. Creatine kinase

Creatine kinase functions in rapid ATP regeneration during muscle activity but does not regulate smooth muscle contraction.

**Buzzword Combo:** ATP regeneration + phosphocreatine shuttle + energy reserve = creatine kinase

### D. Adenylate cyclase

Adenylate cyclase produces cAMP and is involved in signaling pathways rather than direct initiation of smooth muscle contraction.

**Buzzword Combo:** cAMP production + second messenger signaling + G protein pathway = adenylate cyclase
