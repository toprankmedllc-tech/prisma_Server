## Buzzword Question

A young child with a history of severe neglect rarely seeks comfort from caregivers, shows minimal emotional responsiveness, and appears fearful even in safe situations. Which is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Neglect; emotionally withdrawn; rarely seeks comfort; fearful behavior; foster care


## Answer Options

 A) Reactive attachment disorder
 B) Autism spectrum disorder
 C) Social anxiety disorder
 D) Oppositional defiant disorder

## Correct Answer

A. Reactive attachment disorder


## Explanation

Reactive attachment disorder is caused by severe neglect or inconsistent caregiving and presents with emotionally withdrawn behavior and failure to seek comfort from caregivers.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Early neglect + withdrawn behavior + failure to seek comfort = Reactive attachment disorder


## Wrong Options

### B. Autism spectrum disorder


Autism spectrum disorder includes social communication deficits and repetitive behaviors but is not directly caused by neglect.


**Buzzword Combo:** Restricted interests + repetitive behaviors + impaired social communication = Autism spectrum disorder


### C. Social anxiety disorder


Social anxiety disorder involves fear of embarrassment or judgment during social interactions.


**Buzzword Combo:** Fear of scrutiny + avoidance of social situations + anxiety = Social anxiety disorder

### D. Oppositional defiant disorder


ODD presents with angry, defiant, and argumentative behavior rather than attachment disturbances.

**Buzzword Combo:** Defiant behavior + irritability + argumentativeness = Oppositional defiant disorder
