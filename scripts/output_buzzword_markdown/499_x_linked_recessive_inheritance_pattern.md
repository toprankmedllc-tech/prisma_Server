## Buzzword Question

A bleeding disorder affects all males who inherit the mutation but does not affect carrier females. Affected fathers never transmit the condition to their sons but all their daughters become carriers. The condition appears to skip generations when transmitted through asymptomatic carrier females. This pattern is consistent with:

## Buzzwords Used

Males affected; carrier females unaffected; no father-to-son transmission; daughters of affected fathers all carriers; generation-skipping through females

## Answer Options

A) X-linked recessive 
B) Autosomal dominant 
C) Autosomal recessive 
D) X-linked dominant

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

X-linked recessive: (1) primarily affects males (hemizygous, one X, one mutant allele sufficient); (2) carrier females unaffected (heterozygous,  second normal X compensates); (3) father-to-son transmission IMPOSSIBLE (fathers give Y to sons, not X); (4) affected fathers → ALL daughters are carriers (receive father's mutant X); (5) appears to skip generations through asymptomatic carrier females. Classic examples: hemophilia A/B, DMD, G6PD deficiency, color blindness.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Males affected + carrier females unaffected + no father-to-son + all daughters of affected fathers are carriers = X-linked recessive

## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Autosomal dominant

Autosomal dominant: (1) both sexes affected equally; (2) father-to-son transmission IS possible (autosome); (3) every generation typically affected (50% offspring risk). None match the described pattern.

**Buzzword Combo:** Both sexes affected + father-to-son possible + every generation + 50% offspring risk = Autosomal dominant


### 2: C) Autosomal recessive

Autosomal recessive: (1) both sexes affected equally (no sex predilection); (2) both parents typically carriers; (3) horizontal pattern (siblings affected, parents not). The exclusive male involvement and maternal carrier transmission described is inconsistent with AR.

**Buzzword Combo:** Both sexes affected equally + two unaffected carrier parents + 25% risk per sibling = Autosomal recessive


### 3: D) X-linked dominant


X-linked dominant: both sexes affected (heterozygous females are symptomatic, though often less severely than hemizygous males). The described pattern shows unaffected carrier females — females with the mutation are NOT affected,  specifically indicating X-linked recessive, not dominant.

**Buzzword Combo:** Both sexes affected (females with one copy still affected, often less severely) + no carrier state = X-linked dominant
