## Buzzword Question

A 22-year-old woman with easy bruising, petechiae after a recent viral infection, and isolated thrombocytopenia most likely has which laboratory findings?

## Buzzwords Used

young woman; bruising; petechiae; viral infection; isolated thrombocytopenia

## Answer Options

A) Prolonged PT
B) Prolonged aPTT
C) Normal PT and aPTT
D) Elevated fibrinogen

## Correct Answer

C. Normal PT and aPTT

## Explanation

In immune thrombocytopenic purpura, the problem is platelet destruction rather than coagulation factor deficiency. Therefore, PT and aPTT remain normal, with only platelet count decreased.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Isolated thrombocytopenia + normal PT + normal aPTT = ITP

## Wrong Options

### A) Prolonged PT

Prolonged PT suggests an extrinsic pathway defect such as factor VII deficiency, warfarin use, or vitamin K deficiency. It is not seen in isolated platelet disorders like ITP.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prolonged PT + normal aPTT = Extrinsic pathway defect

### B) Prolonged aPTT

Prolonged aPTT indicates intrinsic pathway defects such as hemophilia A, hemophilia B, or heparin use. ITP does not affect coagulation factors.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prolonged aPTT + normal PT = Hemophilia

### D) Elevated fibrinogen

Fibrinogen is an acute-phase reactant and is not a diagnostic feature of ITP. Coagulation factor levels remain normal in ITP.Normal PT/aPTT + thrombocytopenia = ITP

**Buzzword Combo:** Normal PT/aPTT + thrombocytopenia = ITP
