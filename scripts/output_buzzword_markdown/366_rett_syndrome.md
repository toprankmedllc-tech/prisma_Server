## Buzzword Question

A neurodevelopmental disorder caused by an X-linked dominant MECP2 mutation presents almost exclusively in girls because affected male fetuses usually die in utero. Which disorder is this?


## Buzzwords Used

MECP2 mutation; X-linked dominant; girls only; developmental regression


## Answer Options

 A) Rett syndrome
 B) Tuberous sclerosis
 C) Fragile X syndrome
 D) Prader-Willi syndrome


## Correct Answer

A. Rett syndrome


## Explanation

Rett syndrome is caused by an X-linked dominant mutation in the MECP2 gene and almost exclusively affects girls because affected male fetuses often do not survive.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

MECP2 mutation + X-linked dominant + female predominance = Rett syndrome

## Wrong Options

### B. Tuberous sclerosis


Tuberous sclerosis is associated with hamartomas, seizures, and ash-leaf spots due to TSC1/TSC2 mutations.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ash-leaf spots + seizures + hamartomas = Tuberous sclerosis

### C. Fragile X syndrome


Fragile X syndrome is caused by CGG trinucleotide repeat expansion in the FMR1 gene.


**Buzzword Combo:** CGG repeat expansion + macroorchidism + intellectual disability = Fragile X syndrome


### D. Prader-Willi syndrome


Prader-Willi syndrome presents with hyperphagia, obesity, and hypotonia due to paternal chromosome 15 deletion.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hyperphagia + obesity + hypotonia = Prader-Willi syndrome
