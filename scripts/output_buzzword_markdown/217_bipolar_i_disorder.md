## Buzzword Question

A patient presents with grandiosity, pressured speech, decreased need for sleep, excessive spending, and flight of ideas lasting for 8 days with severe social dysfunction. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Grandiosity; decreased need for sleep; pressured speech; flight of ideas; severe dysfunction

## Answer Options

A) Bipolar I disorder
B) Bipolar II disorder
C) Cyclothymic disorder
D) Generalized anxiety disorder

## Correct Answer

A. Bipolar I disorder

## Explanation

Bipolar I disorder requires at least one manic episode causing marked social or occupational impairment. Symptoms such as grandiosity, decreased need for sleep, pressured speech, and risky behavior are classic manifestations of mania.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Grandiosity + decreased need for sleep + risky behavior with social dysfunction = Bipolar I disorder

## Wrong Options

### B. Bipolar II disorder

Bipolar II disorder involves hypomanic episodes, which are less severe and do not cause marked impairment or psychosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hypomania + major depression + no hospitalization = Bipolar II disorder

### C. Cyclothymic disorder

Cyclothymic disorder involves milder chronic mood fluctuations that do not meet full criteria for mania or major depression.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic mood instability + mild hypomanic symptoms + 2 years = Cyclothymia

### D. Generalized anxiety disorder

GAD causes excessive worry and anxiety rather than manic symptoms such as grandiosity and decreased need for sleep.

**Buzzword Combo:** Excessive worry + muscle tension + restlessness = GAD
