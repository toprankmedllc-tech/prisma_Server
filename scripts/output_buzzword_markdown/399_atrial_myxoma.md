## Buzzword Question


A pedunculated mass resected from the left atrium of a patient with positional dyspnea and an early diastolic sound at the apex is examined under microscopy. The most likely histologic finding is:

## Buzzwords Used

Pedunculated left atrial mass; positional dyspnea; early diastolic sound; tumor plop; scattered stellate cells; myxoid stroma

## Answer Options

A) Scattered stellate cells in a mucopolysaccharide-rich myxoid stroma
B) Glycogen-laden "spider cells"
C) Necrotizing granulomas with giant cells
D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining

## Correct Answer

A) Scattered stellate cells in a mucopolysaccharide-rich myxoid stroma

## Explanation

Atrial myxomas are gelatinous tumors composed of scattered stellate cells embedded in a mucopolysaccharide-rich myxoid stroma. This characteristic histology corresponds to the soft, friable gross appearance and explains the tumor's tendency to fragment and embolize.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)


Scattered stellate cells + myxoid stroma + gelatinous mass = atrial myxoma

## Wrong Options

### B) Glycogen-laden "spider cells"

"Spider cells" are the hallmark of cardiac rhabdomyoma, the most common primary cardiac tumor in children, strongly associated with tuberous sclerosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Spider cells + tuberous sclerosis = cardiac rhabdomyoma

### C) Necrotizing granulomas with giant cells


Necrotizing granulomas are seen in granulomatous diseases (TB, sarcoidosis, granulomatosis with polyangiitis), not in cardiac myxomas.

**Buzzword Combo:** Necrotizing granulomas + giant cells = TB / sarcoid / GPA

### D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining

Apple-green birefringence under polarized light with Congo red staining is the hallmark of cardiac amyloidosis, a restrictive cardiomyopathy from extracellular amyloid deposition.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Congo red + apple-green birefringence = amyloidosis
