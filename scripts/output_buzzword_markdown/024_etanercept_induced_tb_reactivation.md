## Buzzword Question

A 44-year-old man with Psoriasis is scheduled to begin therapy with Etanercept. Before starting treatment, his physician orders an interferon-gamma release assay (IGRA).
The primary purpose of this screening test is to prevent which of the following complications?

## Buzzwords Used

Etanercept ;interferon-gamma release assay

## Answer Options

A. Acute hemolytic anemia                                                                                                                        B.  Reactivation of latent tuberculosis infection
C. Stevens-Johnson syndrome
D. Drug-induced pancreatitis

## Correct Answer

B.  Reactivation of latent tuberculosis infection

## Explanation

TNF-α inhibitors significantly increase the risk of reactivation of latent tuberculosis because TNF-α is required for granuloma maintenance and control of latent mycobacterial infection.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

TNF inhibitor + pre-treatment TB screening = prevention of latent TB reactivation

## Wrong Options

### A .Acute hemolytic anemia   

Incorrect — Hemolytic anemia is not the complication of TNF inhibitor therapy. It is associated with Penicillin and Ceftriaxone therapy

**Buzzword Combo:** Low haptoglobin + elevated LDH = complication of  Penicillin, Ceftriaxone

### C. Stevens-Johnson syndrome

Incorrect — Stevens-Johnson syndrome is a severe mucocutaneous drug reaction commonly associated with sulfa drugs, antiepileptics, and allopurinol.

**Buzzword Combo:** Target lesions + mucosal involvement = complication of  sulfa drugs, antiepileptics, and allopurinol.

### D. Drug-induced pancreatitis

Incorrect — Pancreatitis is associated with medications such as azathioprine or valproic acid, not TNF inhibitor screening.

**Buzzword Combo:** Epigastric pain + elevated lipase = complication of azathioprine or valproic acid
