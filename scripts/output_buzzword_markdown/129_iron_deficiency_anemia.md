## Buzzword Question

A 30-year-old woman with fatigue, pallor, brittle nails, heavy menstrual bleeding, and anemia has iron studies performed. Which laboratory pattern is most consistent with iron deficiency anemia?

## Buzzwords Used

30-year-old woman; fatigue; pallor; brittle nails; heavy menstrual bleeding; anemia; iron studies

## Answer Options

A) High ferritin, low TIBC
B) Low ferritin, high TIBC
C) High ferritin, high TIBC
D) Normal ferritin, low TIBC

## Correct Answer

B. Low ferritin, high TIBC

## Explanation

Iron deficiency anemia shows depleted iron stores, resulting in low ferritin. The liver compensates by increasing transferrin production, leading to elevated TIBC.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Low ferritin + high TIBC + menorrhagia = Iron deficiency anemia

## Wrong Options

### A) High ferritin, low TIBC

This pattern is typical of anemia of chronic disease, where iron is trapped in macrophages due to hepcidin. Ferritin is increased as an acute phase reactant and TIBC is reduced.

**Buzzword Combo:** High ferritin + low TIBC + inflammation = Anemia of chronic disease

### C) High ferritin, high TIBC

This is not a classic pattern of any single anemia. Ferritin elevation with high TIBC is inconsistent physiologically and may represent mixed or atypical states.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mixed iron parameters = atypical anemia pattern

### D) Normal ferritin, low TIBC

This pattern may be seen in early anemia of chronic disease or non–iron-related anemia states. It is not characteristic of iron deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Normal ferritin + low TIBC = early anemia of chronic disease
