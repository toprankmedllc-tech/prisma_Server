## Buzzword Question

A newborn is evaluated shortly after a difficult vaginal delivery complicated by excessive upward traction on the infant’s arm. Physical examination reveals weakness of the intrinsic muscles of the hand with a claw-like posture of the fingers. Grip strength is decreased, and sensation is diminished along the medial forearm and hand. Shoulder and elbow movements are normal.
Injury to which of the following structures is the most likely cause of this patient’s condition?

## Buzzwords Used

weakness of the intrinsic muscles of the hand with a claw-like posture of the fingers; Grip strength is decreased; sensation is diminished along the medial forearm and hand

## Answer Options

A. Axillary nerve
B. Lower trunk (C8–T1) of the brachial plexus
C. Upper trunk (C5–C6) of the brachial plexus
D. Radial nerve

## Correct Answer

B. Lower trunk (C8–T1) of the brachial plexus

## Explanation

This patient has Klumpke palsy, which results from injury to the lower trunk (C8–T1) of the brachial plexus. Patients classically develop: Claw hand deformity ,Weak intrinsic hand muscles ,Decreased grip strength ,Sensory loss over the medial forearm and hand .

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Excessive arm traction + claw hand = Klumpke palsy

## Wrong Options

### A. Axillary nerve

it Causes impaired shoulder abduction and sensory loss over the lateral shoulder

**Buzzword Combo:** Surgical neck fracture + inability to abduct arm + lateral shoulder numbness= axillary nerve injury

### C. Upper trunk (C5–C6) of the brachial plexus

it Causes Erb palsy with “waiter’s tip” posture

**Buzzword Combo:** Shoulder dystocia + “waiter’s tip” posture= erbs palsy

### D. Radial nerve

it Causes wrist drop due to impaired wrist extension.

**Buzzword Combo:** Midshaft humeral fracture + wrist drop + impaired extension= radial nerve injury
