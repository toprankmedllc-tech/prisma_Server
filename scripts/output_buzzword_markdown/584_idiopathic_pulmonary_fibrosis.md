## Buzzword Question

A 68-year-old man presents with progressive exertional dyspnea and a chronic dry cough for 2 years. Physical examination reveals bibasilar fine crackles and digital clubbing. High-resolution CT shows lower-lobe predominant honeycombing and subpleural fibrosis. Which of the following pulmonary function test findings is most likely present in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

Progressive dyspnea; chronic dry cough; Velcro crackles; digital clubbing; honeycombing; subpleural fibrosis; restrictive lung disease

## Answer Options

A) ↑ TLC, ↑ FRC, ↓ FEV₁/FVC
B) ↓ TLC, ↓ FRC, normal or ↑ FEV₁/FVC
C) Normal TLC, ↓ DLCO, ↓ FEV₁/FVC
D) ↑ TLC, normal DLCO, normal FEV₁/FVC

## Correct Answer

B) ↓ TLC, ↓ FRC, normal or ↑ FEV₁/FVC

## Explanation

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis is a restrictive lung disease characterized by reduced lung compliance and lung volumes. Pulmonary function tests show decreased TLC and FRC with a normal or increased FEV₁/FVC ratio.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Honeycombing; fine crackles; clubbing = restrictive PFT pattern

## Wrong Options

### A) ↑ TLC, ↑ FRC, ↓ FEV₁/FVC

This is the classic obstructive pattern seen in emphysema and chronic bronchitis, where air trapping increases lung volumes and lowers the FEV₁/FVC ratio.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smoking; hyperinflation; barrel chest = obstructive disease

### C) Normal TLC, ↓ DLCO, ↓ FEV₁/FVC

A reduced FEV₁/FVC ratio indicates airflow obstruction. Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis causes restriction, not obstruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Wheezing; airflow limitation; low FEV₁/FVC = obstructive disease

### D) ↑ TLC, normal DLCO, normal FEV₁/FVC

In IPF, lung volumes are reduced and DLCO is decreased due to thickening of the alveolar interstitium. Increased TLC is inconsistent with pulmonary fibrosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hyperinflation; increased lung volumes; preserved diffusion = non-fibrotic process
