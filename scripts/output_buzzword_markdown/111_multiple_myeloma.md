## Buzzword Question

An elderly patient with multiple myeloma undergoes skeletal survey. Which radiographic finding is most characteristic of this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

multiple myeloma; skeletal survey; lytic lesions; bone destruction

## Answer Options

A) Osteoblastic bone lesions
B) Punched-out lytic lesions
C) Bamboo spine
D) Codman triangle

## Correct Answer

B. Punched-out lytic lesions

## Explanation

Multiple myeloma causes osteoclast activation via cytokines (including RANKL), leading to focal bone destruction without reactive sclerosis. This produces classic “punched-out” lytic lesions in skull, vertebrae, ribs, and pelvis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Punched-out lytic lesions + CRAB + M protein = Multiple myeloma

## Wrong Options

### A. Osteoblastic bone lesions

Osteoblastic lesions are seen in metastatic prostate cancer due to abnormal bone formation. Multiple myeloma instead causes purely lytic lesions.

**Buzzword Combo:** Osteoblastic lesions + elevated PSA = Prostate cancer metastasis

### C. Bamboo spine

Bamboo spine results from syndesmophyte formation and vertebral fusion in ankylosing spondylitis. It is unrelated to plasma cell malignancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sacroiliitis + HLA-B27 + spinal fusion = Ankylosing spondylitis

### D. Codman triangle

Codman triangle is a periosteal reaction seen in aggressive bone tumors such as osteosarcoma. It is not associated with multiple myeloma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Codman triangle + sunburst pattern = Osteosarcoma
