## Buzzword Question

A child with an inherited hemolytic anemia has recurrent pain episodes that worsen with dehydration and increased acidity. The abnormal hemoglobin is due to a β-globin substitution. Which of the following best explains the molecular basis of this patient’s disease?

## Buzzwords Used

Inherited hemolytic anemia; recurrent pain episodes; worsens with dehydration and increased acidity

## Answer Options

A) Gain of stabilizing hydrophobic interactions in deoxygenated hemoglobin
B) Decreased production of structurally normal β-globin chains
C) Defective spectrin anchoring in the RBC membrane
D) Impaired NADPH generation during oxidative stress

## Correct Answer

A) Gain of stabilizing hydrophobic interactions in deoxygenated hemoglobin


## Explanation

Sickle cell disease is caused by a β-globin missense mutation in which glutamic acid is replaced by valine. This creates hydrophobic interactions in deoxygenated HbS, causing polymerization, reduced RBC deformability, and vaso-occlusion.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Glutamic acid to valine + deoxygenated hemoglobin + hydrophobic polymerization = sickle cell disease

## Wrong Options

### B) Decreased production of structurally normal β-globin chains

This describes β-thalassemia, where β-globin production is decreased. Sickle cell disease is caused by abnormal β-globin structure, not reduced β-globin synthesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Microcytic anemia + target cells + decreased β-globin synthesis = β-thalassemia

### C) Defective spectrin anchoring in the RBC membrane

This describes hereditary spherocytosis due to RBC membrane cytoskeleton defects. It does not cause HbS polymerization or vaso-occlusion.

**Buzzword Combo:** Spherocytes + increased osmotic fragility + negative Coombs test = hereditary spherocytosis

### D) Impaired NADPH generation during oxidative stress

This describes G6PD deficiency, which causes oxidative RBC injury. It is triggered by oxidant stress, not deoxygenation-driven hydrophobic polymerization.

**Buzzword Combo:** Oxidant stress + Heinz bodies + bite cells = G6PD deficiency
