## Buzzword Question

A 29-year-old man being treated for tuberculosis develops decreased visual acuity and difficulty distinguishing red from green colors several weeks after starting therapy.
Which of the following drugs is most likely responsible?

## Buzzwords Used

Tuberculosis; Red-green vision

## Answer Options

A) Pyrazinamide
B) Ethambutol
C) Streptomycin
D) Rifapentine

## Correct Answer

B) Ethambutol

## Explanation

Ethambutol inhibits arabinosyltransferase, impairing mycobacterial cell wall synthesis. A classic adverse effect is optic neuropathy causing decreased visual acuity and red-green color blindness.

## Wrong Options

### Pyrazinamide 

Pyrazinamide commonly causes hepatotoxicity and hyperuricemia.

**Buzzword Combo:** TB therapy + hyperuricemia + acidic phagolysosome activity = Pyrazinamide

### Streptomycin

Streptomycin is an aminoglycoside associated with ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity.

**Buzzword Combo:** TB therapy + hearing loss + 30S inhibition = Streptomycin

### Rifapentine

Rifapentine inhibits DNA-dependent RNA polymerase and may cause orange discoloration of body fluids.

**Buzzword Combo:** TB therapy + orange secretions + RNA polymerase inhibition = Rifamycin
