## Buzzword Question

A 29-year-old woman presents with a 4-month history of psychosis. For 3 of those months a manic episode was concurrent. After mood treatment, paranoid delusions and hallucinations persist for 6 additional weeks with a euthymic mood. The single clinical feature that most distinguishes her diagnosis from bipolar I disorder with psychotic features is:

## Buzzwords Used

Mania + concurrent psychosis; psychosis persisting 6 weeks after mood resolves; euthymic during ongoing psychosis

## Answer Options

: A) Psychosis persisting ≥2 weeks in the complete absence of a mood episode B
) Mood episode present for the majority of the total illness duration 
C) Psychosis resolves completely when mood is treated 
D) Duration of active psychosis exceeds 6 months


## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

The defining distinction between schizoaffective disorder and bipolar I with psychotic features is that in schizoaffective disorder, delusions or hallucinations persist for ≥2 weeks in the complete absence of a mood episode. In bipolar I with psychotic features, psychosis occurs only within mood episodes and resolves with them. The 6-week post-mood psychosis here clearly exceeds the 2-week threshold.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Mania + psychosis + psychosis ≥2 weeks after mood resolves = Schizoaffective disorder, bipolar type

## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Mood episode present for the majority of the total illness duration

1 Explanation: This criterion (mood for majority of illness) distinguishes schizoaffective disorder FROM schizophrenia, not from bipolar I with psychotic features. It is a necessary but not sufficient distinguishing feature between schizoaffective disorder and bipolar disorder.


**Buzzword Combo:** Wrong 1: Predominant psychosis + brief mood episodes = Schizophrenia (mood not majority of illness)


### 2: C) Psychosis resolves completely when mood is treated

2 Explanation: Psychosis resolving completely with mood treatment is the hallmark of bipolar I with psychotic features  NOT schizoaffective disorder. In schizoaffective disorder, psychosis persists beyond mood resolution.


**Buzzword Combo:** Wrong 2: Psychosis only during mood episode + resolves with mood = Bipolar I with psychotic features

### 3: D) Duration of active psychosis exceeds 6 months

3 Explanation: Six months of continuous active psychosis would suggest schizophrenia. Schizoaffective disorder does not require 6 months of psychosis; it requires ≥2 weeks of psychosis outside a mood episode.


**Buzzword Combo:** Wrong 3: Continuous psychosis ≥6 months + mood brief relative to psychosis = Schizophrenia
