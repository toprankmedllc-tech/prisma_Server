## Buzzword Question

A patient with severe renal artery stenosis develops secondary hypertension due to activation of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system. Which of the following cell types is primarily responsible for increased renin secretion in this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Renal artery stenosis; Renin; Secondary hypertension

## Answer Options

A) Mesangial cells
B) Podocytes
C) Juxtaglomerular cells
D) Macula densa epithelial cells

## Correct Answer

C) Juxtaglomerular cells

## Explanation

Juxtaglomerular apparatus releases renin in response to decreased renal perfusion pressure caused by renal artery stenosis. Increased renin activates the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system, contributing to secondary hypertension.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Decreased renal perfusion + renin secretion + RAAS activation = Juxtaglomerular cells

## Wrong Options

### Mesangial cells

Mesangial cells provide structural support to glomerular capillaries and help regulate filtration surface area through contractile activity. They also participate in matrix production and phagocytosis within the glomerulus.

**Buzzword Combo:** Glomerular support + matrix production + filtration regulation = Mesangial cells

### Podocytes

Podocytes form part of the glomerular filtration barrier through their interdigitating foot processes. Injury to podocytes can lead to proteinuria and nephrotic syndrome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Foot processes + slit diaphragm + filtration barrier = Podocytes

### Macula densa epithelial cells

Macula densa detect sodium chloride concentration in the distal tubule and indirectly regulate renin release through signaling to juxtaglomerular cells. They are part of the juxtaglomerular apparatus but are not the primary source of renin secretion

**Buzzword Combo:** tubuloglomerular feedback + RAAS regulation = Macula densa
