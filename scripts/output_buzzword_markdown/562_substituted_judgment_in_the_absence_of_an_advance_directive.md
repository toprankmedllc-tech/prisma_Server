## Buzzword Question

A 74-year-old woman is admitted to the intensive care unit following septic shock and is currently intubated and unable to make medical decisions. She has no advance directive on file. The medical team believes ongoing life support is unlikely to lead to meaningful recovery. A man who identifies himself as the patient’s longtime close friend states that she previously told him she would not want prolonged life support in a situation with poor prognosis. However, her adult son insists that “everything possible must be done,” while her estranged sister agrees with continuing full treatment.

Which of the following is the most appropriate guiding principle for the physician?

## Buzzwords Used

No advance directive ; Longtime friend ; Prior patient wishes ; Family disagreement ; Substituted judgment

## Answer Options

A) Follow the decision of the adult son since he is the closest biological relative present

B) Follow the expressed prior wishes reported by a reliable individual and apply substituted judgment standards

C) Continue full life-sustaining treatment until all family members and friends reach unanimous agreement

D) Defer decision-making to the hospital administration when non-family members are involved

## Correct Answer

B) Follow the expressed prior wishes reported by a reliable individual and apply substituted judgment standards

## Explanation

When a patient’s wishes are known, decisions should be guided by substituted judgment. The goal is to honor what the patient would have chosen rather than the preferences of relatives.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Known prior wishes + reliable source + lack of capacity = substituted judgment

## Wrong Options

### A) Follow the decision of the adult son since he is the closest biological relative present

Biological relationship alone does not override clear evidence of the patient’s previously expressed wishes.

**Buzzword Combo:** No known patient preferences + available adult child + no higher-priority surrogate = adult child guides decisions

### C) Continue full life-sustaining treatment until all family members and friends reach unanimous agreement

Medical decision-making does not require unanimous agreement among all involved individuals.

**Buzzword Combo:** Unresolved conflict + no surrogate identified + ongoing stabilization needs = temporary continuation of treatment

### D) Defer decision-making to the hospital administration when non-family members are involved

Hospital administrators do not routinely serve as surrogate decision-makers.

**Buzzword Combo:** No surrogate available + significant dispute + legal uncertainty = institutional consultation may be appropriate
