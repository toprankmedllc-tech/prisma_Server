## Buzzword Question

A patient who recently recovered from major surgery thanks his physician for excellent care and offers two tickets to a championship football match that the physician has previously mentioned wanting to attend.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response?

## Buzzwords Used

Post-operative recovery ; Patient gratitude ; Gift offering ; Physician boundaries

## Answer Options

A) Accept the tickets because the patient is simply expressing gratitude
B) Politely decline the gift because it may interfere with professional boundaries
C) Politely decline the gift because physicians should avoid accepting any non-cash gifts from patients regardless of context
D) Accept the tickets as long as the patient signs a consent form

## Correct Answer

B) Politely decline the gift because it may interfere with professional boundaries

## Explanation

High-value or personally significant gifts may compromise the therapeutic relationship and create real or perceived boundary issues, even in the absence of immediate clinical harm.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Patient gratitude + high-value personal gift + therapeutic relationship risk = decline

## Wrong Options

### A) Accept the tickets because the patient is simply expressing gratitude

Acceptance of high-value or personally significant gifts risks boundary erosion and perceived bias.

**Buzzword Combo:** Gratitude + high-value gift + physician role = inappropriate acceptance

### C) Politely decline the gift because physicians should avoid accepting any non-cash gifts from patients regardless of context

This is overly rigid; ethics depends on context rather than an absolute prohibition on non-cash gifts, as small gifts (e.g. box of chocolates) can be accepted by treating physicians.

**Buzzword Combo:** Patient gift + rigid rule application + lack of nuance = incorrect absolutism

### D) Accept the tickets as long as the patient signs a consent form

Informed consent does not resolve professional boundary concerns or power imbalance issues.

**Buzzword Combo:** Gift + formal consent + boundary issue unresolved = incorrect justification
