## Buzzword Question

A hospitalized patient has temperature 39°C, heart rate 102/min, respiratory rate 24/min, and WBC count 14,500/mm³. Which diagnosis is best supported by these findings?


## Buzzwords Used

 Fever ; tachycardia ; tachypnea ; leukocytosis

## Answer Options

 A) Systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS)
 B) Cardiogenic shock
 C) Addisonian crisis
 D) Pulmonary embolism
 

## Correct Answer

 A) Systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS)


## Explanation

The patient fulfills all four SIRS criteria: elevated temperature, tachycardia, tachypnea, and leukocytosis. SIRS reflects a generalized inflammatory response and may occur in infectious or noninfectious conditions.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Fever + tachycardia + leukocytosis = SIRS


## Wrong Options

###  B) Cardiogenic shock


Cardiogenic shock presents with hypotension and signs of impaired cardiac output rather than isolated inflammatory criteria.


**Buzzword Combo:**  MI + pulmonary edema + hypotension = Cardiogenic shock


###  C) Addisonian crisis


Addisonian crisis commonly presents with hypotension, hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, and hypoglycemia.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hyperkalemia + hypotension + adrenal insufficiency = Addisonian crisis


### D) Pulmonary embolism


Pulmonary embolism usually presents with acute pleuritic chest pain, dyspnea, and hypoxemia rather than generalized inflammatory criteria alone.


**Buzzword Combo:** Pleuritic chest pain + dyspnea + hypoxemia = Pulmonary embolism
