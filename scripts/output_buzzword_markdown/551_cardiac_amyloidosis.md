## Buzzword Question


Endomyocardial biopsy of a patient with restrictive cardiomyopathy shows extracellular eosinophilic deposits that exhibit apple-green birefringence under polarized light following Congo red staining. This finding is most consistent with:

## Buzzwords Used


Restrictive cardiomyopathy; extracellular eosinophilic deposits; apple-green birefringence; Congo red staining; cardiac amyloidosis

## Answer Options

A) Cardiac amyloidosis
B) Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
C) Cardiac sarcoidosis
D) Hemochromatosis

## Correct Answer

A) Cardiac amyloidosis

## Explanation

Apple-green birefringence under polarized light after Congo red staining is pathognomonic for amyloid deposition — a misfolded β-pleated sheet protein that accumulates extracellularly.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Congo red + apple-green birefringence + extracellular deposits = cardiac amyloidosis

## Wrong Options

### B) Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy

HCM shows myocyte disarray with interstitial fibrosis, not extracellular amyloid deposits.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asymmetric septal hypertrophy + myocyte disarray = HCM

### 
C) Cardiac sarcoidosis

Sarcoidosis shows non-caseating granulomas in the myocardium, not amyloid deposits.

**Buzzword Combo:** Non-caseating granulomas + bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy = sarcoidosis

### D) Hemochromatosis

Hemochromatosis shows iron deposition in cardiomyocytes (Prussian blue–positive), causing a restrictive picture that progresses to dilated cardiomyopathy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prussian blue–positive iron deposits + bronze diabetes = hemochromatosis
