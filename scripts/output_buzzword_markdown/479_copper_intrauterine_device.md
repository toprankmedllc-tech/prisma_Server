## Buzzword Question

A 26-year-old woman comes to the clinic requesting emergency contraception after unprotected intercourse 4 days ago. She prefers a long-acting, hormone-free contraceptive method because she previously developed migraines while taking oral contraceptive pills. Pelvic examination is normal, and pregnancy test is negative. The physician recommends a contraceptive device that produces a local inflammatory reaction toxic to sperm and ova, thereby preventing fertilization and implantation. Which of the following is the most likely adverse effect associated with this method?

## Buzzwords Used

Emergency contraception ; hormone-free ; long-acting reversible contraception ; local inflammatory reaction ; toxic to sperm and ova ; prevents fertilization ; copper IUD

## Answer Options

A) Increased risk of endometrial carcinoma
B) Heavier menstrual bleeding and dysmenorrhea
C) Visual disturbances and hot flashes
D) Bone marrow suppression

## Correct Answer

B) Heavier menstrual bleeding and dysmenorrhea

## Explanation

The Copper intrauterine device is a hormone-free contraceptive that creates a local inflammatory environment toxic to sperm and ova. Common adverse effects include heavier menstrual bleeding and dysmenorrhea.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hormone-free contraception + local inflammatory reaction + emergency contraception = Copper intrauterine device

## Wrong Options

### A) Increased risk of endometrial carcinoma

Increased endometrial cancer risk is associated with unopposed estrogen therapy or the uterine agonist effects of Tamoxifen, not copper IUDs.

**Buzzword Combo:** ER-positive breast cancer + uterine agonist activity + endometrial cancer = Tamoxifen

### C) Visual disturbances and hot flashes

Visual disturbances and hot flashes are classic adverse effects of Clomiphene used for ovulation induction.

**Buzzword Combo:** PCOS + infertility + ovulation induction = Clomiphene

### D) Bone marrow suppression

Bone marrow suppression is not associated with copper IUDs and is more commonly linked to chemotherapy or immunosuppressive medications.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chemotherapy + pancytopenia + infection risk = bone marrow suppression
