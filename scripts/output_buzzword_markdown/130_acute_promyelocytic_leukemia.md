## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old patient with fatigue, spontaneous severe mucosal bleeding, pancytopenia, and abnormal promyelocytes with numerous Auer rods on peripheral smear most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

45-year-old; fatigue; severe mucosal bleeding; pancytopenia; promyelocytes; numerous Auer rods; DIC features

## Answer Options

A) Acute myeloid leukemia (non-APL)
B) Acute promyelocytic leukemia
C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
D) Acute lymphoblastic leukemia

## Correct Answer

B. Acute promyelocytic leukemia

## Explanation

Acute promyelocytic leukemia is an AML subtype (M3) caused by t(15;17) leading to PML-RARA fusion and maturation arrest at the promyelocyte stage. It is classically associated with severe DIC due to release of procoagulant granules.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Promyelocytes + Auer rods + DIC = Acute promyelocytic leukemia

## Wrong Options

### A) Acute myeloid leukemia (non-APL)

Other AML subtypes show myeloblasts but do not have the strong association with DIC seen in APL. They also lack the classic promyelocyte predominance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Myeloblasts + MPO+ + Auer rods = AML (non-APL)

### C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL is a mature B-cell leukemia of elderly patients characterized by smudge cells and lymphocytosis. It does not involve promyelocytes or DIC.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + CD5/CD23+ + elderly = CLL

### D) Acute lymphoblastic leukemia

ALL presents with TdT-positive lymphoblasts, primarily in children, and does not show Auer rods or DIC. It is a lymphoid, not myeloid, malignancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** TdT+ lymphoblasts + childhood = ALL
