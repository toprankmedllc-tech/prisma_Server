## Buzzword Question

A young adult presents with painless cervical lymphadenopathy, fever, and night sweats. Lymph node biopsy reveals large binucleated “owl-eye” cells. This finding is most characteristic of which of the following conditions?

## Buzzwords Used

painless lymphadenopathy; fever; night sweats; owl-eye cells; Reed-Sternberg cells

## Answer Options

A) Burkitt lymphoma
B) Hodgkin lymphoma
C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
D) Multiple myeloma

## Correct Answer

B. Hodgkin lymphoma

## Explanation

Reed-Sternberg cells are large binucleated cells with prominent eosinophilic nucleoli producing an “owl-eye” appearance. They are the diagnostic hallmark of Hodgkin lymphoma and are typically found within an inflammatory background.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Owl-eye cells + painless lymphadenopathy = Hodgkin lymphoma

## Wrong Options

### A. Burkitt lymphoma

Burkitt lymphoma is characterized by a starry-sky appearance caused by macrophages scattered among rapidly proliferating lymphocytes. It is associated with t(8;14) involving c-MYC.

**Buzzword Combo:** Starry-sky pattern + t(8;14) = Burkitt lymphoma

### C. Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL shows mature lymphocytes and smudge cells on peripheral smear. Reed-Sternberg cells are not seen in CLL.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + CD5+ B cells = CLL

### D. Multiple myeloma

Multiple myeloma is a plasma cell malignancy associated with monoclonal protein production, lytic bone lesions, and rouleaux formation rather than owl-eye cells.

**Buzzword Combo:** M protein + lytic lesions + plasma cells = Multiple myeloma
