## Buzzword Question


A tall adolescent with arachnodactyly, pectus excavatum, and hypermobile joints is found to have superior and temporal displacement of the lens. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Arachnodactyly; pectus excavatum; hypermobile joints; superior lens dislocation

## Answer Options

A) Ehlers-Danlos syndrome
B) Homocystinuria
C) Marfan syndrome
D) Osteogenesis imperfecta

## Correct Answer

C) Marfan syndrome

## Explanation

Marfan syndrome is an autosomal dominant connective tissue disorder caused by mutations in the FBN1 gene encoding fibrillin-1. Patients classically present with tall stature, arachnodactyly, pectus deformities, and superior-temporal lens subluxation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Upward lens dislocation + arachnodactyly + pectus deformity = Marfan syndrome

## Wrong Options

### A) Ehlers-Danlos syndrome

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome is associated with hyperextensible skin, easy bruising, and poor wound healing rather than superior lens dislocation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hyperextensible skin + easy bruising + poor wound healing = Ehlers-Danlos syndrome

### B) Homocystinuria

Homocystinuria presents with downward and medial lens dislocation rather than upward displacement.

**Buzzword Combo:** Downward lens dislocation + thrombosis + intellectual disability = Homocystinuria

### D) Osteogenesis imperfecta

Osteogenesis imperfecta presents with brittle bones, recurrent fractures, and blue sclerae.

**Buzzword Combo:** Blue sclerae + recurrent fractures = Osteogenesis imperfecta
