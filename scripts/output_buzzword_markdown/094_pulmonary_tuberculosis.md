## Buzzword Question

A 40-year-old man is diagnosed with pulmonary tuberculosis, and anti-tubercular therapy is started. His physician has also added Vitamin B6 supplements to the drug regimen. What is the mechanism of action of the drug causing pyridoxine deficiency?

## Buzzwords Used

Taking ATT + vitamin B6 (pyridoxine)

## Answer Options

A. Inhibits DNA-dependent RNA polymerase
B. Binds the ribosomal 30s subunit to inhibit protein synthesis
C. Inhibit mycolic acid synthesis in the bacterial cell wall
D. Disrupts membrane transport and function

## Correct Answer

C. Inhibit mycolic acid synthesis in the bacterial cell wall

## Explanation

Vitamin B6 deficiency is caused by isoniazid toxicity. Isoniazid is activated by bacterial catalase-peroxidase (KatG). Activated drug then inhibits the synthesis of mycolic acid in the cell wall which is the main virulence factor of Mycobaterium.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Taking ATT + vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) → INH → mycolic acid synthesis inhibition
