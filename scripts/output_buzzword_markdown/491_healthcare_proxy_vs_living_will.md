## Buzzword Question

A 55-year-old man with ALS visits his primary care physician and says: 'When I can no longer make decisions for myself, I want my wife to make all my medical decisions for me.' He signs the appropriate legal document. This document is best classified as:


## Buzzwords Used

Designates specific person; medical decisions if incapacitated; wife as decision-maker; advance directive; ALS; person-based

## Answer Options

A) Healthcare proxy / durable power of attorney for healthcare 
B) Living will / instructional directive 
C) POLST (Physician Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment) 
D) Do-Not-Resuscitate (DNR) order

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

A healthcare proxy (also called durable power of attorney for healthcare or healthcare agent) designates a specific PERSON to make medical decisions if the patient loses decision-making capacity. It is PERSON-based. A living will is INSTRUCTION-based (specifying which treatments are wanted/not wanted). POLST and DNR are physician orders, not patient-authored advance directive documents.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Designates a specific person to make medical decisions if incapacitated = Healthcare proxy / DPOA for healthcare (person-based advance directive)


## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Living will / instructional directive


A living will specifies the patient's treatment preferences for specific scenarios (e.g., 'do not use mechanical ventilation in a persistent vegetative state'),  it is instruction-based, not person-based. This patient is designating a decision-maker (wife), not providing treatment instructions.

**Buzzword Combo:** Written treatment instructions for specific scenarios if incapacitated = Living will (instructional directive), instruction-based, not person-based

### 2: C) POLST

POLST (Physician Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment) is a physician-signed medical order form translating patient preferences into actionable orders for CPR, ventilation, and nutrition,  for use by emergency responders and healthcare facilities. It is a physician order, not a patient-authored advance directive designating a surrogate.

**Buzzword Combo:** Physician-signed actionable orders for CPR/ventilation/artificial nutrition = POLST (for seriously ill patients near end of life)

### 3: D) DNR order

A DNR order is a specific physician order instructing providers not to perform CPR in case of cardiac arrest. It addresses one specific intervention and is a physician order, not a document designating a surrogate decision-maker.

**Buzzword Combo:** Physician order + no CPR if cardiac arrest = DNR; physician order + no intubation = DNI
