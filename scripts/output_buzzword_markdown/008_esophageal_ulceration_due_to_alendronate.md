## Buzzword Question

A 72-year-old man with severe osteoporosis is prescribed weekly Alendronate therapy. His physician instructs him to take the medication with a full glass of water and remain upright for at least 30 minutes after ingestion.
These instructions are primarily intended to prevent which of the following complications?

## Buzzwords Used

Alendronate;take the medication with a full glass of water ;remain upright for at least 30 minutes

## Answer Options

A. Osteonecrosis of the jaw
B. Esophageal ulceration
C. Tendon rupture
D. Ototoxicity

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

Bisphosphonates can directly irritate the esophageal mucosa and cause esophageal ulceration or esophagitis if prolonged contact with the esophagus occurs.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Bisphosphonate + remain upright after use = prevent esophageal ulceration

## Wrong Options

### A. Osteonecrosis of the jaw

Incorrect — Osteonecrosis of the jaw is another adverse effect of bisphosphonates, especially after dental procedures, but remaining upright does not prevent it.


**Buzzword Combo:** Bisphosphonate + dental extraction = osteonecrosis of jaw

### C. Tendon rupture

Incorrect — Tendon rupture is more commonly associated with fluoroquinolone antibiotics.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ciprofloxacin + Achilles tendon rupture = fluoroquinolone toxicity

### D. Ototoxicity

Incorrect — Ototoxicity is classically associated with aminoglycosides and loop diuretics.

**Buzzword Combo:** Gentamicin + hearing loss = ototoxicity
