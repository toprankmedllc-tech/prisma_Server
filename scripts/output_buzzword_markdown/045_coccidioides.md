## Buzzword Question

A 29-year-old man from Arizona presents with fever, cough, arthralgia, and tender red nodules on his shins. Lung biopsy demonstrates large spherules filled with endospores.
Which of the following organisms is the most likely cause of this patient’s disease?

## Buzzwords Used

Arizona; Erythema nodosum; Spherules

## Answer Options

A) Histoplasma capsulatum
B) Coccidioides immitis
C) Blastomyces dermatitidis
D) Cryptococcus neoformans


## Correct Answer

B) Coccidioides immitis

## Explanation

Coccidioidomycosis is a dimorphic fungus endemic to the southwestern United States. Tissue biopsy classically demonstrates spherules filled with endospores. Patients commonly present with pulmonary symptoms, erythema nodosum, and arthralgia.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Arizona + erythema nodosum + spherules = Coccidioides

## Wrong Options

### Histoplasma capsulatum

Histoplasma appears as intracellular yeast within macrophages and is associated with bat droppings.


**Buzzword Combo:** Bat caves + intracellular yeast = Histoplasma

### Blastomyces dermatitidis

Blastomyces classically demonstrates broad-based budding yeast.

**Buzzword Combo:** Broad-based budding + skin lesions = Blastomyces

### Cryptococcus neoformans

Cryptococcus is an encapsulated yeast associated with pigeon droppings and meningitis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Encapsulated yeast + pigeon droppings = Cryptococcus
