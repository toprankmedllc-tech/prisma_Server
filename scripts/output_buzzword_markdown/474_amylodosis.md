## Buzzword Question

Which amyloid precursor protein is most likely elevated in a patient with Congo red–positive renal deposits and an underlying plasma cell dyscrasia?

## Buzzwords Used

Amyloid precursor; renal deposits; Plasma cell dyscarsia  

## Answer Options

 A. Immunoglobulin light chains
 B. Serum amyloid A protein
 C. β₂-microglobulin
 D. Transthyretin

## Correct Answer

 A. Immunoglobulin light chains

## Explanation

AL amyloidosis is caused by deposition of monoclonal immunoglobulin light chains produced by abnormal plasma cells and is strongly associated with plasma cell dyscrasias such as multiple myeloma

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Plasma cell disorder + light chain deposition = AL amyloidosis

## Wrong Options

### Serum amyloid A protein

AA amyloidosis occurs in chronic inflammatory diseases such as rheumatoid arthritis and chronic infections.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic inflammation + serum amyloid A + Rheumatoid arthritis = AA amyloidosis

###  β₂-microglobulin

Dialysis-associated amyloidosis develops in patients receiving long-term hemodialysis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Long-term dialysis + β₂-microglobulin = Dialysis related amyloidosis


### Transthyretin

ATTR amyloidosis is associated with hereditary or senile amyloidosis affecting the heart and peripheral nerves.

**Buzzword Combo:** Restrictive cardiomyopathy + transthyretin + old age = ATTR amyloidosis
