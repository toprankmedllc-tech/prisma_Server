## Buzzword Question

A 6-year-old child presents with recurrent pneumonia, chronic sinus infections, and failure to thrive. Parents report salty-tasting sweat and bulky, greasy stools since infancy. There is a family history of similar lung disease in a sibling who died early in childhood. The disease is caused by a defect in an epithelial chloride channel leading to thick secretions in multiple organs.Which of the following best describes the mode of inheritance of this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Recurrent pneumonia; Chronic sinus infections; Failure to thrive; Salty sweat; Bulky greasy stools; Early sibling death; Thick secretions; Chloride channel defect

## Answer Options

A) Autosomal dominant
B) Autosomal recessive
C) X-linked recessive
D) Mitochondrial inheritance

## Correct Answer

B) Autosomal recessive

## Explanation

Cystic fibrosis is caused by mutations in the CFTR gene and follows an autosomal recessive inheritance pattern. Both alleles must be affected for disease manifestation, explaining sibling recurrence and early childhood mortality in affected families.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Recurrent lung infections + malabsorption + salty sweat + early sibling death = Autosomal recessive CFTR disease

## Wrong Options

### A) Autosomal dominant

Autosomal dominant disorders typically show vertical transmission across generations, which is not characteristic of cystic fibrosis, where affected siblings may occur in unaffected parents.

**Buzzword Combo:** Vertical transmission + multiple generations affected + 50% risk per offspring = Autosomal dominant

### C) X-linked recessive

X-linked recessive diseases predominantly affect males with carrier mothers and typically do not present with pancreatic insufficiency or pulmonary infections like cystic fibrosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Male predominance + carrier mother + hemophilia/DUCHENNE pattern = X-linked recessive

### D) Mitochondrial inheritance

Mitochondrial disorders show maternal inheritance affecting high-energy tissues (muscle, brain), not isolated pulmonary and pancreatic disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Maternal transmission + muscle weakness + neurologic symptoms = Mitochondrial inheritance

## Related Concepts

Hardy-Weinberg principle in carrier diseases; CFTR mutation spectrum (ΔF508); Genetic counseling in autosomal recessive disorders
