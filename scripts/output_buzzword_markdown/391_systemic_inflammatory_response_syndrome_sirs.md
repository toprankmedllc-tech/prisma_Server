## Buzzword Question

A patient with acute pancreatitis develops fever, tachycardia, tachypnea, leukocytosis, and low PaCO2 in the absence of infection. What is the diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Acute pancreatitis ; fever ; tachycardia ; tachypnea ; leukocytosis ; low PaCO2

## Answer Options

 A) Systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS)
 B) Septic shock
 C) Hypovolemic shock
 D) Neurogenic shock


## Correct Answer

 A) Systemic inflammatory response syndrome (SIRS)


## Explanation

SIRS is a systemic inflammatory response that may occur due to infectious or noninfectious conditions such as pancreatitis. The patient meets multiple SIRS criteria including fever, tachycardia, tachypnea, leukocytosis, and low PaCO2.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pancreatitis + fever + leukocytosis = SIRS


## Wrong Options

###  B) Septic shock


Septic shock requires an underlying infection with persistent hypotension and organ dysfunction.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Infection + hypotension + elevated lactate = Septic shock


### C) Hypovolemic shock


Hypovolemic shock results from significant intravascular fluid loss leading to decreased preload and hypotension.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hemorrhage + hypotension + cool extremities = Hypovolemic shock


###  D) Neurogenic shock


Neurogenic shock is characterized by hypotension and bradycardia following spinal cord injury.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Spinal injury + bradycardia + hypotension = Neurogenic shock
