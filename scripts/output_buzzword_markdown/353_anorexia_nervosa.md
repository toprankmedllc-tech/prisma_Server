## Buzzword Question

An underweight teenage girl has bradycardia, lanugo hair, amenorrhea, excessive exercise, and an intense fear of gaining weight despite severe weight loss. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Lanugo hair; bradycardia; fear of weight gain; low BMI; excessive exercise; amenorrhea


## Answer Options

 A) Anorexia nervosa
 B) Bulimia nervosa
 C) Major depressive disorder
 D) Avoidant restrictive food intake disorder

## Correct Answer

A. Anorexia nervosa


## Explanation

Anorexia nervosa presents with restrictive eating, significantly low body weight, distorted body image, and fear of gaining weight. Starvation findings include lanugo hair, bradycardia, hypotension, and amenorrhea.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Low BMI + fear of weight gain + lanugo hair = Anorexia nervosa


## Wrong Options

### B. Bulimia nervosa


Bulimia nervosa usually presents with binge eating and compensatory purging in patients with normal body weight.


**Buzzword Combo:** Binge eating + self-induced vomiting + normal BMI = Bulimia nervosa

### C. Major depressive disorder


Depression may cause appetite loss but does not produce intense fear of weight gain or body image distortion.


**Buzzword Combo:** Depressed mood + anhedonia + SIGECAPS symptoms = Major depressive disorder

### D. Avoidant restrictive food intake disorder


ARFID involves restricted food intake without body image concerns or fear of gaining weight.


**Buzzword Combo:** Selective eating + sensory aversion + no body image disturbance = ARFID
