## Buzzword Question

A physician group is paid a predetermined annual amount for each patient enrolled in the practice regardless of the number of clinic visits, tests, or procedures performed. Several physicians note increasing pressure to minimize unnecessary imaging and specialist referrals.

Which payment model is most strongly associated with this reimbursement structure?

## Buzzwords Used

Fixed per-patient payment ; Reduced utilization ; Referral limitation ; Healthcare reimbursement

## Answer Options

A) Fee-for-service reimbursement

B) Capitation

C) Bundled payment

D) Accountable care organization penalty structure

## Correct Answer

B) Capitation

## Explanation

Capitation reimburses providers with fixed payments per enrolled patient and incentivizes reducing unnecessary healthcare utilization.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Fixed payment + enrolled patient population + utilization reduction = capitation

## Wrong Options

### A) Fee-for-service reimbursement

Fee-for-service incentivizes increased procedures and healthcare utilization rather than reduction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Per-service billing + higher utilization + procedure incentives = fee-for-service

### C) Bundled payment

Episode-based reimbursement + limited care period + incorrect payment structure = bundled payment

**Buzzword Combo:** D) Accountable care organization penalty structure

### Accountable care organizations may use multiple reimbursement strategies and are not defined solely by fixed per-patient payments.

Healthcare network + quality incentives + not primary reimbursement method = incorrect association

**Buzzword Combo:** Healthcare network + quality incentives + not primary reimbursement method = incorrect association
