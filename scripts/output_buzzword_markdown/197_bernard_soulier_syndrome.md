## Buzzword Question

A child with recurrent nosebleeds, easy bruising since early childhood, petechiae, mucosal bleeding, normal PT and aPTT, and failure of platelet aggregation in response to ristocetin most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

nosebleeds; easy bruising; petechiae; mucosal bleeding; normal PT; normal aPTT; failure of platelet aggregation to ristocetin

## Answer Options

A. Glanzmann thrombasthenia
B. Bernard-Soulier syndrome
C. Hemophilia A
D. von Willebrand disease

## Correct Answer

B. Bernard–Soulier syndrome

## Explanation

Bernard–Soulier syndrome is due to GPIb-IX-V receptor deficiency, which prevents platelet binding to von Willebrand factor, leading to defective adhesion and absent ristocetin-induced aggregation. It is associated with giant platelets and mucocutaneous bleeding.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Absent ristocetin aggregation + giant platelets + GPIb defect = Bernard–Soulier syndrome

## Wrong Options

### A. Glanzmann thrombasthenia

Glanzmann thrombasthenia is due to GPIIb/IIIa deficiency and causes impaired aggregation with ADP, epinephrine, and collagen, but normal ristocetin response.

**Buzzword Combo:** Normal ristocetin + absent ADP aggregation + GPIIb/IIIa defect = Glanzmann thrombasthenia

### C. Hemophilia A

Hemophilia A is a coagulation factor VIII deficiency causing prolonged aPTT and hemarthroses, not a platelet function defect.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prolonged aPTT + hemarthrosis + X-linked = Hemophilia A

### D. von Willebrand disease

von Willebrand disease causes decreased ristocetin-induced aggregation that corrects with normal plasma, and platelet count and morphology are normal.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ristocetin defect corrected by plasma + normal platelets = von Willebrand disease
