## Buzzword Question

A child has episodes of sleepwalking and screaming during sleep but does not remember the events the next morning. These episodes most likely occur during:


## Buzzwords Used

Sleepwalking ; Screaming during sleep ; No memory of events 

## Answer Options

A. REM sleep
B. NREM stage 1
C. NREM stage 2
D. NREM stage 3

## Correct Answer

D. NREM stage 3

## Explanation

NREM stage 3 is the deepest stage of sleep and is characterized by delta waves. Parasomnias such as sleepwalking and night terrors commonly occur during this stage, and patients typically do not remember the episodes afterwar

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Delta waves + sleepwalking + night terrors = NREM stage 3

## Wrong Options

### A. REM sleep

REM sleep is associated with vivid dreaming, muscle atonia, and rapid eye movements. Sleepwalking and night terrors occur during NREM stage 3, not REM sleep

**Buzzword Combo:** Dreaming + muscle atonia + rapid eye movements = REM sleep

### B. NREM stage 1

NREM stage 1 is the lightest stage of sleep and is associated with theta waves and transition from wakefulness to sleep. Parasomnias such as sleepwalking and night terrors are not characteristic of this stage

**Buzzword Combo:** Theta waves + light sleep + sleep onset = NREM stage 1

### C. NREM stage 2

NREM stage 2 is characterized by sleep spindles and K complexes and represents the largest portion of total sleep time. Sleepwalking and night terrors are instead associated with NREM stage 3 slee

**Buzzword Combo:** Sleep spindles + K complexes + most total sleep = NREM stage 2
