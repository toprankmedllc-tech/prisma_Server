## Buzzword Question

An obese patient with daytime sleepiness, loud snoring, elevated serum bicarbonate, and PaCO2 of 52 mm Hg is diagnosed with a sleep-related hypoventilation disorder. What is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

 Obesity ; daytime sleepiness ; loud snoring ; elevated bicarbonate ; hypercapnia


## Answer Options

 A) Obesity hypoventilation syndrome
 B) Panic disorder
 C) Pulmonary embolism
 D) Asthma

## Correct Answer

A) Obesity hypoventilation syndrome

## Explanation

OHS is characterized by obesity, daytime hypercapnia (PaCO2 >45 mm Hg), and sleep-disordered breathing. Elevated serum bicarbonate reflects chronic respiratory acidosis compensation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Obesity + hypercapnia + daytime sleepiness = Obesity hypoventilation syndrome


## Wrong Options

### B) Panic disorder


Panic disorder may cause hyperventilation and respiratory alkalosis rather than chronic hypercapnia.

**Buzzword Combo:**  Anxiety + hyperventilation + low PaCO2 = Panic disorder


### C) Pulmonary embolism


Pulmonary embolism commonly causes acute dyspnea, pleuritic chest pain, and hypoxemia rather than chronic hypercapnia.


**Buzzword Combo:** Pleuritic chest pain + tachycardia + hypoxemia = Pulmonary embolism


### D) Asthma


Asthma presents with episodic wheezing and reversible airflow obstruction rather than chronic daytime hypercapnia from obesity.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Wheezing + reversible obstruction + atopy = Asthma
