## Buzzword Question

During a wedding reception, 120 of 200 guests who ate the salmon mousse appetizer developed gastrointestinal illness within 12 hours. Of 150 guests who did not eat the salmon mousse, 15 developed illness. The attack rate among guests who ate the salmon mousse is:

## Buzzwords Used

120/200 ate salmon mousse and became ill; 15/150 did not eat mousse but ill; foodborne outbreak; attack rate = cases among exposed / total exposed

## Answer Options

A) 60% 
B) 10%
 C) 44% 
D) 90%

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Attack rate among the exposed = cases among exposed / total exposed = 120/200 = 0.60 = 60%. The attack rate answers: 'Of all people who ate the salmon mousse, what proportion became ill?' The background illness rate in the unexposed group (15/150 = 10%) and relative risk = 60%/10% = 6 are separate but complementary measures.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Cases in exposed / total exposed = Attack rate in exposed = 120/200 = 60%

## Wrong Options

### B) 10%

10% = 15/150 = the attack rate in the UNEXPOSED group (guests who did not eat salmon mousse). This is the background illness rate. The question asks for the attack rate among the exposed (salmon mousse eaters) = 60%.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cases in unexposed / total unexposed = Attack rate in unexposed = 15/150 = 10% (background rate)

### C) 44%

44% would result from dividing all cases (120 + 15 = 135) by the total population (200 + 150 = 350): 135/350 = 38.6% (not 44%). This is the overall attack rate across the entire reception , a different measure from the exposed-specific attack rate.


**Buzzword Combo:** Total cases / total population = Overall (crude) attack rate = 135/350 = 38.6%

### D) 90%

90% could arise from subtracting the unexposed attack rate (10%) from 100%, which is not a standard epidemiological calculation. The attack rate in the exposed is directly calculated as 120/200 = 60%.

**Buzzword Combo:** Relative risk = exposed attack rate / unexposed attack rate = 60%/10% = 6 (not the attack rate itself)
