## Buzzword Question

A 42-year-old man with fatigue, episodic dark morning urine, and intravascular hemolysis undergoes flow cytometry. Which cell surface proteins are most likely absent in this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; dark morning urine; intravascular hemolysis; flow cytometry

## Answer Options

A. CD4 and CD8
B. CD15 and CD30
C. CD55 and CD59
D. CD19 and CD20

## Correct Answer

C. CD55 and CD59

## Explanation

PNH results from a PIGA gene mutation causing defective GPI anchor synthesis, leading to loss of complement regulatory proteins CD55 and CD59 on RBCs. This causes complement-mediated intravascular hemolysis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

PIGA mutation + CD55/CD59 loss + complement lysis = PNH

## Wrong Options

### A. CD4 and CD8

CD4 and CD8 are T-cell markers and are unrelated to RBC complement regulation or hemolysis.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD4+ helper T cells + CD8+ cytotoxic T cells = T-cell markers

### B. CD15 and CD30

CD15 and CD30 are characteristic of Reed-Sternberg cells in Hodgkin lymphoma, not RBC surface proteins.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD15+ CD30+ Reed-Sternberg cells = Hodgkin lymphoma

### D. CD19 and CD20

CD19 and CD20 are B-cell markers and are not involved in complement regulation or PNH pathogenesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD19+ CD20+ = B-cell lineage markers
