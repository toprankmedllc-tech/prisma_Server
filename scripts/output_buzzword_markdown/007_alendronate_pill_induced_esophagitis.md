## Buzzword Question

woman with osteoporosis develops painful swallowing and retrosternal chest pain shortly after starting weekly Alendronate therapy. She admits she usually takes the medication with very little water before going back to bed.
Which of the following is the most likely adverse effect of this medication?

## Buzzwords Used

 painful swallowing; retrosternal chest pain; Alendronate 

## Answer Options

A. Esophagitis
B. Gingival hyperplasia
C. Pulmonary fibrosis
D. Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Oral bisphosphonates such as alendronate can cause pill-induced esophagitis, especially when taken improperly. Patients should take the medication with a full glass of water and remain upright for at least 30 minutes afterward.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Alendronate + lying down after pill + odynophagia = esophagitis

## Wrong Options

### B. Gingival hyperplasia

Incorrect — Gingival hyperplasia is classically associated with medications such as phenytoin, cyclosporine, and calcium channel blockers.

**Buzzword Combo:** Phenytoin + gum overgrowth = gingival hyperplasia

### C. Pulmonary fibrosis

Incorrect — Pulmonary fibrosis is associated with drugs such as amiodarone, bleomycin, and methotrexate.

**Buzzword Combo:** Amiodarone + restrictive lung disease = pulmonary fibrosis

### D. Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus

Incorrect — Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus is a classic adverse effect of lithium therapy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Lithium + hypernatremia + polyuria = nephrogenic DI
