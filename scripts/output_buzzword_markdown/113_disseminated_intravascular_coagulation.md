## Buzzword Question

A critically ill patient with spontaneous bleeding from IV sites has thrombocytopenia, prolonged PT and aPTT, and evidence of fibrin degradation. Which laboratory finding is most characteristic of this condition?v

## Buzzwords Used

spontaneous bleeding; IV sites; thrombocytopenia; prolonged PT; prolonged aPTT; fibrin degradation

## Answer Options

A) Increased fibrinogen
B) Decreased D-dimer
C) Elevated D-dimer
D) Normal platelet count

## Correct Answer

C. Elevated D-dimer

## Explanation

DIC causes widespread fibrin formation and breakdown, producing fibrin degradation products including D-dimer. Elevated D-dimer is the most sensitive marker of ongoing coagulation and fibrinolysis in DIC.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Elevated D-dimer + thrombocytopenia + prolonged PT/PTT = DIC

## Wrong Options

### A. Increased fibrinogen

Fibrinogen is consumed during widespread clot formation in DIC, leading to decreased levels rather than increased. Low fibrinogen is a classic supportive finding in advanced DIC.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low fibrinogen + consumption coagulopathy = DIC

### B. Decreased D-dimer

D-dimer is elevated in DIC due to fibrinolysis of cross-linked fibrin. A decreased or normal D-dimer makes DIC unlikely.

**Buzzword Combo:** Normal/low D-dimer = DIC unlikely

### D. Normal platelet count

Platelets are consumed in microthrombi formation, leading to thrombocytopenia in DIC. A normal platelet count is not expected in established disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thrombocytopenia + consumption = DIC
