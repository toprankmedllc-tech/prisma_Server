## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old man with fatigue, weight loss, splenomegaly, marked leukocytosis with a left shift, and low LAP score has the Philadelphia chromosome on cytogenetics. Which translocation is responsible for this finding?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; weight loss; splenomegaly; leukocytosis; left shift; low LAP score; Philadelphia chromosome

## Answer Options

A) t(8;14)
B) t(14;18)
C) t(15;17)
D) t(9;22)

## Correct Answer

D. t(9;22)

## Explanation

The Philadelphia chromosome results from t(9;22)(q34;q11), creating the BCR-ABL fusion gene with constitutive tyrosine kinase activity. This drives chronic myeloid leukemia with granulocytosis, basophilia, and splenomegaly.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

t(9;22) + BCR-ABL + low LAP + splenomegaly = CML

## Wrong Options

### A. t(8;14)

t(8;14) activates c-MYC and is characteristic of Burkitt lymphoma, which shows a starry-sky pattern. It is unrelated to CML.

**Buzzword Combo:** t(8;14) + c-MYC + starry sky = Burkitt lymphoma

### B. t(14;18)

t(14;18) leads to BCL2 overexpression, inhibiting apoptosis in follicular lymphoma. It is not associated with myeloproliferative disorders.

**Buzzword Combo:** t(14;18) + BCL2 + follicular lymphoma = Follicular lymphoma

### C. t(15;17)

t(15;17) produces the PML-RARA fusion seen in acute promyelocytic leukemia, which is associated with DIC. It is unrelated to CML.

**Buzzword Combo:** t(15;17) + PML-RARA + DIC = APL
