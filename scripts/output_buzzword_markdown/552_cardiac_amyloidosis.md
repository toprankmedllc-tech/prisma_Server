## Buzzword Question


A patient with cardiac amyloidosis is being evaluated for the underlying cause. Which of the following pairings of amyloid subtype and predisposing condition is correct?

## Buzzwords Used


Cardiac amyloidosis

## Answer Options

A) AL amyloid — multiple myeloma
B) AL amyloid — chronic rheumatoid arthritis
C) AA amyloid — multiple myeloma
D) ATTR amyloid — chronic inflammatory bowel disease

## Correct Answer


A) AL amyloid — multiple myeloma

## Explanation

AL (light-chain) amyloidosis is produced by clonal plasma cells secreting monoclonal immunoglobulin light chains that misfold into amyloid fibrils. It is classically associated with multiple myeloma and MGUS.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Monoclonal light chains + plasma cell dyscrasia + multiple myeloma = AL amyloidosis

## Wrong Options

### B) AL amyloid — chronic rheumatoid arthritis

Chronic inflammation produces AA amyloid from serum amyloid A, not AL amyloid.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic RA / IBD / chronic infection + serum amyloid A = AA amyloidosis

### C) AA amyloid — multiple myeloma

Multiple myeloma is the classic source of AL amyloid (light chains), not AA.

**Buzzword Combo:** Multiple myeloma + light chains = AL amyloidosis (not AA)

### D) ATTR amyloid — chronic inflammatory bowel disease


ATTR amyloid is derived from transthyretin (hereditary mutation or wild-type/senile), not from chronic inflammation. IBD is associated with AA amyloid.

**Buzzword Combo:** Transthyretin + hereditary or senile/wild-type = ATTR amyloidosis

## Related Concepts

Multiple myeloma; ATTR amyloidosis; AA amyloidosis from chronic inflammation
