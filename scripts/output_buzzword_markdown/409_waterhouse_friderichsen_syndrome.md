## Buzzword Question

A patient with meningococcal septicemia suddenly develops hypotension, disseminated intravascular coagulation, and bilateral adrenal hemorrhagic necrosis causing acute adrenal insufficiency. What is this condition called?


## Buzzwords Used

 Meningococcemia ; DIC ; adrenal hemorrhage ; shock ; acute adrenal insufficiency


## Answer Options

 A) Conn syndrome
 B) Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome
 C) Cushing syndrome
 D) Sheehan syndrome


## Correct Answer

B) Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome


## Explanation

Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome is acute adrenal insufficiency caused by bilateral adrenal hemorrhage, classically associated with meningococcal sepsis and disseminated intravascular coagulation.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Meningococcemia + DIC + adrenal hemorrhage = Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome

## Wrong Options

### A) Conn syndrome


Conn syndrome involves primary hyperaldosteronism causing hypertension and hypokalemia.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Hypertension + hypokalemia + aldosterone excess = Conn syndrome

###  C) Cushing syndrome


Cushing syndrome results from excess cortisol production and presents with central obesity, hyperglycemia, and striae.


**Buzzword Combo:** Moon facies + purple striae + hyperglycemia = Cushing syndrome


### D) Sheehan syndrome


Sheehan syndrome is postpartum pituitary infarction causing hypopituitarism after severe obstetric hemorrhage.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Postpartum hemorrhage + failure to lactate + pituitary infarction = Sheehan syndrome
