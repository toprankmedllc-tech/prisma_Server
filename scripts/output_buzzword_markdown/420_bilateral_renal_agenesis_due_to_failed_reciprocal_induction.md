## Buzzword Question

A neonate is born with Potter sequence, pulmonary hypoplasia, and bilateral renal agenesis. Failure of interaction between which two embryologic structures most likely caused this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Potter sequence ; Pulmonary hypoplasia ; Bilateral renal agenesis ; Reciprocal induction

## Answer Options

A) Ureteric bud and metanephric mesenchyme
B) Mesonephric duct and paramesonephric duct
C) Neural tube and somites
D) Allantois and cloaca

## Correct Answer

A) Ureteric bud and metanephric mesenchyme

## Explanation

Reciprocal induction between the ureteric bud and metanephric mesenchyme is required for normal kidney development. Failure of this interaction causes bilateral renal agenesis, leading to oligohydramnios and Potter sequence.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Potter sequence + Pulmonary hypoplasia + Bilateral renal agenesis = Failed reciprocal induction

## Wrong Options

### B) Mesonephric duct and paramesonephric duct

These structures contribute to development of the reproductive tract, not the permanent kidneys.

**Buzzword Combo:** Müllerian anomalies + Wolffian duct defects + Reproductive tract abnormalities = Mesonephric/paramesonephric defect

### C) Neural tube and somites

These embryologic structures are involved in axial skeleton and nervous system development rather than renal organogenesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neural tube defects + Vertebral abnormalities + Myotome defects = Somite involvement

### D) Allantois and cloaca

These structures contribute to bladder and urachal development and are not responsible for formation of the kidneys.

**Buzzword Combo:** Patent urachus + Urine leakage from umbilicus + Bladder anomalies = Allantoic defect
