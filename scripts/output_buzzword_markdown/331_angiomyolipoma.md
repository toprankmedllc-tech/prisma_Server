## Buzzword Question

A patient with seizures and facial angiofibromas is found to have a renal mass containing fat and smooth muscle. Which of the following renal neoplasms is most strongly associated with this patient’s condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Tuberous sclerosis; Facial angiofibromas; renal mass

## Answer Options

A) Angiomyolipoma
B) Wilms tumor
C) Urothelial carcinoma
D) Clear cell renal cell carcinoma

## Correct Answer

A) Angiomyolipoma

## Explanation

Renal angiomyolipoma is a benign renal tumor composed of blood vessels, smooth muscle, and adipose tissue. It is strongly associated with tuberous sclerosis complex and may present with flank pain, hematuria, or spontaneous hemorrhage.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Angiofibromas + Angiomyolipoma + smooth muscle proliferation = Tuberous sclerosis

## Wrong Options

### Wilms tumor

Wilms tumor is a malignant renal tumor of childhood associated with WT1 mutations. It typically presents as an abdominal mass in young children rather than adults with tuberous sclerosis

**Buzzword Combo:** Childhood renal mass + WT1 mutation + nephroblastoma = Wilms tumor

### Urothelial carcinoma

Urothelial carcinoma is associated with smoking and aromatic amine exposure. Patients commonly present with painless hematuria arising from the bladder or renal pelvis urothelium

**Buzzword Combo:** Smoking + painless hematuria + amine exposure = Urothelial carcinoma

### Clear cell renal cell carcinoma

Clear cell renal cell carcinoma is associated with VHL gene abnormalities on chromosome 3p and originates from renal tubular epithelium. It commonly presents with hematuria, flank pain, and paraneoplastic syndromes

**Buzzword Combo:** VHL mutation + renal cortical tumor + VEGF overexpression = Clear cell RCC
