## Buzzword Question

An elderly patient with bone pain, recurrent infections, anemia, renal dysfunction, hypercalcemia, and an M protein spike on electrophoresis most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

elderly; bone pain; recurrent infections; anemia; renal dysfunction; hypercalcemia; M protein spike; CRAB criteria

## Answer Options

A) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
B) Multiple myeloma
C) Hodgkin lymphoma
D) Waldenström macroglobulinemia

## Correct Answer

B. Multiple myeloma

## Explanation

Multiple myeloma is a plasma cell malignancy characterized by monoclonal immunoglobulin (M protein) production and CRAB features (hyperCalcemia, Renal failure, Anemia, Bone lesions). It results from malignant plasma cells in the bone marrow causing bone destruction and organ dysfunction.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CRAB + M protein spike + lytic bone lesions = Multiple myeloma

## Wrong Options

### A. Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL presents with lymphocytosis, smudge cells, and lymphadenopathy in elderly patients. It does not cause lytic bone lesions, hypercalcemia, or M protein spikes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + CD5/CD23+ + lymphocytosis = CLL

### C. Hodgkin lymphoma

Hodgkin lymphoma is defined by Reed-Sternberg cells and presents with painless lymphadenopathy and B symptoms. It is not associated with CRAB features or M protein spike.

**Buzzword Combo:** Reed-Sternberg cells + B symptoms + lymphadenopathy = Hodgkin lymphoma

### D. Waldenström macroglobulinemia

Waldenström macroglobulinemia is an IgM-producing lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma causing hyperviscosity symptoms. It does not cause lytic bone lesions or significant renal failure like multiple myeloma.

**Buzzword Combo:** IgM spike + hyperviscosity + no lytic lesions = Waldenström macroglobulinemia
