## Buzzword Question

A 66-year-old man in the ICU after prolonged mechanical ventilation develops fever, dyspnea, and purulent sputum on hospital day 7. Chest x-ray shows bilateral infiltrates. Sputum culture grows a non-lactose-fermenting Gram-negative rod that produces a blue-green pigment and has a characteristic fruity odor. Which virulence factor is most important for this organism’s pathogenicity?

## Buzzwords Used

ICU patient; ventilator-associated pneumonia; burns; non-lactose fermenting Gram-negative rod; blue-green pigment; fruity odor

## Answer Options

A) Polysaccharide capsule
B) Lipid A endotoxin
C) IgA protease
D) Type III secretion system

## Correct Answer

D) Type III secretion system

## Explanation

The organism is Pseudomonas aeruginosa, a classic cause of ventilator-associated pneumonia in ICU patients, burns, and immunocompromised hosts. Its key virulence factor is the type III secretion system, which injects toxins into host cells leading to tissue damage and necrotizing infection.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Burns + ICU ventilator + blue-green pigment + non-lactose fermenter = Pseudomonas → Type III secretion system

## Wrong Options

### A) Polysaccharide capsule

Capsule-mediated anti-phagocytosis is typical of organisms like Klebsiella and S. pneumoniae, not Pseudomonas, which relies on toxin injection systems rather than a prominent capsule.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mucoid capsule + lactose fermenter + lobar pneumonia = Klebsiella mismatch

### B) Lipid A endotoxin

Lipid A causes fever and septic shock in all Gram-negative bacteria, but is not the primary virulence mechanism explaining tissue necrosis or the characteristic ICU pneumonia pattern here.

**Buzzword Combo:** Gram-negative sepsis + fever + hypotension = endotoxin nonspecific trap

### C) IgA protease

IgA protease is used by mucosal pathogens like Neisseria and H. influenzae to colonize respiratory mucosa, not for necrotizing ventilator-associated pneumonia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mucosal infection + nasopharynx colonization + otitis/meningitis = IgA protease trap
