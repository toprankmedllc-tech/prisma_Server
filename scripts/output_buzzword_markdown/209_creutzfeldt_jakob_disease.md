## Buzzword Question

A 70-year-old man develops rapidly progressive dementia over 3 months together with frequent shock-like muscle jerks reliably triggered by sudden noise. EEG shows periodic biphasic and triphasic sharp wave complexes at approximately 1 Hz. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Rapid dementia (weeks to months); startle-induced myoclonus; 1 Hz periodic sharp waves on EEG

## Answer Options

A) Alzheimer disease
B) Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (SSPE)
C) Dementia with Lewy bodies
D) Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

## Correct Answer

D) Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

## Explanation

Rapidly progressive dementia, startle-induced myoclonus, and periodic 1 Hz sharp wave complexes on EEG together are highly specific for sporadic CJD.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Rapid dementia + startle myoclonus + 1 Hz periodic sharp waves on EEG = Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

## Wrong Options

### Alzheimer disease

Alzheimer disease evolves over years rather than weeks to months, lacks startle myoclonus, and shows nonspecific EEG findings rather than 1 Hz periodic complexes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Insidious amnestic decline + hippocampal atrophy + beta-amyloid plaques + tau tangles = Alzheimer disease

### Subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (SSPE)

SSPE is a late complication of measles in children and adolescents and produces periodic complexes at a slower frequency (every 4 to 15 seconds) along with personality change and myoclonus, not 1 Hz periodic sharp waves in an elderly patient.

**Buzzword Combo:** Child or adolescent + prior measles + behavioral change + slow periodic EEG complexes = SSPE

### Dementia with Lewy bodies

DLB features parkinsonism, fluctuating cognition, recurrent visual hallucinations, and REM sleep behavior disorder over months to years; it lacks startle myoclonus and 1 Hz periodic EEG complexes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Parkinsonism + early dementia + visual hallucinations + REM sleep behavior disorder = dementia with Lewy bodies

## Related Concepts

SSPE (measles, slower periodic EEG complexes); Hashimoto encephalopathy (anti-thyroid antibodies, steroid responsive); Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis (younger patients, orofacial dyskinesia)
