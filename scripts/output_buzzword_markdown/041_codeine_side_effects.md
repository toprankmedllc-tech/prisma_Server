## Buzzword Question

A retired shipyard worker with calcified pleural plaques develops constipation, blurry vision, and urinary retention after cough treatment. Which drug was most likely prescribed?

## Buzzwords Used

Shipyard; Pleural plaques; Urinary retention


## Answer Options

A) Ipratropium
B) Codeine
C) Nintedanib
D) Rifampin


## Correct Answer

B) Codeine

## Explanation

Asbestosis classically presents with progressive dyspnea and pleural plaques after asbestos exposure. Codeine suppresses cough via μ-opioid receptors and commonly causes constipation, urinary retention, blurry vision, and sedation.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pleural plaques + chronic cough + constipation = Codeine

## Wrong Options

### Ipratropium

Ipratropium is an inhaled antimuscarinic bronchodilator mainly used in COPD and asthma.

**Buzzword Combo:** COPD + bronchodilation + M3 blockade = Ipratropium

### Nintedanib

Nintedanib is a tyrosine kinase inhibitor used in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis

**Buzzword Combo:** IPF + tyrosine kinase inhibition + diarrhea = Nintedanib

### Rifampin

Rifampin inhibits bacterial RNA polymerase and is used in tuberculosis treatment

**Buzzword Combo:** TB treatment + orange secretions + RNA polymerase inhibition = Rifampin
