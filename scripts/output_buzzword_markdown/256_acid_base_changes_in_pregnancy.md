## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old woman at 32 weeks gestation presents for a routine prenatal visit. She has no complaints. ABG: pH 7.44, PaCO₂ 30 mmHg, HCO₃ 20 mEq/L. What is the correct interpretation?

## Buzzwords Used

Pregnancy; Third trimester; pH 7.44; PaCO₂ 30; HCO₃ 20; Physiologic respiratory alkalosis; Progesterone

## Answer Options

A) Pathologic respiratory alkalosis requiring workup
B) Metabolic acidosis with respiratory compensation
C) Normal physiologic change of pregnancy
D) Primary metabolic alkalosis

## Correct Answer

C

## Explanation

In pregnancy, progesterone stimulates the respiratory center causing chronic hyperventilation — a physiologic respiratory alkalosis. The kidneys compensate by excreting bicarbonate, lowering HCO₃ to ~18–20 mEq/L. The pH remains near normal. This ABG pattern is entirely expected and normal in pregnancy.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pregnancy + progesterone + low PaCO₂ + low HCO₃ + near-normal pH = Physiologic respiratory alkalosis of pregnancy

## Wrong Options

### A) Pathologic respiratory alkalosis requiring workup

In pregnancy , the physiology of the body is such that it results in mild respiratory alkalosis .This is a normal finding.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low PaCO₂ + alkalotic tendency = Premature pathologic label without recognizing normal pregnancy physiology

### B) Metabolic acidosis with respiratory compensation

Metabolic acidosis requires a primarily low HCO₃ driving an acidemic pH. Here the pH is 7.44 — alkalemic — and the primary driver is the low PaCO₂ from progesterone-driven hyperventilation, not a primary metabolic process.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low HCO₃ seen + acidosis assumed = Incorrect primary label when pH is alkalemic and PaCO₂ is the primary change

### D) Primary metabolic alkalosis

Primary metabolic alkalosis features a high HCO₃ as the primary abnormality. Here HCO₃ is low (20 mEq/L) — the opposite of what metabolic alkalosis produces. The HCO₃ is low because the kidneys are compensating for the chronic respiratory alkalosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Alkalemic pH + near-normal values = Metabolic alkalosis label applied when HCO₃ is actually low, not high
