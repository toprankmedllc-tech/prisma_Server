## Buzzword Question

A 29-year-old pregnant woman at 22 weeks gestation presents with intermittent sharp pain in the right groin that worsens when she suddenly stands up or changes position. She denies vaginal bleeding, contractions, or fever. Physical examination is unremarkable except for mild tenderness near the inguinal region. Stretching of which of the following structures is the most likely cause of this patient’s symptoms?

## Buzzwords Used

Pregnancy; Sharp groin pain; Worse with movement; Second trimester; Inguinal tenderness; Positional pain

## Answer Options

A) Broad ligament
B) Round ligament
C) Cardinal ligament
D) Suspensory ligament of the ovary

## Correct Answer

B) Round ligament

## Explanation

The round ligament helps maintain the anteverted position of the uterus and stretches as the uterus enlarges during pregnancy. This commonly causes sharp groin pain during the second trimester.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pregnancy + Sharp groin pain + Worse with movement = Round ligament pain

## Wrong Options

### A) Broad ligament

The broad ligament is a double layer of peritoneum supporting pelvic organs but is not typically associated with positional groin pain in pregnancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Peritoneal fold + Supports uterus + Mesosalpinx = Broad ligament

### C) Cardinal ligament

The cardinal ligament provides major support to the cervix and uterus and is associated with uterine prolapse when weakened.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pelvic organ prolapse + Multiparity + Cervical descent = Cardinal ligament weakness

### D) Suspensory ligament of the ovary

The suspensory ligament contains ovarian vessels and is important in ovarian torsion and ovarian vessel ligation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Contains ovarian vessels + Ovarian torsion + Lateral pelvic wall = Suspensory ligament of ovary
