## Buzzword Question

A 2-year-old boy has recurrent bacterial infections due to defective complement-mediated marking of microbes for ingestion by phagocytes. Which of the following complement products normally performs this function?

## Buzzwords Used

2-year-old boy; recurrent bacterial infections; defective complement-mediated marking of microbes for ingestion

## Answer Options

A) C3b
B) C5a
C) C3a
D) C5b-C9

## Correct Answer

A) C3b


## Explanation

C3b is a major opsonin that coats bacteria and allows phagocytes to ingest them more efficiently. C3 deficiency decreases C3b-mediated opsonization and predisposes to recurrent bacterial infections.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Bacterial surface coating + enhanced phagocytosis + major complement opsonin = C3b

## Wrong Options

### B) C5a

C5a is a strong neutrophil chemotactic factor and anaphylatoxin. It does not primarily function as the major complement opsonin.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neutrophil recruitment + inflammation + potent chemotactic factor = C5a

### C) C3a

C3a is an anaphylatoxin that triggers mast-cell degranulation. It does not coat bacteria to facilitate phagocytosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mast-cell degranulation + inflammation + anaphylatoxin = C3a

### D) C5b-C9

C5b-C9 forms the membrane attack complex, which lyses cells. Deficiency predisposes to recurrent Neisseria infections rather than impaired opsonization.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cell lysis + terminal complement pathway + recurrent Neisseria infections = C5b-C9 deficiency
