## Buzzword Question

A 63-year-old woman is evaluated for gradually worsening peripheral vision loss. Tonometry reveals elevated intraocular pressure. She is started on a medication that increases uveoscleral outflow of aqueous humor. Several months later, she notices darkening of her irises and excessive eyelash growth. Which medication was most likely prescribed?

## Buzzwords Used

Peripheral vision loss ; increased intraocular pressure ; increased uveoscleral outflow ; iris browning ; eyelash growth ; prostaglandin analog

## Answer Options

A) Timolol
B) Acetazolamide
C) Pilocarpine
D) Latanoprost

## Correct Answer

D) Latanoprost

## Explanation

Latanoprost is a prostaglandin F2α analog used for open-angle glaucoma. It lowers intraocular pressure by increasing uveoscleral outflow and classically causes iris darkening and eyelash growth.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Open-angle glaucoma + eyelash growth + iris browning = Latanoprost

## Wrong Options

### A) Timolol

Timolol decreases aqueous humor production but does not cause iris pigmentation or eyelash growth.

**Buzzword Combo:** Reduced aqueous humor production + beta blocker + no pupillary change = Timolol

### B) Acetazolamide

Acetazolamide lowers intraocular pressure by decreasing aqueous humor synthesis and is not associated with cosmetic eyelash changes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Carbonic anhydrase inhibition + decreased aqueous production + diuretic = Acetazolamide

### C) Pilocarpine

Pilocarpine increases trabecular outflow through ciliary muscle contraction and commonly causes miosis and cyclospasm.

**Buzzword Combo:** Miosis + trabecular meshwork opening + acute angle closure = Pilocarpine
