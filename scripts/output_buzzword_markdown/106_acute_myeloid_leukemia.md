## Buzzword Question

A patient with fatigue, infections, easy bruising, and numerous myeloblasts with needle-shaped inclusions on peripheral smear most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; infections; easy bruising; myeloblasts; needle-shaped inclusions; Auer rods

## Answer Options

A) Acute lymphoblastic leukemia
B) Acute myeloid leukemia
C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
D) Multiple myeloma

## Correct Answer

B. Acute myeloid leukemia

## Explanation

Auer rods are needle-shaped azurophilic inclusions composed of fused primary granules and are pathognomonic for myeloid lineage. Their presence in myeloblasts confirms acute myeloid leukemia.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Auer rods + myeloblasts + pancytopenia = Acute myeloid leukemia

## Wrong Options

### A. Acute lymphoblastic leukemia

ALL shows TdT-positive lymphoblasts and does not form Auer rods. It is most common in children and lacks myeloid granules.

**Buzzword Combo:** TdT-positive lymphoblasts + childhood + no Auer rods = ALL

### C. Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL presents with mature B-cell lymphocytosis and smudge cells, typically in elderly patients. It does not involve myeloblasts or Auer rods.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + CD5/CD23+ lymphocytosis = CLL

### D. Multiple myeloma

Multiple myeloma is a plasma cell neoplasm characterized by M protein, lytic bone lesions, and CRAB features. It does not involve myeloblasts or Auer rods.

**Buzzword Combo:** M protein spike + lytic lesions + plasma cells = Multiple myeloma
