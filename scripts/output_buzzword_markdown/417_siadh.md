## Buzzword Question

A patient with small cell lung carcinoma develops euvolemic hypotonic hyponatremia, low serum osmolality, concentrated urine, and elevated urine sodium. What is the diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Small cell lung carcinoma ; euvolemic hyponatremia ; concentrated urine ; elevated urine sodium ; low serum osmolality


## Answer Options

 A) Diabetes insipidus
 B) SIADH
 C) Hyperaldosteronism
 D) Nephrotic syndrome


## Correct Answer

B) SIADH


## Explanation

SIADH causes inappropriate water retention leading to euvolemic hypotonic hyponatremia with concentrated urine and elevated urinary sodium. Small cell lung carcinoma is a classic cause.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

 Small cell lung cancer + euvolemic hyponatremia + concentrated urine = SIADH

## Wrong Options

### A) Diabetes insipidus


 Diabetes insipidus causes dilute urine and hypernatremia due to impaired ADH activity.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Polyuria + dilute urine + hypernatremia = Diabetes insipidus


###  C) Hyperaldosteronism


Hyperaldosteronism causes hypertension and hypokalemia rather than hypotonic hyponatremia.

**Buzzword Combo:**  Hypertension + hypokalemia + high aldosterone = Hyperaldosteronism


### D) Nephrotic syndrome


Nephrotic syndrome causes hypervolemic edema due to hypoalbuminemia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Proteinuria + edema + hypoalbuminemia = Nephrotic syndrome
