## Buzzword Question

A previously healthy infant girl develops developmental regression at 12 months of age with loss of purposeful hand movements and repetitive hand-wringing motions. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

repetitive hand-wringing motions; loss of purposeful hand movements; developmental regression; 

## Answer Options

 A) Rett syndrome
 B) Autism spectrum disorder
 C) Fragile X syndrome
 D) Angelman syndrome


## Correct Answer

A. Rett syndrome


## Explanation

Rett syndrome is characterized by normal early development followed by developmental regression, stereotypic hand-wringing movements, and intellectual disability in girls due to MECP2 mutation.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Female child + developmental regression + hand wringing = Rett syndrome


## Wrong Options

### B. Autism spectrum disorder


Autism spectrum disorder involves social communication deficits and repetitive behaviors but not classic loss of purposeful hand movements after normal development.

**Buzzword Combo:** Social communication deficits + repetitive behaviors + restricted interests = Autism spectrum disorder


### C. Fragile X syndrome


Fragile X syndrome presents with intellectual disability and autistic features but lacks hand-wringing movements and developmental regression.


**Buzzword Combo:** Macroorchidism + large ears + intellectual disability = Fragile X syndrome

### D. Angelman syndrome


Angelman syndrome commonly presents with inappropriate laughter, seizures, and severe developmental delay rather than hand-wringing regression.


**Buzzword Combo:** Happy demeanor + seizures + ataxia = Angelman syndrome
