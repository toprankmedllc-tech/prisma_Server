## Buzzword Question

In a prospective cohort study, women who exercise ≥150 min/week have a 3% incidence of postmenopausal breast cancer, compared to 6% in sedentary women. The relative risk of breast cancer in exercising women compared to sedentary women is 0.50. Which of the following correctly interprets this finding?

## Buzzwords Used

Exercise 150 min/week; incidence 3% vs 6%; RR = 0.50; exercising vs sedentary; breast cancer


## Answer Options

A) Exercising women have half the risk of breast cancer compared to sedentary women; exercise is associated with a 50% risk reduction 
B) Exercising women have twice the risk of breast cancer compared to sedentary women 
C) There is no association between exercise and breast cancer because RR = 0.50 is close to 1.0 
D) The absolute risk reduction is 50%, meaning 50 fewer cases per 100 women exercised

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Relative risk (RR) = Risk in exposed / Risk in unexposed = 3%/6% = 0.50. When RR < 1, the exposure is protective. RR = 0.50 means exercising women have HALF the risk of developing breast cancer compared to sedentary women — a 50% relative risk reduction (RRR = 1 - RR = 1 - 0.50 = 50%). This does not mean the absolute risk is reduced by 50 percentage points; ARR = 6% - 3% = 3% (3 fewer cases per 100 women). RR = 1 indicates no association; RR > 1 indicates increased risk; RR < 1 indicates protective association.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

RR = exposed risk / unexposed risk; RR < 1 = protective; RRR = 1 - RR = percent risk reduction

## Wrong Options

### B) Exercising women have twice the risk of breast cancer compared to sedentary women

This inverts the ratio. RR = 0.50 means exercising women have HALF (not twice) the risk. If sedentary women had been the reference group with exercising as unexposed, the RR would be 6%/3% = 2.0. The direction of the comparison determines interpretation.

**Buzzword Combo:** RR = unexposed/exposed (inverted) = 6%/3% = 2.0 would mean exposure doubles risk  this is the wrong direction

### C) There is no association between exercise and breast cancer because RR = 0.50 is close to 1.0

RR = 1.0 indicates no association (null value). RR = 0.50 is substantially different from 1.0 and represents a strong protective association a 50% relative risk reduction. Whether this is statistically significant requires a confidence interval or p-value, but the point estimate of 0.50 strongly suggests an association.

**Buzzword Combo:** RR = 1.0 = no association (null); RR far from 1.0 = association present (direction depends on whether >1 or <1)

### D) The absolute risk reduction is 50%, meaning 50 fewer cases per 100 women exercised

RRR (relative risk reduction) = 50%, but ARR (absolute risk reduction) = 6% - 3% = 3% meaning 3 fewer cases per 100 women who exercise. Confusing RRR with ARR is a classic USMLE trap. NNT = 1/ARR = 1/0.03 ≈ 33 women need to exercise to prevent 1 case.

**Buzzword Combo:** ARR = unexposed risk - exposed risk = 6% - 3% = 3%; RRR = 1 - RR = 50%; NNT = 1/ARR
