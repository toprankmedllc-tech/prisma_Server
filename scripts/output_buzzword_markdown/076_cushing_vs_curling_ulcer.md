## Buzzword Question

A 35-year-old patient is admitted to the Intensive Care Unit after sustaining severe 3rd-degree burns over 40% of their total body surface area. Several days later, the patient develops coffee-ground emesis. Endoscopy reveals multiple acute gastric ulcers.These stress-induced ulcers are classically referred to as:

## Buzzwords Used

burns; multiple acute gastric ulcers;stress-induced ulcers

## Answer Options

A) Cushing ulcers
B) Curling ulcers 
C) Barrett ulcers
D) Dieulafoy lesions

## Correct Answer

B) Curling ulcers 

## Explanation

Curling ulcers are acute gastric ulcers associated with severe burns (due to reduced plasma volume leading to ischemia and necrosis of the gastric mucosa).Cushing ulcers are acute ulcers associated with severe head trauma or increased intracranial pressure (caused by vagal stimulation and hypersecretion of acid). 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Burns + ulcers = Curling ulcers 

## Wrong Options

### A) Cushing ulcers

A Cushing ulcer is a type of stress ulcer in the stomach, duodenum, or esophagus caused by elevated intracranial pressure (ICP). It is most commonly seen in patients with severe head trauma, brain tumors, or following intracranial surgery.

**Buzzword Combo:** Increased Intracranial Pressure (ICP) + Vagus Nerve Stimulation + Increased Gastric Acid = Cushing's Ulcer.

### C) Barrett ulcers

Barrett ulcers are deep, open sores that develop in the abnormal columnar lining of the esophagus (known as Barrett’s esophagus). They are caused by long-standing gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) and stomach acid chronically damaging the esophageal tissue.

**Buzzword Combo:** GERD + abnormal columnar line = Barret Ulcers

### D) Dieulafoy lesions

A Dieulafoy lesion is a relatively rare but potentially life-threatening vascular abnormality characterized by an abnormally large, tortuous artery in the submucosa that erodes and bleeds. It is a well-known, yet sometimes elusive, cause of sudden, massive gastrointestinal (GI) hemorrhages. 

**Buzzword Combo:** vascular abnormality + Hematochezia = Dieulafoy lesions
