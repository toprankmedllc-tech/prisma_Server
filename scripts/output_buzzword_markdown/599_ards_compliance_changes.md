## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old ICU patient develops acute respiratory distress syndrome. He requires high PEEP and high inspiratory pressures to maintain oxygenation. What is the key abnormality in lung mechanics?

## Buzzwords Used

ARDS; ICU patient; acute respiratory distress; high PEEP requirement; high inspiratory pressures; hypoxemia; stiff lungs; low compliance

## Answer Options

A) Increased lung compliance due to fluid-filled alveoli
B) Decreased lung compliance due to stiff, noncompliant lungs
C) Increased airway compliance with low resistance
D) Normal compliance with isolated diffusion defect

## Correct Answer

B) Decreased lung compliance due to stiff, noncompliant lungs

## Explanation

ARDS causes diffuse alveolar damage with inflammatory fluid accumulation, surfactant loss, and atelectasis. This makes the lungs stiff and poorly distensible, leading to markedly decreased lung compliance and increased work of ventilation

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

ARDS; alveolar damage; surfactant loss; pulmonary edema; ↓ compliance

## Wrong Options

### A) Increased lung compliance due to fluid-filled alveoli

Fluid-filled alveoli reduce distensibility rather than increase it. ARDS causes stiff lungs, not more compliant lungs.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pulmonary edema; alveolar flooding; impaired expansion

### C) Increased airway compliance with low resistance

ARDS is primarily a parenchymal (alveolar) problem, not an airway resistance disorder. Compliance is reduced due to stiff lung tissue.

**Buzzword Combo:** Airway disease; obstruction; bronchial pathology

### D) Normal compliance with isolated diffusion defect

ARDS affects both gas exchange and lung mechanics; compliance is significantly reduced, not normal. Diffusion defect alone does not explain high ventilatory pressures.

**Buzzword Combo:** Diffusion limitation; normal mechanics; isolated gas exchange defect
