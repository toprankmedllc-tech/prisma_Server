## Buzzword Question

A 72-year-old man with hypertension and chronic kidney disease recently retired and is reviewing his healthcare coverage options. He lives independently with his spouse and has a stable retirement income. During a clinic visit, he asks which government-sponsored insurance program he is most likely eligible to receive based primarily on his age.

Which of the following insurance programs is this patient most likely eligible for?

## Buzzwords Used

Older adult ; Retirement ; Chronic disease ; Government insurance eligibility ; Age-based qualification

## Answer Options

A) Medicaid

B) Medicare

C) Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)-linked coverage

D) Veterans Health Administration coverage

## Correct Answer

B) Medicare

## Explanation

Medicare is a federal health insurance program primarily available to individuals aged 65 years and older, regardless of income or employment status. Medicaid is income-based, SSDI-linked coverage depends on qualifying disability status, and Veterans Health Administration coverage requires military service history.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Age ≥65 + retirement status + government insurance eligibility = Medicare

## Wrong Options

### A) Medicaid

Medicaid eligibility is primarily based on low income or disability rather than age alone.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low income + resource limitation + (or) disability + need for state/federal assistance

### C) Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)-linked coverage

SSDI-linked Medicare eligibility applies to individuals who are unable to work due to a qualifying disability expected to last at least 12 months and who have sufficient work credits. Age alone does not qualify a patient for this pathway.

**Buzzword Combo:** Disabling medical condition + inability to maintain employment + ≥12 months duration + work credit history

### D) Veterans Health Administration coverage

VA healthcare eligibility is based on prior military service and discharge status, not age, income, or medical conditions alone. Patients without qualifying service history are not eligible.

**Buzzword Combo:** History of military service + qualifying discharge status + VA enrollment eligibility + service-connected or general VA care access
