## Buzzword Question

Which of the following is the strongest predisposing risk factor for endocarditis caused by Streptococcus viridans?

## Buzzwords Used

Streptococcus viridans; subacute endocarditis

## Answer Options

A) Recent dental procedure in a patient with a previously damaged valve
B) Active intravenous drug use
C) Recent prosthetic valve replacement
D) Underlying colon cancer

## Correct Answer


A) Recent dental procedure in a patient with a previously damaged valve

## Explanation

Streptococcus viridans is part of normal oral flora and is the most common cause of subacute bacterial endocarditis. It is seeded into the bloodstream during dental procedures and preferentially colonizes previously damaged or abnormal valves (e.g., mitral valve prolapse, rheumatic heart disease).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Dental procedure + previously damaged valve = Streptococcus viridans endocarditis

## Wrong Options

### 
B) Active intravenous drug use

IVDU predisposes to acute endocarditis caused by Staphylococcus aureus on the tricuspid valve, not subacute S. viridans endocarditis.

**Buzzword Combo:** IV drug use + tricuspid valve = S. aureus endocarditis

### C) Recent prosthetic valve replacement

Within 60 days of valve surgery, the most common cause is Staphylococcus epidermidis, not S. viridans.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recent prosthetic valve + coagulase-negative staph = S. epidermidis endocarditis

### D) Underlying colon cancer

Colon cancer is associated with Streptococcus gallolyticus (bovis) endocarditis — endocarditis from S. gallolyticus mandates a colonoscopy to rule out a colonic malignancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
S. gallolyticus endocarditis + colon cancer = mandatory colonoscopy
