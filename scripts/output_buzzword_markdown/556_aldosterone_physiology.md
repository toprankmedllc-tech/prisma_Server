## Buzzword Question

A 48-year-old man with a history of primary adrenal insufficiency (Addison disease) is admitted to the intensive care unit due to an acute adrenal crisis triggered by a severe upper respiratory tract infection. He is hypotensive, tachycardic, and obtunded. Fluid resuscitation is initiated. Laboratory studies reveal severe hyponatremia, hyperkalemia, and a profound decrease in serum aldosterone concentration. Given the complete disruption of the distal adrenal limb of the RAAS pathway, which of the following logical adaptations will be maximally functional within the juxtaglomerular apparatus (JGA) to preserve systemic blood pressure, and how will it directly impact the filtration fraction (FF)?

## Buzzwords Used

Primary adrenal insufficiency; Adrenal crisis; Serum aldosterone concentration; Juxtaglomerular apparatus; Filtration fraction

## Answer Options

A)Maximal renin secretion driven by a loss of aldosterone-mediated volume expansion, leading to intense Angiotensin II-mediated efferent arteriolar constriction that increases the filtration fraction (FF=GFR/RPF).
B) Direct feedback suppression of ACE expression in the pulmonary capillaries, leading to a profound decrease in renal plasma flow and an isolated drop in filtration fraction (FF).
C) Upregulation of atrial natriuretic peptide (ANP) to directly stimulate Macula Densa sodium delivery, causing parallel dilation of both afferent and efferent vessels to normalize filtration fraction (FF).
D) Complete structural atrophy of juxtaglomerular granular cells due to toxic potassium accumulation, causing a drop in filtration fraction (FF) due to a failure of Angiotensin II synthesis.


## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

The absence of aldosterone causes profound renal sodium wasting and volume depletion, which shifts the intrarenal baroreceptors and macula densa into a state of maximal stimulation, spiking renin release. The resulting high levels of Angiotensin II selectively constrict the efferent arteriole; this maintains glomerular filtration rate (GFR) despite a drop in renal plasma flow (RPF), mathematically driving up the filtration fraction (FF=GFR/RPF).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Renin hypersecretion+Angiotensin II defense+Efferent constriction+ Filtration fraction elevation= Addisonian volume depletion


## Wrong Options

### B

Pulmonary ACE expression is constitutively active and is not regulated by a direct, short-term negative feedback loop driven by adrenal steroid or aldosterone concentration fluctuations.

**Buzzword Combo:**  Steroid feedback trap+Endothelial enzyme regulation+Isolated filtration failure= Pulmonary ACE suppression

### C

ANP is secreted in response to high atrial stretch (volume overload). In an adrenal crisis, the patient is severely volume-depleted, meaning ANP secretion would be completely suppressed to prevent further natriuresis.


**Buzzword Combo:** Volume expansion trap +Natriuresis cross-talk+ Paradoxical loop activation =Atrial natriuretic peptide

### D

While hyperkalemia directly stimulates aldosterone release from healthy adrenal glands, it does not cause toxic structural atrophy of the juxtaglomerular granular cells in the kidney; the granular cells continue to hyper-secrete renin in response to the systemic hypotension.


**Buzzword Combo:** Granular cell atrophy trap+JGA functional failure+Potassium-driven shutdown= Hyperkalemic cell toxicity
