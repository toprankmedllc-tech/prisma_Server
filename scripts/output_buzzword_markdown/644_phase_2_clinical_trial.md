## Buzzword Question

Researchers have completed preliminary safety testing of a new medication. They now enroll several hundred patients with the target disease and compare outcomes between patients receiving the medication and those receiving placebo.

The primary goal of this stage is to determine which of the following?

## Buzzwords Used

Patients with disease ; Placebo comparison ; Drug development ; Efficacy assessment ; Clinical trials

## Answer Options

A) Whether the drug demonstrates efficacy in patients with the disease

B) Whether rare adverse effects occur after widespread use

C) The maximum tolerated dose

D) Basic pharmacokinetic properties

## Correct Answer

A) Whether the drug demonstrates efficacy in patients with the disease

## Explanation

Phase 2 trials primarily evaluate whether a drug works in patients with the target disease while also collecting additional short-term safety data.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Patients with disease + moderate-sized study + does it work = Phase 2

## Wrong Options

### B) Whether rare adverse effects occur after widespread use

Rare adverse effects are typically identified during Phase 4 postmarketing surveillance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Approved drug + widespread use + rare side effects = Phase 4

### C) The maximum tolerated dose

Determining the maximum tolerated dose is a primary objective of Phase 1 studies.

**Buzzword Combo:** Dose escalation + safety testing = Phase 1

### D) Basic pharmacokinetic properties

Pharmacokinetic assessment is performed early in drug development, often before efficacy testing.

**Buzzword Combo:** Drug absorption and metabolism assessment = early-phase testing
