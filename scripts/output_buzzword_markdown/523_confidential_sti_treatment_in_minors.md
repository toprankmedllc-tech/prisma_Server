## Buzzword Question

A 17-year-old girl is diagnosed with gonorrhea and asks her physician not to tell her parents. She is not in danger and denies abuse. Which of the following is the most appropriate physician response?

## Buzzwords Used

Minor confidentiality ; Gonorrhea ; STI treatment ; No abuse ; Adolescent patient

## Answer Options

A) “I am legally required to inform your parents because you are a minor.”
B) “Your diagnosis will remain confidential.”
C) “I can only provide treatment if your parents consent.”
D) “You should return with a parent before I prescribe antibiotics.”

## Correct Answer

B) “Your diagnosis will remain confidential.”

## Explanation

Adolescents can generally consent to confidential diagnosis and treatment of sexually transmitted infections. Disclosure without consent is unnecessary unless there is concern for abuse or danger.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Minor patient + STI diagnosis + No safety concern = Maintain confidentiality

## Wrong Options

### A) “I am legally required to inform your parents because you are a minor.”

Routine STI diagnoses in adolescents do not require automatic parental notification.

**Buzzword Combo:** Minor status + STI + Mandatory disclosure misconception = Incorrect parental notification

### C) “I can only provide treatment if your parents consent.”

Parental consent is not generally required for STI treatment in minors.

**Buzzword Combo:** Adolescent STI + Need for antibiotics + False consent requirement = Incorrect delay in care

### D) “You should return with a parent before I prescribe antibiotics.”

Delaying treatment until parental involvement may discourage adolescents from seeking care.

**Buzzword Combo:** Confidential request + STI symptoms + Delayed treatment = Ethical violation
