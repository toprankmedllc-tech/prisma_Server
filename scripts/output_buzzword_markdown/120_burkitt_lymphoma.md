## Buzzword Question

A young boy with a rapidly enlarging jaw mass has a biopsy showing "starry-sky" histology and c-MYC activation. Which translocation is most likely responsible?

## Buzzwords Used

young boy; rapidly enlarging jaw mass; starry-sky histology; c-MYC activation

## Answer Options

A) t(14;18)
B) t(15;17)
C) t(8;14)
D) t(9;22)

## Correct Answer

C. t(8;14)

## Explanation

Burkitt lymphoma is driven by c-MYC overexpression due to translocation t(8;14), placing the MYC gene under the immunoglobulin heavy chain promoter. This leads to rapid cellular proliferation and the classic starry-sky appearance.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

c-MYC + starry-sky + t(8;14) = Burkitt lymphoma

## Wrong Options

### A. t(14;18)

t(14;18) causes BCL2 overexpression and is associated with follicular lymphoma, an indolent lymphoma with follicular patterns, not starry-sky histology.

**Buzzword Combo:** BCL2 + follicular pattern + indolent course = Follicular lymphoma

### B. t(15;17)

t(15;17) produces the PML-RARA fusion seen in acute promyelocytic leukemia, which is associated with DIC and Auer rods, not lymphoma.

**Buzzword Combo:** PML-RARA + promyelocytes + DIC = Acute promyelocytic leukemia

### D. t(9;22)

t(9;22) produces the BCR-ABL fusion tyrosine kinase seen in chronic myeloid leukemia, characterized by granulocytosis and basophilia, not lymphoma.

**Buzzword Combo:** BCR-ABL + basophilia + low LAP = CML
