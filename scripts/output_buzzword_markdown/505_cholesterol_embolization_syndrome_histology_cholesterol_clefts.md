## Buzzword Question

A patient with acute kidney injury several weeks after coronary angiography undergoes a skin biopsy. Microscopic examination reveals needle-shaped empty spaces within small arteries surrounded by an inflammatory infiltrate. This finding is most consistent with:

## Buzzwords Used


Post-angiography AKI; needle-shaped empty spaces; small arteries; inflammatory infiltrate; cholesterol clefts; cholesterol embolization

## Answer Options

A) Cholesterol clefts in small arteries
B) Fibrinoid necrosis of arterioles
C) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining
D) Pauci-immune glomerular crescents

## Correct Answer

A) Cholesterol clefts in small arteries

## Explanation

Needle-shaped empty spaces (cholesterol clefts) within small arteries are pathognomonic for cholesterol embolization syndrome. The clefts represent the spaces left behind when cholesterol crystals are dissolved during tissue processing.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Post-vascular procedure + needle-shaped empty spaces in small arteries = cholesterol clefts (cholesterol embolization syndrome)

## Wrong Options

### B) Fibrinoid necrosis of arterioles

Seen in malignant hypertension and polyarteritis nodosa — bright pink glassy necrotic arteriolar walls, not needle-shaped empty clefts.

**Buzzword Combo:** Malignant HTN / PAN + bright pink glassy arteriolar necrosis = fibrinoid necrosis

### C) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining


The hallmark of amyloidosis from extracellular amyloid deposition, not cholesterol embolization.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Congo red + apple-green birefringence = amyloidosis

### 
D) Pauci-immune glomerular crescents


Characteristic of ANCA-associated vasculitis (GPA, MPA, EGPA) on renal biopsy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pauci-immune crescents + positive ANCA = ANCA-associated vasculitis

## Related Concepts

Atherosclerosis of the aorta; Malignant hypertension (fibrinoid necrosis); ANCA-associated vasculitis
