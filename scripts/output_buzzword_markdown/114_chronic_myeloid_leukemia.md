## Buzzword Question

A patient with fatigue, weight loss, night sweats, splenomegaly, marked granulocytosis with basophilia, and a low LAP score most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; weight loss; night sweats; splenomegaly; granulocytosis; basophilia; low LAP score

## Answer Options

A) Acute myeloid leukemia
B) Chronic myeloid leukemia
C) Leukemoid reaction
D) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

## Correct Answer

B. Chronic myeloid leukemia

## Explanation

Chronic myeloid leukemia is a myeloproliferative neoplasm caused by the BCR-ABL fusion gene from t(9;22). It leads to uncontrolled proliferation of mature granulocytes with basophilia and a low leukocyte alkaline phosphatase (LAP) score.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Low LAP + basophilia + splenomegaly + BCR-ABL = CML

## Wrong Options

### A. Acute myeloid leukemia

AML presents with immature myeloblasts and Auer rods, typically with acute severe illness rather than chronic granulocytosis. LAP score is not characteristically low in AML.

**Buzzword Combo:** Auer rods + >20% blasts = AML

### C. Leukemoid reaction

Leukemoid reaction is a reactive neutrophilia (often due to infection) and shows a high LAP score with toxic granulation and Döhle bodies. It lacks basophilia and Philadelphia chromosome.

**Buzzword Combo:** High LAP + toxic granulation + infection = Leukemoid reaction

### D. Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL is a lymphoproliferative disorder characterized by lymphocytosis and smudge cells in elderly patients. It does not cause granulocytosis or low LAP.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + CD5/CD23+ lymphocytes = CLL
