## Buzzword Question

A physiology student is performing an experiment on an isolated neuromuscular junction preparation. During the study, the presynaptic terminal of a motor neuron is electrically stimulated, causing voltage-gated calcium (Ca²⁺) channels in the nerve terminal membrane to open normally. Which of the following is the most direct consequence of opening presynaptic voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels at the neuromuscular junction?

## Buzzwords Used

the presynaptic terminal of a motor neuron is electrically stimulated, causing voltage-gated calcium (Ca²⁺) channels in the nerve terminal membrane to open normally

## Answer Options

A. Breakdown of acetylcholine within the synaptic cleft
B. Depolarization of T tubules inside the muscle fiber
C. Exocytosis of acetylcholine vesicles from the motor neuron terminal
D. Binding of calcium to troponin on actin filaments

## Correct Answer

C. Exocytosis of acetylcholine vesicles from the motor neuron terminal

## Explanation

At the neuromuscular junction, arrival of an action potential at the presynaptic motor neuron terminal causes depolarization of the membrane. This depolarization opens voltage-gated calcium channels, allowing calcium to enter the nerve terminal, the immediate consequence of opening presynaptic Ca²⁺ channels is acetylcholine vesicle exocytosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Presynaptic depolarization + Ca²⁺ influx + vesicle fusion = acetylcholine release

## Wrong Options

### A. Breakdown of acetylcholine within the synaptic cleft

This is performed by acetylcholinesterase after acetylcholine has already been released.


**Buzzword Combo:** Acetylcholinesterase + synaptic cleft + ACh degradation = termination of signal

### B. Depolarization of T tubules inside the muscle fiber

T tubules depolarize later during excitation-contraction coupling within skeletal muscle.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sarcolemmal action potential + T tubules + excitation-contraction coupling = Ca²⁺ release from SR

### D. Binding of calcium to troponin on actin filaments

This occurs inside the muscle fiber after calcium is released from the sarcoplasmic reticulum.


**Buzzword Combo:** Ca²⁺ binding troponin + tropomyosin shift + actin-myosin interaction = muscle contraction
