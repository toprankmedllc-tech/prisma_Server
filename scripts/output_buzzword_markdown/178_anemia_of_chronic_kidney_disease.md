## Buzzword Question

A 58-year-old man with chronic kidney disease, fatigue, pallor, normocytic normochromic anemia, and a low reticulocyte count most likely has deficiency of which hormone?

## Buzzwords Used

chronic kidney disease; fatigue; pallor; normocytic normochromic anemia; low reticulocyte count; hypoproliferative anemia

## Answer Options

A) Iron
B) Erythropoietin
C) Vitamin B12
D) Folate

## Correct Answer

B. Erythropoietin

## Explanation

Chronic kidney disease causes decreased erythropoietin production by renal peritubular interstitial cells, leading to reduced red blood cell production. This results in a hypoproliferative normocytic normochromic anemia with a low reticulocyte count.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CKD + normocytic anemia + low reticulocytes = Erythropoietin deficiency

## Wrong Options

### A. Iron

Iron deficiency causes microcytic hypochromic anemia with low ferritin and elevated TIBC rather than normocytic anemia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + high TIBC + microcytosis = Iron deficiency anemia

### C. Vitamin B12

Vitamin B12 deficiency causes macrocytic megaloblastic anemia with neurologic symptoms and hypersegmented neutrophils.

**Buzzword Combo:** Macrocytosis + neurologic deficits + elevated MMA = B12 deficiency

### D. Folate

Folate deficiency causes macrocytic anemia without neurologic symptoms and with normal methylmalonic acid levels.

**Buzzword Combo:** Macrocytosis + normal MMA + no neurologic findings = Folate deficiency
