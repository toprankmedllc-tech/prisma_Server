## Buzzword Question

A hospital tracks three heart failure quality metrics: (1) percentage of HF patients receiving ACE inhibitor or ARB at discharge, (2) 30-day HF readmission rate, and (3) number of cardiologists credentialed in advanced heart failure management. Using the Donabedian model, these three metrics are respectively classified as:

## Buzzwords Used

ACE inhibitor discharge rate; 30-day readmission; cardiologist credentialing; Donabedian model; process/outcome/structure


## Answer Options

A) Process, Outcome, Structure 
B) Outcome, Process, Structure 
C) Structure, Process, Outcome
 D) Process, Structure, Outcome

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Donabedian model: Process = clinical actions taken during care delivery (ACE inhibitor discharge rate = evidence-based action performed). Outcome = results of care for patients (30-day readmission = patient health result). Structure = resources and capacity for care (cardiologist credentialing = structural resource/infrastructure). Metric 1 = Process; Metric 2 = Outcome; Metric 3 = Structure.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Evidence-based clinical action performed = Process; Patient health result = Outcome; Resources/capacity/infrastructure = Structure

## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Outcome, Process, Structure

ACE inhibitor discharge rate is a Process measure (clinical action performed, not a patient result). 30-day readmission is an Outcome (patient result,  not an action). Cardiologist credentialing is Structure. Correct order = Process, Outcome, Structure.

**Buzzword Combo:** 30-day readmission + 30-day mortality + complication rate = Outcome measures (patient health results)


### 2: C) Structure, Process, Outcome

ACE inhibitor rate = Process (action taken), not Structure. Cardiologist credentialing = Structure (resource/capacity). 30-day readmission = Outcome. This answer reverses metrics 1 and 3.


**Buzzword Combo:** Staffing ratios + EHR availability + bed count + board certification + credentialing = Structure measures (capacity)


### 3: D) Process, Structure, Outcome

ACE inhibitor rate = Process . 30-day readmission = Outcome (patient result), not Structure. Cardiologist credentialing = Structure, not Outcome. Metrics 2 and 3 are reversed in this option.

**Buzzword Combo:** 30-day readmission + mortality = Outcome; credentialing + staffing = Structure; medication rates = Process
