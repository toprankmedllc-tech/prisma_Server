## Buzzword Question

A patient has repeated seizure-like episodes characterized by side-to-side head movements, crying, closed eyes during the event, and no postictal confusion. EEG is normal. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Side-to-side head movement; crying during seizure; eyes closed; normal EEG; no postictal confusion


## Answer Options

 A) Psychogenic nonepileptic seizures
 B) Temporal lobe epilepsy
 C) Absence seizure
 D) Status epilepticus


## Correct Answer

A. Psychogenic nonepileptic seizures


## Explanation

Psychogenic nonepileptic seizures are a manifestation of functional neurological symptom disorder. Features such as prolonged episodes, crying, side-to-side head movements, closed eyes, and absence of postictal confusion are characteristic.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Closed eyes + crying during seizure + no postictal confusion = Psychogenic nonepileptic seizure


## Wrong Options

### B. Temporal lobe epilepsy


Temporal lobe epilepsy commonly causes automatisms and impaired awareness with abnormal EEG findings.

**Buzzword Combo:** Automatisms + aura + impaired awareness = Temporal lobe epilepsy


### C. Absence seizure


Absence seizures are brief staring spells with impaired awareness and characteristic 3-Hz spike-wave EEG findings.


**Buzzword Combo:** Brief staring spell + childhood onset + 3-Hz spike-wave EEG = Absence seizure

### D. Status epilepticus


Status epilepticus involves prolonged true epileptic seizure activity and is associated with impaired consciousness and neurological risk.


**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous seizure + altered consciousness + medical emergency = Status epilepticus
