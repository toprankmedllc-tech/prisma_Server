## Buzzword Question

A patient with frequent migraines is prescribed sumatriptan for acute attacks. Sumatriptan aborts the headache primarily by:

## Buzzwords Used

Sumatriptan; acute migraine abortive; serotonin receptor agonism; cerebral vasoconstriction

## Answer Options

A) Inhibiting cyclooxygenase to reduce prostaglandin synthesis
B) Blocking dopamine D2 receptors in the chemoreceptor trigger zone
C) Activating serotonin 5HT-1B and 5HT-1D receptors, causing cerebral vasoconstriction and inhibition of vasoactive neuropeptide release from trigeminal nerve endings
D) Inhibiting voltage-gated calcium channels in cerebral arteries

## Correct Answer

C) Activating serotonin 5HT-1B and 5HT-1D receptors, causing cerebral vasoconstriction and inhibition of vasoactive neuropeptide release from trigeminal nerve endings

## Explanation

Sumatriptan is a selective 5HT-1B/1D agonist. It constricts dilated cranial vessels (5HT-1B) and inhibits release of vasoactive neuropeptides (CGRP, substance P) from trigeminal nerve terminals (5HT-1D). It is contraindicated in coronary artery disease and uncontrolled hypertension due to vasoconstriction.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Acute migraine abortive + 5HT-1B/1D agonist + cerebral vasoconstriction + inhibits neuropeptide release = sumatriptan

## Wrong Options

### Inhibiting cyclooxygenase to reduce prostaglandin synthesis

Describes NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen), which are useful for mild to moderate migraine but are not the mechanism of sumatriptan.

**Buzzword Combo:** NSAID + mild migraine + COX inhibition = ibuprofen / naproxen

### Blocking dopamine D2 receptors in the chemoreceptor trigger zone

Describes antiemetics such as metoclopramide and prochlorperazine, often used as ADJUNCTS for migraine-associated nausea, but not the abortive mechanism of sumatriptan.

**Buzzword Combo:** Migraine-associated nausea + D2 antagonist = metoclopramide / prochlorperazine

### Inhibiting voltage-gated calcium channels in cerebral arteries

Verapamil (a calcium channel blocker) is used as PREVENTIVE therapy for cluster headache, not as an abortive for migraine.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cluster headache prophylaxis + Ca channel blocker = verapamil

## Related Concepts

CGRP receptor antagonists (gepants) and monoclonal antibodies (erenumab) for migraine; Triptan contraindication in CAD/uncontrolled HTN; Ergotamine (older 5HT agonist, more vasoconstrictor toxicity)
