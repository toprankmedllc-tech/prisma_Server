## Buzzword Question

A patient undergoes polysomnography demonstrating sleep spindles and K complexes. Which stage of sleep is represented?

## Buzzwords Used

Polysomnography ; Sleep spindles ; K complexes

## Answer Options

A. REM sleep
B. NREM stage 1
C. NREM stage 2
D. NREM stage 3

## Correct Answer

C. NREM stage 2

## Explanation

NREM stage 2 sleep is classically identified by the presence of sleep spindles and K complexes on EEG. It represents the largest proportion of total sleep time in adults

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Sleep spindles + K complexes + largest proportion of sleep = NREM stage 2

## Wrong Options

### A. REM sleep

REM sleep is characterized by vivid dreaming, rapid eye movements, and muscle atonia. Sleep spindles and K complexes are not seen during REM sleep.

**Buzzword Combo:** Dreaming + rapid eye movements + muscle atonia = REM sleep

### B. NREM stage 1

NREM stage 1 is the lightest stage of sleep and is associated with theta waves and transition from wakefulness to sleep. Sleep spindles and K complexes are characteristic of NREM stage 2, not stage 1.

**Buzzword Combo:** Theta waves + lightest sleep + sleep onset = NREM stage 1

### D. NREM stage 3

NREM stage 3 is deep slow-wave sleep characterized by delta waves. It is associated with parasomnias such as sleepwalking and night terrors rather than sleep spindles and K complexes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Delta waves + deep slow-wave sleep + parasomnias = NREM stage 3
