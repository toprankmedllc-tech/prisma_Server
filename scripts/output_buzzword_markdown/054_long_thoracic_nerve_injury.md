## Buzzword Question

A 54-year-old woman undergoes a radical mastectomy with axillary lymph node dissection for treatment of breast cancer. Several days after surgery, she notices difficulty lifting her arm above her head and weakness when pushing objects forward. Physical examination reveals prominence of the medial border of the scapula when she pushes against a wall.
Which of the following mechanisms best explains this patient’s condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Radical mastectomy; difficulty lifting her arm above her head; weakness when pushing objects forward

## Answer Options

A. Loss of serratus anterior function causing impaired scapular stabilization
B. Paralysis of the deltoid muscle causing impaired arm abduction
C. Denervation of the trapezius muscle causing shoulder droop
D. Injury to the rotator cuff causing glenohumeral instability

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

During mastectomy with axillary dissection, the long thoracic nerve may be injured. This denervates the serratus anterior muscle, which normally anchors the scapula against the thoracic wall and assists with upward rotation during arm elevation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Mastectomy + winged scapula + difficulty pushing against wall = long thoracic nerve injury

## Wrong Options

### B. Paralysis of the deltoid muscle causing impaired arm abduction

Incorrect — This occurs with axillary nerve injury, often associated with surgical neck fractures of the humerus.

**Buzzword Combo:** Surgical neck fracture + loss of shoulder contour = axillary nerve injury

### C. Denervation of the trapezius muscle causing shoulder droop

Incorrect — Injury to the spinal accessory nerve causes trapezius weakness and shoulder droop.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neck surgery + shoulder droop + difficulty shrugging = spinal accessory nerve injury

### D. Injury to the rotator cuff causing glenohumeral instability

Incorrect — Rotator cuff injuries typically cause shoulder pain and weakness with abduction or rotation, not scapular winging.

**Buzzword Combo:** Painful overhead movement + rotator cuff weakness = rotator cuff injury
