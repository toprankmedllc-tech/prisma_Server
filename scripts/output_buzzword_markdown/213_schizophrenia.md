## Buzzword Question

A young adult with hallucinations, persecutory delusions, flat affect, and social withdrawal most likely has excess dopamine activity in which pathway?


## Buzzwords Used

Hallucinations; persecutory delusions; flat affect; social withdrawal;

## Answer Options

 A. Nigrostriatal pathway
 B. Mesolimbic pathway
 C. Tuberoinfundibular pathway
 D. Mesocortical pathway


## Correct Answer

B. Mesolimbic pathway


## Explanation

Positive symptoms of schizophrenia are caused by increased dopamine activity in the mesolimbic pathway.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hallucinations + delusions = ↑ mesolimbic dopamine


## Wrong Options

### A. Nigrostriatal pathway


Dopamine blockade in this pathway causes extrapyramidal symptoms like dystonia, akathisia.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Parkinsonism + antipsychotics = nigrostriatal pathway


### C. Tuberoinfundibular pathway


Dopamine inhibition in this pathway increases prolactin levels.


**Buzzword Combo:** Galactorrhea + antipsychotics = tuberoinfundibular pathway


### D. Mesocortical pathway



Decreased dopamine activity here contributes to negative symptoms.


**Buzzword Combo:** Flat affect + anhedonia = mesocortical dopamine deficiency
