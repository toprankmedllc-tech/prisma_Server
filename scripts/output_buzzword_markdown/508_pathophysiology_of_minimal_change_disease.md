## Buzzword Question

A 4-year-old boy is brought to the clinic by his mother because of progressive swelling around his eyes and ankles over the past week. Urinalysis reveals 4+ proteinuria, but no hematuria or casts. The primary defect in this patient's condition involves a barrier that limits the filtration of molecules based on both size and charge. Which of the following components of this barrier is primarily responsible for preventing the filtration of negatively charged proteins like albumin?

## Buzzwords Used

4-year-old boy; swelling around his eyes; 4+ proteinuria; charge barrier

## Answer Options

A) Fenestrated capillary endothelium
B) Glomerular basement membrane polyanions
C) Podocyte slit diaphragms
D) Proximal convoluted tubule brush border

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

The glomerular filtration barrier utilizes a charge barrier composed of negatively charged glycoproteins (such as heparan sulfate) within the glomerular basement membrane and endothelial surface to repel like-charged molecules. Since albumin is negatively charged, this electrostatic repulsion prevents its filtration into Bowman's space. Effacement of podocyte foot processes disrupts this architecture, leading to massive selective proteinuria.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

4+ proteinuria + Effacement of podocyte foot processes + Charge barrier = Glomerular basement membrane polyanions

## Wrong Options

### A) Fenestrated capillary endothelium

The fenestrated capillary endothelium acts primarily as a size barrier that prevents the entry of large molecules and cellular elements (> 100 nm) into the wall. It does not provide the primary negative charge repulsion that selectively halts albumin.

**Buzzword Combo:** Swelling around eyes + 4+ proteinuria + Size barrier = Fenestrated capillary endothelium

### C) Podocyte slit diaphragms

The slit diaphragms between podocyte foot processes function primarily as a physical size barrier preventing the entry of molecules larger than 40–50 nm.

**Buzzword Combo:** Effacement of podocyte foot processes + Size barrier = Podocyte slit diaphragms

### D) Proximal convoluted tubule brush border

The proximal convoluted tubule brush border is responsible for macromolecule reabsorption via endocytosis, not the initial filtration barrier filtration screening. It does not establish the charge barrier of the glomerulus.

**Buzzword Combo:** Proteinuria + Macromolecule reabsorption = Proximal convoluted tubule brush border
