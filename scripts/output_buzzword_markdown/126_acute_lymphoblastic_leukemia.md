## Buzzword Question

A 6-year-old child with fatigue, fever, easy bruising, recurrent infections, anemia, thrombocytopenia, and TdT-positive blasts most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

6-year-oldchild; fatigue; fever; easy bruising; recurrent infections; anemia; thrombocytopenia; TdT-positive blasts; pancytopenia

## Answer Options

A) Acute myeloid leukemia
B) Acute lymphoblastic leukemia
C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
D) Hodgkin lymphoma

## Correct Answer

B. Acute lymphoblastic leukemia

## Explanation

Acute lymphoblastic leukemia is the most common childhood leukemia and presents with bone marrow failure symptoms such as anemia, thrombocytopenia, and recurrent infections. TdT positivity indicates immature lymphoid blasts, which is characteristic of ALL.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

TdT-positive blasts + childhood + pancytopenia = ALL

## Wrong Options

### A) Acute myeloid leukemia

AML is characterized by myeloblasts that are MPO-positive and often contain Auer rods. It is more common in adults and is TdT-negative.

**Buzzword Combo:** Auer rods + MPO positivity + adult = AML

### C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL is an indolent leukemia of elderly patients characterized by lymphocytosis and smudge cells. It does not present as an acute pediatric illness.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + CD5/CD23+ + elderly = CLL

### D) Hodgkin lymphoma

Hodgkin lymphoma features Reed-Sternberg cells and painless lymphadenopathy rather than bone marrow failure with circulating blasts. It does not present with TdT-positive blasts.

**Buzzword Combo:** Reed-Sternberg cells + CD15/CD30 + lymphadenopathy = Hodgkin lymphoma
