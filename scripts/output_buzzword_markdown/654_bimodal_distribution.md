## Buzzword Question

Researchers during a phase III clinical trial analyze the therapeutic response times of a new antihypertensive drug across a large population. When graphing the data, the resulting frequency curve displays two distinct, separated peaks instead of a standard single bell shape. Statistical analysis reveals this is due to a genetic polymorphism where half the population are fast metabolizers and the other half are slow metabolizers. Which of the following is true regarding the statistical properties of this distribution curve?

## Buzzwords Used

Two distinct separated peaks; fast and slow metabolizers; frequency curve properties

## Answer Options

A) The mean and median sit between the two modes.
B) The mean, median, and mode will all be equal.
C) The distribution will have only one true mode.
D) The mean will be significantly less than the lowest mode.

## Correct Answer

A) The mean and median sit between the two modes.

## Explanation

The trial results display a bimodal distribution due to the two distinct patient metabolic rates creating two separate peaks. In a standard bimodal distribution, the overall mean and median act as balancing points for the entire dataset, causing them to fall in the lower-frequency valley between the lower and higher modes.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Two distinct peaks + Measures of central tendency = Mean and median land in the valley between modes.

## Wrong Options

### B) The mean, median, and mode will all be equal.

This only occurs in perfectly symmetrical, unimodal distributions like a standard normal Gaussian curve. A bimodal distribution inherently possesses two distinct modes, making this equality impossible.

**Buzzword Combo:** Symmetrical Dataset + Unimodal Curve = Mean, median, and mode are equal.

### C) The distribution will have only one true mode.

By definition, a bimodal distribution contains exactly two separate modes representing the two highest peaks of data frequency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Bimodal Graph + Peak Count = Two distinct modes.

### D) The mean will be significantly less than the lowest mode.

The mean is a calculated average pulled toward the middle of both groups. It will naturally be greater than the lower peak mode and less than the higher peak mode.

**Buzzword Combo:** Calculated Average + Bimodal Left Peak = Mean is greater than the lowest mode.
