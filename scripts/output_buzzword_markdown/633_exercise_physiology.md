## Buzzword Question

Which of the following groups of substances are released by exercising skeletal muscle and act locally to dilate arterioles supplying the working muscle?

## Buzzwords Used

Exercising skeletal muscle; local vasodilation; metabolic vasodilators; adenosine; lactate; K⁺; CO₂; nitric oxide

## Answer Options

A) Thromboxane A₂, serotonin, and ADP
B) Endothelin-1, angiotensin II, and vasopressin
C) Norepinephrine, epinephrine, and dopamine
D) Adenosine, lactate, K⁺, CO₂, and nitric oxide

## Correct Answer

D) Adenosine, lactate, K⁺, CO₂, and nitric oxide

## Explanation

Exercising skeletal muscle releases adenosine (from ATP breakdown), lactate, K⁺, CO₂ / H⁺ (low pH), and nitric oxide — all of which dilate local arterioles, lowering vascular resistance in working muscle and contributing to the drop in diastolic blood pressure during exercise.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)


Exercising muscle + adenosine + lactate + K⁺ + CO₂/H⁺ + NO = local metabolic vasodilation (↓ SVR, ↓ DBP)

## Wrong Options

### A) Thromboxane A₂, serotonin, and ADP

These are platelet-derived mediators that promote platelet aggregation and vasoconstriction during hemostasis — not metabolic vasodilators of exercising muscle.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thromboxane A₂ + serotonin + ADP = platelet activation and vasoconstriction (hemostasis)

### B) Endothelin-1, angiotensin II, and vasopressin

These are systemic vasoconstrictors that raise SVR, not metabolic vasodilators.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Endothelin-1 + angiotensin II + vasopressin = systemic vasoconstrictors

### C) Norepinephrine, epinephrine, and dopamine


Catecholamines released by the sympathetic nervous system and adrenal medulla — their dominant peripheral effect is α1-mediated vasoconstriction, not metabolic vasodilation in working muscle.

**Buzzword Combo:** Catecholamines + α1 → vasoconstriction + β1 → ↑ HR/contractility = sympathetic activation
