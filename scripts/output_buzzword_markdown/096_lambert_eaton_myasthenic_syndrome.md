## Buzzword Question

A 64-year-old man with a 50-pack-year smoking history presents with 3 months of difficulty rising from chairs and climbing stairs. He notes that his strength briefly improves after a few seconds of exertion and that he has chronic dry mouth and impotence. Repetitive nerve stimulation at HIGH frequency shows a marked INCREMENTAL response in CMAP amplitude. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

50-pack-year smoking history; difficulty rising form chairs and climbing stairs; incremental response on repetitive nerve stimulation

## Answer Options

A) Myasthenia gravis
B) Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome
C) Botulism
D) Polymyositis

## Correct Answer

B) Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome

## Explanation

LEMS is a presynaptic disorder caused by anti-voltage-gated calcium channel (VGCC) antibodies, most often paraneoplastic from small cell lung cancer. Classic features: proximal lower-extremity weakness that briefly IMPROVES with exercise, DIMINISHED reflexes that AUGMENT after sustained contraction, AUTONOMIC dysfunction (dry mouth, impotence), and INCREMENTAL response on high-frequency repetitive stimulation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Smoker + proximal weakness improves with exercise + autonomic features + incremental EMG = LEMS (anti-VGCC, SCLC)

## Wrong Options

### Myasthenia gravis

MG (postsynaptic, anti-AChR) presents with fatigable ocular/bulbar weakness that WORSENS with use, normal reflexes, no autonomic involvement, and a DECREMENTAL EMG response — the OPPOSITE of LEMS in nearly every parameter.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fatigable ptosis/diplopia + decremental EMG + thymoma = MG (anti-AChR)

### Botulism

Presynaptic disorder from Clostridium botulinum toxin cleaving SNARE proteins to block acetylcholine release. Causes acute DESCENDING flaccid paralysis hours after exposure (canned food, infant honey ingestion), with autonomic features. 

**Buzzword Combo:**  Floppy baby + honey + descending paralysis = botulism

### Polymyositis

Inflammatory myopathy with proximal muscle weakness, elevated CK, and endomysial CD8+ T-cell infiltrate. There is no exercise-induced improvement, no autonomic involvement, and no EMG incremental response.

**Buzzword Combo:** Proximal weakness + elevated CK + endomysial CD8+ infiltrate = polymyositis

## Related Concepts

Myasthenia gravis (anti-AChR, postsynaptic); Botulism (SNARE cleavage); Paraneoplastic syndromes of small cell lung cancer (SIADH, Cushing, LEMS)
