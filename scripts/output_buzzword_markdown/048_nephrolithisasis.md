## Buzzword Question

A young man with recurrent nephrolithiasis has hexagonal crystals and a positive cyanide-nitroprusside test. Which amino acid has impaired renal tubular reabsorption?

## Buzzwords Used

Hexagonal crystals; Nitroprusside; Nephrolithiasis

## Answer Options

A) Cystine
B) Glycine
C) Alanine
D) Phenylalanine


## Correct Answer

A) Cystine

## Explanation

Cystinuria is caused by defective proximal tubular reabsorption of cystine and dibasic amino acids. Patients classically develop recurrent kidney stones composed of cystine, which appear as hexagonal crystals on urinalysis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hexagonal crystals + positive cyanide-nitroprusside test = Cystinuria


## Wrong Options

### Glycine

Glycine transport defects are not associated with classic hereditary nephrolithiasis syndromes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Simple amino acid structure + nonessential amino acid = Glycine

### Alanine

Alanine abnormalities are not associated with renal stone formation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Glucose-alanine cycle + pyruvate transamination = Alanine

### Phenylalanine

Phenylalanine metabolism is classically associated with phenylketonuria rather than kidney stones.

**Buzzword Combo:** Musty odor + intellectual disability = Phenylketonuria
