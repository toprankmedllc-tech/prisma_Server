## Buzzword Question

A sudden rise in arterial pressure stretches the carotid sinus baroreceptors. Which of the following efferent responses is expected?

## Buzzwords Used

Carotid sinus stretch; ↑ baroreceptor firing; ↑ parasympathetic output; ↓ sympathetic output; bradycardia; vasodilation

## Answer Options

A) ↑ Parasympathetic output and ↓ sympathetic output, producing bradycardia and vasodilation
B) ↑ Sympathetic output and ↓ parasympathetic output, producing tachycardia and vasoconstriction
C) No change in autonomic output until BP exceeds 200 mm Hg
D) Activation of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system to raise BP further

## Correct Answer

A) ↑ Parasympathetic output and ↓ sympathetic output, producing bradycardia and vasodilation

## Explanation

Increased arterial pressure stretches carotid sinus baroreceptors, increasing their firing rate. The NTS responds by increasing parasympathetic (vagal) output and decreasing sympathetic outflow, producing bradycardia and vasodilation — restoring BP toward normal.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

↑ BP / carotid sinus stretch + ↑ baroreceptor firing → ↑ parasympathetic + ↓ sympathetic = bradycardia + vasodilation

## Wrong Options

### B) ↑ Sympathetic output and ↓ parasympathetic output, producing tachycardia and vasoconstriction

This is the response to decreased BP (e.g., hemorrhage), not increased BP.

**Buzzword Combo:** ↓ BP + ↓ baroreceptor firing → ↑ sympathetic + ↓ parasympathetic = tachycardia + vasoconstriction

### C) No change in autonomic output until BP exceeds 200 mm Hg

Baroreceptors fire continuously across a wide physiologic range; they do not have a high threshold of 200 mm Hg.

**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous baroreceptor firing across physiologic BP range = normal baroreflex

### D) Activation of the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system to raise BP further


RAAS activation raises BP and is triggered by low renal perfusion, not high BP. Activating RAAS in response to high BP would be counterproductive.

**Buzzword Combo:** ↓ Renal perfusion + ↑ renin from JG cells = RAAS activation (raises BP)
