## Buzzword Question

A male infant has markedly decreased circulating B cells and low levels of all immunoglobulin classes. His infections are mainly due to encapsulated bacteria. Which of the following defects best explains this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Male infant; markedly decreased circulating B cells; low levels of all immunoglobulin classes; infections mainly due to encapsulated bacteria

## Answer Options

A) Mutation blocking maturation of pre-B cells
B) Defective purine metabolism causing lymphocyte toxicity
C) Failure of third and fourth pharyngeal pouch development
D) Defective T-cell help for immunoglobulin class switching

## Correct Answer

A) Mutation blocking maturation of pre-B cells


## Explanation

Bruton X-linked agammaglobulinemia is caused by a BTK mutation that prevents normal B-cell maturation. This leads to markedly decreased or absent B cells and low immunoglobulins of all classes.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Male infant + absent/decreased B cells + low immunoglobulins of all classes = BTK mutation blocking B-cell maturation

## Wrong Options

### B) Defective purine metabolism causing lymphocyte toxicity

This describes adenosine deaminase deficiency, which causes SCID. SCID causes combined immune dysfunction with recurrent bacterial, viral, fungal, and protozoal infections.

**Buzzword Combo:** ADA deficiency + toxic purine metabolites + recurrent bacterial, viral, fungal, and protozoal infections = SCID

### C) Failure of third and fourth pharyngeal pouch development

This describes DiGeorge syndrome, which causes T-cell deficiency due to thymic aplasia and hypocalcemia due to parathyroid aplasia. It does not primarily cause absent B cells from failed B-cell maturation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Third/fourth pouch failure + thymic aplasia + hypocalcemia = DiGeorge syndrome

### D) Defective T-cell help for immunoglobulin class switching

This describes hyper-IgM syndrome, usually due to CD40 ligand deficiency. B cells are present, but class switching is impaired.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD40L defect + high IgM + low IgG/IgA/IgE = Hyper-IgM syndrome

## Related Concepts

Hyper-IgM syndrome; ADA deficiency; Humoral immunodeficiency
