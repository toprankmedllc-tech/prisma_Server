## Buzzword Question

A patient develops acute kidney injury shortly after receiving intravenous radiographic contrast for CT imaging. Which of the following best describes the pathogenesis of this patient’s kidney injury?

## Buzzwords Used

IV contrast; Tubular injury; AKI

## Answer Options

A) Renal vasoconstriction causing tubular ischemia
B) Immune complex deposition within glomeruli
C) Obstruction of the ureters by crystal precipitation
D) Selective effacement of podocyte foot processes

## Correct Answer

A) Renal vasoconstriction causing tubular ischemia

## Explanation

Contrast-induced nephropathy occurs when radiographic contrast agents cause renal vasoconstriction and direct tubular toxicity, leading to decreased renal perfusion and ischemic injury of tubular epithelial cells

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

IV contrast + renal vasoconstriction + tubular ischemia = Contrast nephropathy

## Wrong Options

### Immune complex deposition within glomeruli

Immune complex deposition is characteristic of glomerulonephritis rather than contrast-induced kidney injury

**Buzzword Combo:** Immune complexes + glomerular inflammation + hematuria = Glomerulonephritis

### Obstruction of the ureters by crystal precipitation

Crystal-induced obstruction may occur with uric acid nephropathy or certain medications, not typical contrast nephropathy

**Buzzword Combo:** Crystal precipitation + urinary obstruction + Hydronephrosis = Obstructive nephropathy

### Selective effacement of podocyte foot processes

Podocyte foot process effacement is characteristic of minimal change disease and causes nephrotic-range proteinuria

**Buzzword Combo:** Podocyte injury + selective proteinuria + Young Age = Minimal change disease
