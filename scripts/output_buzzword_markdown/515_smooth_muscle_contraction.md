## Buzzword Question

 61-year-old man with a history of coronary artery disease presents to the emergency department with crushing substernal chest pain. He is given sublingual nitroglycerin, and within minutes his chest pain improves due to relaxation of vascular smooth muscle and dilation of coronary vessels. A medical student asks how nitric oxide produces smooth muscle relaxation at the intracellular level.
The attending physician explains that nitric oxide diffuses into smooth muscle cells and activates an enzyme that converts guanosine triphosphate (GTP) into cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), leading to vasodilation.
Which of the following enzymes is most directly responsible for the conversion of GTP to cGMP in smooth muscle cells?

## Buzzwords Used

conversion of GTP to cGMP

## Answer Options

A. Adenylate cyclase
B. Guanylate cyclase
C. Myosin light-chain kinase
D. Phosphodiesterase type 5

## Correct Answer

B. Guanylate cyclase

## Explanation

Nitric oxide activates soluble guanylate cyclase within smooth muscle cells. Guanylate cyclase converts GTP into cGMP, which subsequently activates pathways leading to smooth muscle relaxation and vasodilation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Nitric oxide + GTP → cGMP + smooth muscle relaxation = guanylate cyclase

## Wrong Options

### A. Adenylate cyclase

Adenylate cyclase converts ATP into cAMP, not GTP into cGMP.

**Buzzword Combo:** ATP → cAMP + G protein signaling + β-adrenergic pathway = adenylate cyclase

### C. Myosin light-chain kinase

MLCK promotes smooth muscle contraction by phosphorylating myosin light chains.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ca2+–calmodulin + myosin phosphorylation + contraction = MLCK

### D. Phosphodiesterase type 5

PDE-5 breaks down cGMP and terminates smooth muscle relaxation signaling.

**Buzzword Combo:** cGMP degradation + sildenafil target + erectile physiology = PDE-5
