## Buzzword Question

A newborn develops severe hypoxemia shortly after birth with cyanosis that does not improve significantly with oxygen therapy. Preductal and postductal oxygen saturation show a difference. Echocardiography reveals right-to-left shunting through the ductus arteriosus. What is the key pathophysiologic failure?

## Buzzwords Used

Newborn; cyanosis after birth; refractory hypoxemia; differential preductal-postductal saturation; right-to-left shunt; persistent fetal circulation; pulmonary hypertension

## Answer Options

A) Failure of ductus arteriosus closure
B) Failure of pulmonary vasodilation after birth
C) Excess surfactant production
D) Increased systemic vascular resistance

## Correct Answer

B) Failure of pulmonary vasodilation after birth

## Explanation

At birth, pulmonary vascular resistance normally drops due to lung expansion and increased oxygen, allowing pulmonary blood flow to increase. In PPHN, this transition fails, so pulmonary arteries remain constricted, maintaining high resistance and causing right-to-left shunting through fetal circulatory pathways.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Newborn + hypoxemia + right-to-left shunt + high PVR → failure of postnatal pulmonary vasodilation

## Wrong Options

### A) Failure of ductus arteriosus closure

This causes patent ductus arteriosus, which is usually left-to-right shunt physiology, not severe persistent neonatal hypoxemia due to high pulmonary resistance.

**Buzzword Combo:** PDA + left-to-right shunt + murmur + not severe cyanosis

### C) Excess surfactant production

Surfactant excess is not a pathologic neonatal condition; instead, surfactant deficiency causes respiratory distress syndrome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Surfactant physiology + alveolar stability + unrelated to pulmonary vascular resistance

### D) Increased systemic vascular resistance

Systemic vascular resistance changes do not explain neonatal hypoxemia with right-to-left shunting; the primary issue is persistently elevated pulmonary vascular resistance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Systemic circulation + afterload changes + not primary cause of neonatal cyanosis
