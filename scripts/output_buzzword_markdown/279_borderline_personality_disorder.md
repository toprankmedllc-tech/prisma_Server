## Buzzword Question

A 24-year-old female presents to the clinic for a routine evaluation. She has a history of multiple superficial wrist lacerations following arguments with her significant other, whom she alternately describes as "the most perfect human alive" or "a manipulative monster." She admits to frequent, intense outbursts of anger, chronic feelings of emptiness, and an intense fear of being abandoned. On examination, several linear scars are noted on her bilateral forearms. Which of the following defense mechanisms is most characteristically associated with this patient's underlying condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Superficial wrist lacerations; Alternately describes as perfect or monster; Fear of abandonment; Chronic emptiness

## Answer Options

A) Splitting
B) Projection
C) Regression
D) Sublimation

## Correct Answer

A) Splitting

## Explanation

The patient exhibits features of Borderline Personality Disorder (BPD), which is strongly associated with the immature defense mechanism of splitting. Splitting involves the inability to integrate positive and negative qualities of oneself or others into a cohesive image, leading to all-or-nothing thinking ("all good" or "all bad"). This manifests here as her fluctuating, extreme views of her significant other.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Alternately describes as perfect or monster + Fear of abandonment + Superficial wrist lacerations = Splitting

## Wrong Options

### B) Projection

Projection involves attributing one's own unacceptable thoughts or feelings onto another person. While common in paranoid personality disorder, it does not explain this patient's alternating extreme views of her partner or her self-harming behaviors.

**Buzzword Combo:** Attributing own feelings to others + Mistrust + Blaming = Projection

### C) Regression

Regression is a defense mechanism where an individual reverts to an earlier stage of development when faced with stress. It does not account for the characteristic black-and-white interpersonal dynamics and chronic emptiness seen in this patient.

**Buzzword Combo:** Reverting to childhood behaviour + Stress response + Immature coping = Regression

### D) Sublimation

Sublimation is a mature defense mechanism where unacceptable impulses are channeled into socially acceptable behaviors. This patient is engaging in maladaptive behaviors like self-harm and emotional outbursts rather than mature coping strategies.

**Buzzword Combo:** Channeling impulses + Productive/Healthy outlets + Mature defense = Sublimation
