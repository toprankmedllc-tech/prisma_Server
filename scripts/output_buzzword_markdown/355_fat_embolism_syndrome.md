## Buzzword Question

A 23-year-old man is brought to the emergency department 36 hours after a motor vehicle accident with a femoral shaft fracture. He develops hypoxemia, altered mental sensorium, and a petechial rash over the chest and conjunctiva. Chest X-ray shows diffuse bilateral infiltrates. Which of the following best describes the pathogenesis of this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

femoral shaft fracture; 48 hours post trauma; hypoxemia; confusion; petechial rash; conjunctival petechiae; bilateral infiltrates; acute respiratory distress

## Answer Options

A) Fat emboli causing pulmonary microvascular obstruction and free fatty acid–mediated injury
B) DVT embolus causing pulmonary arterial obstruction and V/Q mismatch
C) Amniotic fluid embolism causing DIC and cardiovascular collapse
D) Air embolism causing right heart obstruction and sudden collapse

## Correct Answer

A) Fat emboli causing pulmonary microvascular obstruction and free fatty acid–mediated injury

## Explanation

Fat embolism syndrome occurs after long bone fractures when fat globules enter circulation, causing pulmonary capillary obstruction and inflammation via toxic free fatty acids. This leads to ARDS-like hypoxemia, neurologic symptoms, and petechial rash

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

long bone fracture + 24–72 hours delay + petechiae + hypoxemia + confusion + ARDS

## Wrong Options

### B) DVT embolus causing pulmonary arterial obstruction and V/Q mismatch

Pulmonary embolism presents with sudden dyspnea and pleuritic chest pain but lacks the petechial rash and neurologic dysfunction seen in fat embolism.

**Buzzword Combo:** post-op + sudden dyspnea + pleuritic chest pain + clear lung

### C) Amniotic fluid embolism causing DIC and cardiovascular collapse



Occurs during labor or delivery with sudden hypotension, hypoxia, and DIC, not delayed post-trauma respiratory and neurologic symptoms.




**Buzzword Combo:** labor/delivery + shock + DIC + bleeding + sudden hypoxia

### D) Air embolism causing right heart obstruction and sudden collapse

Air embolism is associated with central line placement or neurosurgery and presents with acute cardiovascular collapse and “mill wheel” murmur, not petechiae or delayed ARDS-like presentation.

**Buzzword Combo:** central line + sudden collapse + mill wheel murmur
