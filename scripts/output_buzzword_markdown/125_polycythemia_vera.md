## Buzzword Question

A 60-year-old man with headache, dizziness, aquagenic pruritus, and elevated hemoglobin and hematocrit most likely has which mutation?

## Buzzwords Used

headache; dizziness; aquagenic pruritus; elevated hemoglobin; elevated hematocrit; myeloproliferative disorder

## Answer Options

A) JAK2
B) BCR-ABL
C) c-MYC
D) APC

## Correct Answer

A) JAK2

## Explanation

Polycythemia vera is caused by a gain-of-function JAK2 V617F mutation leading to constitutive activation of the JAK-STAT pathway. This drives EPO-independent erythrocytosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

JAK2 V617F + low EPO + high hematocrit = Polycythemia vera

## Wrong Options

### B) BCR-ABL

BCR-ABL fusion (t(9;22)) causes chronic myeloid leukemia with granulocytosis and basophilia. It does not cause isolated erythrocytosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** t(9;22) + basophilia + low LAP = CML

### C) c-MYC

c-MYC translocation t(8;14) is associated with Burkitt lymphoma, a rapidly proliferating B-cell malignancy with starry-sky histology. It is unrelated to erythrocytosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** t(8;14) + starry-sky + jaw mass = Burkitt lymphoma

### D) APC

APC mutation causes familial adenomatous polyposis leading to numerous colonic adenomas and colorectal cancer risk. It is unrelated to hematologic proliferation disorders.

**Buzzword Combo:** APC mutation + polyposis + colon cancer risk = FAP
