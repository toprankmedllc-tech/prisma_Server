## Buzzword Question

A 35-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after a motor vehicle collision. He was wearing a seatbelt and reports severe suprapubic pain and inability to urinate. Examination reveals lower abdominal tenderness. Laboratory studies show elevated serum creatinine and BUN despite normal baseline renal function. CT cystography demonstrates contrast leaking into the peritoneal cavity. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Seatbelt injury; full bladder; suprapubic pain; difficulty voiding; elevated BUN; elevated creatinine; contrast in peritoneal cavity

## Answer Options

A) Intraperitoneal bladder rupture

B) Extraperitoneal bladder rupture

C) Posterior urethral injury

D) Anterior urethral injury

## Correct Answer

A) Intraperitoneal bladder rupture

## Explanation

A sudden increase in intravesical pressure against a full bladder can rupture the bladder dome, the weakest portion of the bladder. Urine accumulates in the peritoneal cavity, and absorption of urinary waste products causes elevated BUN and creatinine.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Seatbelt injury + Full bladder + Elevated BUN/Creatinine = Intraperitoneal bladder rupture

## Wrong Options

### B) Extraperitoneal bladder rupture

Extraperitoneal rupture is classically associated with pelvic fractures and urine accumulation in the retropubic space rather than the peritoneal cavity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pelvic fracture + Retropubic urine collection + Anterior bladder wall injury = Extraperitoneal bladder rupture

### C) Posterior urethral injury

Posterior urethral injury presents with blood at the urethral meatus and a high-riding prostate, usually after pelvic fracture.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pelvic fracture + High-riding prostate + Blood at urethral meatus = Posterior urethral injury

### D) Anterior urethral injury

Anterior urethral injury is usually due to a straddle injury causing disruption of the bulbar urethra and scrotal/perineal hematoma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Straddle injury + Bulbar urethra disruption + Scrotal hematoma = Anterior urethral injury
