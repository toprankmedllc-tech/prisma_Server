## Buzzword Question

A patient with hematuria, hypertension, and edema develops renal disease several weeks after streptococcal pharyngitis. Which of the following renal disorders is most strongly associated with this presentation?

## Buzzwords Used

Streptococcal infection; Hematuria; Edema

## Answer Options

A) Minimal change disease
B) Membranous nephropathy
C) Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis
D) Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis

## Correct Answer

D) Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis

## Explanation

Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis occurs after streptococcal pharyngitis or skin infection and presents with nephritic syndrome including hematuria, hypertension, edema, and red blood cell casts. The disease is caused by immune complex deposition with low complement levels and subepithelial hump-shaped deposits

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Recent streptococcal infection + cola-colored urine + Subepithelial Humps = PSGN

## Wrong Options

### Minimal change disease

Minimal change disease is most commonly associated with children. It is characterized by diffuse podocyte foot process effacement with normal light microscopy

**Buzzword Combo:** Childhood nephrotic syndrome + podocyte effacement = Minimal change disease

### Membranous nephropathy

Membranous nephropathy is associated with hepatitis B infection, solid tumors, systemic lupus erythematosus, and anti-PLA2R antibodies. The disease is characterized by subepithelial immune complex deposition and thickened glomerular basement membranes

**Buzzword Combo:** Subepithelial deposits + spike-and-dome appearance = Membranous nephropathy

### Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis

Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis is strongly associated with HIV infection, heroin use, obesity, and sickle cell disease. Patients commonly develop nephrotic-range proteinuria and progressive renal dysfunction

**Buzzword Combo:** HIV + heroin use + Proteinuria = FSGS
