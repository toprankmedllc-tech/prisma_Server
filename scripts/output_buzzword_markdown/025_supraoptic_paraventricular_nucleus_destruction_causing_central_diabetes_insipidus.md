## Buzzword Question

A patient develops excessive thirst and large-volume dilute urine after traumatic brain injury involving the hypothalamus. Laboratory studies show hypernatremia and low urine osmolality. Which hypothalamic nuclei were most likely damaged?

## Buzzwords Used

Polyuria ; hypernatremia ; dilute urine ; traumatic brain injury ; ADH deficiency

## Answer Options

A) Suprachiasmatic nucleus
B) Lateral nucleus
C) Supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei
D) Ventromedial nucleus

## Correct Answer

C) Supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei

## Explanation

The supraoptic and paraventricular nuclei synthesize ADH and oxytocin. Damage causes central diabetes insipidus characterized by polyuria, polydipsia, hypernatremia, and dilute urine.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Polyuria + hypernatremia + ADH deficiency = Supraoptic/paraventricular nuclei

## Wrong Options

### A) Suprachiasmatic nucleus

The suprachiasmatic nucleus regulates circadian rhythm and receives retinal light input.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sleep disorder + optic chiasm + circadian rhythm = Suprachiasmatic nucleus

### B) Lateral nucleus

The lateral nucleus controls hunger and feeding behavior rather than water balance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Anorexia + weight loss + feeding center lesion = Lateral nucleus

### D) Ventromedial nucleus

The ventromedial nucleus regulates satiety and appetite suppression.

**Buzzword Combo:** Obesity + hyperphagia + satiety center lesion = Ventromedial nucleus
