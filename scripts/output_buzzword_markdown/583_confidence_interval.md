## Buzzword Question

Researchers investigate the association between chronic sleep deprivation and hypertension. The study reports an odds ratio of 1.9 with a 95% confidence interval of 0.92–3.8. The authors conclude that individuals with chronic sleep deprivation may have higher odds of hypertension and recommend further investigation in a larger population.

Which of the following best explains why the investigators were unable to draw a definitive conclusion regarding the association?

## Buzzwords Used

Odds ratio ; Confidence interval ; Statistical significance ; Null value

## Answer Options

A) The exposure is significantly associated with the outcome because the odds ratio is greater than 1

B) The exposure nearly doubles the risk of hypertension

C) The confidence interval includes the null value, so a statistically significant association was not demonstrated

D) The study establishes a causal relationship between sleep deprivation and hypertension

## Correct Answer

C) The confidence interval includes the null value, so a statistically significant association was not demonstrated

## Explanation

For odds ratios, the null value is 1. Because the confidence interval includes 1, the observed association is not statistically significant at the 95% confidence level.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Odds ratio reported + confidence interval crosses 1 + null value included = Not statistically significant

## Wrong Options

### A) The exposure is significantly associated with the outcome because the odds ratio is greater than 1

Statistical significance depends on the confidence interval, not the odds ratio alone.

**Buzzword Combo:** OR >1 + confidence interval ignored = Incorrect significance interpretation

### B) The exposure nearly doubles the risk of hypertension

The study reports odds rather than risk, and statistical significance has not been established.

**Buzzword Combo:** Odds ratio + risk interpretation = Incorrect measure

### D) The study establishes a causal relationship between sleep deprivation and hypertension

An observed association does not prove causation, particularly when statistical significance has not been demonstrated.

**Buzzword Combo:** Observed association + causation claim = Invalid conclusion
