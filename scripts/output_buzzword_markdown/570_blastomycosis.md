## Buzzword Question

A 35-year-old construction worker in Arizona develops fever, cough, and erythema nodosum after a dust storm. Microscopy demonstrates large spherules filled with endospores. Which organism is responsible?

## Buzzwords Used

Great Lakes region; Chronic cough; Verrucous skin lesions; Broad-based budding yeast

## Answer Options

A) Histoplasma capsulatum
B) Blastomyces dermatitidis
C) Coccidioides immitis
D) Sporothrix schenckii

## Correct Answer

B) Blastomyces dermatitidis

## Explanation

Blastomyces dermatitidis is a dimorphic fungus endemic to the Great Lakes and Ohio River Valley regions. It characteristically appears as a broad-based budding yeast in tissue and commonly causes pulmonary disease with cutaneous involvement.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Great Lakes region + Verrucous skin lesions + Broad-based budding yeast = Blastomyces dermatitidis

## Wrong Options

### A) Histoplasma capsulatum

Histoplasma is associated with bat or bird droppings and appears as small intracellular yeasts within macrophages rather than broad-based budding yeasts.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ohio/Mississippi River Valley + Bat droppings + Intracellular yeast in macrophages = Histoplasma capsulatum

### C) Coccidioides immitis

Coccidioides is endemic to the southwestern United States and forms spherules filled with endospores in tissue.

**Buzzword Combo:** Southwestern United States + Dust exposure + Spherules with endospores = Coccidioides immitis

### D) Sporothrix schenckii

Sporothrix typically causes nodular lymphangitis following traumatic inoculation from rose thorns and demonstrates cigar-shaped yeast forms.

**Buzzword Combo:** Rose thorn injury + Ascending nodules along lymphatics + Cigar-shaped yeast = Sporothrix schenckii
