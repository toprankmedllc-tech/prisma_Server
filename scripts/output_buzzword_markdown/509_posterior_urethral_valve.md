## Buzzword Question

A male infant develops urinary retention and bilateral hydronephrosis shortly after birth due to bladder outlet obstruction. Which of the following congenital urinary tract abnormalities is the most likely cause?

## Buzzwords Used

Male infant; Bladder outlet obstruction; Bilateral hydronephrosis

## Answer Options

A) Posterior urethral valves
B) Vesicoureteral reflux
C) Horseshoe kidney
D) Ureteropelvic junction obstruction

## Correct Answer

A) Posterior urethral valves

## Explanation

Posterior urethral valves are obstructing membranous folds within the posterior urethra that cause bladder outlet obstruction in male infants. Severe obstruction may lead to urinary retention, bilateral hydronephrosis, oligohydramnios, and progressive renal damage

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Male infant + bladder outlet obstruction + bilateral hydronephrosis = Posterior urethral valves

## Wrong Options

### Vesicoureteral reflux

Vesicoureteral reflux results from incompetence of the ureterovesical junction and commonly presents with recurrent urinary tract infections and renal scarring. The disorder causes retrograde urine flow rather than direct bladder outlet obstruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recurrent childhood UTIs + retrograde urine flow + renal scarring = Vesicoureteral reflux

### Horseshoe kidney

Horseshoe kidney results from fusion of the lower poles of the kidneys and failure of normal ascent due to entrapment beneath the inferior mesenteric artery. Many patients are asymptomatic, although urinary obstruction and infections may occur.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fused lower poles + Turner Syndrome = Horseshoe kidney

### Ureteropelvic junction obstruction

Ureteropelvic junction obstruction causes hydronephrosis due to impaired urine drainage from the renal pelvis into the ureter. It typically produces unilateral hydronephrosis rather than bladder outlet obstruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hydronephrosis + impaired  drainage + UPJ narrowing = UPJ obstruction
