## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old woman attends multiple clinic visits over 6 months for chest tightness and fatigue rated 8/10. All workup is normal. She spends hours online researching these symptoms and is convinced she has an undiagnosed cardiac disease. A coworker with similar anxiety but no physical symptoms was diagnosed with a different condition. The feature that best distinguishes this patient's diagnosis from her coworker's is:


## Buzzwords Used

Physical symptoms present; excessive preoccupation; normal workup; convinced of serious illness; coworker has anxiety without symptoms


## Answer Options

A) Presence of one or more distressing somatic symptoms 
B) Duration of symptoms greater than 6 months 
C) Absence of deliberate symptom fabrication
 D) Significant functional impairment


## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Somatic symptom disorder requires actual distressing somatic symptoms + excessive preoccupation. Illness anxiety disorder is characterized by preoccupation with disease with minimal or absent somatic symptoms. The presence of the somatic symptoms themselves is the defining distinguishing feature between the two diagnoses.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Physical symptoms + excessive preoccupation + no organic cause = Somatic symptom disorder


## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Duration of symptoms greater than 6 months

Both SSD and illness anxiety disorder require >6 months duration. Duration does not distinguish them. The differentiating factor is whether actual somatic symptoms are present.


**Buzzword Combo:** Anxiety about disease without physical symptoms + >6 months = Illness anxiety disorder

### 2: C) Absence of deliberate symptom fabrication

Both SSD and illness anxiety disorder involve unconscious (non-deliberate) symptom experience. Neither requires nor rules out conscious fabrication  that distinction separates them from factitious disorder and malingering, not from each other.


**Buzzword Combo:** Deliberate symptom production for sick role = Factitious disorder; for external gain = Malingering

### 3: D) Significant functional impairment

Both SSD and illness anxiety disorder can cause significant functional impairment. Impairment is not the distinguishing criterion between these two diagnoses.

**Buzzword Combo:** Functional impairment + health preoccupation + absent somatic symptoms = Illness anxiety disorder
