## Buzzword Question

A 72-year-old man is admitted to the hospital after a stroke that leaves him bedridden for several months. During follow-up examination, the physician notices marked thinning and weakness of the muscles in his lower extremities compared with their previous size. A muscle biopsy shows decreased muscle fiber diameter due to loss of intracellular proteins.
Which of the following best describes the change occurring in this patient’s skeletal muscles?

## Buzzwords Used

thinning and weakness of the muscles in his lower extremities compared with their previous size

## Answer Options

A. Hyperplasia
B. Hypertrophy
C. Metaplasia
D. Atrophy

## Correct Answer

D. Atrophy

## Explanation

Atrophy is a decrease in cell size caused by loss of cellular proteins and organelles. In skeletal muscle, prolonged disuse or denervation leads to reduced muscle fiber size through increased protein degradation, commonly mediated by the ubiquitin-proteasome system.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Disuse + decreased muscle size + protein degradation = atrophy

## Wrong Options

### A.Hyperplasia

Hyperplasia involves an increase in the number of cells rather than shrinking of existing muscle fibers.

**Buzzword Combo:** increased number of cells + cellular proliferation = hyperplasia

### B.Hypertrophy

Hypertrophy refers to enlargement of cells, commonly seen with resistance exercise.

**Buzzword Combo:** increased cell size + resistance training + larger muscle fibers = hypertrophy

### C. Metaplasia

Metaplasia is a reversible substitution of one differentiated cell type for another and does not describe muscle wasting.

**Buzzword Combo:** Replacement of one mature cell type by another = metaplasia
