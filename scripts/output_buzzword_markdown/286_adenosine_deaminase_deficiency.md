## Buzzword Question

An infant has severe recurrent bacterial, viral, and fungal infections early in life. Laboratory studies show markedly decreased T cells with poor B-cell function. Which of the following enzyme deficiencies most likely explains this presentation?

## Buzzwords Used

Severe recurrent bacterial, viral, and fungal infections early in life; markedly decreased T cells; poor B-cell function

## Answer Options

A) NADPH oxidase deficiency
B) Adenosine deaminase deficiency
C) Myeloperoxidase deficiency
D) Bruton's tyrosine kinase deficiency

## Correct Answer

B) Adenosine deaminase deficiency


## Explanation

Adenosine deaminase deficiency causes toxic accumulation of deoxyadenosine and dATP. These metabolites damage developing lymphocytes, causing decreased T cells and impaired B-cell function.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Severe recurrent infections early in life + decreased T cells + impaired B-cell function = ADA deficiency

## Wrong Options

### A) NADPH oxidase deficiency

NADPH oxidase deficiency causes chronic granulomatous disease due to impaired phagocyte respiratory burst. It does not cause combined T- and B-cell immune dysfunction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recurrent catalase-positive infections + impaired respiratory burst + granulomas = chronic granulomatous disease

### C) Myeloperoxidase deficiency

Myeloperoxidase deficiency impairs neutrophil production of hypochlorous acid. It is not a cause of severe combined immunodeficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Impaired hypochlorous acid production + neutrophil killing defect + Candida risk = myeloperoxidase deficiency

### D) Bruton's tyrosine kinase deficiency

Bruton's tyrosine kinase deficiency causes failed B-cell maturation with low immunoglobulins. T-cell function is preserved, unlike in ADA deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Boy after 6 months + recurrent bacterial infections + absent mature B cells = Bruton agammaglobulinemia
