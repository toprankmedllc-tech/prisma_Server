## Buzzword Question

A 60-year-old smoker has chronic productive cough and cyanosis. Bronchial biopsy shows mucus glands occupying >40% of bronchial wall thickness. What is the diagnosis?

A. Emphysema
B. Chronic bronchitis
C. Bronchiectasis
D. Asthma

## Buzzwords Used

chronic productive cough; cyanosis; Reid Index > 0.4/40%

## Answer Options

A. Emphysema
B. Chronic bronchitis
C. Bronchiectasis
D. Asthma

## Correct Answer

B. Chronic Bronchitis

## Explanation

The Reid index is the ratio of the thickness of the mucous gland layer in the bronchial wall to the total thickness of the bronchial wall between the epithelium and cartilage. It is used to assess mucus gland enlargement in Chronic Bronchitis, and a value > 0.4 is diagnostic of increased mucus gland hypertrophy

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

chronic productive cough + cyanosis + Reid Index > 0.4/40% = Chronic bronchtis

## Wrong Options

### A. Emphysema 

 This shows destruction of alveolar septa with loss of elastic recoil and airspace enlargement, not mucus gland hypertrophy. It is seen in Emphysema.


**Buzzword Combo:** chronic smoker+ dyspnea + hyperinflated lung+ airspaces enlargement = Emphysema

### C. Bronchiectasis 

This involves permanent dilation of bronchi due to destruction of bronchial walls, not an increase in mucus gland thickness. It is seen in Bronchiectasis.

**Buzzword Combo:** purulent copious sputum production + recurrent respiratory infection + dilatation of bronchial tree = Bronchiectasis

### D. Asthma 


Asthma is characterized by reversible airway obstruction with smooth muscle hyperreactivity and eosinophilic inflammation, not structural gland enlargement. It is seen in Asthma.

**Buzzword Combo:** reversible airway obstruction + episodic wheezing+ airway hyperactivity + eosinophilic inflammation= Asthma
