## Buzzword Question

Which of the following best describes the mechanism of action of azithromycin?

## Buzzwords Used

Azithromycin ; Mechanism of action

## Answer Options

 A) Inhibits DNA gyrase
 B) Binds 30S ribosomal subunit and prevents tRNA attachment
 C) Binds 50S ribosomal subunit and inhibits translocation
 D) Inhibits peptidoglycan cross-linking

## Correct Answer

 C) Binds 50S ribosomal subunit and inhibits translocation

## Explanation

Azithromycin is a macrolide commonly used with beta-lactams for community-acquired pneumonia and parapneumonic effusions. Macrolides inhibit the 50S ribosomal subunit and are associated with QT prolongation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CAP + QT prolongation + 50S inhibition = Macrolide

## Wrong Options

###  Inhibits DNA gyrase

This is the mechanism of fluoroquinolones such as levofloxacin

**Buzzword Combo:** DNA gyrase inhibition + tendon rupture = Fluoroquinolone

###  Binds 30S ribosomal subunit and prevents tRNA attachment

This is the mechanism of tetracyclines such as doxycycline.

**Buzzword Combo:** 30S inhibition + photosensitivity = Tetracycline

### Inhibits peptidoglycan cross-linking

This is the mechanism of beta-lactam antibiotics such as ceftriaxone.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cell wall inhibition + beta-lactam ring = Cephalosporin
