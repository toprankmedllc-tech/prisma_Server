## Buzzword Question

A 68-year-old man presents with 1 year of slow movement, axial rigidity, frequent backward falls, and an inability to look downward voluntarily. He has no resting tremor and shows minimal improvement with levodopa. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Early postural instability with backward falls; vertical (downward) gaze palsy; axial rigidity > limb rigidity; absent resting tremor; poor response to levodopa

## Answer Options

A) Idiopathic Parkinson disease
B) Progressive supranuclear palsy
C) Multiple system atrophy
D) Dementia with Lewy bodies

## Correct Answer

B) Progressive supranuclear palsy

## Explanation

PSP is a tauopathy characterized by early postural instability with falls, axial rigidity, vertical (especially downward) gaze palsy, and a poor response to levodopa. Pathology shows tau-positive globose neurofibrillary tangles in the midbrain and basal ganglia.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Early falls + vertical (downward) gaze palsy + axial rigidity + poor levodopa response = tau pathology → progressive supranuclear palsy

## Wrong Options

### Idiopathic Parkinson disease

Idiopathic PD typically has asymmetric resting tremor, preserved early postural stability, intact eye movements, and a robust response to levodopa, opposite of this presentation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asymmetric resting tremor + cogwheel rigidity + bradykinesia + good levodopa response = idiopathic Parkinson disease

### Multiple system atrophy

MSA is a synucleinopathy presenting with parkinsonism plus prominent autonomic dysfunction (orthostatic hypotension, urinary incontinence) and/or cerebellar signs, not vertical gaze palsy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Parkinsonism + orthostatic hypotension + cerebellar ataxia = multiple system atrophy

###  Dementia with Lewy bodies

DLB presents with parkinsonism, fluctuating cognition, recurrent visual hallucinations, and REM sleep behavior disorder, with dementia preceding or appearing within 1 year of motor symptoms, not falls and gaze palsy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Parkinsonism + visual hallucinations + fluctuating cognition + REM sleep behavior disorder = dementia with Lewy bodies
