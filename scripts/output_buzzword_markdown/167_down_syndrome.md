## Buzzword Question

A newborn has hypotonia, upward-slanting palpebral fissures, epicanthal folds, and a single transverse palmar crease. Which congenital heart defect is most commonly associated with this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Hypotonia; epicanthal folds; single transverse palmar crease; congenital heart defect

## Answer Options

A) Tetralogy of Fallot
B) Atrioventricular septal defect
C) Coarctation of the aorta
D) Truncus arteriosus

## Correct Answer

B) Atrioventricular septal defect

## Explanation

Atrioventricular septal defect (endocardial cushion defect) is the most common congenital heart defect associated with Down syndrome.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Down syndrome facies + hypotonia + AV septal defect = Trisomy 21

## Wrong Options

### A) Tetralogy of Fallot

Tetralogy of Fallot is strongly associated with DiGeorge syndrome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Conotruncal defects + thymic aplasia + hypocalcemia = DiGeorge syndrome

### C) Coarctation of the aorta

Coarctation of the aorta is classically associated with Turner syndrome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Webbed neck + coarctation of aorta + streak ovaries = Turner syndrome

### D) Truncus arteriosus

Persistent truncus arteriosus results from failure of neural crest cell migration.

**Buzzword Combo:** Single outflow tract + cyanosis + neural crest defect = Truncus arteriosus
