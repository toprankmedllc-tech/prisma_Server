## Buzzword Question

A 32-year-old woman with fatigue, glossitis, anemia with hypersegmented neutrophils, and normal methylmalonic acid most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; glossitis; anemia; hypersegmented neutrophils; normal methylmalonic acid; macrocytosis

## Answer Options

A. Vitamin B12 deficiency
B. Folic acid deficiency
C. Iron deficiency anemia
D. Aplastic anemia

## Correct Answer

B. Folic acid deficiency

## Explanation

Folate deficiency causes megaloblastic anemia with macrocytosis and hypersegmented neutrophils, with normal methylmalonic acid because folate is not involved in methylmalonyl-CoA metabolism. It is commonly seen in pregnancy, alcoholism, and anticonvulsant use.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Normal MMA + elevated homocysteine + macrocytosis = Folate deficiency

## Wrong Options

### A. Vitamin B12 deficiency

Vitamin B12 deficiency also causes macrocytic anemia but is distinguished by elevated methylmalonic acid and neurologic symptoms like subacute combined degeneration.

**Buzzword Combo:** Elevated MMA + neurologic symptoms + macrocytosis = B12 deficiency

### C. Iron deficiency anemia

Iron deficiency anemia causes microcytic hypochromic anemia with low ferritin and high TIBC, not macrocytosis or hypersegmented neutrophils.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + high TIBC + microcytosis = Iron deficiency anemia

### D. Aplastic anemia

Aplastic anemia presents with pancytopenia and a hypocellular fatty marrow, not isolated macrocytic anemia with hypersegmented neutrophils.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pancytopenia + hypocellular fatty marrow = Aplastic anemia
