## Buzzword Question


A patient with no significant past medical history is brought to the emergency department with an acute ST-elevation myocardial infarction and an anticipated door-to-balloon time of less than 120 minutes. Which of the following is the most appropriate definitive treatment?

## Buzzwords Used

STEMI; emergency department; door-to-balloon time <120 minutes; primary percutaneous coronary intervention

## Answer Options

A) Primary percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI)
B) Intravenous fibrinolytic therapy (tissue plasminogen activator)
C) Coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG)
D) Optimal medical management alone

## Correct Answer

A) Primary percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI)

## Explanation

Primary PCI is the gold-standard reperfusion therapy for STEMI when it can be performed within 120 minutes of first medical contact at a PCI-capable facility. It directly opens the occluded artery, restores blood flow, and has lower mortality and reinfarction rates than fibrinolytic therapy.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

STEMI + door-to-balloon ≤120 min = primary PCI (gold standard)

## Wrong Options

### B) Intravenous fibrinolytic therapy (tissue plasminogen activator)

Fibrinolytics are reserved for STEMI patients who cannot reach a PCI-capable facility within 120 minutes of first medical contact, and carry higher bleeding risk than PCI.

**Buzzword Combo:** STEMI + no PCI access within 120 min = IV fibrinolytic therapy (tPA, alteplase)

### C) Coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG)

CABG is reserved for multivessel disease, failed PCI, mechanical complications of MI, or stable angina with left main disease — not for acute STEMI reperfusion.

**Buzzword Combo:** Multivessel disease / left main / failed PCI = CABG

### D) Optimal medical management alone

Medical therapy (aspirin, P2Y12 inhibitor, anticoagulation, β-blocker, statin) is adjunctive to reperfusion in STEMI, not a substitute. Failure to reperfuse increases mortality.

**Buzzword Combo:** STEMI + medical therapy without reperfusion = inadequate (↑ mortality)
