## Buzzword Question

A patient with advanced HIV presents with odynophagia and dysphagia. Endoscopy shows large, linear ulcers in the distal esophagus. Biopsy reveals owl’s eye intranuclear inclusions.

## Buzzwords Used

## Answer Options

A) Candida esophagitis
B) Cytomegalovirus esophagitis
C) Herpes simplex virus esophagitis
D) Pill-induced esophagitis

## Correct Answer

B) Cytomegalovirus esophagitis

## Explanation

Cytomegalovirus (CMV) esophagitis is an opportunistic infection of the esophagus caused by the human herpesvirus 5. It overwhelmingly affects immunocompromised individuals (e.g., organ transplant recipients, patients with advanced HIV/AIDS, or those on immunosuppressive therapies). 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

HIV + Intranuclear inclusion bodies = CMV esophagitis 

## Wrong Options

### A) Candida esophagitis

Candida esophagitis is a fungal infection of the esophagus caused by the overgrowth of Candida yeast (usually Candida albicans). It is particularly common in immunocompromised individuals (such as patients with HIV/AIDS), people using antibiotics or corticosteroids, and those taking proton pump inhibitors (PPIs).

**Buzzword Combo:** HIV + ATB/Corticosteriods = Candida Albicans 

### C) Herpes simplex virus esophagitis

Herpes simplex virus (HSV) esophagitis is an inflammation of the esophagus caused primarily by HSV-1. It is most common in immunocompromised individuals (e.g., those with HIV, malignancies, or on immunosuppressants), though it can occasionally affect immunocompetent patients. 

**Buzzword Combo:** Punched ulcer +esophagitis + HIV = Herpes simplex virus esophagitis

### D) Pill-induced esophagitis

Pill-induced esophagitis is an acute mucosal injury caused by a medication direct toxic or caustic effect when it becomes temporarily lodged in the esophagus. It typically occurs in patients who swallow tablets or capsules with inadequate water or immediately before lying down.

**Buzzword Combo:** Tetracycline/Dox + esophagitis =  Pill-induced esophagitis
