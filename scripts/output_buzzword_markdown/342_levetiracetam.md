## Buzzword Question

Focal and generalized seizures, minimal drug interactions, fatigue, dizziness, behavioral changes, irritability, psychosis, anticonvulsant acting through synaptic vesicle modulation most strongly corresponds to which mechanism of action?

## Buzzwords Used

Behavioral changes; psychosis; irritability; focal and generalized seizures; minimal drug interactions; synaptic vesicle modulation; fatigue

## Answer Options

A) Blocks voltage-gated Na⁺ channels
B) Blocks T-type Ca²⁺ channels
C) Binds synaptic vesicle protein SV2A
D) Inhibits GABA transaminase

## Correct Answer

C) Binds synaptic vesicle protein SV2A

## Explanation

Levetiracetam works by binding synaptic vesicle protein 2A (SV2A), which modulates neurotransmitter release. It is commonly associated with neuropsychiatric adverse effects such as irritability, agitation, and psychosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Psychosis + minimal drug interactions + synaptic vesicle modulation = SV2A binding

## Wrong Options

### A) Blocks voltage-gated Na⁺ channels

Na⁺ channel blockade is the mechanism of drugs such as phenytoin, carbamazepine, and lamotrigine. These drugs are more associated with rash, diplopia, and DRESS/SJS.

**Buzzword Combo:** Diplopia + gingival hyperplasia + CYP450 induction = Na⁺ channel blockade

### B) Blocks T-type Ca²⁺ channels

T-type Ca²⁺ channel blockade is characteristic of ethosuximide, which is primarily used for absence seizures.

**Buzzword Combo:** Absence seizures + 3-Hz spike-wave + T-type Ca²⁺ blockade = Ethosuximide MOA

### D) Inhibits GABA transaminase

Valproate inhibits GABA transaminase and increases CNS GABA levels. It is associated with hepatotoxicity, pancreatitis, and neural tube defects.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pancreatitis + weight gain + teratogenicity = GABA transaminase inhibition
