## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old man with chronic kidney disease develops peaked T waves and widening of the QRS complex on ECG.
Which of the following electrolyte abnormalities is most likely present?


## Buzzwords Used

Kidney disease; Peaked T waves; Widened QRS

## Answer Options

A) Hyperkalemia
B) Hypocalcemia
C) Hypernatremia
D) Hypomagnesemia

## Correct Answer

A) Hyperkalemia

## Explanation

Hyperkalemia commonly occurs in chronic kidney disease due to impaired renal potassium excretion. Elevated serum potassium produces characteristic ECG changes including peaked T waves, widened QRS complexes, and potentially fatal arrhythmias.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CKD + peaked T waves + widened QRS = Hyperkalemia

## Wrong Options

### Hypocalcemia

Hypocalcemia commonly causes tetany, muscle spasms, and prolonged QT interval.

**Buzzword Combo:** Tetany + muscle spasms + prolonged QT = Hypocalcemia

### Hypernatremia

Hypernatremia usually presents with neurologic symptoms caused by cellular dehydration.

**Buzzword Combo:** Water loss + hyperosmolar state + neurologic symptoms = Hypernatremia

### Hypomagnesemia

Hypomagnesemia may cause tremors, arrhythmias, and refractory hypokalemia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Alcoholism + tremors + refractory hypokalemia = Hypomagnesemia
