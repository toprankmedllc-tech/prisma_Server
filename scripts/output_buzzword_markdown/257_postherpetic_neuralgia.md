## Buzzword Question

A 75-year-old woman had a painful unilateral dermatomal vesicular rash on her right lower back 4 months ago that resolved with crusting. She now reports persistent severe burning pain and exquisite sensitivity to clothing in the same area, although the rash has long since cleared. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Prior herpes zoster rash in the same dermatome more than 3 months ago; persistent burning dermatomal pain after rash resolution; allodynia

## Answer Options

A) Disseminated zoster
B) Herpes zoster encephalitis
C) Ramsay Hunt syndrome
D) Postherpetic neuralgia

## Correct Answer

D) Postherpetic neuralgia

## Explanation

Postherpetic neuralgia is persistent neuropathic pain (burning, dysesthesia, allodynia) lasting more than 3 months in the dermatome of a prior herpes zoster eruption. It is the most common chronic complication, particularly in older adults, and is treated with gabapentin, pregabalin, tricyclic antidepressants, or topical lidocaine.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Prior dermatomal zoster + persistent burning dermatomal pain more than 3 months after rash resolves + allodynia in elderly = postherpetic neuralgia

## Wrong Options

### Disseminated zoster

Disseminated zoster involves more than one dermatome or extensive systemic involvement, typically in severely immunocompromised patients, with active vesicles rather than a healed rash.

**Buzzword Combo:** Severely immunocompromised + zoster involving more than two dermatomes or visceral involvement = disseminated zoster

### Herpes zoster encephalitis

VZV CNS involvement presents acutely with fever, confusion, focal deficits, and CSF lymphocytic pleocytosis with positive PCR, not persistent localized dermatomal pain months after rash resolution.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recent zoster + fever + confusion + focal neurologic deficits + CSF VZV PCR positive = VZV encephalitis

### Ramsay Hunt syndrome

Ramsay Hunt syndrome is VZV reactivation in the GENICULATE GANGLION producing peripheral facial palsy with ear vesicles and sensorineural hearing loss, not chronic truncal dermatomal pain after rash resolution.

**Buzzword Combo:** Peripheral facial palsy + ear pain with vesicles + sensorineural hearing loss = Ramsay Hunt syndrome (VZV in geniculate ganglion)

## Related Concepts

Antiviral therapy within 72 hours of rash onset to reduce PHN risk; Recombinant zoster vaccine (Shingrix) to prevent zoster and PHN; Neuropathic pain pharmacology (gabapentin, pregabalin, TCAs, topical lidocaine)
