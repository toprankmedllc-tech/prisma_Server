## Buzzword Question

Which of the following histologic findings is pathognomonic for acute rheumatic fever?

## Buzzwords Used


Acute rheumatic fever

## Answer Options

A) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red
B) Non-caseating granulomas
C) Coagulative necrosis with neutrophils
D) Aschoff bodies with Anitschkow cells

## Correct Answer

D) Aschoff bodies with Anitschkow cells

## Explanation

Aschoff bodies — granulomas with central fibrinoid necrosis surrounded by Anitschkow cells (macrophages with "caterpillar" nuclei) — are pathognomonic for rheumatic carditis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)


Aschoff body + Anitschkow cell (caterpillar nuclei) = acute rheumatic fever

## Wrong Options

### 
B) Non-caseating granulomas

Seen in cardiac sarcoidosis, not rheumatic fever.

**Buzzword Combo:** Non-caseating granulomas + bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy = sarcoidosis

### 
C) Coagulative necrosis with neutrophils

Seen in early myocardial infarction (12–24 hours)

**Buzzword Combo:** Coagulative necrosis + neutrophils = early MI

### D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red

Seen in cardiac amyloidosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Congo red + apple-green birefringence = amyloidosis
