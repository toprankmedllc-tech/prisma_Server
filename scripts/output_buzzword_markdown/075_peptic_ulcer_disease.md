## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old man presents with a 3-month history of a gnawing, burning epigastric pain. He reports that his symptoms typically wake him up at night and consistently improve shortly after eating meals.Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis and pathophysiology?

## Buzzwords Used

Gnawing; burning; epigastric pain; improve after meals 

## Answer Options

A) Gastric ulcer; pain worsens with food due to direct acid irritation on the ulcer base.
B) Duodenal ulcer; pain improves with food as the food acts to buffer gastric acid temporarily.
C) Gastric ulcer; pain improves with food because of increased mucus production in the stomach.
D) Duodenal ulcer; pain worsens with food because of increased pancreatic secretions

## Correct Answer

B) Duodenal ulcer; pain improves with food as the food acts to buffer gastric acid temporarily.

## Explanation

Explanation: Duodenal ulcers typically get better with food intake because the ingested food buffers the acid and the pyloric sphincter closes, preventing acid from washing over the ulcer. Conversely, gastric ulcers are irritated and worsen immediately with eating.
