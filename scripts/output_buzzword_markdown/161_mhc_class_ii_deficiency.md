## Buzzword Question

Full Buzzword question:
A 9-month-old infant has recurrent infections due to absent expression of class II antigen-presenting molecules on macrophages, dendritic cells, and B cells. Which of the following interactions is directly impaired?

## Buzzwords Used

9-month-old infant; absent class II antigen-presenting molecules; macrophages, dendritic cells, and B cells

## Answer Options

A) MHC II on an APC binding the TCR and CD4 on a helper T cell
B) MHC I on a nucleated cell binding the TCR and CD8 on a cytotoxic T cell
C) B7 on an APC binding CD28 on a naive T cell
D) CD40L on a helper T cell binding CD40 on a B cell

## Correct Answer

A) MHC II on an APC binding the TCR and CD4 on a helper T cell


## Explanation

MHC II on APCs presents extracellular antigen to CD4+ helper T cells. The primary activation signal is MHC II binding the TCR together with the CD4 coreceptor.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Extracellular antigen + APC + MHC II binding TCR/CD4 = CD4+ T-cell activation

## Wrong Options

### B) MHC I on a nucleated cell binding the TCR and CD8 on a cytotoxic T cell

This describes MHC I restriction and CD8+ T-cell activation. MHC I presents intracellular antigen, whereas MHC II presents extracellular antigen to CD4+ helper T cells.

**Buzzword Combo:** Intracellular antigen + MHC I + CD8+ cytotoxic T cell = cell-mediated killing

### C) B7 on an APC binding CD28 on a naive T cell

This is the shared co-stimulatory signal required for full T-cell activation. It does not define MHC II restriction to CD4+ helper T cells.

**Buzzword Combo:** B7/CD80-CD86 + CD28 + full naive T-cell activation = co-stimulation

### D) CD40L on a helper T cell binding CD40 on a B cell

This interaction allows B-cell activation and immunoglobulin class switching. It is classically defective in Hyper-IgM syndrome.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD40L defect + increased IgM + decreased IgG/IgA/IgE = Hyper-IgM syndrome

## Related Concepts

MHC I restriction; CD8 cytotoxic T cells; CD40-CD40L interaction
