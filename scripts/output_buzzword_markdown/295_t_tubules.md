## Buzzword Question

A physiology student is reviewing the microscopic anatomy of skeletal muscle fibers. During the lecture, the professor explains that T tubules work closely with a specialized intracellular membrane system that stores calcium and releases it during excitation-contraction coupling.
Which of the following cellular structures is most closely associated with T tubules in skeletal muscle?

## Buzzwords Used

T tubules

## Answer Options

A. Golgi apparatus
B. Sarcoplasmic reticulum
C. Lysosomes
D. Nuclear envelope

## Correct Answer

B. Sarcoplasmic reticulum

## Explanation

In skeletal muscle, T tubules are closely associated with the sarcoplasmic reticulum (SR), a specialized form of smooth endoplasmic reticulum that stores calcium ions. Together, a T tubule and two terminal cisternae of the sarcoplasmic reticulum form a structure called a triad.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Calcium storage + excitation-contraction coupling + terminal cisternae = sarcoplasmic reticulum

Functions in protein modification and packaging.
