## Buzzword Question

A patient with recurrent epistaxis, menorrhagia, prolonged bleeding time, and normal platelet count most likely has:

## Buzzwords Used

recurrent epistaxis; menorrhagia; prolonged bleeding time; normal platelet count; mucocutaneous bleeding

## Answer Options

A) Hemophilia A
B) von Willebrand disease
C) Disseminated intravascular coagulation
D) Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

## Correct Answer

B. von Willebrand disease

## Explanation

von Willebrand disease is caused by vWF deficiency leading to impaired platelet adhesion, producing mucocutaneous bleeding with prolonged bleeding time. Platelet count remains normal, which helps distinguish it from thrombocytopenic disorders.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Mucosal bleeding + prolonged bleeding time + normal platelets = vWD

## Wrong Options

### A. Hemophilia A

Hemophilia A causes deep tissue bleeding such as hemarthroses due to factor VIII deficiency, with prolonged aPTT but normal bleeding time. It typically affects males.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hemarthrosis + prolonged aPTT + X-linked inheritance = Hemophilia A

### C. Disseminated intravascular coagulation

DIC causes widespread consumption of platelets and clotting factors, leading to prolonged PT/PTT, thrombocytopenia, and elevated D-dimer. It presents acutely and not with isolated mucosal bleeding.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thrombocytopenia + prolonged PT/PTT + elevated D-dimer = DIC

### D. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

ITP causes isolated thrombocytopenia with normal PT and aPTT. It would not present with a normal platelet count as described in this case.

**Buzzword Combo:** solated low platelets + petechiae = ITP
