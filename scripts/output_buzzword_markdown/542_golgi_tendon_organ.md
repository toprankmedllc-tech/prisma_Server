## Buzzword Question

A 30-year-old man is performing heavy resistance training at the gym. While attempting to lift a very heavy weight, sensory receptors within his tendon become activated and trigger a protective reflex that causes relaxation of the contracting muscle to prevent injury. A medical student asks what stimulus primarily activates these receptors.
Which of the following most strongly activates Golgi tendon organs?

## Buzzwords Used

 what stimulus primarily activates these receptors

## Answer Options

A. Decrease in muscle length
B. Increase in muscle tension
C. Vibration of the skin
D. Stretch of joint capsules

## Correct Answer

B. Increase in muscle tension

## Explanation

Golgi tendon organs are proprioceptive receptors located within tendons that respond primarily to increased muscle tension, especially during strong muscle contraction. Activation of these receptors triggers inhibitory reflexes that protect muscles and tendons from excessive force.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Strong contraction + tendon tension + protective inhibitory reflex = Golgi tendon organ activation

## Wrong Options

### A. Decrease in muscle length

Golgi tendon organs are not primarily activated by shortening of muscle fibers. They respond mainly to increased tension generated during contraction.


**Buzzword Combo:** Muscle shortening + reduced stretch = not primary Golgi tendon organ stimulus

### C. Vibration of the skin

this stimulus is detected mainly by Pacinian corpuscles, which are specialized for vibration and deep pressure sensation.                                                                                  

**Buzzword Combo:** Deep vibration + rapidly adapting mechanoreceptor = Pacinian corpuscle

### D. Stretch of joint capsules

Joint receptors help detect joint position and movement rather than monitoring muscle tension within tendons.

**Buzzword Combo:** Joint position sensation + proprioception around joints = joint receptors
