## Buzzword Question

A 55-year-old man with headaches, dizziness, intense itching after hot showers, splenomegaly, and elevated hematocrit most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

headaches; dizziness; aquagenic pruritus; splenomegaly; elevated hematocrit; hyperviscosity symptoms

## Answer Options

A) Chronic myeloid leukemia
B) Polycythemia vera
C) Multiple myeloma
D) Iron deficiency anemia

## Correct Answer

B. Polycythemia vera

## Explanation

Polycythemia vera is a JAK2-driven myeloproliferative neoplasm causing increased red cell mass independent of erythropoietin. It presents with hyperviscosity symptoms, splenomegaly, and aquagenic pruritus (itching after hot showers).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Aquagenic pruritus + high hematocrit + splenomegaly = Polycythemia vera

## Wrong Options

### A) Chronic myeloid leukemia

CML is characterized by granulocytosis, basophilia, and low LAP score due to BCR-ABL fusion. It does not primarily cause elevated hematocrit or aquagenic pruritus.

**Buzzword Combo:** t(9;22) + basophilia + low LAP = CML

### C) Multiple myeloma

Multiple myeloma presents with CRAB features (hypercalcemia, renal failure, anemia, bone lesions) and M protein spike. It does not cause erythrocytosis or pruritus after hot showers.

**Buzzword Combo:** CRAB + M protein + lytic lesions = Multiple myeloma

### D) Iron deficiency anemia

Iron deficiency anemia causes microcytic, hypochromic anemia with low hemoglobin and ferritin. It is associated with fatigue and pallor, not elevated hematocrit.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + microcytosis + pallor = Iron deficiency anemia
