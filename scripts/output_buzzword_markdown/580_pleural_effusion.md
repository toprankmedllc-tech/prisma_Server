## Buzzword Question

A 70-year-old man with long-standing congestive heart failure presents with progressive dyspnea and orthopnea. Chest imaging reveals a right-sided pleural effusion. Thoracentesis shows clear, straw-colored fluid. Which of the following pleural fluid findings is most consistent with this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Congestive heart failure; pleural effusion; straw-colored fluid; dyspnea; orthopnea; Light’s criteria

## Answer Options

A) Protein ratio >0.5; LDH ratio >0.6; LDH >2/3 ULN
B) Protein ratio <0.5; LDH ratio <0.6; LDH <2/3 ULN
C) Low pH; low glucose; positive Gram stain
D) High triglycerides; milky fluid; chylomicrons

## Correct Answer

B) Protein ratio <0.5; LDH ratio <0.6; LDH <2/3 Upper normal limit

## Explanation

Transudative effusions (e.g., CHF) fail Light’s criteria due to systemic hydrostatic pressure changes without inflammation, leading to low protein and LDH levels in pleural fluid. All three parameters are below cutoff values, confirming a transudate.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CHF; straw-colored effusion; low protein ratio + low LDH ratio + low LDH = transudative effusion

## Wrong Options

### A) Protein ratio >0.5; LDH ratio >0.6; LDH >2/3 ULN

This pattern fulfills all Light’s criteria and indicates an exudative effusion due to infection, malignancy, or inflammation rather than CHF.

**Buzzword Combo:** Infection; malignancy; inflammation; high protein and LDH = exudate

### C) Low pH; low glucose; positive Gram stain

These findings indicate empyema or complicated parapneumonic effusion, not hydrostatic transudative effusion from heart failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pneumonia; pus; low pH + low glucose + bacteria = empyema

### D) High triglycerides; milky fluid; chylomicrons

This describes chylothorax due to lymphatic obstruction (e.g., lymphoma or trauma), unrelated to CHF or Light’s criteria classification.

**Buzzword Combo:** Lymphatic obstruction; milky pleural fluid; chylomicrons = chylothorax
