## Buzzword Question

A young patient has hypertension, hypokalemia, metabolic alkalosis, suppressed plasma renin activity, and low aldosterone levels due to constitutively active epithelial sodium channels in the collecting duct. What is the diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

 Hypertension ; hypokalemia ; low renin ; low aldosterone ; ENaC activation

## Answer Options

 A) Primary hyperaldosteronism
 B) Liddle syndrome
 C) Cushing syndrome
 D) Renal artery stenosis


## Correct Answer

B) Liddle syndrome


## Explanation

Liddle syndrome is a form of pseudohyperaldosteronism caused by constitutive activation of epithelial sodium channels, resulting in sodium retention, hypertension, hypokalemia, low renin, and low aldosterone.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Low aldosterone + ENaC activation + hypertension = Liddle syndrome


## Wrong Options

###  A) Primary hyperaldosteronism


Primary hyperaldosteronism causes elevated aldosterone levels rather than suppressed aldosterone.


**Buzzword Combo:** High aldosterone + low renin + hypokalemia = Primary hyperaldosteronism


### C) Cushing syndrome


Cushing syndrome presents with cortisol excess causing truncal obesity, hyperglycemia, and skin changes.


**Buzzword Combo:** Purple striae + moon facies + hyperglycemia = Cushing syndrome


###  D) Renal artery stenosis


Renal artery stenosis activates RAAS, causing elevated renin and secondary hyperaldosteronism.


**Buzzword Combo:** Abdominal bruit + high renin + hypertension = Renal artery stenosis
