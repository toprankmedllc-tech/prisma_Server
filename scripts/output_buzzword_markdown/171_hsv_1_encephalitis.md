## Buzzword Question

A 50-year-old immunocompetent man develops fever, severe headache, confusion, and bizarre behavior over 2 days. MRI shows asymmetric T2 hyperintensity with petechial hemorrhage involving the medial temporal lobe and adjacent inferior frontal cortex. The most likely causative organism is:

## Buzzwords Used

Acute febrile encephalopathy; behavioral/personality change; temporal lobe involvement; petechial hemorrhage on MRI

## Answer Options

A) Neisseria meningitidis
B) Herpes simplex virus type 1
C) JC virus
D) Cryptococcus neoformans

## Correct Answer

B) Herpes simplex virus type 1

## Explanation

HSV-1 has a striking predilection for the medial temporal and inferior frontal lobes (the limbic system), producing hemorrhagic necrotizing encephalitis with prominent behavioral and memory disturbance.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Acute fever + behavioral change + temporal lobe T2 hyperintensity + petechial hemorrhage = HSV-1 encephalitis

## Wrong Options

### Neisseria meningitidis

N. meningitidis causes bacterial MENINGITIS with neck stiffness, neutrophilic CSF pleocytosis, low glucose, and a petechial/purpuric SKIN rash, not focal temporal lobe brain hemorrhage.

**Buzzword Combo:** Dorm resident + neutrophilic CSF + purpuric rash + gram-negative diplococci = Neisseria meningitidis

### JC virus

JC virus reactivation causes progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy in immunocompromised patients (HIV, natalizumab), with multifocal non-enhancing WHITE MATTER lesions, not acute hemorrhagic temporal lobe disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** HIV / natalizumab + multifocal non-enhancing white matter lesions = JC virus → PML

### Cryptococcus neoformans

Cryptococcal meningoencephalitis affects immunocompromised patients (HIV with CD4 <100); CSF shows budding yeast on India ink stain and a positive cryptococcal antigen — not temporal lobe hemorrhage.

**Buzzword Combo:** HIV + headache + India ink budding yeast + soap-bubble brain lesions = Cryptococcus neoformans
