## Buzzword Question

A young woman with easy bruising, petechiae on her lower extremities after a recent viral illness, and isolated thrombocytopenia with normal PT and aPTT most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

young woman; petechiae; viral illness; isolated thrombocytopenia; normal PT; normal aPTT

## Answer Options

A) Immune thrombocytopenic purpura
B) Hemophilia A
C) Disseminated intravascular coagulation
D) von Willebrand disease

## Correct Answer

A) Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

## Explanation

A. Immune thrombocytopenic purpuraImmune thrombocytopenic purpura is an autoimmune condition where IgG antibodies target platelet membrane glycoproteins, leading to splenic destruction of platelets. It classically presents after a viral infection with petechiae and isolated thrombocytopenia while PT and aPTT remain normal.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Viral illness + petechiae + isolated thrombocytopenia + normal PT/aPTT = ITP

## Wrong Options

### B. Hemophilia A

Hemophilia A causes factor VIII deficiency leading to deep tissue bleeding and hemarthrosis with isolated prolonged aPTT. Platelet count is normal, so isolated thrombocytopenia is not seen.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hemarthrosis + prolonged aPTT + normal platelets = Hemophilia A

### C. Disseminated intravascular coagulation

DIC causes consumption of platelets and coagulation factors leading to prolonged PT and aPTT, low fibrinogen, and elevated D-dimer. It is not an isolated thrombocytopenia disorder.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sepsis + low fibrinogen + ↑D-dimer + prolonged PT/aPTT = DIC

### D. von Willebrand disease

von Willebrand disease causes defective platelet adhesion with mucocutaneous bleeding and normal platelet count. It does not cause isolated thrombocytopenia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mucosal bleeding + normal platelets + prolonged bleeding time = vWD
