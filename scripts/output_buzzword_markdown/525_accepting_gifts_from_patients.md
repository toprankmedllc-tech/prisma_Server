## Buzzword Question

A patient who has been under the care of a family physician for several years brings homemade baked goods during a holiday follow-up visit. She expresses appreciation for the care she has received over time and insists the physician take them home.

Which of the following is the most appropriate physician response?

## Buzzwords Used

Long-term patient relationship ; Gift offering ; Gratitude expression ; Primary care continuity

## Answer Options

A) Politely accept the gift and thank the patient
B) Decline the gift because accepting any gift from patients is unethical
C) Report the interaction to the medical board
D) Accept the gift only after documenting it in the medical record

## Correct Answer

A) Politely accept the gift and thank the patient

## Explanation

In long-standing therapeutic relationships, modest gifts offered as expressions of gratitude may be accepted when they do not affect clinical judgment or create dependency or coercion.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Long-term care relationship + modest gift + gratitude expression = acceptable acceptance

## Wrong Options

### B) Decline the gift because accepting any gift from patients is unethical

Ethical guidelines do not prohibit all gifts; context and value matter.

**Buzzword Combo:** Patient gratitude + blanket prohibition + ethical misconception = incorrect absolutism

### C) Report the interaction to the medical board

No regulatory reporting is required for appropriate, low-value gratitude gifts.

**Buzzword Combo:** Normal gift + no coercion + unnecessary escalation = inappropriate response

### D) Accept the gift only after documenting it in the medical record

Documentation is not routinely required for minor gifts in standard clinical settings.

**Buzzword Combo:** Minor gift + routine visit + unnecessary formal documentation = overmedicalisation
