## Buzzword Question

A 74-year-old man with systolic heart failure (EF 26%) and atrial fibrillation presents with 3 days of loss of appetite, persistent nausea, and a complaint that the world appears to have a yellowish tint. He takes carvedilol, sacubitril/valsartan, furosemide, spironolactone, and digoxin. Heart rate is 38/min and blood pressure is 94/60 mmHg. ECG shows first-degree AV block and frequent premature ventricular complexes.
Which of the following medications is most likely responsible for this presentation?

A) Carvedilol 
B) Furosemide 
C) Sacubitril/valsartan 
D) Digoxin

## Buzzwords Used

Systolic HF and AFib; yellowish tint; digoxin; AV block and PVCs

## Answer Options

A) Carvedilol 
B) Furosemide 
C) Sacubitril/valsartan 
D) Digoxin

## Correct Answer

D) Digoxin

## Explanation

The combination of anorexia, nausea, xanthopsia (yellow-green visual disturbance), bradycardia, PR prolongation, and ventricular ectopy constitutes the classic toxicity triad of digoxin. Among all medications listed, only digoxin produces this specific pattern of gastrointestinal, visual, and cardiac toxicity.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Anorexia/nausea + xanthopsia + bradycardia + PR prolongation + PVCs = Digoxin toxicity

## Wrong Options

### A) Carvedilol 

Carvedilol causes bradycardia and fatigue through beta-adrenergic and alpha-1 blockade but does not produce visual disturbances or the gastrointestinal symptoms described here. 

**Buzzword Combo:** Bradycardia + bronchospasm + fatigue + hypoglycemia + hypotension = Beta-blocker toxicity 

### B) Furosemide 

Furosemide causes electrolyte disturbances and volume depletion. It does not cause visual disturbances, AV block, or ventricular ectopy directly. 

**Buzzword Combo:** Hypokalemia + ototoxicity + hypovolemia + metabolic alkalosis = Furosemide toxicity 

### C) Sacubitril/valsartan 

Sacubitril/valsartan can cause hypotension, hyperkalemia, and angioedema from increased bradykinin. It does not cause xanthopsia, bradycardia, or AV conduction disturbances

**Buzzword Combo:** Sacubitril + valsartan = improves survival in HF patients
