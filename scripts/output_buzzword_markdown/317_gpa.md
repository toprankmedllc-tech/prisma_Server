## Buzzword Question

A patient develops hematuria, renal biopsy demonstrates crescent formation with minimal immune complex deposition, and serology is positive for c-ANCA. Which of the following diseases is most strongly associated with this presentation?

## Buzzwords Used

c-ANCA; Hematuria; Crescents

## Answer Options

A) Granulomatosis with polyangiitis
B) Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis
C) Lupus nephritis
D) Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis

## Correct Answer

A) Granulomatosis with polyangiitis

## Explanation

Granulomatosis with polyangiitis is a necrotizing vasculitis associated with positive c-ANCA (PR3-ANCA) antibodies. Patients commonly develop rapidly progressive crescentic glomerulonephritis with little or no immune complex deposition on immunofluorescence, producing a pauci-immune pattern.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

c-ANCA + pauci-immune crescents + RPGN = Granulomatosis with polyangiitis

## Wrong Options

### Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis

Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis is an immune complex–mediated nephritic syndrome associated with low complement levels and subepithelial hump-shaped deposits after streptococcal infection.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recent strep infection + low C3 + subepithelial humps = PSGN

### Lupus nephritis

Lupus nephritis is caused by immune complex deposition producing a “full-house” immunofluorescence pattern and wire-loop capillary thickening. Patients commonly have positive ANA and anti-dsDNA antibodies.

**Buzzword Combo:** SLE + wire-loop lesions + full-house immunofluorescence = Lupus nephritis

### Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis

Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis is a small-vessel vasculitis associated with asthma, eosinophilia, and p-ANCA positivity

**Buzzword Combo:** Asthma + eosinophilia + p-ANCA = EGPA
