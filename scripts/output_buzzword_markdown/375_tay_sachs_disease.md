## Buzzword Question

An Ashkenazi Jewish infant develops developmental regression, exaggerated startle response, hypotonia, and a cherry-red macula without hepatosplenomegaly. What is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Ashkenazi Jewish; cherry-red macula; hyperacusis; developmental regression; no hepatomegaly


## Answer Options

 A) Tay-Sachs disease
 B) Niemann-Pick disease
 C) Gaucher disease
 D) Krabbe disease


## Correct Answer

A. Tay-Sachs disease


## Explanation

Tay-Sachs disease is caused by hexosaminidase A deficiency and classically presents with developmental regression, hyperacusis, cherry-red macula, and absence of hepatosplenomegaly.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Cherry-red spot + hyperacusis + no hepatomegaly = Tay-Sachs disease

## Wrong Options

### B. Niemann-Pick disease


Niemann-Pick disease also causes a cherry-red spot but is associated with hepatosplenomegaly.


**Buzzword Combo:** Cherry-red spot + hepatosplenomegaly + sphingomyelinase deficiency = Niemann-Pick disease


### C. Gaucher disease


Gaucher disease presents with hepatosplenomegaly, pancytopenia, and bone pain rather than hyperacusis.


**Buzzword Combo:** Bone crises + hepatosplenomegaly + glucocerebrosidase deficiency = Gaucher disease


### D. Krabbe disease


Krabbe disease presents with developmental regression and peripheral neuropathy but not a cherry-red macula.


**Buzzword Combo:** Peripheral neuropathy + globoid cells + galactocerebrosidase deficiency = Krabbe disease
