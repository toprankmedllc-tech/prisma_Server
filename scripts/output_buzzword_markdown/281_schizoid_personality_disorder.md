## Buzzword Question

A 38-year-old male is encouraged to visit a physician by his sister, who is concerned about his extreme social isolation. The patient works from home as a night-shift computer programmer, has zero close friends, and explicitly states that he has absolutely no desire for meaningful relationships or sexual partners. He appears completely indifferent to both praise and criticism during the interview, maintaining a flat affect throughout. He denies hallucinations, delusions, or a family history of schizophrenia. Which of the following features most reliably distinguishes this patient's presentation from Avoidant Personality Disorder?

## Buzzwords Used

Works night shift alone; No desire for relationships; Indifferent to praise or criticism; Flat affect

## Answer Options

A) Lack of desire for close relationships
B) Presence of severe social anxiety
C) Despair over his lack of social connections
D) Occupational impairment due to poor focus

## Correct Answer

A) Lack of desire for close relationships

## Explanation

This patient's presentation is classic for Schizoid Personality Disorder, characterized by voluntary social withdrawal and a restricted range of emotional expression. The core feature distinguishing it from Avoidant Personality Disorder is that schizoid individuals genuinely prefer to be alone and lack a desire for relationships, whereas individuals with avoidant personality disorder actively desire social connection but avoid it due to intense fears of rejection and inadequacy.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Indifferent to praise/criticism + No desire for relationships + Flat affect = Lack of desire for close relationships (Schizoid)

## Wrong Options

### B) Presence of severe social anxiety

Severe social anxiety and fear of embarrassment are hallmark characteristics of Avoidant Personality Disorder, not Schizoid Personality Disorder, where patients are typically content with their isolation and emotionally detached.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fear of rejection + Desires relationships + Hypersensitive to criticism = Avoidant Personality Disorder

### C) Despair over his lack of social connections

Despair or distress over loneliness is common in patients with Avoidant or Dependent personality disorders. A patient with Schizoid Personality Disorder experiences no such distress and is entirely comfortable with an isolated lifestyle.

**Buzzword Combo:** Distress over isolation + Loneliness + Fear of inadequacy = Despair over lack of connections

### D) Occupational impairment due to poor focus

Occupational impairment due to poor focus or disorganized thoughts would point toward Schizotypal Personality Disorder or Schizophrenia. Schizoid individuals can often hold down jobs successfully, particularly isolated ones like programming, as their cognitive faculties remain entirely intact.

**Buzzword Combo:** Disorganized thoughts + Odd beliefs + Cognitive impairment = Occupational impairment due to poor focus
