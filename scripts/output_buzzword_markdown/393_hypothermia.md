## Buzzword Question

A patient rescued from freezing temperatures develops bradycardia and an ECG showing elevated J points with prolonged PR, QRS, and QT intervals. Which ECG finding is classically associated with this condition?


## Buzzwords Used

Freezing exposure ; bradycardia ; elevated J point ; prolonged ECG intervals


## Answer Options

 A) Delta wave
 B) Osborn (J) wave
 C) ST-segment elevation
 D) Electrical alternans


## Correct Answer

 B) Osborn (J) wave


## Explanation

Osborn (J) waves are characteristic ECG findings in hypothermia and appear as elevated deflections at the J point. Hypothermia also causes prolongation of PR, QRS, and QT intervals.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

 Cold exposure + bradycardia + elevated J point = Osborn wave


## Wrong Options

###  A) Delta wave


Delta waves are associated with Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome due to ventricular preexcitation.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Short PR interval + delta wave + tachyarrhythmia = WPW syndrome


###  C) ST-segment elevation


ST-segment elevation is classically associated with acute myocardial infarction or pericarditis.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Chest pain + ST elevation + elevated troponins = STEMI


### D) Electrical alternans


Electrical alternans is commonly seen in large pericardial effusions and cardiac tamponade.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hypotension + muffled heart sounds + electrical alternans = Cardiac tamponade
