## Buzzword Question

A 62-year-old woman has 4 months of recurrent brief, electric-shock-like pain in the right cheek and jaw triggered by chewing, brushing her teeth, or a light breeze on the face. Each stab lasts 1 to 2 seconds. Between attacks her facial sensation and the rest of her neurologic examination are normal. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Older adult; brief lancinating V2-V3 distribution face pain; trigger zones (chewing, brushing teeth, light wind); normal interictal exam

## Answer Options

A) Cluster headache
B) Temporomandibular joint dysfunction
C) Trigeminal neuralgia
D) Postherpetic neuralgia

## Correct Answer

C) Trigeminal neuralgia

## Explanation

Brief, lancinating, trigger-evoked V2-V3 pain lasting 1 to 2 seconds with a normal interictal neurologic examination is the classic pattern of trigeminal neuralgia. In older adults the most common underlying cause is vascular loop compression of the CN V root at the pontine entry zone.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Older adult + brief lancinating V2-V3 face pain + trigger zones + normal interictal exam = classic trigeminal neuralgia

## Wrong Options

### Cluster headache

Cluster headache produces 15 to 180 minute strictly unilateral periorbital attacks with ipsilateral autonomic features (tearing, rhinorrhea, ptosis, miosis) and restlessness, not 1 to 2 second triggered stabs.

**Buzzword Combo:** Brief unilateral periorbital pain + ipsilateral autonomic features + circa-annual clusters + pacing = cluster headache

### Temporomandibular joint dysfunction

TMJ dysfunction causes dull preauricular pain reproducible by palpation over the TMJ, often with clicking and limited jaw opening, not paroxysmal trigger-zone-evoked stabbing pain.

**Buzzword Combo:** Preauricular pain + clicking + limited jaw opening + tender TMJ = TMJ dysfunction

### Postherpetic neuralgia

Postherpetic neuralgia produces PERSISTENT BURNING dysesthetic pain in a previously affected dermatome after shingles, not brief trigger-evoked stabs without a preceding rash.

**Buzzword Combo:** Persistent burning dermatomal pain + prior vesicular rash = postherpetic neuralgia
