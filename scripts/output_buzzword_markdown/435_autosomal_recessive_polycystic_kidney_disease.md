## Buzzword Question

An infant develops enlarged bilateral kidneys and progressive renal dysfunction. Imaging demonstrated bilateral renal cysts. Which of the following extrarenal manifestations is most strongly associated with this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Infant; Cystic kidneys; Hepatic fibrosis

## Answer Options

A) Congenital hepatic fibrosis
B) Berry aneurysm
C) Mitral valve prolapse
D) Colonic diverticulosis

## Correct Answer

A) Congenital hepatic fibrosis

## Explanation

Autosomal recessive polycystic kidney disease is caused by mutations in the PKHD1 gene and commonly presents during infancy or childhood with enlarged cystic kidneys and congenital hepatic fibrosis due to biliary duct abnormalities.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Infantile cystic kidneys + biliary abnormalities + hepatic fibrosis = ARPKD

## Wrong Options

### Berry aneurysm

Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease is classically associated with intracranial berry aneurysms and subarachnoid hemorrhage. These aneurysms result from vascular wall weakness associated with connective tissue abnormalities.

**Buzzword Combo:** Adult polycystic kidneys + berry aneurysm + subarachnoid hemorrhage = ADPKD

### Mitral valve prolapse

Mitral valve prolapse is commonly associated with autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease. Patients may develop midsystolic click and late systolic murmur due to valvular leaflet prolapse.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mitral valve prolapse + connective tissue abnormality + ADPKD = Valvular disease

### Colonic diverticulosis

Colonic diverticulosis may occur in adults with autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease due to connective tissue abnormalities. It is not a classic feature of autosomal recessive disease presenting in infancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Adult polycystic kidneys + connective tissue defects + diverticulosis = ADPKD
