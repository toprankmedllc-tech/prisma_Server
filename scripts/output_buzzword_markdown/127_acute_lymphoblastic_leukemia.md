## Buzzword Question

A 5-year-old child with fatigue, fever, easy bruising, anemia, thrombocytopenia, and circulating lymphoblasts undergoes immunophenotyping. Which marker is most characteristic of this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

5-year-old child; fatigue; fever; easy bruising; anemia; thrombocytopenia; circulating lymphoblasts; immunophenotyping

## Answer Options

A) CD20
B) TdT
C) CD15
D) Myeloperoxidase (MPO)

## Correct Answer

B. TdT

## Explanation

TdT (terminal deoxynucleotidyl transferase) is a DNA polymerase expressed in immature pre-B and pre-T lymphoblasts and is a hallmark marker of acute lymphoblastic leukemia. It helps distinguish ALL from mature lymphoid neoplasms and AML.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

TdT + lymphoblasts + pediatric leukemia = ALL

## Wrong Options

### A) CD20

CD20 is a marker of mature B cells and B-cell lymphomas such as CLL and diffuse large B-cell lymphoma. It is not expressed in immature lymphoblasts of ALL.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD20 + mature B cells = B-cell lymphoma/CLL

### C) CD15

CD15 is expressed on Reed-Sternberg cells in classical Hodgkin lymphoma along with CD30. It is not a marker of ALL.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD15 + CD30 + Reed-Sternberg cells = Hodgkin lymphoma

### D) Myeloperoxidase (MPO)

MPO is a marker of myeloid lineage and is positive in acute myeloid leukemia. ALL is MPO-negative.

**Buzzword Combo:** MPO positive + Auer rods = AML
