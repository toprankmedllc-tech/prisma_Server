## Buzzword Question

A 70-year-old man with headache, dizziness, blurred vision, recurrent nosebleeds, dilated retinal veins on fundoscopy, and markedly increased serum viscosity most likely has elevation of which immunoglobulin?

## Buzzwords Used

headache; dizziness; blurred vision; epistaxis; retinal vein dilation; increased serum viscosity

## Answer Options

A. IgA
B. IgM
C. IgG
D. IgE

## Correct Answer

B. IgM

## Explanation

IgM is a large pentameric immunoglobulin that remains intravascular and significantly increases serum viscosity when elevated. This leads to hyperviscosity syndrome seen in Waldenström macroglobulinemia.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pentameric IgM + hyperviscosity + retinal hemorrhages = Waldenström macroglobulinemia

## Wrong Options

### A. IgA

IgA is mainly a mucosal antibody and does not typically cause hyperviscosity syndrome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Dimeric IgA + mucosal immunity = IgA function

### C. IgG

IgG is a monomeric immunoglobulin commonly elevated in multiple myeloma but does not usually cause hyperviscosity due to its smaller size.

**Buzzword Combo:** IgG M protein + CRAB features = Multiple myeloma

### D. IgE

IgE is involved in type I hypersensitivity reactions and parasite defense, not hyperviscosity syndromes.

**Buzzword Combo:** IgE + mast cell degranulation = Allergy/anaphylaxis
