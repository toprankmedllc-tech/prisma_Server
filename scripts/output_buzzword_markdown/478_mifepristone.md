## Buzzword Question

A 24-year-old woman presents to the clinic requesting termination of an early unintended pregnancy. Ultrasound confirms an intrauterine pregnancy at 7 weeks gestation. She is given a medication that competitively blocks progesterone receptors and is then instructed to take another medication that induces uterine contractions. Which of the following drugs was most likely prescribed first?

## Buzzwords Used

Early pregnancy termination ; progesterone receptor antagonist ; medical abortion ; followed by prostaglandin analog ; uterine contractions ; first trimester

## Answer Options

A) Levonorgestrel
B) Mifepristone
C) Clomiphene
D) Medroxyprogesterone

## Correct Answer

B) Mifepristone

## Explanation

Mifepristone is a progesterone receptor antagonist used for medical abortion. It is commonly administered with Misoprostol, which induces uterine contractions to expel products of conception.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Early pregnancy termination + progesterone receptor blockade + followed by prostaglandin = Mifepristone

## Wrong Options

### A) Levonorgestrel

Levonorgestrel is primarily used for contraception and emergency contraception. It does not terminate an established intrauterine pregnancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Emergency contraception + progestin + prevention of ovulation = Levonorgestrel

### C) Clomiphene

Clomiphene induces ovulation by blocking estrogen receptors in the hypothalamus and increasing LH/FSH release.

**Buzzword Combo:** Infertility + PCOS + ovulation induction = Clomiphene

### D) Medroxyprogesterone

Medroxyprogesterone is used for contraception and abnormal uterine bleeding. It supports progesterone activity rather than blocking it.

**Buzzword Combo:** Depot injection + contraception + thickened cervical mucus = Medroxyprogesterone
