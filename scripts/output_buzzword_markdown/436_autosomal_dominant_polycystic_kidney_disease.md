## Buzzword Question

Which of the following complications is most likely to occur in a patient with autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease?

## Buzzwords Used

Complications; ADKD

## Answer Options

A. Progressive hypertension due to increased renin secretion
 B. Hypercalcemia due to parathyroid hyperplasia
 C. Hemarthroses due to factor VIII deficiency
 D. Restrictive cardiomyopathy due to amyloid deposition

## Correct Answer

A. Progressive hypertension due to increased renin secretion

## Explanation

Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease commonly causes progressive hypertension due to cyst enlargement compressing renal vasculature and activating the renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

renal cysts + renin activation + Hypertension = ADPKD

## Wrong Options

### Hypercalcemia due to parathyroid hyperplasia

Parathyroid hyperplasia is more commonly associated with chronic kidney disease causing secondary hyperparathyroidism.

**Buzzword Combo:** CKD + elevated PTH = Secondary hyperparathyroidism

### Hemarthroses due to factor VIII deficiency

Factor VIII deficiency causes hemophilia A, which presents with recurrent joint bleeding.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hemarthrosis + factor VIII deficiency = Hemophilia A

###  Restrictive cardiomyopathy due to amyloid deposition

Amyloid deposition can cause restrictive cardiomyopathy in systemic amyloidosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Amyloid deposition + restrictive heart disease = Amyloidosis
