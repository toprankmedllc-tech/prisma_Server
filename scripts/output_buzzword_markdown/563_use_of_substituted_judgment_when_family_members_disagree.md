## Buzzword Question

A 60-year-old man with end-stage metastatic cancer is unconscious in the ICU and lacks decision-making capacity. There is no advance directive available. The medical team recommends transitioning to comfort-focused care. His spouse believes he would not want further invasive treatment in this situation. However, his adult daughter strongly disagrees and requests continued chemotherapy and ICU-level support. A close lifelong friend recalls prior conversations in which the patient expressed fear of “being kept alive on machines with no real recovery.”

Which of the following is the most appropriate next step for the physician?

## Buzzwords Used

Spouse ; Adult daughter ; Lifelong friend ; No advance directive ; Patient preferences

## Answer Options

A) Follow the adult daughter’s request since she is a direct descendant

B) Base the decision on substituted judgment using the most consistent and credible account of the patient’s prior wishes

C) Continue all treatment until the hospital ethics committee reaches a formal ruling

D) Transfer decision-making authority to the eldest available family member

## Correct Answer

B) Base the decision on substituted judgment using the most consistent and credible account of the patient’s prior wishes

## Explanation

When prior preferences can be reasonably determined, physicians should use substituted judgment to honor the patient’s values and treatment goals.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Prior statements + consistent reports + lack of capacity = substituted judgment

## Wrong Options

### A) Follow the adult daughter’s request since she is a direct descendant

Being a direct descendant does not automatically grant decision-making authority over known patient preferences.

**Buzzword Combo:** No spouse + no advance directive + adult child highest available surrogate = adult child decision-maker

### C) Continue all treatment until the hospital ethics committee reaches a formal ruling

Ethics consultation may assist difficult cases but is not routinely required before treatment decisions can be made.

**Buzzword Combo:** Persistent unresolved dispute + uncertain patient wishes + conflict among surrogates = ethics consultation

### D) Transfer decision-making authority to the eldest available family member

Age does not determine surrogate authority.

**Buzzword Combo:** No spouse + no children + parents unavailable + oldest sibling as highest-ranked surrogate = sibling decision-maker
