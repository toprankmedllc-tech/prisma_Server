## Buzzword Question

A 22-year-old male athlete is diagnosed with HOCM following sudden cardiac death in a first-degree relative. Genetic counseling is initiated. Which of the following best describes the inheritance pattern and most commonly implicated gene in this condition?

A) X-linked recessive; dystrophin gene mutation 
B) Autosomal dominant; beta-myosin heavy chain mutation 
C) Autosomal recessive; titin (TTN) gene mutation 
D) Mitochondrial inheritance; MT-ND1 gene mutation

## Buzzwords Used

HOCM

## Answer Options

A) X-linked recessive; dystrophin gene mutation 
B) Autosomal dominant; beta-myosin heavy chain mutation 
C) Autosomal recessive; titin (TTN) gene mutation 
D) Mitochondrial inheritance; MT-ND1 gene mutation

## Correct Answer

B) Autosomal dominant; beta-myosin heavy chain mutation 

## Explanation

HOCM is inherited in an autosomal dominant pattern with variable penetrance and expressivity. The two most commonly mutated genes are beta-myosin heavy chain and myosin-binding protein C, both encoding sarcomeric proteins. A family member has a 50% chance of inheriting the mutation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

HOCM + autosomal dominant + sarcomere mutation = beta-myosin heavy chain or myosin-binding protein C

## Wrong Options

### A) X-linked recessive; dystrophin 

Dystrophin mutations cause Duchenne/Becker muscular dystrophy, which can cause a dilated cardiomyopathy, not hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy. 

**Buzzword Combo:** Dystrophin mutation + X-linked + dilated cardiomyopathy = Duchenne muscular dystrophy

### C) Autosomal recessive; titin 

Titin (TTN) mutations are the most common cause of familial dilated cardiomyopathy, not HOCM. HOCM is autosomal dominant, not recessive. 

**Buzzword Combo:** TTN mutation + dilated cardiomyopathy + autosomal dominant = Familial dilated cardiomyopathy

### D) Mitochondrial; MT-ND1 

Mitochondrial inheritance is associated with conditions like MELAS and Leber hereditary optic neuropathy. While mitochondrial cardiomyopathy can cause hypertrophy, HOCM as classically defined is a sarcomeric protein disorder with autosomal dominant inheritance. 

**Buzzword Combo:** Mitochondrial inheritance + myopathy + lactic acidosis = MELAS syndrome
