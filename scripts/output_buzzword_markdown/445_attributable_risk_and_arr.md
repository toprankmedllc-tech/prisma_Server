## Buzzword Question

A 10-year cohort study finds that the incidence of myocardial infarction is 15% in patients with untreated hypertension and 5% in normotensive controls matched for age, sex, and smoking status. The attributable risk of hypertension for myocardial infarction is:

## Buzzwords Used

 Incidence in hypertensives 15%; incidence in normotensives 5%; cohort study; MI; attributable risk = absolute risk difference

## Answer Options

A) 10% 
B) 3 (relative risk) 
C) 67% (attributable risk fraction) 
D) 5% (incidence in unexposed)

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Attributable risk (AR) = absolute risk difference = risk in exposed − risk in unexposed = 15% − 5% = 10%. This represents the excess risk of MI attributable specifically to hypertension, above the baseline risk of MI that exists even without hypertension. It answers: 'How much additional risk does hypertension contribute?'

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Risk in exposed − risk in unexposed = Attributable risk (absolute risk difference) = excess risk due to exposure

## Wrong Options

### B) 3 (relative risk)

RR = risk in exposed / risk in unexposed = 15%/5% = 3. This is a relative measure (hypertensives have 3× the risk). Attributable risk is an absolute measure = 15% − 5% = 10%, not the ratio.

**Buzzword Combo:** Risk exposed / risk unexposed = Relative risk = multiplicative measure (RR = 3 here)


### C) 67% (attributable risk fraction)

Attributable risk fraction (etiologic fraction) = AR / risk in exposed = 10%/15% = 67%. This represents the proportion of MI risk in hypertensive patients attributable to hypertension itself. It is different from the attributable risk (absolute excess = 10%).

**Buzzword Combo:** AR / risk in exposed = Attributable risk fraction = proportion of exposed risk due to exposure

###  D) 5% (incidence in unexposed)

5% is the background incidence of MI in normotensive controls  the baseline risk that exists independent of hypertension. The attributable risk requires subtracting this background rate from the exposed rate: 15% − 5% = 10%.


**Buzzword Combo:** Background incidence (unexposed) = baseline risk; attributable risk = exposed risk − background risk
