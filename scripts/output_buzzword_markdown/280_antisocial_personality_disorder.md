## Buzzword Question

A 21-year-old male is brought to the clinic by court order for a psychiatric evaluation after being arrested for grand theft auto. Review of his records reveals a history of multiple school suspensions for physical fights, cruelty to neighborhood animals at age 12, and a total lack of remorse for his actions, stating, "If they left their keys in the car, they deserved to have it stolen." He is charming during the interview but frequently lies about his past employment. Which of the following childhood diagnoses must have been present before age 15 to fulfill the criteria for this patient's adult condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Court order evaluation; Cruelty to animals at age 12; Lack of remorse; History of physical fights

## Answer Options

A) Oppositional Defiant Disorder
B) Conduct Disorder
C) Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder
D) Intermittent Explosive Disorder

## Correct Answer

B) Conduct Disorder

## Explanation

This patient presents with Antisocial Personality Disorder (ASPD), characterized by a pervasive pattern of disregard for and violation of the rights of others occurring since age 15. To meet formal diagnostic criteria for ASPD, there must be clear evidence of Conduct Disorder with onset before age 15, which is highlighted by his childhood history of animal cruelty and physical aggression.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Cruelty to animals + Aggression before age 15 + Violation of rules/laws = Conduct Disorder

## Wrong Options

### A) Oppositional Defiant Disorder

Oppositional Defiant Disorder (ODD) involves a pattern of angry/irritable mood and defiant behavior toward authority figures, but it does not include severe aggression toward people or animals, destruction of property, or a pattern of theft, which are required for a history of Conduct Disorder.

**Buzzword Combo:** Defiance toward authority + Argumentative + No physical aggression/theft = Oppositional Defiant Disorder

### C) Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder

Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is characterized by inattention, hyperactivity, and impulsivity. While it is frequently comorbid with conduct issues, it is not a prerequisite diagnostic criterion for establishing a diagnosis of Antisocial Personality Disorder.

**Buzzword Combo:** Inattention + Hyperactivity + Impulsivity = Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder

### D) Intermittent Explosive Disorder

Intermittent Explosive Disorder involves recurrent behavioral outbursts representing a failure to control aggressive impulses, but these outbursts are unpremeditated and are not part of a pervasive pattern of antisocial behavior, lack of remorse, or childhood conduct rule-breaking.

**Buzzword Combo:** Unprovoked aggressive outbursts + Grossly disproportionate + Lack of general antisocial traits = Intermittent Explosive Disorder
