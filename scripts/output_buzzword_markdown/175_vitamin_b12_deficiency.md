## Buzzword Question

A 60-year-old man with fatigue, numbness and tingling in his feet, difficulty with balance, macrocytic anemia, and hypersegmented neutrophils most likely has which deficiency?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; numbness and tingling; balance difficulty; macrocytic anemia; hypersegmented neutrophils; neurologic symptoms

## Answer Options

A) Folate
B) Vitamin B12
C) Iron
D) Vitamin B6

## Correct Answer

B. Vitamin B12

## Explanation

Vitamin B12 deficiency causes megaloblastic anemia with hypersegmented neutrophils and neurologic dysfunction due to impaired myelin synthesis. Neurologic findings such as paresthesias and gait instability help distinguish it from folate deficiency.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Neurologic deficits + macrocytosis + hypersegmented neutrophils = Vitamin B12 deficiency

## Wrong Options

### A. Folate

Folate deficiency also causes megaloblastic anemia and hypersegmented neutrophils but does not produce neurologic symptoms. Methylmalonic acid levels remain normal.

**Buzzword Combo:** Macrocytosis + no neurologic symptoms + normal MMA = Folate deficiency

### C. Iron

Iron deficiency causes microcytic hypochromic anemia with low ferritin and high TIBC, not macrocytic anemia or neurologic deficits.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + high TIBC + microcytosis = Iron deficiency anemia

### D. Vitamin B6

Vitamin B6 deficiency may cause neuropathy and sideroblastic anemia but does not cause megaloblastic macrocytic anemia with hypersegmented neutrophils.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sideroblastic anemia + isoniazid use + neuropathy = Vitamin B6 deficiency
