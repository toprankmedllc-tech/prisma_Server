## Buzzword Question

A patient with cocaine use disorder experiences intense euphoria due to increased dopamine release in the nucleus accumbens. The dopaminergic neurons responsible for this reward pathway primarily originate from which brain structure?

A. Substantia nigra pars compacta
B. Ventral tegmental area
C. Mammillary bodies
D. Suprachiasmatic nucleus

## Buzzwords Used

Cocaine use ; Euphoria ; Dopamine release ; Nucleus accumbens ; Reward pathway ; Addiction

## Answer Options

A) Substantia nigra pars compacta
B) Ventral tegmental area
C) Mammillary bodies
D) Suprachiasmatic nucleus

## Correct Answer

B. Ventral tegmental area

## Explanation

The Ventral Tegmental Area is the origin of the mesolimbic dopaminergic pathway, which projects to the nucleus accumbens and mediates reward, pleasure, and addiction. Drugs such as cocaine increase dopamine signaling in this pathway, producing euphoria.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Cocaine + nucleus accumbens + reward pathway = Ventral tegmental area

## Wrong Options

### A. Substantia nigra pars compacta

The substantia nigra pars compacta is part of the nigrostriatal pathway involved in movement regulation. Degeneration here is associated with Parkinson Disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Resting tremor + rigidity + bradykinesia = Substantia nigra

### C. Mammillary bodies

The mammillary bodies are involved in memory processing and are classically damaged in Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome due to thiamine deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Confabulation + alcoholism + memory loss = Mammillary bodies

### D. Suprachiasmatic nucleus

The suprachiasmatic nucleus regulates circadian rhythm and sleep-wake cycles. It is not part of the dopaminergic reward system.

**Buzzword Combo:** Circadian rhythm + sleep cycle + hypothalamus = Suprachiasmatic nucleus
