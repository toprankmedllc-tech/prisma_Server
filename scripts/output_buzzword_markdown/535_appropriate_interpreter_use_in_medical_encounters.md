## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old man presents to the emergency department with chest pain. He recently moved to the country and speaks very limited English. His teenage son attempts to answer questions on his behalf while the physician conducts the interview. A certified telephone interpreter is immediately available.

Which of the following is the most appropriate physician action?

## Buzzwords Used

Limited English proficiency ; Family interpreter ; Emergency department ; Professional interpreter

## Answer Options

A) Continue using the son as interpreter because he knows the patient best

B) Use the professional interpreter service and speak directly to the patient

C) Ask only yes-or-no questions in English to simplify communication

D) Delay the interview until an in-person interpreter arrives later in the day

## Correct Answer

B) Use the professional interpreter service and speak directly to the patient

## Explanation

Professionally trained interpreters improve communication accuracy, confidentiality, and informed decision-making. Physicians should address the patient directly rather than communicating primarily through relatives.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Language barrier + family translation + interpreter available = use professional interpreter

## Wrong Options

### A) Continue using the son as interpreter because he knows the patient best

Family members may inaccurately translate information or filter sensitive details.

**Buzzword Combo:** Relative translator + complex discussion + communication risk = inappropriate interpretation

### C) Ask only yes-or-no questions in English to simplify communication

Oversimplified questioning does not adequately address communication barriers in medical evaluation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Limited English + reduced questioning + inadequate communication = incomplete assessment

### D) Delay the interview until an in-person interpreter arrives later in the day

Telephone or video interpretation is appropriate when immediate professional interpretation is needed.

**Buzzword Combo:** Available interpreter service + unnecessary delay + impaired care access = incorrect management
