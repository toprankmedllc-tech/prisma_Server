## Buzzword Question

A child has eye blinking, shoulder shrugging, throat clearing, and grunting noises for more than 1 year. Symptoms worsen with stress and are preceded by a premonitory urge. Which is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

 Motor tics; vocal tics; premonitory urge; suppressible; worsening with stress; >1 year


## Answer Options

 A) Tourette syndrome
 B) Persistent motor tic disorder
 C) Provisional tic disorder
 D) Sydenham chorea

## Correct Answer

A. Tourette syndrome


## Explanation

Tourette syndrome requires at least 2 motor tics and 1 vocal tic persisting for more than 1 year with onset before age 18.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Motor tics + vocal tics + >1 year = Tourette syndrome

## Wrong Options

### B. Persistent motor tic disorder


Persistent motor tic disorder includes either motor or vocal tics alone, not both together.


**Buzzword Combo:** Motor tics only + >1 year + no vocal tics = Persistent motor tic disorder

### C. Provisional tic disorder


Provisional tic disorder lasts for less than 1 year.


**Buzzword Combo:** Motor/vocal tics + duration <1 year + childhood onset = Provisional tic disorder

### D. Sydenham chorea


Sydenham chorea causes involuntary choreiform movements following streptococcal infection and lacks suppressible tics.


**Buzzword Combo:** Rheumatic fever + choreiform movements + emotional lability = Sydenham chorea
