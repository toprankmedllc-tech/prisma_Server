## Buzzword Question

A patient with schizophrenia has flat affect, anhedonia, and social withdrawal. These symptoms are most strongly associated with ?


## Buzzwords Used

 Flat affect; Anhedonia; Social withdrawal 

## Answer Options

 A. Increased mesolimbic dopamine activity
 B. Decreased dopamine activity in the prefrontal cortex
 C. Increased serotonin activity in the basal ganglia
 D. Degeneration of the substantia nigra


## Correct Answer

B. Decreased dopamine activity in the prefrontal cortex


## Explanation

Negative symptoms of schizophrenia are associated with decreased dopamine activity in the prefrontal cortical (mesocortical) pathway.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Flat affect + anhedonia + social withdrawal = ↓ prefrontal dopamine


## Wrong Options

### A. Increased mesolimbic dopamine activity


This mechanism causes positive symptoms such as hallucinations and delusions.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hallucinations + delusions = ↑ mesolimbic dopamine


### C. Increased serotonin activity in the basal ganglia


Serotonin may modulate schizophrenia, but this is not the primary mechanism of negative symptoms.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Negative symptoms = mesocortical dopamine deficiency


### D. Degeneration of the substantia nigra


Degeneration of the substantia nigra is characteristic of Parkinson disease.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Resting tremor + bradykinesia = Parkinson disease
