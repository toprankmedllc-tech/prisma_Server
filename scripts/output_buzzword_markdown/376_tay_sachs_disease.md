## Buzzword Question

A child is diagnosed with Tay-Sachs disease. Which of the following best describes the underlying pathophysiology?


## Buzzwords Used

GM2 ganglioside; hexosaminidase A deficiency; neurodegeneration


## Answer Options

 A) Accumulation of GM2 ganglioside due to hexosaminidase A deficiency
 B) Accumulation of sphingomyelin due to sphingomyelinase deficiency
 C) Accumulation of glucocerebroside due to glucocerebrosidase deficiency
 D) Accumulation of galactocerebroside due to galactocerebrosidase deficiency


## Correct Answer

A. Accumulation of GM2 ganglioside due to hexosaminidase A deficiency

## Explanation

Tay-Sachs disease occurs due to deficiency of hexosaminidase A, resulting in accumulation of GM2 gangliosides within neurons and progressive neurodegeneration.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hexosaminidase A deficiency + GM2 ganglioside accumulation + neurodegeneration = Tay-Sachs disease


## Wrong Options

### B. Accumulation of sphingomyelin due to sphingomyelinase deficiency


This describes Niemann-Pick disease rather than Tay-Sachs disease.


**Buzzword Combo:** Sphingomyelin accumulation + hepatosplenomegaly + cherry-red spot = Niemann-Pick disease


### C. Accumulation of glucocerebroside due to glucocerebrosidase deficiency

This is the underlying defect in Gaucher disease.


**Buzzword Combo:** Glucocerebroside accumulation + Gaucher cells + bone pain = Gaucher disease


### D. Accumulation of galactocerebroside due to galactocerebrosidase deficiency

This mechanism is seen in Krabbe disease.


**Buzzword Combo:** Galactocerebrosidase deficiency + globoid cells + peripheral neuropathy = Krabbe disease
