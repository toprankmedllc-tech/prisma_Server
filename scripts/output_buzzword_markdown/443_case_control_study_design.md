## Buzzword Question

Investigators identify 300 patients with newly diagnosed bladder cancer and 300 patients without bladder cancer matched for age and sex. Both groups are interviewed about past occupational chemical exposures going back 20 years. The investigators then calculate an odds ratio of 4.2 (95% CI: 2.8–6.3) for aromatic amine exposure. This study design is best classified as:


## Buzzwords Used

Start with outcome (bladder cancer); look backward for exposure; controls matched; retrospective; calculate odds ratio

## Answer Options

A) Case-control study 
B) Prospective cohort study 
C) Randomized controlled trial 
D) Cross-sectional study


## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Case-control studies begin with known outcomes (cases = bladder cancer; controls = no bladder cancer) and then look backward retrospectively to determine prior exposure (aromatic amines). The effect measure is the odds ratio. Case-control designs are efficient for studying rare diseases and exposures with long latency periods.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Start with outcome (cases/controls) + look backward for exposure + calculate odds ratio = Case-control study

## Wrong Options

### B) Prospective cohort study

Cohort studies identify participants based on exposure status first (exposed vs unexposed to aromatic amines) and then follow them forward for outcomes (bladder cancer). The temporal direction is opposite: cohort = exposure → outcome; case-control = outcome → exposure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Exposure first → follow forward for outcome → calculate relative risk = Cohort study

### C) Randomized controlled trial

RCTs involve random allocation to intervention or control groups with prospective follow-up. There is no randomization, intervention, or prospective element in this retrospective exposure-assessment study.

**Buzzword Combo:** Random allocation + intervention + prospective + blinding = RCT (gold standard for causation)

### D) Cross-sectional study

Cross-sectional studies measure exposure and outcome simultaneously at a single point in time. This study uses retrospective historical exposure data and a pre-existing outcome (bladder cancer)  it spans multiple time points and uses matching, characteristic of a case-control design

**Buzzword Combo:** Exposure + outcome measured simultaneously → calculate prevalence ratio = Cross-sectional study
