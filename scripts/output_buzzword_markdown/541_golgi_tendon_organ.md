## Buzzword Question

A 26-year-old man attempts to deadlift an extremely heavy weight at the gym. During maximal contraction, his quadriceps suddenly relaxes, causing him to drop the barbell. A medical student explains that this protective reflex is mediated by receptors located within tendons that detect excessive muscle tension.
Which of the following structures and nerve fibers are responsible for this reflex?

## Buzzwords Used

mediated by receptors located within tendons that detect excessive muscle tension.

## Answer Options

A. Muscle spindle with Ia sensory fibers
B. Golgi tendon organ with type Ib sensory axons
C. Pacinian corpuscle with Aβ sensory fibers
D. Free nerve ending with C fiber

## Correct Answer

B. Golgi tendon organ with type Ib sensory axons

## Explanation

Golgi tendon organs are located in tendons and detect excessive muscle tension. They transmit signals through type Ib sensory axons, which activate inhibitory interneurons in the spinal cord and cause relaxation of the contracting muscle to prevent injury

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Excessive muscle tension + tendon receptor + type Ib fibers = Golgi tendon organ

## Wrong Options

### A.Muscle spindle with Ia sensory fibers

Detects muscle stretch and mediates deep tendon reflexes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Muscle stretch + deep tendon reflex + Ia afferent = muscle spindle

### C. Pacinian corpuscle with Aβ sensory fibers

Detects vibration and deep pressure. 

**Buzzword Combo:** Vibration sensation + deep pressure + rapidly adapting receptor = Pacinian corpuscle

### D. Free nerve ending with C fibers

Detects pain and temperature.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pain sensation + temperature detection + slow unmyelinated fibers = free nerve ending
