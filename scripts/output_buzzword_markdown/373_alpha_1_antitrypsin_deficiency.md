## Buzzword Question

A young adult with minimal smoking history develops lower-lobe panacinar emphysema and liver cirrhosis. Which condition is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Young patient; panacinar emphysema; lower-lobe emphysema; liver cirrhosis; minimal smoking history

## Answer Options

 A) Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency
 B) Chronic bronchitis
 C) Cystic fibrosis
 D) Sarcoidosis


## Correct Answer

A. Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency


## Explanation

Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency classically presents with early-onset lower-lobe panacinar emphysema and liver disease due to abnormal protein accumulation in hepatocytes.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Lower-lobe emphysema + liver disease + young nonsmoker = Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency


## Wrong Options

### B. Chronic bronchitis


Chronic bronchitis is associated with chronic productive cough and smoking-related airway inflammation.


**Buzzword Combo:** Productive cough + smoking + mucus hypersecretion = Chronic bronchitis

### C. Cystic fibrosis


Cystic fibrosis presents with recurrent pulmonary infections, pancreatic insufficiency, and bronchiectasis.


**Buzzword Combo:** Bronchiectasis + pancreatic insufficiency + recurrent infections = Cystic fibrosis

### D. Sarcoidosis

Sarcoidosis is characterized by noncaseating granulomas and bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy.


**Buzzword Combo:** Noncaseating granulomas + hilar lymphadenopathy + elevated ACE = Sarcoidosis
