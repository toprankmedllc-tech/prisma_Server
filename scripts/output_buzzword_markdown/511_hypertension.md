## Buzzword Question

Which of the following vascular findings is most characteristic of malignant hypertension affecting the kidneys?

## Buzzwords Used

Severe hypertension; Vascular Findings; Kidneys

## Answer Options

A) Hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis with “onion-skin” thickening
B) Hyaline arteriolosclerosis with gradual luminal narrowing
C) Nodular glomerulosclerosis with mesangial expansion
D) Thickened glomerular basement membrane with spike-and-dome appearance

## Correct Answer

A) Hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis with “onion-skin” thickening

## Explanation

Malignant hypertension causes severe vascular injury leading to hyperplastic arteriolosclerosis characterized by concentric “onion-skin” thickening of arteriolar walls. Fibrinoid necrosis may also occur in affected vessels

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Severe hypertension + onion-skin arterioles + fibrinoid necrosis = Malignant hypertension

## Wrong Options

### Hyaline arteriolosclerosis with gradual luminal narrowing

Benign nephrosclerosis is caused by long-standing hypertension leading to hyaline thickening of small renal arterioles and chronic renal ischemia. The process develops gradually and is associated with slowly progressive renal dysfunction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic hypertension + hyaline arterioles +Chronic  renal ischemia = Benign nephrosclerosis

### Nodular glomerulosclerosis with mesangial expansion

Diabetic nephropathy is characterized by Kimmelstiel-Wilson nodules due to chronic hyperglycemia and nonenzymatic glycosylation injury. Patients commonly develop proteinuria and progressive chronic kidney disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Diabetes mellitus + Kimmelstiel-Wilson nodules + Proteinuria= Diabetic nephropathy

### Thickened glomerular basement membrane with spike-and-dome appearance

Membranous nephropathy demonstrates subepithelial immune complex deposition producing a characteristic spike-and-dome appearance. It is a common cause of nephrotic syndrome in adult

**Buzzword Combo:** Spike-and-dome deposits + subepithelial immune complexes + nephrotic syndrome = Membranous nephropathy
