## Buzzword Question

A patient is diagnosed with clear cell renal cell carcinoma after presenting with hematuria and a renal mass. Which of the following genetic abnormalities is most strongly associated with this malignancy?

## Buzzwords Used

Hematuria; Renal mass; Genetic Abnormality

## Answer Options

A) Mutation of the APC gene on chromosome 5
B) Mutation of the CFTR gene on chromosome 7
C) Deletion of the VHL tumor suppressor gene on chromosome 3
D) Trinucleotide repeat expansion on chromosome 4

## Correct Answer

C) Deletion of the VHL tumor suppressor gene on chromosome 3

## Explanation

Clear cell renal cell carcinoma is strongly associated with loss of the VHL tumor suppressor gene on chromosome 3p. Loss of VHL function increases hypoxia-inducible factor activity, promoting angiogenesis and tumor growth through increased VEGF production.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Chromosome 3p deletion + increased VEGF + clear cell RCC = VHL mutation

## Wrong Options

### Mutation of the APC gene on chromosome 5

Familial adenomatous polyposis is caused by APC mutations and is strongly associated with numerous colonic adenomatous polyps and colorectal carcinoma. APC normally regulates β-catenin degradation within the Wnt signaling pathway.

**Buzzword Combo:** APC mutation + colon polyps + colorectal cancer = FAP

### Mutation of the CFTR gene on chromosome 7

Cystic fibrosis results from CFTR mutations causing abnormal chloride transport and thick mucus secretions. Patients commonly develop recurrent pulmonary infections, pancreatic insufficiency, and bronchiectasis.

**Buzzword Combo:** CFTR mutation + Pancreatic Insufficiency  + recurrent lung infections = Cystic fibrosis

### Trinucleotide repeat expansion on chromosome 4

Huntington disease is caused by CAG trinucleotide repeat expansion on chromosome 4. Patients classically develop chorea, psychiatric disturbances, and progressive neurodegeneration

**Buzzword Combo:** CAG repeat expansion + chorea + caudate degeneration = Huntington disease
