## Buzzword Question

A 48-year-old woman who works as a secretary presents to the clinic with progressively worsening numbness and tingling in her right hand over the past several months. She reports that the symptoms are most noticeable at night and frequently wake her from sleep. She also notices discomfort while typing for long periods and difficulty gripping objects such as coffee mugs and pens. Physical examination reveals decreased sensation over the palmar surface of the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of the ring finger. There is mild weakness of thumb opposition. Tapping over the volar aspect of the wrist reproduces tingling in the affected fingers.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Tapping over the volar aspect of the wrist reproduces tingling; weakness of thumb opposition; decreased sensation over the palmar surface of the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of the ring finger

## Answer Options

A. Carpal tunnel syndrome
B. Cubital tunnel syndrome
C. Cervical radiculopathy
D. De Quervain tenosynovitis

## Correct Answer

A. Carpal tunnel syndrome

## Explanation

Carpal tunnel syndrome is caused by compression of the median nerve within the carpal tunnel beneath the flexor retinaculum. This patient developed: Symptoms in the lateral 3½ digits ,Weakness of thumb opposition, Positive Tinel sign at the wrist .These findings are classic for carpal tunnel syndrome.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Nighttime hand numbness + tingling in first 3½ digits + positive Tinel sign = carpal tunnel syndrome

## Wrong Options

### B. Cubital tunnel syndrome

Incorrect — Compression of the ulnar nerve at the elbow causes numbness in the fourth and fifth digits and weakness of intrinsic hand muscles.

**Buzzword Combo:** Medial hand numbness + elbow compression = cubital tunnel syndrome

### C. Cervical radiculopathy

Incorrect — Cervical nerve root compression often causes neck pain radiating into the arm with dermatomal sensory deficits and weakness.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neck pain + radiating arm symptoms = cervical radiculopathy

### D. De Quervain tenosynovitis

Incorrect — Inflammation of the abductor pollicis longus and extensor pollicis brevis tendons causes pain near the radial styloid, especially with thumb movement.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thumb pain + positive Finkelstein test = De Quervain tenosynovitis
