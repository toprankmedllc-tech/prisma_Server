## Buzzword Question

Which of the following is the classic symptom triad of severe aortic stenosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Severe aortic stenosis; syncope; angina; exertional heart failure; classic triad

## Answer Options


A) Syncope, angina, and exertional heart failure (dyspnea)
B) Hemoptysis, dysphagia, and hoarseness
C) Chest pain, fever, and embolic phenomena
D) Palpitations, lightheadedness, and orthostatic hypotension

## Correct Answer


A) Syncope, angina, and exertional heart failure (dyspnea)

## Explanation


The classic symptom triad of severe aortic stenosis is syncope, angina, and exertional heart failure. Onset of any of these marks a sharp decline in survival and is an indication for valve replacement.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Syncope + angina + exertional heart failure = severe aortic stenosis (triad)

## Wrong Options

### B) Hemoptysis, dysphagia, and hoarseness

Features of long-standing mitral stenosis with massive left atrial enlargement (Ortner syndrome).

**Buzzword Combo:** Hemoptysis + dysphagia + hoarseness = mitral stenosis with LA enlargement

### C) Chest pain, fever, and embolic phenomena

Features of infective endocarditis, not aortic stenosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fever + new regurgitant murmur + embolic phenomena = infective endocarditis

### 
D) Palpitations, lightheadedness, and orthostatic hypotension

Non-specific symptoms of autonomic or arrhythmic disease — not the classic AS triad.

**Buzzword Combo:** Orthostatic hypotension + autonomic features = dysautonomia / volume depletion
