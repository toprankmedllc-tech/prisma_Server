## Buzzword Question

A patient has a congenital platelet disorder in which platelets can adhere to damaged endothelium but cannot form stable platelet-to-platelet connections. Which of the following platelet functions is most directly impaired?

## Buzzwords Used

Congenital platelet disorder; platelets can adhere to damaged endothelium; cannot form stable platelet-to-platelet connections

## Answer Options

A) Binding of von Willebrand factor to GpIb
B) Binding of fibrinogen to GpIIb/IIIa
C) Stabilization of factor VIII in plasma
D) Conversion of fibrinogen to fibrin

## Correct Answer

B) Binding of fibrinogen to GpIIb/IIIa


## Explanation

Glanzmann thrombasthenia is due to GpIIb/IIIa deficiency. GpIIb/IIIa binds fibrinogen, allowing fibrinogen bridges to link platelets during aggregation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

GpIIb/IIIa deficiency + fibrinogen bridges absent + defective platelet aggregation = Glanzmann thrombasthenia

## Wrong Options

### A) Binding of von Willebrand factor to GpIb

This describes platelet adhesion, which is defective in Bernard-Soulier syndrome. Glanzmann thrombasthenia affects platelet aggregation through GpIIb/IIIa.

**Buzzword Combo:** GpIb defect + impaired vWF binding + defective platelet adhesion = Bernard-Soulier syndrome

### C) Stabilization of factor VIII in plasma

This is a function of von Willebrand factor. It explains why von Willebrand disease can mildly prolong PTT, but it does not explain defective platelet-to-platelet aggregation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low vWF + decreased factor VIII stability + mucocutaneous bleeding = von Willebrand disease

### D) Conversion of fibrinogen to fibrin

This is part of secondary hemostasis and depends on thrombin. Glanzmann thrombasthenia is a primary hemostasis disorder involving platelet aggregation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thrombin activity + fibrinogen to fibrin + secondary hemostasis = coagulation cascade
