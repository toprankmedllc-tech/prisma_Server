## Buzzword Question

Researchers study the relationship between body mass index (BMI) and systolic blood pressure in 300 adults. BMI and blood pressure are recorded as numerical measurements for each participant.

Which statistical test is most appropriate?

## Buzzwords Used

BMI ; Systolic blood pressure ; Continuous variables ; Three outcome categories ; Original measurements analyzed

## Answer Options

A) ANOVA

B) Pearson correlation coefficient

C) Chi-square test

D) Independent samples t-test

## Correct Answer

B) Pearson correlation coefficient

## Explanation

Both BMI and systolic blood pressure are continuous variables. Pearson correlation is used to assess the relationship between two continuous measurements.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Two continuous variables + relationship assessment + numerical measurements = Pearson correlation coefficient

## Wrong Options

### A) ANOVA

ANOVA is used to compare means across three or more independent groups.

**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous outcome + three or more groups + comparison of means = ANOVA

### C) Chi-square test

Chi-square testing evaluates associations between categorical variables.

**Buzzword Combo:** Categorical exposure + categorical outcome + association testing = Chi-square test

### D) Independent samples t-test

A t-test compares means between two groups rather than assessing relationships between continuous variables.

**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous outcome + two independent groups + comparison of means = Independent samples t-test
