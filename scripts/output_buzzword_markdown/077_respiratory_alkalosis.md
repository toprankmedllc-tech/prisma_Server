## Buzzword Question

A 23-year-old woman presents with anxiety and rapid breathing after a panic attack. Arterial blood gas shows pH 7.50 and PaCO₂ 28 mmHg.
Which of the following acid-base disturbances is most likely present?


## Buzzwords Used

Anxiety; Hyperventilation; Low PaCO₂

## Answer Options

A) Respiratory alkalosis
B) Metabolic alkalosis
C) High anion gap metabolic acidosis
D) Respiratory acidosis

## Correct Answer

A) Respiratory alkalosis

## Explanation

Respiratory alkalosis occurs when hyperventilation causes excessive loss of CO₂, leading to increased blood pH. Panic attacks are a classic cause of acute respiratory alkalosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Panic attack + hyperventilation + low PaCO₂ = Respiratory alkalosis

## Wrong Options

### . Metabolic alkalosis

Metabolic alkalosis is characterized by elevated bicarbonate, commonly caused by vomiting or diuretic use.

**Buzzword Combo:** Vomiting + elevated bicarbonate = Metabolic alkalosis

### High anion gap metabolic acidosis

High anion gap metabolic acidosis occurs in conditions such as diabetic ketoacidosis and lactic acidosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ketoacidosis + elevated anion gap = Metabolic acidosis

### Respiratory acidosis

Respiratory acidosis occurs due to hypoventilation and CO₂ retention.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hypoventilation + elevated PaCO₂ = Respiratory acidosis
