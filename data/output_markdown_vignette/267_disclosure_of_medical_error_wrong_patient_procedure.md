## Chief Complaint

Unexplained endoscopic procedure performed

## Vignette

A 58-year-old man is admitted for evaluation of suspected biliary obstruction. He is scheduled for an endoscopic procedure requiring sedation. Due to a scheduling and identity verification error in the endoscopy unit, the procedure is mistakenly performed on another patient with a similar name. The error is identified shortly afterward when staff review the chart and realize the mismatch in identifiers. The patient who underwent the procedure is clinically stable but confused about why the test was performed. The attending physician is informed that the mistake originated from a combination of clerical mislabeling and failure of the final identity check before the procedure.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?
