## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 24-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after falling onto an abducted and externally rotated arm while playing basketball. He immediately developed severe shoulder pain and reports inability to move his arm. His medical history is notable for recurrent joint laxity and several ankle sprains during adolescence. He takes no medications.

Physical examination shows flattening of the normal rounded contour of the right shoulder. The arm is held slightly abducted and externally rotated. Passive movement is markedly painful. Sensation is decreased over the lateral aspect of the shoulder. Strength testing reveals impaired shoulder abduction from 15° to 90°. Radial pulse is intact.

Anteroposterior radiograph of the shoulder is described as showing displacement of the humeral head anteroinferior to the glenoid fossa without fracture.

Which of the following structures was most likely injured in this patient and best explains his neurologic findings?
