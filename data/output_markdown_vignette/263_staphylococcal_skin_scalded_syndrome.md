## Vignette

A 2-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department because of fever, irritability, and diffuse skin tenderness for 24 hours. Three days earlier he was evaluated for purulent conjunctivitis and treated conservatively. His mother reports that he now cries when touched and refuses to wear clothes because “everything hurts.”
Past medical history is unremarkable. Immunizations are up to date.
Vital signs:
* Temperature: 39.2°C (102.6°F)
* Pulse: 132/min
* Blood pressure: 94/58 mmHg
Physical examination demonstrates diffuse erythema involving the trunk, axillae, neck, and proximal extremities. Large, fragile flaccid bullae are present with diffuse erythematous skin, superficial wrinkling, and large flaccid bullae. Areas of superficial desquamation appear to peel away with light pressure.
