## Chief Complaint

Progressive fatigue, early satiety, and unintentional weight loss

## Vignette

A 52-year-old man presents with progressive fatigue, early satiety, and unintentional weight loss over the past several months. Physical examination reveals marked splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show WBC count of 156,000/mm³, hemoglobin of 10.2 g/dL, and platelet count of 485,000/mm³. Differential reveals increased mature granulocytes with myelocytes, metamyelocytes, and basophils of 7%. Leukocyte alkaline phosphatase (LAP) score is 8 (low). Cytogenetic analysis identifies a t(9;22) translocation.
