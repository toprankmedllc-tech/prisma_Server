## Vignette

A 24-year-old woman presents to the emergency department with fever, chills, nausea, and right-sided flank pain for 2 days. She also reports dysuria and urinary frequency for the past week. Temperature is 39.1°C, blood pressure is 108/66 mmHg, pulse is 112/min, and respiratory rate is 20/min. Physical examination reveals marked right costovertebral angle tenderness. Laboratory studies show leukocyte count of 15,800/mm³ with neutrophilic predominance. Urinalysis demonstrates positive leukocyte esterase, positive nitrites, numerous white blood cells, and white blood cell casts. Urine culture grows a lactose-fermenting gram-negative rod.
Which of the following virulence factors most likely allowed this organism to ascend and establish infection within the upper urinary tract?
