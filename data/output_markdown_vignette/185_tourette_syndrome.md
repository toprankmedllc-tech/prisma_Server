## Chief Complaint

Repetitive movements and vocalizations

## Vignette

A 12-year-old boy is brought to the clinic because of repetitive movements and sounds that have progressively worsened over the past 2 years. His parents report frequent eye blinking, shoulder shrugging, throat clearing, and occasional grunting noises. The symptoms temporarily improve when he concentrates but worsen during periods of stress and excitement. He describes an uncomfortable “urge” before the movements occur, followed by relief afterward. He also has difficulty concentrating in school and was recently diagnosed with ADHD. Physical examination is otherwise normal. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
