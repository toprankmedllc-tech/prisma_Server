## Chief Complaint

Headaches, dizziness, and generalized itching that worsens after hot showers

## Vignette

A 61-year-old man presents with headaches, dizziness, and generalized itching that worsens after hot showers. Physical examination reveals facial plethora and splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 18.4 g/dL, hematocrit of 56%, RBC count of 6.8 million/mm³, platelet count of 520,000/mm³, and WBC count of 13,200/mm³. Serum erythropoietin level is 2.1 mU/mL (low). Bone marrow biopsy shows hypercellularity with increased erythroid precursors.
