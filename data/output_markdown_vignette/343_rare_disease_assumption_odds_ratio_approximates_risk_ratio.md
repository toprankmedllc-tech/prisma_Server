## Vignette

Public health researchers investigate whether long-term exposure to a newly identified industrial solvent is associated with a rare neurodegenerative disorder seen in a small subset of factory workers. Because the condition is uncommon, investigators identify affected individuals first and then match them with unaffected workers of similar age and job role. The analysis yields a measure of association of 1.8 between exposure and disease. 

Which of the following best describes how this measure relates to the true increase in disease risk?
