## Chief Complaint

Fever and diffuse rash after starting anticonvulsant therapy

## Vignette

A 24-year-old woman is brought to the clinic by her roommate due to recurrent episodes of staring spells associated with lip smacking and brief unresponsiveness over the past 4 months. After each episode, the patient appears confused and fatigued for several minutes. MRI of the brain is unremarkable, and she is started on an anticonvulsant medication. Two weeks later, she develops fever, malaise, diffuse pruritic rash, and facial swelling. Physical examination reveals cervical lymphadenopathy and a widespread morbilliform eruption over the trunk and extremities. Laboratory studies demonstrate eosinophilia and elevated liver transaminases. Which of the following medications is the most likely cause of this patient’s condition?
