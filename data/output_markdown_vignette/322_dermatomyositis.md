## Vignette

A 62-year-old woman presents with progressive difficulty climbing stairs and rising from a seated position for the past 4 months. She reports increasing fatigue, difficulty lifting grocery bags overhead, and mild dysphagia to solid foods. She also notes an intensely pruritic rash around her eyelids and over her knuckles.
Past medical history includes type 2 diabetes mellitus, obesity, and hypertension. She smoked one pack of cigarettes daily for 35 years before quitting 5 years ago.
Physical examination demonstrates:
* Violaceous discoloration with mild edema surrounding both eyelids
* Scaly erythematous papules over the MCP and PIP joints
* Symmetric proximal muscle weakness involving the shoulder and pelvic girdle
* Mild tenderness of proximal muscles
Laboratory findings:
Test	Result
Creatine kinase (CK)	markedly elevated
Aldolase	elevated
ESR	mildly elevated
AST/ALT	elevated
Electromyography demonstrates inflammatory myopathy.
Muscle biopsy is described as:
Perivascular inflammation with perifascicular muscle fiber atrophy and deposition of complement components in capillaries.
Which of the following best explains the underlying pathophysiologic mechanism responsible for this patient’s disease?
