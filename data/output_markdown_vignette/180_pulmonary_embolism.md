## Chief Complaint

Sudden-onset shortness of breath and pleuritic chest pain

## Vignette

A 35-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department with sudden-onset shortness of breath and pleuritic chest pain that started 2 hours ago. She underwent caesarean section at 35 weeks of gestation due to pre-term labor, a week ago. On examination, she is anxious and tachypneic. Temperature is 37.2°C, blood pressure 100/60 mmHg, pulse 118/min, and respiratory rate 28/min. Oxygen saturation is 90% on room air. Lung exam is clear. Heart sounds reveal a loud P2. Arterial blood gas shows: pH is 7.48, PaCO₂ is 30 mmHg and PaO₂ is low. Chest X-ray is normal. Which of the following is the most likely underlying pathophysiologic mechanism responsible for this patient’s condition?

## Key Symptoms

Sudden dyspnea; pleuritic chest pain; tachycardia; tachypnea; anxiety; hypoxia; postpartum state

## Physical Exam

Clear lung fields; loud P2 (pulmonary hypertension); tachycardia; tachypnea

## Main Clue

Postpartum state + sudden dyspnea + pleuritic chest pain + hypoxia with clear lungs

## Supporting Clue

Recent cesarean section; tachycardia; respiratory alkalosis; normal chest X-ray; loud P2

## Question

Which of the following is the most likely underlying pathophysiologic mechanism responsible for this patient’s condition?

## Correct Answer

C) Deep vein thrombosis from lower extremity embolizing to pulmonary arteries
