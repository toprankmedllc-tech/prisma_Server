## Vignette

A 52-year-old woman comes to the clinic because of painful mouth ulcers and fatigue. Three months ago, she was diagnosed with a chronic inflammatory joint disease after developing symmetric pain and swelling of the wrists and metacarpophalangeal joints. She was started on a weekly oral medication that is also used in some chemotherapy regimens. Physical examination shows shallow oral ulcers and mild tenderness of both wrists. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 9.4 g/dL, leukocyte count of 2,100/mm3, platelet count of 86,000/mm3, AST of 82 U/L, and ALT of 91 U/L. The physician starts folinic acid therapy to reduce drug toxicity.
