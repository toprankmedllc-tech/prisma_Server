## Chief Complaint

Syncope during vigorous exercise (exertional syncope)

## Vignette

A 17-year-old male high school basketball player is brought to the emergency department after collapsing on the court during the final quarter of a game. Bystanders report he had no warning and lost consciousness for approximately 30 seconds before regaining it spontaneously. He denies chest pain prior to the event but reports episodes of "graying out" when he stands up quickly after sitting, which he has attributed to dehydration. His father died suddenly at age 34 from an unexplained cardiac event. On exam, he is well-built with no dysmorphic features. Cardiac auscultation reveals a harsh, crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur heard best at the left lower sternal border. The murmur increases in intensity when the patient stands up from a squatting position.
Which of the following additional maneuvers would also cause an increase in the intensity of this patient's murmur?
A) Passive leg raise 
B) Sustained handgrip 
C) Administration of phenylephrine 
D) Valsalva maneuver (strain phase) 
E) Squatting from a standing position


## Key Symptoms

Exercise-induced syncope; Prodromal "graying out" with positional changes; No chest pain; Family history of sudden cardiac death at young age
