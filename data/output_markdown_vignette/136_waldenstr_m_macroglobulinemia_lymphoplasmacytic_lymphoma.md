## Vignette

A 67-year-old man presents with fatigue, blurred vision, and recurrent nosebleeds. He also reports headaches and confusion. Vital signs show temperature of 37.0°C, heart rate of 86/min, blood pressure of 132/80 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 16/min. Physical examination reveals mild hepatosplenomegaly and pallor. Fundoscopy shows dilated, tortuous retinal veins with "sausage-link" segmentation. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 9.4 g/dL, MCV of 86 fL, and total serum protein of 10.2 g/dL. Serum protein electrophoresis demonstrates an IgM monoclonal spike of 5.2 g/dL.
