## Vignette

Researchers conduct a study to evaluate the association between regular consumption of sugar-sweetened beverages and the development of type 2 diabetes. The study includes 800 adults with type 2 diabetes and 800 adults without diabetes. After adjusting for age, body mass index, and physical activity level, the investigators report an odds ratio of 2.4 (95% CI: 1.8–3.1) for type 2 diabetes among individuals who regularly consume sugar-sweetened beverages compared with those who do not.

Which of the following is the most appropriate conclusion?
