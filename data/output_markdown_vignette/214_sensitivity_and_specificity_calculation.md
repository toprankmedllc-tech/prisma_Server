## Vignette

Researchers at a tertiary care center evaluate a new rapid urine dipstick test for diagnosing urinary tract infections (UTIs) against the gold standard of urine culture with bacterial colony counts ≥100,000 CFU/mL. The study enrolled 1,000 consecutive patients presenting to an outpatient clinic with symptoms suggestive of UTI (dysuria, frequency, urgency). All patients underwent both the rapid dipstick test and a standard midstream urine culture collected simultaneously.  Of the 1,000 patients enrolled, 200 had culture-confirmed UTIs. Among these 200 culture-positive patients, the rapid dipstick test returned a positive result in 180 patients and a negative result in the remaining 20. Among the 800 patients whose cultures were negative, the rapid dipstick test returned a positive result in 80 patients and a negative result in the remaining 720.  The investigators are preparing to submit their findings to a peer-reviewed journal and need to report the test's intrinsic performance characteristics for their abstract.  Which of the following correctly identifies the sensitivity and specificity of this rapid urine dipstick test?

## Key Symptoms

2x2 table: Disease+ (n=200): TP=180, FN=20; Disease- (n=800): FP=80, TN=720; Sensitivity = TP/(TP+FN); Specificity = TN/(TN+FP)

## Labs

2x2 contingency table: | | Culture+ | Culture- | TOTAL | | Dipstick+ | 180 (TP) | 80 (FP) | 260 | | Dipstick- | 20 (FN) | 720 (TN) | 740 | | TOTAL | 200 | 800 | 1,000 |

## Main Clue

Sensitivity = TP/(TP+FN) = 180/200 = 90%; Specificity = TN/(TN+FP) = 720/800 = 90%


## Supporting Clue

Sensitivity reads down the disease-positive column; specificity reads down the disease-negative column; both are intrinsic test properties unaffected by prevalence
