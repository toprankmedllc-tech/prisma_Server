## Vignette

A large outpatient primary care network transitions from a traditional fee-for-service reimbursement structure to a capitated payment model with annual quality-based bonuses. Six months later, internal review data show that physicians in the network are ordering significantly fewer specialist referrals, imaging studies, and elective procedures compared with the previous year. Hospital admissions for preventable complications have remained unchanged, and patient satisfaction scores have slightly improved. During a departmental meeting, one physician expresses concern that the new payment structure may incentivize undertreatment of complex patients in order to reduce overall healthcare spending.

Which of the following best describes the primary financial incentive associated with the current reimbursement model?
