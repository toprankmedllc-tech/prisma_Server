## Chief Complaint

Inability to conceive

## Vignette

A 29-year-old woman presents to the infertility clinic after 18 months of inability to conceive. She reports irregular menstrual cycles with long gaps between periods. She denies galactorrhea or hirsutism. BMI is 23 kg/m². Pregnancy tests are negative.

Laboratory evaluation shows:

* Normal prolactin
* Normal TSH
* Low-normal estrogen levels
* Inadequate LH surge on mid-cycle testing

She is started on a medication that blocks estrogen receptors in the hypothalamus, leading to increased GnRH secretion and ultimately increased LH and FSH release. After 3 cycles, she successfully ovulates.

Which of the following is the most likely mechanism of action of this drug?
