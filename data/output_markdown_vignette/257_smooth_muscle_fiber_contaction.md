## Vignette

A 58-year-old man with a long history of untreated hypertension is brought to the emergency department after developing a sudden severe headache and markedly elevated blood pressure. During evaluation, physicians note persistent vasoconstriction caused by prolonged contraction of vascular smooth muscle. A medical student asks how smooth muscle relaxation normally occurs after contraction has been initiated.
The attending physician explains that relaxation depends on an enzyme that reverses the action of myosin light-chain kinase (MLCK) by removing phosphate groups from myosin, thereby preventing continued actin-myosin interaction.
Which of the following intracellular mechanisms is most directly responsible for smooth muscle relaxation in this patient?
