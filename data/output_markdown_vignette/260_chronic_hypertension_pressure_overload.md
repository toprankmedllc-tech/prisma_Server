## Vignette

A 62-year-old man presents to clinic for a routine evaluation. He has a 20-year history of essential hypertension that has been only partially controlled on his current regimen. He reports mild exertional dyspnea over the past 6 months. On examination, his blood pressure is 162/96 mm Hg, pulse is 78/min, and respirations are 14/min. Cardiac auscultation reveals a soft S4 gallop. Lung auscultation is clear. ECG shows tall R waves in the lateral precordial leads with strain pattern, consistent with left ventricular hypertrophy. Echocardiography demonstrates concentric thickening of the left ventricular wall with preserved ejection fraction but impaired diastolic relaxation.
Which of the following best describes the underlying cellular mechanism of this patient's left ventricular remodeling?

## Main Clue

Longstanding hypertension + ECG LVH with strain + concentric LV thickening on echo + S4 gallop (stiff ventricle)

## Supporting Clue

Preserved ejection fraction with impaired diastolic relaxation indicates pressure-overload concentric hypertrophy, not eccentric (volume-overload) hypertrophy
