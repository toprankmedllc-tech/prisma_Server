## Chief Complaint

Reckless behavior and decreased need for sleep

## Vignette

A 29-year-old man is brought to the emergency department by his sister because of increasingly bizarre and reckless behavior over the past 10 days. He has been sleeping only 2 hours per night but states that he “doesn’t need sleep anymore because my mind is operating at a genius level.” He recently spent thousands of dollars on unnecessary electronics and attempted to start multiple unrealistic business ventures. During the interview, he speaks rapidly and frequently jumps between unrelated topics. He becomes irritable when interrupted and states that he has been “chosen to revolutionize the world economy.” Urine toxicology screening is negative. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
