## Vignette

A 5-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department because of vomiting, fever, and rapid breathing. His mother reports that earlier in the day she found him playing near an open bottle of his grandfather’s medications .His grandfather has a history of coronary artery disease and osteoarthritis. The child is tachypneic and mildly confused. On physical examination, the child appears ill and dehydrated. Temperature is 38.7°C (101.7°F), blood pressure is 92/56 mm Hg, pulse is 128/min, and respiratory rate is 36/min.
Arterial blood gas analysis shows:
pH: 7.48
PaCO₂: 28 mm Hg
HCO₃⁻: 18 mEq/L
Several hours later, repeat testing reveals an elevated anion gap metabolic acidosis.
Which of the following best explains this patient’s acid-base abnormalities?
