## Vignette

A 23-year-old man is brought to the emergency department by his parents because of increasingly bizarre behavior over the past 8 months. He recently dropped out of college after claiming that “the television is sending secret messages directly to me.” His parents report that he spends most of the day isolated in his room and rarely shows emotion during conversations. On examination, the patient appears disheveled and has minimal facial expression. During the interview, he suddenly stops speaking mid-sentence and begins talking about unrelated topics. Urine toxicology screening is negative. Which of the following best explains the neurochemical abnormality responsible for this patient’s positive symptoms?
