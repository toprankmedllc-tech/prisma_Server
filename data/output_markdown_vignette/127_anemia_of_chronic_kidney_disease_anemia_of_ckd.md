## Vignette

A 72-year-old man with chronic kidney disease presents with fatigue and decreased exercise tolerance. Vital signs show temperature of 36.7°C, heart rate of 78/min, blood pressure of 142/86 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 15/min. Physical examination is unremarkable except for pallor. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 8.9 g/dL, MCV of 88 fL, MCH of 29 pg, reticulocyte count of 1.2% (absolute reticulocyte count 42,000/mm³), serum iron of 82 µg/dL, TIBC of 290 µg/dL, ferritin of 185 ng/mL, transferrin saturation of 28%, and serum creatinine of 4.2 mg/dL.
