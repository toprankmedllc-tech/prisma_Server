## Chief Complaint

 Confusion and difficulty breathing

## Vignette

A 26-year-old man is brought to the emergency department by his roommate because of progressive confusion and difficulty breathing. The roommate states that the patient recently underwent dental surgery and has been taking increasing amounts of oxycodone for pain control. On examination, the patient is somnolent with shallow respirations. Arterial blood gas analysis shows decreased PaO2 and elevated PaCO2. Chest x-ray is unremarkable. The alveolar-arterial (A-a) gradient is normal. Which of the following is the most likely mechanism responsible for this patient’s hypoxemia?
