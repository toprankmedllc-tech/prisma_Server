## Vignette

A 32-year-old previously healthy man develops fever, chills, and pleuritic chest pain after a recent upper respiratory tract infection. He now has a productive cough with “rust-colored” sputum. On Examination, blood pressure is 118/76 mm Hg, Heart Rate is  104/min, oxygen saturation is 94%, and temperature °C is 38.5°C. on auscultation, crackles are heard over the lower right lobe of the lung. Chest x-ray shows lobar consolidation in the right lower lobe with air bronchograms. Gram stain reveals Gram-positive, lancet-shaped diplococci. The organism is identified as α-hemolytic on blood agar and sensitive to optochin. Which of the following best describes the pathogenesis of this infection?
