## Vignette

A 55-year-old man with a history of hypertension and type 2 diabetes mellitus presents for a routine follow-up visit. He reports a persistent dry cough that has been present for approximately 3 months. He denies fever, nasal congestion, sore throat, or any recent illness. He was started on a medication approximately 4 months ago to help control his blood pressure and provide kidney protection given his diabetes. His blood pressure today is 138/88 mmHg, heart rate is 76 bpm, oxygen saturation is 98% on room air, and temperature is 37.0°C. Laboratory results show serum potassium of 5.1 mEq/L, creatinine of 1.1 mg/dL, and BUN of 18 mg/dL. Physical examination is unremarkable. Which of the following medications would be most appropriate to switch this patient to while maintaining renal protection?
