## Vignette

A 67-year-old man is admitted to the intensive care unit for septic shock secondary to pneumonia. He is treated with intravenous fluids, vasopressors, and broad-spectrum antibiotics. Two days later, his urine output progressively decreases. Temperature is 38.7°C, blood pressure is 88/54 mmHg, pulse is 122/min, and respiratory rate is 24/min. Laboratory studies show blood urea nitrogen of 46 mg/dL and serum creatinine of 3.2 mg/dL, increased from 1.0 mg/dL on admission. Urinalysis demonstrates muddy brown granular casts and renal tubular epithelial cells. Fractional excretion of sodium is 3.1%. Renal ultrasound demonstrates normal-sized kidneys without hydronephrosis.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying mechanism of this patient’s renal failure?
