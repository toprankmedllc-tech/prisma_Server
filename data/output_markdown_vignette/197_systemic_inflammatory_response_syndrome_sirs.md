## Chief Complaint

Fever and rapid breathing after trauma

## Vignette

A 24-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after sustaining multiple long-bone fractures in a motor vehicle collision. Six hours later, he develops fever and tachypnea. He appears anxious but is alert and oriented. Laboratory studies show leukocytosis with increased band forms. Arterial blood gas analysis reveals a PaCO2 of 30 mm Hg. Which of the following best describes this patient’s condition?

## Main Clue

## Supporting Clue

Fever, leukocytosis, tachypnea, and respiratory alkalosis shortly after trauma.
