## Chief Complaint

 Excessive daytime sleepiness and morning headaches

## Vignette

A 52-year-old man comes to the clinic because of excessive daytime sleepiness, morning headaches, and progressive shortness of breath. His wife reports loud snoring and episodes during sleep in which he appears to stop breathing. He has a BMI of 42 kg/m². Physical examination shows cyanosis and obesity. Laboratory studies reveal serum bicarbonate of 31 mEq/L. Arterial blood gas analysis shows PaCO2 of 54 mm Hg and mild hypoxemia. Polysomnography demonstrates repeated episodes of nocturnal hypoventilation with obstructive apnea events. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
