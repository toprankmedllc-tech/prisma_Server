## Chief Complaint

Excessive concern about perceived facial defect

## Vignette

A 19-year-old woman is brought to the psychiatry clinic by her mother because she spends several hours each day examining her face in the mirror. She is convinced that her nose is “severely deformed,” despite reassurance from family members and physicians that her appearance is normal. She frequently compares her appearance to others on social media and has recently stopped attending college classes due to embarrassment about her looks. She repeatedly applies makeup and avoids leaving the house unless she feels her appearance is “acceptable.” She denies binge eating, purging, or weight concerns.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Key Symptoms

Preoccupation with appearance; repetitive mirror checking; excessive grooming; social withdrawal; comparing appearance to others; impaired functioning; embarrassment about perceived defect; preserved eating habits

## Main Clue

Excessive preoccupation with minimal or imperceptible physical flaw


## Supporting Clue

Repetitive mirror checking and social impairment without eating disorder symptoms
