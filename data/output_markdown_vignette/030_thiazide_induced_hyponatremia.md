## Vignette

A 72-year-old woman is brought to the emergency department because of progressive confusion, headache, and nausea for 2 days. Her family reports that she recently began taking a new medication for hypertension. Temperature is 36.9°C, blood pressure is 112/70 mmHg, pulse is 78/min, and respiratory rate is 16/min. Physical examination shows no peripheral edema, jugular venous distention, or signs of dehydration. Laboratory studies reveal serum sodium of 118 mEq/L, serum osmolality of 260 mOsm/kg, urine sodium of 48 mEq/L, and urine osmolality of 520 mOsm/kg. Serum glucose is normal. Chest radiograph is unremarkable, and CT scan of the head shows no acute abnormalities.
Which of the following medications most likely contributed to this patient’s electrolyte abnormality?
