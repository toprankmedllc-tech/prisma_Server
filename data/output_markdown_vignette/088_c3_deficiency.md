## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 3-year-old girl is brought to the clinic because of recurrent episodes of pneumonia and otitis media. Her parents say she has required multiple courses of antibiotics since infancy. The infections are usually caused by encapsulated bacteria. She has no history of delayed umbilical cord separation, chronic oral thrush, or severe viral infections. Physical examination shows mild fever and right-sided crackles. Laboratory studies show a normal neutrophil count, normal lymphocyte count, and normal serum immunoglobulin concentrations. A sample of the patient’s serum is incubated with bacteria and neutrophils; compared with control serum, there is markedly reduced bacterial uptake by neutrophils.
