## Vignette

A 52-year-old man presents with progressive shortness of breath, chronic cough, and reduced exercise tolerance for the past 3 years. He has a 30-pack-year smoking history and reports frequent wheezing episodes. On examination, he has a barrel-shaped chest and a prolonged expiratory phase. Pulmonary function testing is performed to evaluate his lung function. Spirometry results are as follows:
FEV₁: 1.2 L 
FVC: 2.8 L 
FEV₁/FVC ratio: 43%
The physician explains that his condition is due to loss of elastic recoil leading to air trapping and progressive hyperinflation. Which of the following best describes the lung volume abnormality seen in this patient?
