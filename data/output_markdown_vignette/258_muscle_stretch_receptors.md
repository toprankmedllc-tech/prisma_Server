## Vignette

A 29-year-old graduate student visits a neurology clinic because of progressive difficulty maintaining balance while walking on uneven ground. During the physical examination, the physician taps the patient’s patellar tendon with a reflex hammer, producing rapid contraction of the quadriceps muscle. The physician explains to a medical student that this reflex depends on specialized muscle stretch receptors that detect sudden muscle elongation and transmit sensory signals to the spinal cord through a specific neural pathway.
The medical student asks which type of sensory nerve fiber carries signals from muscle stretch receptors to the spinal cord to initiate the stretch reflex.
Which of the following nerve fibers is primarily responsible for transmitting signals from muscle stretch receptors?
