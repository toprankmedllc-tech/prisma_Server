## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 12-year-old boy is brought to the clinic because of fatigue and intermittent jaundice. Physical examination reveals splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 9.4 g/dL, reticulocyte count of 8.2%, MCHC of 37.2 g/dL, and indirect bilirubin of 3.1 mg/dL. Peripheral blood smear shows numerous small, round red blood cells lacking central pallor.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying defect?
