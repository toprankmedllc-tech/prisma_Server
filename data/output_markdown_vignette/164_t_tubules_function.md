## Vignette

A 24-year-old graduate student volunteers for a physiology laboratory experiment studying excitation-contraction coupling in skeletal muscle fibers. Researchers obtain an isolated skeletal muscle preparation connected to an intact motor neuron and expose the muscle tissue to a novel pharmacologic agent that selectively disrupts the function of transverse (T) tubules without affecting the sarcolemma, neuromuscular junction, or sarcoplasmic reticulum directly.
During the experiment, electrical stimulation of the motor neuron produces normal release of acetylcholine into the neuromuscular junction. Electromyography demonstrates successful generation of an end-plate potential followed by a normal action potential along the surface membrane of the muscle fiber. However, despite preserved depolarization of the sarcolemma, the muscle develops only weak, poorly coordinated contractions with markedly reduced force generation.
Further analysis reveals significantly decreased calcium release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum during muscle depolarization.
Which of the following is the primary physiologic function of the structure most directly affected in this experiment?
