## Vignette

A 24-year-old professional baseball pitcher presents to the sports medicine clinic with gradually worsening right shoulder pain over the past several months. The pain is especially noticeable during pitching and when lifting objects above shoulder level. He also reports difficulty combing his hair and occasionally feels weakness when attempting to throw at high velocity. He denies any recent trauma. Physical examination reveals tenderness over the lateral aspect of the shoulder and weakness during initiation of arm abduction and external rotation against resistance. MRI of the shoulder demonstrates inflammation and partial tearing involving the rotator cuff tendons.
The physician explains that the patient’s injury involves a group of muscles responsible for stabilizing the glenohumeral joint during upper extremity movement.
Which of the following groups correctly identifies the muscles that make up the rotator cuff muscles?
