## Chief Complaint

Progressive weight loss

## Vignette

A 17-year-old girl is brought to the clinic by her parents because of progressive weight loss over the past 8 months. She restricts her food intake to “avoid getting fat” despite appearing visibly underweight. She exercises excessively for several hours daily and becomes distressed when unable to exercise. Her BMI is 16 kg/m². Physical examination reveals dry skin, lanugo hair, bradycardia, and hypotension. She insists that she is “still overweight” and denies the seriousness of her condition. Laboratory studies reveal mild hypokalemia.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Key Symptoms

Restrictive eating; fear of weight gain; low BMI; excessive exercise; distorted body image; lanugo hair; bradycardia; hypotension; denial of illness severity


## Labs

Hypokalemia


## Physical Exam

Thin/cachectic appearance; lanugo hair; dry skin; bradycardia; hypotension

## Main Clue

Low body weight with intense fear of gaining weight


## Supporting Clue

Distorted body image despite severe underweight status
