## Chief Complaint

Fever, fatigue, and loose stools

## Vignette

A 24-year-old man comes to the clinic because of fever, fatigue, and loose stools for the past 5 weeks. He has also noticed painless swelling in his neck and groin. He has had two episodes of oral thrush during the past year. Physical examination shows generalized nontender lymphadenopathy and mild hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin 10.2 g/dL, leukocytes 2,700/mm³, platelets 185,000/mm³, IgG 2,250 mg/dL, and IgM 340 mg/dL. A peripheral blood smear shows no blasts. Further immune testing shows marked reduction in helper T lymphocytes.
