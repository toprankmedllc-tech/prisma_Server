## Chief Complaint

Fatigue, recurrent infections, and easy bruising

## Vignette

A 6-year-old boy is brought to the clinic because of fatigue, recurrent infections, and easy bruising for the past month. Vital signs show temperature of 38.3°C, heart rate of 112/min, blood pressure of 96/60 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 22/min. Physical examination reveals pallor, petechiae over the lower extremities, and hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 7.2 g/dL, platelet count of 38,000/mm³, WBC count of 28,400/mm³ with 68% lymphoblasts on differential. Bone marrow biopsy shows >20% lymphoblasts that are TdT-positive on immunohistochemistry.
