## Vignette

A 23-year-old physiology student is participating in a laboratory experiment evaluating skeletal muscle contraction and relaxation. During the experiment, an electrical stimulus is applied to a skeletal muscle fiber, leading to depolarization of the sarcolemma and release of calcium ions from the sarcoplasmic reticulum into the cytoplasm. The muscle contracts normally, but the instructor explains that relaxation of the muscle requires active transport of calcium ions back into the sarcoplasmic reticulum after contraction is complete.
The professor further notes that this process depends on a specialized ATP-dependent membrane protein located in the sarcoplasmic reticulum. Dysfunction of this protein would prolong elevated cytosolic calcium levels and impair muscle relaxation.
Which of the following best describes the normal function of SERCA in skeletal muscle?
