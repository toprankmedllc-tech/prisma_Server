## Vignette

A 66-year-old man presents to the clinic because of progressive bilateral lower extremity swelling and increasing fatigue over the past 5 months. He also reports decreased appetite and an unintentional 8-kg weight loss. His medical history is significant for long-standing rheumatoid arthritis treated intermittently with NSAIDs. Physical examination reveals periorbital edema and bilateral pitting edema of the lower extremities. Laboratory studies show serum albumin of 2.0 g/dL, total cholesterol of 310 mg/dL, and creatinine of 2.1 mg/dL. Urinalysis demonstrates 4+ proteinuria with fatty casts. Serum complement levels are normal. Renal biopsy demonstrates amorphous eosinophilic extracellular deposits within the mesangium and vessel walls. Congo red staining reveals apple-green birefringence under polarized light.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying mechanism responsible for this patient’s renal disease?
