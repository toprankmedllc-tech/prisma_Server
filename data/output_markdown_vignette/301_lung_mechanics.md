## Chief Complaint

Severe shortness of breath

## Vignette

A 67-year-old man with a long history of COPD presents to the emergency department with severe shortness of breath and use of accessory muscles of respiration. He is sitting upright in a tripod position and is visibly fatigued. Arterial blood gas shows hypercapnia and hypoxemia. Despite supplemental oxygen, he continues to have increased respiratory effort and complains of “working very hard just to breathe.” The attending explains that his condition is associated with increased energy expenditure required to expand the lungs and move air in and out of the airways. Which of the following best explains the increased work of breathing in this patient?

## Key Symptoms

Dyspnea; use of accessory muscles; tripod position; fatigue; increased respiratory effort

## Labs

## Physical Exam

## Main Clue

Increased effort to ventilate despite oxygen therapy

## Supporting Clue

COPD with air trapping and hyperinflation
