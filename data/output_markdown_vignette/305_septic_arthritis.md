## Vignette

A 74-year-old woman presents to the emergency department with severe left knee pain and swelling that began 2 days ago. She reports progressive inability to bear weight and subjective fevers. Her medical history is significant for long-standing seropositive rheumatoid arthritis treated with low-dose prednisone and methotrexate, type 2 diabetes mellitus, and chronic osteoarthritis of both knees. Two weeks ago, she received an intra-articular glucocorticoid injection for worsening knee pain.
On physical examination, temperature is 38.7°C (101.7°F), pulse is 108/min, and blood pressure is 136/80 mmHg. The left knee is markedly swollen, erythematous, warm, and held in slight flexion. Passive and active range of motion are severely limited due to pain. No skin rash is noted.
