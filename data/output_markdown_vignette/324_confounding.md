## Vignette

Researchers investigate whether daily coffee consumption is associated with an increased risk of myocardial infarction. In the initial analysis, individuals who consume at least 4 cups of coffee per day have a significantly higher rate of myocardial infarction compared with those who consume less coffee (RR = 1.8, p < 0.001). Further analysis showed that cigarette smoking was substantially more prevalent among heavy coffee drinkers than among light coffee drinkers. The investigators therefore performed a subgroup analysis stratified by smoking status. Among smokers, myocardial infarction rates are similar between heavy and light coffee drinkers. Among nonsmokers, myocardial infarction rates are also similar between heavy and light coffee drinkers.

Which of the following best explains these findings?
