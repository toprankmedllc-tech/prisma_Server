## Vignette

A 29-year-old woman who immigrated to the United States from rural Pakistan 2 years ago presents with 6 months of progressive dyspnea on exertion, orthopnea, and palpitations. She reports a vague history of joint pains as a child, for which she received only intermittent treatment. On examination, her blood pressure is 110/68 mm Hg, pulse is 96/min and irregular, and respirations are 20/min. There is a malar flush across her cheeks. Auscultation at the apex reveals a low-pitched, rumbling diastolic murmur.Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Supporting Clue

Mitral facies (malar flush) reflects chronic pulmonary venous congestion and low cardiac output — a hallmark of long-standing mitral stenosis
