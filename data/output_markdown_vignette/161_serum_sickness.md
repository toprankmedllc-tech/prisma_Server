## Chief Complaint

Fever, rash, and joint pain

## Vignette

A 17-year-old boy comes to the clinic because of fever, rash, and joint pain for 4 days. Ten days ago, he received an equine-derived antitoxin after a possible toxin exposure during a camping trip. He initially felt well but then developed malaise, swelling of both knees and wrists, and a diffuse pruritic rash. Temperature is 38.7°C, blood pressure is 118/72 mm Hg, heart rate is 96/min, and pulse oximetry is 99% on room air. Physical examination shows a diffuse erythematous maculopapular rash over the trunk and extremities and mild tenderness of the wrists and knees. Laboratory studies show leukocytes 9,800/mm³, creatinine 0.9 mg/dL, C3 complement 48 mg/dL, and C4 complement 7 mg/dL. Urinalysis shows protein 320 mg/day and no red blood cell casts.
