## Vignette

A 3-week-old girl is brought to the pediatric clinic for routine newborn follow-up. She was delivered via uncomplicated vaginal delivery at 39 weeks after a pregnancy complicated by persistent breech positioning during the third trimester. Birth history is otherwise unremarkable. Her mother reports that the infant feeds normally and moves both legs spontaneously.

Family history is significant for a maternal aunt who required treatment with a harness during infancy for a congenital hip abnormality.

Physical examination shows asymmetric gluteal folds and apparent shortening of the left thigh. During examination, flexion of the hips and knees to 90° followed by gentle posterior pressure and adduction of the left hip produces a palpable “clunk.” Re-abduction of the hip reproduces another palpable movement as the femoral head relocates.

A hip ultrasound is described as demonstrating lateral displacement of the femoral head relative to a shallow acetabulum.

Which of the following best explains the underlying pathophysiology of this patient’s condition?
