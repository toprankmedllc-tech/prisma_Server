## Chief Complaint

Chest pain, palpitations, fatigue; multiple prior somatic complaints; excessive health preoccupation; functional impairment

## Vignette

: A 41-year-old woman presents to her primary care physician for her sixth visit in 4 months. On each visit she has presented with a different constellation of physical complaints. Today she describes severe burning chest pain, intermittent palpitations, and profound fatigue that she rates as 9/10 in severity. She states these symptoms have made it impossible for her to return to her job as a high school teacher and that she has withdrawn from all social activities. During the interview, she becomes tearful and says "I know something is seriously wrong inside my body  I can feel it  but no doctor will listen to me." She spends considerable time each day researching her symptoms online and has brought printed articles about rare cardiac and autoimmune conditions she believes she may have.  Previous visits resulted in the following completed workup: ECG (normal sinus rhythm, no ST changes), echocardiogram (normal left ventricular function, no structural abnormality), 48-hour Holter monitor (no clinically significant arrhythmia), complete blood count (normal), comprehensive metabolic panel (normal), thyroid panel (normal), erythrocyte sedimentation rate (4 mm/hr), C-reactive protein (<0.5 mg/L), antinuclear antibody (negative), and rheumatoid factor (negative). A previous gastroenterology referral for abdominal pain was similarly unrevealing. The patient has no prior psychiatric history and is not currently taking any medications. She denies substance use and is not seeking opioid prescriptions.  Physical examination today reveals an anxious-appearing woman in no acute distress. Cardiovascular and pulmonary examinations are unremarkable. Abdomen is soft and non-tender without organomegaly. Neurological examination is non-focal. She becomes visibly frustrated when the physician begins to review her normal test results.  Which of the following most accurately characterizes this patient's condition?
