## Vignette

A 6-month-old boy is brought to the clinic because of recurrent respiratory infections, chronic diarrhea, and poor weight gain. His parents report that he has required multiple courses of antibiotics for pneumonia and otitis media. He has also had persistent oral thrush. Physical examination shows failure to thrive and sparse lymphoid tissue. Laboratory studies show decreased serum immunoglobulin concentrations and markedly decreased CD4+ T cells. Flow cytometry shows absent expression of MHC class II molecules on antigen-presenting cells.
