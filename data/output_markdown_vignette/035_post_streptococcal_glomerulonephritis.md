## Vignette

A 23-year-old man presents to the clinic because of dark-colored urine and facial puffiness for 3 days. He reports that 2 weeks ago he developed a sore throat associated with fever that resolved without treatment. Temperature is 37.2°C, blood pressure is 152/94 mmHg, pulse is 84/min, and respiratory rate is 16/min. Physical examination reveals mild periorbital edema. Laboratory studies show serum creatinine of 1.8 mg/dL and decreased serum C3 complement levels. Urinalysis demonstrates hematuria, mild proteinuria, and red blood cell casts. Antistreptolysin O titers are elevated. Renal biopsy demonstrates enlarged hypercellular glomeruli with subepithelial immune complex deposits appearing as “humps” on electron microscopy.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
