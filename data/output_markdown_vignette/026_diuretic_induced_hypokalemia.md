## Vignette

A 72-year-old man is brought to the emergency department because of progressive muscle weakness and palpitations for 2 days. He has a history of hypertension and chronic heart failure treated with furosemide. Temperature is 36.7°C, blood pressure is 98/60 mmHg, pulse is 118/min and irregular, and respiratory rate is 18/min. Physical examination demonstrates decreased deep tendon reflexes and mild lower-extremity cramping. Laboratory studies show serum potassium of 2.7 mEq/L, sodium of 138 mEq/L, chloride of 92 mEq/L, bicarbonate of 33 mEq/L, and magnesium of 1.4 mg/dL. Electrocardiogram demonstrates flattened T waves, U waves, and premature ventricular contractions.
Which of the following best explains this patient’s electrolyte abnormality?
