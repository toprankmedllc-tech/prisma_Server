## Chief Complaint

Excessive daytime sleepiness and sudden episodes of collapsing while laughing

## Vignette

A 24-year-old medical student is brought to the clinic because of excessive daytime sleepiness and episodes of suddenly collapsing when laughing with friends. He reports vivid dream-like hallucinations when falling asleep and occasionally wakes up unable to move for several seconds. Overnight sleep study demonstrates rapid entry into REM sleep shortly after sleep onset.

Which of the following is the most likely underlying pathophysiologic abnormality?

A. Degeneration of orexin-producing neurons in the hypothalamus

B. Obstruction of the upper airway during sleep

C. Decreased dopamine levels in the substantia nigra

D. Lesion of the suprachiasmatic nucleus

E. Excessive slow-wave sleep during NREM stage 3
