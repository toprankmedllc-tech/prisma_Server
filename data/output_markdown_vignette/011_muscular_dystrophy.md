## Chief Complaint

Clumsy, frequent falls.

## Vignette

A 4-year-old male is brought to the clinic by his mother due to concerns about his physical development. She reports that he has become increasingly clumsy, frequently falling while playing, and has significant difficulty keeping up with his peers at preschool. He achieved his motor milestones slightly behind schedule, walking independently at 16 months. On examination, the boy is noted to use his hands to "walk up" his thighs in order to transition from a sitting position on the floor to a standing position. Physical examination reveals prominent enlargement of the calves bilaterally and an exaggerated lumbar curve when standing. Laboratory results are significant for a serum creatine kinase level of 15,000 U/L (normal < 200 U/L) and mildly elevated aspartate aminotransferase (AST).
