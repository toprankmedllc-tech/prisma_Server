## Chief Complaint

Blistering skin lesions on the backs of his hands that worsen with sun exposure

## Vignette

A 48-year-old man presents with blistering skin lesions on the backs of his hands that worsen with sun exposure. Physical examination reveals hyperpigmented skin and increased facial hair growth. Vital signs show temperature of 36.8°C, heart rate of 76/min, blood pressure of 124/80 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 14/min. Laboratory studies show urine uroporphyrin of 1,850 µg/24 hr (reference <50 µg/24 hr), serum ferritin of 620 ng/mL, transferrin saturation of 52%, and normal urinary PBG. The patient has a history of chronic alcohol use and hepatitis C infection.
