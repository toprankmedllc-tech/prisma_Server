## Chief Complaint

Severe vaginal itching with thick white discharge

## Vignette

A 28-year-old woman comes to the clinic due to intense vaginal itching and discomfort for the past 5 days. She reports thick vaginal discharge and pain during intercourse. The patient recently completed a 10-day course of broad-spectrum antibiotics for sinusitis. She denies fever, abdominal pain, or foul-smelling discharge. She is sexually active with one partner and uses oral contraceptive pills. Pelvic examination reveals vulvar erythema and thick white adherent discharge along the vaginal walls. Vaginal pH is 4.2. Potassium hydroxide preparation demonstrates budding yeast with pseudohyphae. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
