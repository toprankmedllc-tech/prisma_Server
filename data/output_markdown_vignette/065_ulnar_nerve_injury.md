## Vignette

A 19-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after falling off his skateboard and landing directly on his elbow. He immediately develops severe pain around the elbow and difficulty moving his hand. On physical examination, there is swelling and tenderness over the medial aspect of the elbow. He is unable to abduct or adduct his fingers against resistance and reports numbness over the medial one and a half fingers of the affected hand. Grip strength is decreased.
X-ray imaging reveals a fracture involving a bony prominence at the distal humerus.
