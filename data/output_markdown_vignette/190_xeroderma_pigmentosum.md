## Chief Complaint

 Severe sun sensitivity and progressive skin lesions

## Vignette

An 8-year-old girl is brought to the pediatric clinic because of worsening skin lesions and severe sensitivity to sunlight. Her parents report that she develops blistering sunburns after only brief outdoor exposure. Over the past 2 years, she has developed numerous dark freckles over her face and arms despite regular sunscreen use. She also complains of dry eyes and difficulty tolerating bright light. Physical examination shows xerotic skin with multiple hyperpigmented macules and areas of poikiloderma over sun-exposed regions. Ophthalmologic examination reveals keratitis and corneal clouding. Her maternal uncle died from metastatic melanoma at a young age. Genetic testing confirms a defect in DNA nucleotide excision repair. Which of the following is the most important long-term management strategy for this patient?
