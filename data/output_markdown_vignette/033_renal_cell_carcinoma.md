## Vignette

A 64-year-old man presents to the clinic because of intermittent painless hematuria and dull left flank pain for the past 3 months. He has also noticed an unintentional 7-kg weight loss during this period. His medical history is significant for a 40-pack-year smoking history and long-standing hypertension. Temperature is 37.1°C, blood pressure is 168/94 mmHg, pulse is 84/min, and respiratory rate is 16/min. Physical examination reveals a palpable left flank mass. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 18.2 g/dL and serum calcium of 11.4 mg/dL. Urinalysis demonstrates numerous red blood cells without casts. CT scan of the abdomen reveals a large heterogeneous mass arising from the upper pole of the left kidney with extension into the renal vein.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
