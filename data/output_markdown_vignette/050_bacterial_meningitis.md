## Vignette

A 19-year-old man is brought to the emergency department by his roommate because of a severe headache, high fever, and confusion that began 6 hours ago. He is a college freshman who lives in a dormitory and returned from a weekend trip with his floormates two days ago. On arrival, his temperature is 39.8°C (103.6°F), blood pressure is 86/52 mm Hg, and pulse is 124/min. He is lethargic but arousable. Examination shows neck stiffness.  Numerous non-blanching purpuric lesions are present on the trunk and extremities. Lumbar puncture is performed; CSF analysis shows opening pressure of 320 mm H2O, 4500 neutrophils/µL, glucose 18 mg/dL (serum 110 mg/dL), and protein 280 mg/dL. Gram stain reveals gram-negative diplococci.
