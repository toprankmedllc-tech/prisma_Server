## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 67-year-old man is brought to the physician because of progressively slow movements and difficulty initiating walking over the past year. His wife reports that he has become less expressive and has developed a resting tremor in his right hand. On examination, he has cogwheel rigidity, bradykinesia, and decreased arm swing while walking. Degeneration of dopaminergic neurons in the substantia nigra pars compacta is suspected. Loss of dopamine in this patient most likely causes which of the following changes in the basal ganglia pathways?
