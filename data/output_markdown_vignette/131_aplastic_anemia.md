## Chief Complaint

Progressive fatigue, recurrent infections, and easy bruising

## Vignette

A 27-year-old woman presents with progressive fatigue, recurrent infections, and easy bruising over the past 2 months. She recently completed treatment with chloramphenicol for typhoid fever. Physical examination reveals pallor and scattered petechiae but no lymphadenopathy or splenomegaly. Vital signs show temperature of 38.1°C, heart rate of 104/min, blood pressure of 108/66 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 18/min. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 6.8 g/dL, WBC count of 1,800/mm³ with absolute neutrophil count of 340/mm³, platelet count of 22,000/mm³, and reticulocyte count of 0.3%. Bone marrow biopsy shows markedly hypocellular marrow with 8% cellularity replaced by fat.
