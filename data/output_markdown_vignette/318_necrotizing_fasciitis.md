## Vignette

A 67-year-old man presents to the emergency department with severe right lower extremity pain for 18 hours. He reports progressive swelling and worsening tenderness after scraping his shin while gardening 2 days ago. Past medical history is significant for poorly controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus, chronic kidney disease stage III, peripheral vascular disease, and obesity. His medications include insulin glargine, lisinopril, and atorvastatin.
Vital signs reveal:
* Temperature: 39.2°C (102.6°F)
* Blood pressure: 88/54 mmHg
* Heart rate: 124/min
* Respiratory rate: 24/min
Physical examination demonstrates diffuse edema of the right calf extending to the thigh with tense skin, erythema, and exquisite tenderness that appears disproportionate to the visible lesion. Several violaceous patches and hemorrhagic bullae are present. Palpation produces a subtle crackling sensation beneath the skin. Sensation over the involved area is decreased.
