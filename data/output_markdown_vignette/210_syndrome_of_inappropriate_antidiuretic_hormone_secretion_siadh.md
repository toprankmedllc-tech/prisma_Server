## Chief Complaint

Confusion and nausea

## Vignette

A 68-year-old man comes to the emergency department because of progressive confusion, nausea, and muscle cramps for 3 days. He has a 40-pack-year smoking history and was recently diagnosed with small cell lung carcinoma. Physical examination shows no peripheral edema, dry mucous membranes, or jugular venous distention. Laboratory studies reveal serum sodium of 118 mEq/L and serum osmolality of 260 mOsm/kg. Urine osmolality is 450 mOsm/kg, and urine sodium is elevated. Which of the following is the most likely cause of this patient’s hyponatremia?

## Main Clue

## Supporting Clue

Small cell lung carcinoma causing ectopic ADH secretion
