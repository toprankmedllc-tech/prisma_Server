## Vignette

A 32-year-old woman presents with fatigue, sore tongue, and difficulty concentrating. She is pregnant and has been taking anticonvulsant medication for epilepsy. Physical examination reveals glossitis. Vital signs show temperature of 36.9°C, heart rate of 88/min, blood pressure of 110/68 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 15/min. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 8.6 g/dL, MCV of 114 fL, reticulocyte count of 0.8%, serum vitamin B12 of 340 pg/mL, methylmalonic acid of 0.18 µmol/L (reference <0.40 µmol/L), and homocysteine of 26 µmol/L (reference 5–15 µmol/L). Peripheral smear shows macro-ovalocytes and hypersegmented neutrophils.
