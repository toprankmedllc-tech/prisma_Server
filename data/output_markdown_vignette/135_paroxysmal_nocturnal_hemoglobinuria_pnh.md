## Chief Complaint

Episodic dark-colored urine in the morning, especially after waking up

## Vignette

A 42-year-old man presents with episodic dark-colored urine in the morning, especially after waking up. He reports fatigue and mild shortness of breath. Vital signs show temperature of 36.9°C, heart rate of 96/min, blood pressure of 116/72 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 16/min. Physical examination reveals pallor but no splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 8.4 g/dL, MCV of 88 fL, reticulocyte count of 7.2%, LDH of 1,240 U/L, haptoglobin of <10 mg/dL, and urine positive for hemosiderin. Flow cytometry reveals absence of CD55 and CD59 on red blood cells.
