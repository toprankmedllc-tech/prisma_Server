## Vignette

A 35-year-old man presents with fever, bleeding gums, and fatigue for the past two weeks. Vital signs show temperature of 38.6°C, heart rate of 114/min, blood pressure of 86/50 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 24/min. Physical examination reveals pallor, gingival oozing, and diffuse ecchymoses. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 7.8 g/dL, platelet count of 28,000/mm³, WBC count of 18,400/mm³ with 54% promyelocytes containing multiple Auer rods on peripheral smear. The patient develops sudden bleeding from IV sites. Coagulation studies show PT of 24.8 seconds, PTT of 52.4 seconds, fibrinogen of 65 mg/dL, and D-dimer of 12.6 µg/mL.
