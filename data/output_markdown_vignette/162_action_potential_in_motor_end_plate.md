## Vignette

A physiology student is studying neuromuscular transmission using an isolated skeletal muscle preparation. During the experiment, the presynaptic membrane of the motor neuron terminal is selectively exposed to a toxin that blocks voltage-gated calcium (Ca²⁺) channels. Following electrical stimulation of the motor neuron, an action potential successfully reaches the presynaptic terminal, but skeletal muscle contraction does not occur.
Which of the following is the most likely explanation for the failure of muscle contraction in this experiment?
