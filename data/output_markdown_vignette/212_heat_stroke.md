## Chief Complaint

Collapse with altered mental status

## Vignette

A 22-year-old military recruit is brought to the emergency department after collapsing during outdoor training on a hot and humid day. Witnesses report that he became confused and staggered before losing consciousness. On arrival, he is obtunded and profusely sweating. His temperature is 41.3°C (106.3°F). Laboratory studies show elevated creatine phosphokinase levels and acute kidney injury. Which of the following is the most appropriate next step in management?


## Key Symptoms

 Confusion ; collapse ; profuse sweating ; loss of consciousness ; tachycardia


## Labs

 Elevated creatine phosphokinase ; elevated creatinine ; elevated BUN ; mild transaminitis


## Physical Exam

 Profuse sweating ; hot skin ; obtunded mental status ; tachypnea

## Main Clue

Core temperature > 40°C with severe CNS dysfunction after strenuous exertion

## Supporting Clue

 Rhabdomyolysis and acute kidney injury
