## Vignette

A physiology researcher performs intracellular recordings from a ventricular myocyte and a skeletal muscle fiber. Both cells generate an action potential when stimulated. The skeletal muscle fiber repolarizes within several milliseconds, while the ventricular myocyte exhibits a prolonged plateau phase that lasts approximately 200 milliseconds before repolarization. When the researcher applies rapid repetitive stimulation, the skeletal muscle fiber summates and tetanizes, but the ventricular myocyte does not.Which of the following best explains why cardiac muscle cannot tetanize?

## Main Clue

Ventricular myocyte AP with a prolonged plateau (~200 ms) + inability to tetanize despite rapid repetitive stimulation

## Supporting Clue

The plateau phase coincides with the absolute refractory period of cardiac muscle — preventing repeat depolarization, summation of contractions, and re-entrant arrhythmias, while allowing time for ventricular filling

## Question


Which of the following best explains why cardiac muscle cannot tetanize?

## Correct Answer

B) The prolonged plateau creates a long refractory period that prevents summation
