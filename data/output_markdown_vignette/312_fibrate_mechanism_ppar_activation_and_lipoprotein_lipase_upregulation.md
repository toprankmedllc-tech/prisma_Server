## Vignette

A 44-year-old man presents 2 weeks after a second hospitalization for acute pancreatitis in 6 months. Multiple first-degree relatives have suffered myocardial infarctions before age 55. Physical examination reveals yellowish-orange papular deposits over the bilateral Achilles tendons and extensor surfaces of the elbows. Fasting lipid panel shows:

Total cholesterol	305 mg/dL
HDL	34 mg/dL 
LDL	162 mg/dL
Triglycerides	1,380 mg/dL

The physician initiates a drug that increases his risk of gallstone disease.
The expected reduction in triglycerides from this drug is primarily due to which of the following mechanisms?

A) Inhibition of hepatic HMG-CoA reductase 
B) Activation of PPAR-α
C) Blockade of intestinal cholesterol absorption via NPC1L1 
D) Inhibition of PCSK9
E) Nicotinic acid receptor activation
