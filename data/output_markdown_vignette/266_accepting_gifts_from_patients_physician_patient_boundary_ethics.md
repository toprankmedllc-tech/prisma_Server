## Vignette

A 72-year-old woman presents for a routine follow-up visit with her primary care physician, whom she has been seeing regularly for the past 15 years. Over this time, the physician has managed her chronic hypertension and diabetes and supported her through several major life events, including the loss of her spouse. At the end of today’s visit, she expresses gratitude for his care and offers a wrapped box of gourmet chocolates. She explains that she wanted to show appreciation for the support and continuity of care she has received over the years. The physician has never received gifts from this patient before.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response by the physician?
