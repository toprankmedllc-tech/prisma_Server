## Vignette

A 61-year-old man presents to the clinic because of worsening fatigue, generalized pruritus, and decreased exercise tolerance over the past several months. He has a 20-year history of poorly controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus and hypertension. Temperature is 36.9°C, blood pressure is 168/96 mmHg, pulse is 82/min, and respiratory rate is 16/min. Physical examination reveals bilateral lower-extremity edema and pale conjunctivae. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 8.9 g/dL, blood urea nitrogen of 52 mg/dL, creatinine of 4.1 mg/dL, potassium of 5.8 mEq/L, calcium of 7.8 mg/dL, and phosphate of 6.2 mg/dL. Urinalysis demonstrates persistent proteinuria. Renal ultrasound shows bilaterally shrunken echogenic kidneys.
Which of the following mechanisms most directly explains this patient’s anemia?
