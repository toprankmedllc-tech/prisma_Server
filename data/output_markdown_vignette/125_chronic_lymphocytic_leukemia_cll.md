## Chief Complaint

Fatigue and painless lymphadenopathy

## Vignette

A 70-year-old man presents with fatigue and painless lymphadenopathy. He reports recurrent respiratory infections over the past year. Vital signs show temperature of 37.3°C, heart rate of 88/min, blood pressure of 126/78 mm Hg, and respiratory rate of 16/min. Physical examination reveals generalized lymphadenopathy and mild splenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 11.4 g/dL, platelet count of 158,000/mm³, WBC count of 72,000/mm³ with 82% small mature lymphocytes and numerous smudge cells on peripheral smear. Flow cytometry shows CD5+, CD20+ (dim), and CD23+ B cells.
