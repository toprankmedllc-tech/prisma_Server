## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 27-year-old man is brought to the clinic because of excessive thirst and frequent urination for the past 4 months. He reports waking up multiple times each night to drink water and urinate. He recently sustained a traumatic head injury in a motor vehicle accident. Laboratory studies show serum sodium of 151 mEq/L and serum osmolality of 308 mOsm/kg. Urine osmolality is 85 mOsm/kg. A water deprivation test is performed and demonstrates minimal increase in urine osmolality. Administration of desmopressin subsequently results in a marked increase in urine osmolality.
Which of the following is the most likely underlying mechanism of this patient’s condition?
