Physical examination reveals hypotonia, upward-slanting palpebral fissures, epicanthal folds, a protruding tongue, and a single transverse palmar crease. Abdominal radiography demonstrates a “double-bubble” sign. Echocardiography reveals an atrioventricular septal defect. Which of the following is the most likely underlying genetic mechanism responsible for this patient’s condition?

## Main Clue

Advanced maternal age with characteristic Down syndrome features (hypotonia, upslanting palpebral fissures, AV septal defect, double-bubble sign) indicating trisomy 21 due to meiotic nondisjunction.

## Supporting Clue
