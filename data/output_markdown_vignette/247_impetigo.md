## Vignette

A 6-year-old boy is brought to clinic because of worsening skin lesions around his nose and mouth for 5 days. His mother states that symptoms began after an upper respiratory infection and frequent nose rubbing. He has mild atopic dermatitis and often scratches his face.
He remains playful and afebrile.
Vital signs are normal.
Physical examination reveals multiple superficial erosions with golden-yellow crusts surrounding the nares and upper lip. Mild pruritus is present. No fluctuance, warmth, or deep tenderness is noted.
