Physical examination shows a thin, barrel-shaped chest, decreased breath sounds bilaterally, and mild hepatomegaly. Laboratory studies show:

ALP: 165 U/L
Total bilirubin: 1.8 mg/dL
Pulmonary function tests show ↓ FEV₁, ↓ FEV₁/FVC ratio, and ↑ total lung capacity. Which of the following best describes the underlying pathogenesis?
