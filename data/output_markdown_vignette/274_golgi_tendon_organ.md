## Vignette

A 24-year-old man begins an intense weightlifting program in preparation for a regional powerlifting competition. During a training session, he attempts to deadlift a weight far heavier than he has previously lifted. As he strains against the barbell, the muscle suddenly relaxes and he drops the weight despite maximal effort. His coach explains that this protective reflex occurs when specialized sensory receptors within the muscle-tendon junction detect excessive tension and trigger inhibition of the contracting muscle to prevent injury.
A medical student observing the training session asks which sensory receptor is primarily responsible for detecting this excessive muscle tension and mediating the protective reflex.
Which of the following structures is most likely responsible for this response?
