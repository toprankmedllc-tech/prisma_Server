## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 24-year-old woman presents with easy bruising and petechiae for the past 2 weeks. She recently recovered from a viral upper respiratory tract infection. Physical examination reveals scattered petechiae on the lower extremities but no hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory studies show hemoglobin of 13.2 g/dL, WBC count of 7,100/mm³, platelet count of 18,000/mm³, PT of 12.3 seconds, and aPTT of 28.1 seconds. Peripheral smear shows normal platelet morphology with occasional large platelets.
