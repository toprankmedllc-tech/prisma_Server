## Vignette

A 8-year-old boy is brought to the pediatric clinic because of progressive facial swelling and dark urine for the past week. His parents report that the swelling is most noticeable around the eyes in the morning. He recently recovered from an upper respiratory infection 2 weeks ago. Temperature is 36.8°C, blood pressure is 104/66 mmHg, pulse is 88/min, and respiratory rate is 18/min. Physical examination reveals periorbital edema and bilateral pitting edema of the lower extremities. Laboratory studies show serum albumin of 2.1 g/dL, total cholesterol of 312 mg/dL, and creatinine of 0.7 mg/dL. Urinalysis demonstrates 4+ proteinuria without hematuria, and urine microscopy reveals oval fat bodies. Renal biopsy shows normal glomeruli on light microscopy with diffuse effacement of podocyte foot processes on electron microscopy.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
