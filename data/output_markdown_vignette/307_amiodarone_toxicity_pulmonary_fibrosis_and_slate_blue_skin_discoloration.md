## Vignette

A 68-year-old man presents with a 5-month history of progressive exertional dyspnea and a persistent dry cough. He has a history of ischemic cardiomyopathy with recurrent ventricular tachycardia and hypertension. He has been taking several cardiac medications for years but cannot identify them by name. Temperature is 37.0°C, heart rate is 62/min, respirations are 19/min, blood pressure is 146/88 mmHg, and oxygen saturation is 91% on room air. Examination reveals a slate-blue discoloration of the face and dorsal hands confined to sun exposed areas. Bibasilar inspiratory crackles are present. Chest X-ray shows bilateral peripheral and basal reticular opacities. Pulmonary function testing demonstrates a restrictive pattern with a markedly reduced DLCO of 44% predicted. The patient's findings are most likely caused by an adverse effect of which of the following medications?

A) Digoxin 
B) Metoprolol 
C) Spironolactone 
D) Lisinopril
