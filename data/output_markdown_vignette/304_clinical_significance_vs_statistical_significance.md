## Vignette

Researchers conduct a randomized controlled trial comparing Drug A with standard therapy for treatment of a rare neuromuscular disorder. Drug A is available only at specialized tertiary referral centers and requires monthly intravenous infusions. A total of 6,000 patients are enrolled. After 18 months, patients receiving standard therapy have an average functional disability score of 50.0 compared with 48.8 among patients receiving Drug A (p < 0.001). Adverse effects occur in 12% of patients receiving standard therapy and 8% of patients receiving Drug A (p < 0.001). After reviewing the study, a neurologist decides not to routinely prescribe Drug A.

Which of the following best characterizes the neurologist's decision?
