## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

A 60-year-old man is admitted to the ICU following emergency surgery for bowel perforation. On hospital day 6, he develops fever, worsening productive cough, and shortness of breath. He has a history of chronic alcoholism and diabetes mellitus. Temperature is 39°C (102.2°F), blood pressure is 100/60 mm Hg, pulse is 112/min, and respiratory rate is 28/min. Crackles are heard over the right upper lung field. Chest x-ray shows cavitary consolidation in the right upper lobe. Gram stain of sputum demonstrates numerous neutrophils and gram-negative lactose-fermenting bacilli surrounded by a prominent polysaccharide capsule. Culture on MacConkey agar produces large, mucoid pink colonies.
