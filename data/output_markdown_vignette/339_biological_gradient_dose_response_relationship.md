## Vignette

Researchers investigate whether long-term exposure to Chemical X increases the risk of a rare neurologic disorder. Several observational studies from different countries have reported similar findings. In a recent cohort study, individuals with low exposure had a disease incidence of 2 cases per 10,000 persons, whereas individuals with high exposure had a disease incidence of 18 cases per 10,000 persons. Furthermore, workers exposed to Chemical X for longer durations were found to have progressively higher disease rates. Laboratory studies have demonstrated a mechanism by which Chemical X causes neuronal injury in animal models.

Which of the following principles supporting a causal relationship is most strongly demonstrated by these findings?
