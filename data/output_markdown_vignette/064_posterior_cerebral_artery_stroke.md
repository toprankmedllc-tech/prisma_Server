## Chief Complaint

Sudden inability to see objects on left side and visual recognition difficulty

## Vignette

A 71-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after suddenly bumping into objects on his left side while walking at home. His wife reports that he also seemed confused and was unable to recognize familiar objects placed in front of him. He has a history of hypertension, atrial fibrillation, and hyperlipidemia.

On examination, he is alert but repeatedly ignores stimuli coming from the left side of his visual field. When shown a pen, he can describe its color and shape but cannot identify its use. Neurologic examination reveals left homonymous hemianopia with macular sparing. No significant motor weakness is noted.

CT scan of the head shows no hemorrhage.

## Key Symptoms

Left homonymous hemianopia ; visual agnosia ; hemispatial neglect-like behavior ; confusion ; intact motor strength ; sudden onset
