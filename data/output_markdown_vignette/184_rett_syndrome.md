## Chief Complaint

## Vignette

An 18-month-old girl is brought to the pediatric clinic because of developmental regression. She had normal early development until approximately 12 months of age, when she gradually lost previously acquired language and fine motor skills. Her parents report repetitive “hand-wringing” movements and difficulty walking. Physical examination reveals microcephaly, truncal ataxia, and scoliosis. Genetic testing identifies a mutation in the MECP2 gene. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?
