## Buzzword Question

An HIV positive man with a CD4 count of 45 cells/mm³ presents with 2 weeks of headache and mental status changes. MRI of the brain shows multiple small, cystic, non enhancing "soap bubble" lesions in the basal ganglia and along perivascular spaces. The most likely organism is:

## Buzzwords Used

Advanced HIV (CD4 less than 100); subacute headache and mental status change; "soap bubble" non enhancing basal ganglia lesions; perivascular pseudocysts

## Answer Options

A) Toxoplasma gondii
B) JC virus
C) Cryptococcus neoformans
D) Cytomegalovirus

## Correct Answer

C) Cryptococcus neoformans

## Explanation

Cryptococcal organisms collect along perivascular (Virchow Robin) spaces in the basal ganglia, producing gelatinous "soap bubble" pseudocysts that are typically non enhancing on MRI. This pattern in an AIDS patient is highly characteristic of cryptococcal CNS disease.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Advanced HIV + subacute meningoencephalitis + "soap bubble" non enhancing basal ganglia lesions = Cryptococcus neoformans

## Wrong Options

### Toxoplasma gondii

Toxoplasma also affects AIDS patients but produces MULTIPLE RING ENHANCING lesions on MRI, often at the gray white junction or basal ganglia, not non enhancing soap bubble cysts.

**Buzzword Combo:** Advanced HIV + multiple ring enhancing lesions + positive Toxoplasma IgG = Toxoplasma gondii

### JC virus

JC virus reactivation causes progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy with multifocal NON enhancing WHITE MATTER (not basal ganglia) lesions in immunocompromised patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Advanced HIV or natalizumab + multifocal non enhancing white matter lesions = JC virus and PML

### Cytomegalovirus

CMV encephalitis in AIDS classically causes periventricular ENHANCEMENT (ventriculitis) with neutrophilic CSF, not soap bubble basal ganglia cysts.

**Buzzword Combo:** Advanced HIV + periventricular enhancement + retinitis with cotton wool spots = cytomegalovirus

## Related Concepts

Toxoplasmosis (ring enhancing lesions); PML (JC virus, white matter); CMV ventriculitis
