## Buzzword Question

A 25-year-old man has chronic progressive distal symmetric weakness with high-arched feet, hammer toes, marked distal calf atrophy giving his legs an inverted-bottle appearance, absent ankle reflexes, and a similar phenotype in his father and paternal grandfather. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Chronic distal symmetric weakness; pes cavus; hammer toes; stork-leg / inverted-bottle distal atrophy; absent ankle reflexes; autosomal-dominant family history

## Answer Options

A) Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease
B) Guillain-Barre syndrome
C) Diabetic peripheral neuropathy
D) Multifocal motor neuropathy

## Correct Answer

A) Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease

## Explanation

The combination of chronic distal symmetric weakness, pes cavus and hammer toes, stork-leg atrophy, absent ankle reflexes, and autosomal-dominant family history is the classic clinical pattern of Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Chronic distal weakness + pes cavus + stork legs + absent ankle reflexes + autosomal-dominant family history = Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease

## Wrong Options

### Guillain-Barre syndrome

GBS presents with ACUTE ascending flaccid paralysis and areflexia after a recent infection, not chronic hereditary distal weakness with foot deformity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ascending flaccid paralysis + areflexia + recent Campylobacter diarrhea = Guillain-Barre syndrome

### Diabetic peripheral neuropathy

Diabetic neuropathy produces a length-dependent sensorimotor neuropathy with burning pain and distal sensory loss in patients with longstanding hyperglycemia, not hereditary foot deformity with normal glycemic control.

**Buzzword Combo:** Long-standing diabetes + stocking-glove burning pain + reduced vibration sense = diabetic peripheral neuropathy

### Multifocal motor neuropathy

MMN is an acquired immune-mediated motor neuropathy with ASYMMETRIC weakness, conduction block on nerve studies, and response to IVIG, not the hereditary symmetric distal pattern with foot deformity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asymmetric upper-limb weakness + conduction block + anti-GM1 antibodies + responds to IVIG = multifocal motor neuropathy
