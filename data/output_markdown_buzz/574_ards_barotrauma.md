## Buzzword Question

A 58-year-old man with septic shock due to pneumonia is intubated for severe hypoxemia. Despite 100% FiO₂, his oxygenation remains poor, and chest imaging shows bilateral diffuse infiltrates consistent with ARDS. He is placed on mechanical ventilation with the following settings: tidal volume 6 mL/kg, PEEP 12 cm H₂O, and plateau pressure <30 cm H₂O. Why is low tidal volume ventilation used in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

ARDS; Sepsis; Pneumonia; Mechanical ventilation; Low tidal volume; Bilateral infiltrates; Hypoxemia; High PEEP; Plateau pressure <30

## Answer Options

A) Better oxygenation
B) Prevent barotrauma
C) Increase CO₂ clearance
D) Improve cardiac output

## Correct Answer

B) Prevent barotrauma

## Explanation

Low tidal volume ventilation is used in ARDS to minimize overdistension of fragile, non-compliant alveoli and prevent ventilator-induced lung injury, especially barotrauma and volutrauma. This lung-protective strategy reduces mortality in ARDS.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

ARDS + low tidal volume + decreased compliance + high PEEP = prevent ventilator-induced lung injury (barotrauma)

## Wrong Options

### A) Better oxygenation

Oxygenation in ARDS is improved primarily by PEEP and recruitment of alveoli, not by increasing tidal volume, which can worsen lung injury.

**Buzzword Combo:** ARDS + hypoxemia + bilateral infiltrates + low PaO₂/FiO₂ = oxygenation improves via PEEP, not tidal volume

### C) Increase CO₂ clearance

Low tidal volume ventilation actually reduces CO₂ elimination, often leading to permissive hypercapnia, which is tolerated in ARDS to protect the lungs.

**Buzzword Combo:** ARDS + hypoventilation strategy + permissive hypercapnia = CO₂ retention is accepted

### D) Improve cardiac output

Tidal volume reduction does not directly improve cardiac output; in fact, high intrathoracic pressures (not low tidal volume) typically reduce venous return and cardiac output.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mechanical ventilation + high intrathoracic pressure + reduced venous return = cardiac output effects unrelated to low tidal volume
