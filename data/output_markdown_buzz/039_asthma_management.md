## Buzzword Question

A 24-year-old woman with poorly controlled asthma develops oral thrush after frequent use of her inhaler. The medication most likely responsible acts through which of the following mechanisms?

## Buzzwords Used

Asthma; Thrush; Inhaler

## Answer Options

 A) Inhibition of phospholipase A2 transcription factors
 B) Blockade of M3 muscarinic receptors
 C) Increased cAMP via β2-receptor stimulation
 D) Inhibition of phosphodiesterase

## Correct Answer

 A) Inhibition of phospholipase A2 transcription factors

## Explanation

This patient developed oropharyngeal candidiasis from an inhaled corticosteroid such as fluticasone or budesonide. Corticosteroids suppress inflammatory gene transcription and inhibit phospholipase A2 activity through increased lipocortin expression.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Asthma + oral thrush + inhaler = Inhaled corticosteroids

## Wrong Options

### Blockade of M3 muscarinic receptors

This mechanism describes antimuscarinic agents such as ipratropium and tiotropium, which are commonly associated with dry mouth and urinary retention rather than oral candidiasis.

**Buzzword Combo:** COPD + dry mouth + M3 blockade = Antimuscarinic

###  Increased cAMP via β2-receptor stimulation

This mechanism describes β2-agonists such as albuterol, which increase cAMP and cause bronchodilation. Major adverse effects include tremor and tachycardia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Bronchodilation + tremor + ↑cAMP = β2-agonist

### Inhibition of phosphodiesterase

This mechanism describes theophylline, a methylxanthine bronchodilator associated with seizures, arrhythmias, and narrow therapeutic index toxicity.

**Buzzword Combo:** COPD + seizures + PDE inhibition = Theophylline
