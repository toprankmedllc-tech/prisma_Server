## Buzzword Question

A 56-year-old woman has fatigue and pallor. Peripheral blood smear shows large oval red blood cells and neutrophils with increased nuclear lobes. Laboratory studies show increased MCV. The most likely underlying mechanism is:

## Buzzwords Used

56-year-old woman; fatigue; pallor; large oval red blood cells; hypersegmented neutrophils; increased MCV

## Answer Options

A) Impaired DNA synthesis in erythroid precursors

B) Defective α-globin chain production

C) Decreased iron availability for heme synthesis

D) Increased destruction of red blood cells in the spleen

## Correct Answer

A) Impaired DNA synthesis in erythroid precursors


## Explanation

Vitamin B12 deficiency causes impaired DNA synthesis. When DNA synthesis is defective, erythroid precursors remain in the bone marrow longer and fail to mature normally. This produces large, immature red blood cells, causing macrocytic/megaloblastic anemia. Neutrophils also spend longer in the marrow, leading to hypersegmented neutrophils.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

High MCV + macro-ovalocytes + hypersegmented neutrophils = megaloblastic anemia

## Wrong Options

### B. Defective α-globin chain production


This describes α-thalassemia, which causes microcytic anemia due to decreased α-globin synthesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Microcytic anemia + α-globin deletion = α-thalassemia

### C. Decreased iron availability for heme synthesis


This can occur in iron deficiency anemia or anemia of chronic disease, both of which are usually microcytic or normocytic.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low iron + low MCV = impaired heme synthesis

### D. Increased destruction of red blood cells in the spleen


This describes extravascular hemolysis, which causes anemia due to premature RBC destruction rather than impaired DNA synthesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Splenomegaly + jaundice + spherocytes = extravascular hemolysis
