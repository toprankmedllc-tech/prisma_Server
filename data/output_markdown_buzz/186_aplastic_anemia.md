## Buzzword Question

A 27-year-old patient with pancytopenia and a hypocellular marrow with fatty replacement most likely has immune-mediated destruction of which of the following?

## Buzzwords Used

pancytopenia; hypocellular marrow; fatty replacement; immune-mediated destruction

## Answer Options

A. Hematopoietic stem cells
B. Mature neutrophils
C. Platelet precursors only
D. Erythrocyte membranes

## Correct Answer

A. Hematopoietic stem cells

## Explanation

Aplastic anemia is caused by T-cell–mediated immune destruction of hematopoietic stem cells, leading to pancytopenia and fatty marrow replacement.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Stem cell destruction + pancytopenia + hypocellular marrow = Aplastic anemia

## Wrong Options

### B. Mature neutrophils

Autoimmune destruction of mature neutrophils causes isolated neutropenia, not pancytopenia or marrow failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isolated neutropenia + normal marrow = Autoimmune neutropenia

### C. Platelet precursors only

ITP involves destruction of platelets or megakaryocytes leading to isolated thrombocytopenia, not pancytopenia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isolated thrombocytopenia + megakaryocyte increase = ITP

### D. Erythrocyte membranes

Autoimmune hemolytic anemia affects mature RBCs causing hemolysis, positive Coombs test, and does not cause fatty marrow or pancytopenia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Positive Coombs + spherocytes = Autoimmune hemolytic anemia
