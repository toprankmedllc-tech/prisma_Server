## Buzzword Question

A 17-year-old boy is brought to the emergency department after falling from a bicycle and striking the side of his head. He briefly loses consciousness, regains awareness, and later becomes progressively confused and drowsy. CT scan of the head reveals a lens-shaped hyperdense collection that does not cross suture lines. Which of the following structures was most likely injured?

## Buzzwords Used

Lucid interval; temporal bone trauma; lens-shaped hematoma; does not cross sutures; rapidly expanding bleed

## Answer Options

A) Middle meningeal artery

B) Bridging veins

C) Anterior communicating artery

D) Superior sagittal sinus

## Correct Answer

A) Middle meningeal artery

## Explanation

Epidural hematoma is usually caused by rupture of the middle meningeal artery following temporal bone fracture. Patients classically present with a lucid interval followed by rapid neurologic deterioration.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Temporal bone trauma + lucid interval + lens-shaped hematoma = Epidural hematoma

## Wrong Options

### B) Bridging veins

Bridging vein rupture causes subdural hematoma, which appears crescent-shaped and commonly occurs in elderly patients after trauma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Elderly patient + crescent-shaped bleed + bridging veins = Subdural hematoma

### C) Anterior communicating artery

Anterior communicating artery aneurysm rupture causes subarachnoid hemorrhage presenting with thunderclap headache rather than trauma-associated lucid interval.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thunderclap headache + subarachnoid blood + berry aneurysm = Subarachnoid hemorrhage

### D) Superior sagittal sinus

Superior sagittal sinus thrombosis presents with headache, seizures, and signs of increased intracranial pressure rather than epidural bleeding.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hypercoagulable state + headache + venous thrombosis = Dural venous sinus thrombosis
