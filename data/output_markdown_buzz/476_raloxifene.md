## Buzzword Question

A 67-year-old postmenopausal woman comes to the clinic for osteoporosis management after sustaining a vertebral compression fracture. She is started on a medication that acts as an estrogen agonist in bone but antagonist in both breast and uterine tissue. Several months later, she develops hot flashes and leg swelling. Which of the following drugs was most likely prescribed?

## Buzzwords Used

Postmenopausal woman ; osteoporosis ; vertebral compression fracture ; SERM ; agonist at bone ; antagonist at breast and uterus ; hot flashes ; thromboembolic risk

## Answer Options

A) Tamoxifen
B) Raloxifene
C) Clomiphene
D) Alendronate

## Correct Answer

B) Raloxifene

## Explanation

Raloxifene is a SERM that acts as an estrogen agonist in bone, helping prevent osteoporosis, while acting as an antagonist in breast and uterine tissue. Unlike tamoxifen, it does not increase the risk of endometrial cancer but still increases thromboembolic risk.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Postmenopausal osteoporosis + SERM without endometrial cancer risk + thromboembolic events = Raloxifene

## Wrong Options

### A) Tamoxifen

Tamoxifen is primarily used for ER-positive breast cancer and acts as a uterine agonist, increasing the risk of endometrial carcinoma.

**Buzzword Combo:** ER-positive breast cancer + uterine agonist activity + endometrial cancer risk = Tamoxifen

### C) Clomiphene

Clomiphene is used to induce ovulation in infertility due to anovulation, especially in PCOS.

**Buzzword Combo:** Infertility + PCOS + ovulation induction = Clomiphene

### D) Alendronate

Alendronate inhibits osteoclast-mediated bone resorption and is commonly used for osteoporosis but is not a SERM.

**Buzzword Combo:** Osteoporosis + osteoclast inhibition + esophagitis risk = Alendronate
