## Buzzword Question

A young boy from West Africa presents with a rapidly enlarging jaw mass. Biopsy shows a "starry-sky" appearance with tingible body macrophages and c-MYC activation. What is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

West Africa; rapidly enlarging jaw mass; starry-sky appearance; tingible body macrophages; c-MYC activation

## Answer Options

A) Hodgkin lymphoma
B) Burkitt lymphoma
C) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
D) Multiple myeloma

## Correct Answer

B. Burkitt lymphoma

## Explanation

Endemic Burkitt lymphoma is an aggressive B-cell lymphoma strongly associated with EBV and c-MYC translocation t(8;14). It classically presents in African children with a rapidly growing jaw mass and starry-sky histology.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Endemic Burkitt lymphoma is an aggressive B-cell lymphoma strongly associated with EBV and c-MYC translocation t(8;14). It classically presents in African children with a rapidly growing jaw mass and starry-sky histology.

## Wrong Options

### A. Hodgkin lymphoma

Hodgkin lymphoma features Reed-Sternberg cells and presents with painless lymphadenopathy and B symptoms rather than a rapidly enlarging jaw mass.

**Buzzword Combo:** Reed-Sternberg cells + B symptoms + lymphadenopathy = Hodgkin lymphoma

### C. Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

CLL is an indolent B-cell leukemia of elderly patients characterized by lymphocytosis and smudge cells, not a pediatric jaw mass.

**Buzzword Combo:** Smudge cells + elderly + lymphocytosis = CLL

### D. Multiple myeloma

Multiple myeloma is a plasma cell malignancy in older adults presenting with CRAB features and lytic bone lesions, not jaw masses in children.

**Buzzword Combo:** CRAB + M protein + lytic lesions = Multiple myeloma
