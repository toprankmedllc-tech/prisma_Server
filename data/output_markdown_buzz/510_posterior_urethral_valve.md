## Buzzword Question

A fetus develops severe bilateral hydronephrosis due to bladder outlet obstruction from posterior urethral valves. Which of the following fetal complications is most strongly associated with this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Posterior urethral valves; Hydronephrosis

## Answer Options

A) Polyhydramnios due to impaired swallowing
B) Pulmonary hypoplasia due to oligohydramnios
C) Neural tube defects due to folate deficiency
D) Meconium ileus due to pancreatic insufficiency

## Correct Answer

B) Pulmonary hypoplasia due to oligohydramnios

## Explanation

Posterior urethral valves can cause severe fetal urinary tract obstruction leading to oligohydramnios due to decreased fetal urine output. Reduced amniotic fluid impairs normal lung development and may result in pulmonary hypoplasia as part of Potter sequence

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

urinary obstruction + oligohydramnios + pulmonary hypoplasia = Potter sequence

## Wrong Options

### Polyhydramnios due to impaired swallowing

Polyhydramnios commonly occurs in conditions that impair fetal swallowing, such as esophageal atresia or anencephaly, rather than urinary tract obstruction. Excess amniotic fluid accumulates because swallowed fluid cannot be properly absorbed

**Buzzword Combo:** Impaired swallowing + excess fluid + esophageal atresia = Polyhydramnios

### Neural tube defects due to folate deficiency

Neural tube defects result from failure of neural tube closure during embryogenesis and are associated with folate deficiency. Common examples include spina bifida and anencephaly

**Buzzword Combo:** Folate deficiency + failed neural tube closure = Neural tube defects

### Meconium ileus due to pancreatic insufficiency

Cystic fibrosis is associated with meconium ileus due to thick abnormal secretions causing neonatal intestinal obstruction. Patients also commonly develop pancreatic insufficiency and recurrent pulmonary infections

**Buzzword Combo:** Pancreatic insufficiency + meconium ileus + CFTR mutation = Cystic fibrosis
