## Buzzword Question

A severely underweight patient with anorexia nervosa is started on pharmacotherapy for comorbid depression. Which medication is contraindicated because it lowers the seizure threshold?


## Buzzwords Used

anorexia nervosa; seizure threshold; underweight; antidepressant contraindication

## Answer Options

 A) Fluoxetine
 B) Sertraline
 C) Bupropion
 D) Olanzapine


## Correct Answer

C. Bupropion


## Explanation

Bupropion is contraindicated in patients with eating disorders because it lowers the seizure threshold. Malnutrition and electrolyte abnormalities further increase seizure risk.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Eating disorder + seizure risk + antidepressant contraindication = Bupropion


## Wrong Options

### A. Fluoxetine


SSRIs such as fluoxetine are commonly used to manage comorbid depression and anxiety disorders in eating disorder patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Bulimia nervosa + depression + SSRI treatment = Fluoxetine


### B. Sertraline


Sertraline is an SSRI and does not significantly lower seizure threshold like bupropion.


**Buzzword Combo:** Depression + anxiety + SSRI = Sertraline


### D. Olanzapine


Olanzapine may be used in selected anorexia nervosa patients to promote weight gain.


**Buzzword Combo:** Weight gain promotion + atypical antipsychotic + anorexia nervosa = Olanzapine
