## Buzzword Question

A 30-year-old patient with fatigue, recurrent infections, easy bruising, pancytopenia, and a hypocellular marrow with fatty replacement and no malignant infiltration most likely has which condition?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; recurrent infections; easy bruising; pancytopenia; hypocellular marrow; fatty replacement; no malignant infiltration

## Answer Options

A. Acute myeloid leukemia
B. Aplastic anemia
C. Chronic myeloid leukemia
D. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

## Correct Answer

B. Aplastic anemia

## Explanation

Aplastic anemia is a bone marrow failure syndrome characterized by pancytopenia and a hypocellular fatty marrow without blasts or malignancy. It results from immune-mediated destruction of hematopoietic stem cells.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pancytopenia + hypocellular fatty marrow = Aplastic anemia

## Wrong Options

### A. Acute myeloid leukemia

AML typically shows pancytopenia but with a hypercellular marrow containing myeloblasts and Auer rods, not fatty hypocellular marrow.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hypercellular marrow + Auer rods + blasts = AML

### C. Chronic myeloid leukemia

CML presents with marked leukocytosis, basophilia, and Philadelphia chromosome, not pancytopenia or marrow failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Leukocytosis + basophilia + t(9;22) = CML

### D. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

ITP causes isolated thrombocytopenia with normal or increased megakaryocytes, not pancytopenia or fatty marrow.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isolated thrombocytopenia + megakaryocytes = ITP
