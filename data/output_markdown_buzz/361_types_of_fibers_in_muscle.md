## Buzzword Question

A 24-year-old man begins training for a long-distance triathlon. After several months of endurance conditioning, he notices significant improvement in his ability to perform prolonged exercise without muscle fatigue. A muscle biopsy performed during a physiology research study demonstrates increased numbers of fibers with abundant mitochondria, high myoglobin content, and dense capillary networks. These fibers contract slowly and rely primarily on aerobic metabolism.
Which of the following muscle fiber types is most responsible for this athlete’s improved endurance performance?

## Buzzwords Used

a long-distance triathlon; abundant mitochondria, high myoglobin content, and dense capillary networks

## Answer Options

A. Type I muscle fibers
B. Type IIb muscle fibers
C. Type IIx fast glycolytic fibers
D. Fast fatigable muscle fibers

## Correct Answer

A. Type I muscle fibers

## Explanation

Type I muscle fibers are slow oxidative fibers specialized for endurance activities. They contain: Numerous mitochondria ,High myoglobin concentration ,Rich capillary supply ,Predominantly aerobic metabolism ,Slow contraction speed ,Excellent resistance to fatigue These adaptations make them highly effective for marathon running and triathlons.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Endurance athlete + aerobic metabolism + high myoglobin + fatigue resistance = Type I muscle fibers

## Wrong Options

### B. Type IIb muscle fibers

Type IIb fibers generate rapid and powerful contractions but fatigue quickly due to reliance on anaerobic glycolysis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Explosive activity + anaerobic metabolism + rapid fatigue = Type IIb fibers

### C. Type IIx fast glycolytic fibers

These fibers are adapted for short bursts of intense activity rather than prolonged endurance exercise.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fast contraction + glycolytic metabolism + poor endurance = Type IIx fibers

### D. Fast fatigable muscle fibers

Fast fatigable fibers produce high force output but are not resistant to fatigue during sustained exercise.

**Buzzword Combo:** High power output + quick exhaustion + short-duration activity = fast fatigable fibers
