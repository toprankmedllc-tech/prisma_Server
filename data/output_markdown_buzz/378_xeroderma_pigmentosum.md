## Buzzword Question

A teenager with severe photosensitivity has dry eyes, photophobia, multiple actinic keratoses, and progressive hearing loss. Genetic testing reveals defective DNA repair. Which disorder is most likely?

## Buzzwords Used

Photosensitivity ; actinic keratoses ; defective DNA repair ; progressive hearing loss

## Answer Options

 A) Xeroderma pigmentosum
 B) Neurofibromatosis type 1
 C) Tuberous sclerosis
 D) Sturge-Weber syndrome


## Correct Answer

 A) Xeroderma pigmentosum


## Explanation

Xeroderma pigmentosum causes defective nucleotide excision repair leading to photosensitivity, premalignant skin lesions, ocular disease, and progressive neurologic dysfunction.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Photosensitivity + premalignant skin lesions + defective DNA repair = Xeroderma pigmentosum


## Wrong Options

###  B) Neurofibromatosis type 1


NF1 presents with café-au-lait spots, neurofibromas, and Lisch nodules rather than UV sensitivity and DNA repair defects.


**Buzzword Combo:** Café-au-lait spots + neurofibromas + optic glioma = Neurofibromatosis type 1


###  C) Tuberous sclerosis


Tuberous sclerosis is characterized by hamartomas, seizures, and ash-leaf spots due to TSC gene mutations.


**Buzzword Combo:** Ash-leaf spots + seizures + renal angiomyolipomas = Tuberous sclerosis


### D) Sturge-Weber syndrome


Sturge-Weber syndrome presents with port-wine stain and leptomeningeal angiomas rather than severe UV sensitivity.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Port-wine stain + seizures + leptomeningeal angiomas = Sturge-Weber syndrome
