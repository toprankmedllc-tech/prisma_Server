## Buzzword Question

A woman with progressive exertional dyspnea and elevated pulmonary arterial pressure is started on a drug that increases cGMP in vascular smooth muscle. Which drug was most likely prescribed?


## Buzzwords Used

Dyspnea; Pulmonary pressure; cGMP

## Answer Options

A) Bosentan
B) Epoprostenol
C) Sildenafil
D) Montelukast


## Correct Answer

C) Sildenafil

## Explanation

Sildenafil is a phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor used in pulmonary hypertension. It increases cGMP levels in pulmonary vascular smooth muscle, leading to nitric oxide–mediated vasodilation. Common adverse effects include headache, flushing, and hypotension.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pulmonary hypertension + ↑cGMP + flushing = Sildenafil

## Wrong Options

###  Bosentan

Bosentan is an endothelin receptor antagonist used in pulmonary arterial hypertension and is associated with hepatotoxicity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pulmonary hypertension + endothelin blockade + hepatotoxicity = Bosentan

###  Epoprostenol

Epoprostenol is a prostacyclin analog that increases cAMP and causes potent pulmonary vasodilation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pulmonary hypertension + prostacyclin analog + ↑cAMP = Epoprostenol

### Montelukast

Montelukast is a leukotriene receptor antagonist used in asthma management.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asthma + aspirin sensitivity + leukotriene blockade = Montelukast
