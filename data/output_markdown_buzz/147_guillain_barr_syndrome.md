## Buzzword Question

A 30-year-old man develops ascending flaccid paralysis and areflexia 2 weeks after an episode of bloody diarrhea. The mechanism producing his peripheral nerve injury is best described as:

## Buzzwords Used

Antecedent Campylobacter diarrhea; ascending flaccid paralysis; areflexia

## Answer Options

A) Antibodies cross-reacting between bacterial lipopolysaccharide and peripheral nerve gangliosides (molecular mimicry)
B) Direct bacterial invasion of dorsal root ganglia
C) Toxin-mediated blockade of presynaptic acetylcholine release
D) Hereditary mutation in peripheral myelin protein 22 (PMP22)

## Correct Answer

A) Antibodies cross-reacting between bacterial lipopolysaccharide and peripheral nerve gangliosides (molecular mimicry)

## Explanation

Campylobacter jejuni LPS shares epitopes with gangliosides (GM1, GD1a) on peripheral nerve myelin. Antibodies raised against the bacteria cross-react with the host nerve, producing immune-mediated demyelination, the hallmark mechanism of GBS.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Antecedent Campylobacter + ascending flaccid paralysis + areflexia = molecular mimicry (anti-ganglioside antibodies) → GBS

## Wrong Options

###  Direct bacterial invasion of dorsal root ganglia

Describes viral ganglionitis (e.g., VZV reactivation causing shingles), not GBS. GBS is immune-mediated, not infectious within the nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Dermatomal vesicular rash + burning pain = VZV reactivation → herpes zoster

### Toxin-mediated blockade of presynaptic acetylcholine release

This is the mechanism of botulism (cleavage of SNARE proteins by botulinum toxin), which produces DESCENDING flaccid paralysis, opposite direction from GBS.

**Buzzword Combo:** Floppy baby + honey + descending paralysis = SNARE cleavage → botulism

### Hereditary mutation in peripheral myelin protein 22 (PMP22)

Describes Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 1A, a hereditary demyelinating neuropathy with slowly progressive distal weakness, high-arched feet, and stork-leg deformity, not acute post-infectious paralysis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic distal weakness + pes cavus + stork legs + PMP22 duplication = Charcot-Marie-Tooth

## Related Concepts

Botulism (SNARE cleavage, descending paralysis); Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease (PMP22); Herpes zoster (dorsal root ganglion reactivation)
