## Buzzword Question

A 16-year-old boy has delayed puberty and anosmia due to failure of migration of GnRH-producing neurons from the olfactory placode. Which hypothalamic function is primarily affected?

## Buzzwords Used

Anosmia ; delayed puberty ; GnRH deficiency ; Kallmann syndrome ; olfactory placode

## Answer Options

A) Hunger
B) Satiety
C) Sexual behavior
D) Circadian rhythm

## Correct Answer

C) Sexual behavior

## Explanation

The preoptic nucleus regulates sexual behavior and releases GnRH. Failure of GnRH neuron migration causes Kallmann syndrome.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Anosmia + delayed puberty + GnRH deficiency = Sexual behavior

## Wrong Options

### A) Hunger

Hunger is regulated mainly by the lateral hypothalamic nucleus stimulated by ghrelin.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ghrelin + feeding center + anorexia after lesion = Hunger

### B) Satiety

Satiety is mediated by the ventromedial nucleus stimulated by leptin.

**Buzzword Combo:** Leptin + obesity after lesion + hyperphagia = Satiety

### D) Circadian rhythm

Circadian rhythm is controlled by the suprachiasmatic nucleus near the optic chiasm.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sleep disturbance + optic chiasm + biologic clock = Circadian rhythm
