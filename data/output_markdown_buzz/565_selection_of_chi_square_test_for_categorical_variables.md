## Buzzword Question

A study examines whether smoking status (smoker vs nonsmoker) is associated with the presence or absence of chronic cough in a sample of adults.

Which statistical test is most appropriate?

## Buzzwords Used

Smoking status ; Chronic cough ; Two categorical variables ; Association

## Answer Options

A) ANOVA

B) Independent samples t-test

C) Pearson correlation coefficient

D) Chi-square test

## Correct Answer

D) Chi-square test

## Explanation

The chi-square test is used to evaluate the association between two categorical variables.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Categorical exposure + categorical outcome + association testing = Chi-square test

## Wrong Options

### A) ANOVA

ANOVA compares means of continuous variables across multiple groups.

**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous outcome + three or more groups + comparison of means = ANOVA

### B) Independent samples t-test

A t-test compares means of a continuous variable between two groups.

**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous outcome + two groups + mean comparison = Independent samples t-test

### C) Pearson correlation coefficient

Pearson correlation measures the relationship between two continuous variables.

**Buzzword Combo:** Two continuous variables + linear association + correlation measurement = Pearson correlation
