## Buzzword Question

A patient with anemia, jaundice, splenomegaly, and spherocytes on peripheral smear has increased osmotic fragility. Deficiency of which protein is most commonly associated with this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

anemia; jaundice; splenomegaly; spherocytes; osmotic fragility; RBC membrane defect

## Answer Options

A) Spectrin
B) Factor VIII
C) Pyruvate kinase
D) Beta-globin

## Correct Answer

A. Spectrin

## Explanation

Hereditary spherocytosis is most commonly due to spectrin (or ankyrin) defects, which disrupt vertical interactions in the RBC cytoskeleton. This leads to membrane loss, spherocyte formation, and increased splenic destruction.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Spectrin defect + spherocytes + elevated MCHC = Hereditary spherocytosis

## Wrong Options

### B. Factor VIII

Factor VIII deficiency causes hemophilia A, an X-linked bleeding disorder characterized by hemarthroses and prolonged aPTT. It does not affect RBC membrane structure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hemarthrosis + prolonged aPTT + X-linked = Hemophilia A

### C. Pyruvate kinase

Pyruvate kinase deficiency causes ATP depletion in RBCs, leading to chronic hemolysis with echinocytes (burr cells). Osmotic fragility and spherocytes are not typical.

**Buzzword Combo:** Burr cells + ATP depletion + chronic hemolysis = Pyruvate kinase deficiency

### D. Beta-globin

Beta-globin mutations cause sickle cell disease or beta-thalassemia, which present with sickling or target cells respectively, not spherocytes or osmotic fragility.

**Buzzword Combo:** HbS mutation + sickling + vaso-occlusion = Sickle cell disease
