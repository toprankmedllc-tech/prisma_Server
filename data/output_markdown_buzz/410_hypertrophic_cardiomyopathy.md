## Buzzword Question

A young athlete dies suddenly during practice. Autopsy reveals asymmetric septal hypertrophy. Histology of the myocardium is most likely to show:

## Buzzwords Used

Young athlete; sudden cardiac death; asymmetric septal hypertrophy

## Answer Options

A) Myocyte disarray with interstitial fibrosis
B) Concentric uniform myocyte hypertrophy
C) Wavy fibers with neutrophilic infiltrate
D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining

## Correct Answer


A) Myocyte disarray with interstitial fibrosis

## Explanation

The pathognomonic histologic finding in HCM is myocyte disarray with interstitial fibrosis, which serves as the substrate for re-entrant ventricular arrhythmias and sudden cardiac death.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Asymmetric septal hypertrophy + myocyte disarray + interstitial fibrosis = HCM

## Wrong Options

### 
B) Concentric uniform myocyte hypertrophy

Seen in pressure-overload hypertrophy from chronic hypertension or aortic stenosis — symmetric and organized, not disarrayed.

**Buzzword Combo:** Concentric uniform hypertrophy = pressure-overload LVH (HTN, AS)

### 
C) Wavy fibers with neutrophilic infiltrate

Seen in early myocardial infarction (12–24 hours), not in HCM.

**Buzzword Combo:** Wavy fibers + neutrophils = early MI (12–24 hr)

### D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining

The hallmark of cardiac amyloidosis, a restrictive cardiomyopathy with extracellular amyloid deposition

**Buzzword Combo:** Congo red + apple-green birefringence = amyloidosis
