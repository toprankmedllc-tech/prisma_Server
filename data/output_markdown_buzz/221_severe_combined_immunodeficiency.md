## Buzzword Question

A 2-month-old infant has had persistent diarrhea, recurrent pneumonia, oral white plaques, and severe viral respiratory infections since the neonatal period. Chest radiograph shows no visible thymic shadow. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

2-month-old infant; infections since the neonatal period; viral respiratory infections; oral white plaques; absent thymic shadow

## Answer Options

A) Bruton X-linked agammaglobulinemia
B) Severe combined immunodeficiency
C) Common variable immunodeficiency
D) Selective IgA deficiency

## Correct Answer

B) Severe combined immunodeficiency


## Explanation

Severe combined immunodeficiency causes profound T-cell dysfunction with impaired cell-mediated immunity and impaired T-cell–dependent humoral responses. It presents from birth or early infancy with recurrent viral, fungal, bacterial, and protozoal infections.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

2-month-old infant + infections since neonatal period + viral/fungal/bacterial infections + absent thymic shadow = Severe combined immunodeficiency

## Wrong Options

### A) Bruton X-linked agammaglobulinemia

Bruton agammaglobulinemia causes recurrent encapsulated bacterial infections starting around 6 months of age, after maternal IgG wanes. It does not cause broad viral, fungal, bacterial, and protozoal infections from birth.

**Buzzword Combo:** 6-month-old boy + recurrent encapsulated bacterial infections + absent tonsils/lymph nodes = Bruton X-linked agammaglobulinemia

### C) Common variable immunodeficiency

Common variable immunodeficiency presents later with recurrent sinopulmonary infections and low immunoglobulins. It is not the classic cause of severe infections beginning in the neonatal period.

**Buzzword Combo:** Older child/adult + recurrent sinopulmonary infections + low immunoglobulins = Common variable immunodeficiency

### D) Selective IgA deficiency

Selective IgA deficiency mainly causes mucosal infections and is often asymptomatic. It does not cause severe combined T- and B-cell immunodeficiency in early infancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recurrent mucosal infections + low IgA + normal IgG/IgM = Selective IgA deficiency

## Related Concepts

Bruton agammaglobulinemia; DiGeorge syndrome; Common variable immunodeficiency
