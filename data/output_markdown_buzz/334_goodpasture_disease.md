## Buzzword Question

Which of the following diseases is most strongly associated with linear IgG deposition along the glomerular basement membrane and hemoptysis?

## Buzzwords Used

IgG deposition; basement Membrane; Hemoptysis

## Answer Options

 A) Goodpasture syndrome
 B) IgA nephropathy
 C) Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis
 D) Membranous nephropathy

## Correct Answer

 A) Goodpasture syndrome

## Explanation

Goodpasture syndrome is caused by antibodies directed against type IV collagen in the glomerular and alveolar basement membranes. Patients classically develop rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis with hematuria and pulmonary hemorrhage.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hemoptysis + RPGN + linear IgG = Goodpasture syndrome

## Wrong Options

###  IgA nephropathy

IgA nephropathy is associated with mesangial IgA deposition and recurrent hematuria occurring shortly after upper respiratory infections

**Buzzword Combo:** URI + hematuria + mesangial IgA = IgA nephropathy

### Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis

Poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis occurs after streptococcal infection and is characterized by low complement levels and subepithelial hump-shaped deposits.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recent strep infection + low C3 + subepithelial humps = PSGN

### Membranous nephropathy

Membranous nephropathy is a nephrotic syndrome associated with subepithelial immune complex deposition producing a spike-and-dome appearance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Nephrotic syndrome + spike-and-dome deposits = Membranous nephropathy
