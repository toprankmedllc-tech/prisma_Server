## Buzzword Question

A 70-year-old man presents with fatigue and painless lymphadenopathy. Laboratory studies show lymphocytosis, and peripheral blood smear reveals fragile lymphocytes that appear as “smudge cells” during slide preparation. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

70-year-old man; fatigue; painless lymphadenopathy; lymphocytosis; smudge cells; fragile lymphocytes

## Answer Options

A) Chronic lymphocytic leukemia
B) Acute myeloid leukemia
C) Acute lymphoblastic leukemia
D) Hodgkin lymphoma

## Correct Answer

A. Chronic lymphocytic leukemia

## Explanation

Chronic lymphocytic leukemia is a malignancy of mature B cells that accumulate in blood, lymph nodes, and bone marrow. The fragile lymphocytes rupture during smear preparation, forming characteristic “smudge cells,” and patients typically present with lymphocytosis and painless lymphadenopathy in older age.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Smudge cells + lymphocytosis + elderly patient = CLL

## Wrong Options

### B. Acute myeloid leukemia

AML presents with myeloblasts and Auer rods and typically causes acute bone marrow failure with anemia, infections, and thrombocytopenia. Smudge cells are not a feature.

**Buzzword Combo:** Auer rods + MPO+ blasts + pancytopenia = AML

### C. Acute lymphoblastic leukemia

ALL is a pediatric leukemia characterized by TdT-positive lymphoblasts and bone marrow failure. It does not present with smudge cells or chronic lymphocytosis in elderly patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** TdT+ blasts + childhood + marrow failure = ALL

### D. Hodgkin lymphoma

Hodgkin lymphoma presents with painless lymphadenopathy and Reed-Sternberg cells on lymph node biopsy. Peripheral smear findings like smudge cells are not seen.

**Buzzword Combo:** Reed-Sternberg cells + owl-eye nucleoli + B symptoms = Hodgkin lymphoma
