## Buzzword Question

A patient learns that a diagnostic procedure was performed on the wrong chart due to an administrative labeling error in the hospital system. The patient is clinically stable but upset and asks for an explanation. The physician is preparing to respond to the patient.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response?

## Buzzwords Used

Medical error disclosure ; Administrative labeling error ; Patient distress ; Transparency in care

## Answer Options

A) “Nothing harmful occurred, so there is no need for concern or further explanation.”
B) “I will explain what happened and ensure you understand the situation, and we will review how this occurred.”
C) “This was caused entirely by administrative staff, and they will be responsible for explaining it to you.”
D) “We will document this as a routine system issue and move forward with your care.”

## Correct Answer

B) “I will explain what happened and ensure you understand the situation, and we will review how this occurred.”

## Explanation

Ethical practice requires transparent disclosure, patient-centered communication, and commitment to understanding and reviewing the error through appropriate systems processes.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Error event + patient concern + duty of disclosure = explain and review

## Wrong Options

### A) “Nothing harmful occurred, so there is no need for concern or further explanation.”

Even without harm, patients have the right to know about medical errors affecting their care.

**Buzzword Combo:** Error occurrence + dismissal + lack of disclosure = unethical minimisation

### C) “This was caused entirely by administrative staff, and they will be responsible for explaining it to you.”

Shifting blame avoids physician responsibility for disclosure and undermines system-based understanding of errors.

**Buzzword Combo:** System error + blame shifting + avoidance of disclosure = incorrect response

### D) “We will document this as a routine system issue and move forward with your care.”

Labeling a specific error as routine without explanation denies the patient informed understanding of what occurred.

**Buzzword Combo:** Medical error + normalization + lack of explanation = inappropriate response
