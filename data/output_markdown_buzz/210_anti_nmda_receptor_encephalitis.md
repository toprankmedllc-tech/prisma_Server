## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old woman has 3 weeks of subacute psychiatric symptoms, new seizures, repetitive lip smacking and facial grimacing, and CSF lymphocytic pleocytosis. Pelvic ultrasound reveals an ovarian mass. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Young woman; psychiatric symptoms; seizures; orofacial dyskinesias; lymphocytic CSF; ovarian mass (teratoma)

## Answer Options

A) Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease
B) Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis
C) Herpes simplex encephalitis
D) Hashimoto encephalopathy

## Correct Answer

B) Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis

## Explanation

Anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis is a paraneoplastic or autoimmune disorder, most commonly seen in young women with ovarian teratoma. It presents with psychiatric symptoms, seizures, orofacial dyskinesias, and autonomic instability, with lymphocytic CSF pleocytosis. Tumor removal and immunotherapy can produce dramatic recovery.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Young woman + psychiatric symptoms + seizures + orofacial dyskinesias + ovarian teratoma = anti-NMDA receptor encephalitis

## Wrong Options

### Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

CJD typically affects patients in their 60s, evolves with rapidly progressive dementia plus startle myoclonus, and is not associated with ovarian teratoma. The age and tumor association point strongly away from CJD.

**Buzzword Combo:** Rapid dementia + startle myoclonus + 1 Hz periodic EEG + positive 14-3-3 / RT-QuIC = Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease

### Herpes simplex encephalitis

HSV encephalitis presents with fever, focal deficits, and seizures with temporal lobe hemorrhage on MRI and RBCs in CSF, not orofacial dyskinesias with an associated ovarian mass.

**Buzzword Combo:** Acute fever + behavioral change + temporal lobe T2 hyperintensity + RBCs in CSF = HSV-1 encephalitis

### Hashimoto encephalopathy

Hashimoto encephalopathy is associated with high titers of anti-thyroid antibodies (anti-TPO) and is highly steroid responsive. It does not specifically produce orofacial dyskinesias or have a teratoma association.

**Buzzword Combo:** Subacute encephalopathy + high anti-TPO antibodies + steroid responsive = Hashimoto encephalopathy
