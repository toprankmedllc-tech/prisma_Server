## Buzzword Question

A 21-year-old athlete visits a sports medicine clinic after experiencing repeated episodes of muscle tightness during sprint training. During the neurologic examination, the physician taps the patient’s patellar tendon, causing immediate contraction of the quadriceps muscle along with relaxation of the hamstring muscles. A medical student asks how activation of muscle stretch receptors produces both contraction of the agonist muscle and inhibition of the antagonist muscle during the stretch reflex.
The physician explains that sensory fibers from muscle spindles enter the spinal cord and produce two simultaneous effects that coordinate the reflex response.
Which of the following spinal cord events is most directly responsible for this coordinated reflex action?

## Buzzwords Used

physician taps the patient’s patellar tendon, causing immediate contraction of the quadriceps muscle along with relaxation of the hamstring muscles

## Answer Options

A. Activation of inhibitory interneurons and α motor neurons
B. Activation of Golgi tendon organ Ib fibers
C. Inhibition of muscle spindle afferent fibers
D. Activation of postganglionic sympathetic neurons

## Correct Answer

A. Activation of inhibitory interneurons and α motor neurons

## Explanation

Muscle spindle Ia afferent fibers mediate the stretch reflex by directly activating α motor neurons supplying the stretched muscle while simultaneously activating inhibitory interneurons that suppress α motor neurons to the antagonist muscle. This coordinated mechanism produces contraction of the agonist muscle and relaxation of the antagonist muscle (reciprocal inhibition).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Muscle stretch reflex + reciprocal inhibition + agonist contraction = activation of inhibitory interneurons and α motor neurons

## Wrong Options

### B. Activation of Golgi tendon organ Ib fibers

Golgi tendon organs respond to excessive muscle tension rather than sudden muscle stretch.

**Buzzword Combo:** Muscle tension + tendon receptor + autogenic inhibition = Golgi tendon organ

### C. Inhibition of muscle spindle afferent fibers

Muscle spindle afferents are activated, not inhibited, during the stretch reflex.

**Buzzword Combo:** Decreased spindle firing + reduced stretch signaling ≠ stretch reflex

### D. Activation of postganglionic sympathetic neurons

Sympathetic fibers regulate smooth muscle and glandular activity, not skeletal muscle stretch reflexes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Autonomic output + smooth muscle innervation = sympathetic nervous system
