## Buzzword Question

A patient with long-standing rheumatoid arthritis develops nephrotic-range proteinuria and progressive renal dysfunction. Which of the following staining characteristics is most characteristic of this disease process?

## Buzzwords Used

Rheumatoid arthritis; Renal Dysfunction; Nephrotic proteinuria

## Answer Options

A) Linear IgG deposition on immunofluorescence microscopy
B) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining under polarized light
C) Silver staining demonstrating spike-and-dome appearance
D) Granular mesangial IgA deposition on immunofluorescence

## Correct Answer

Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining under polarized light

## Explanation

Amyloidosis is characterized by extracellular deposition of β-pleated sheet protein fibrils that stain with Congo red and demonstrate classic apple-green birefringence under polarized light microscopy. It is commonly associated with rheumatoid arthritis 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Congo red positivity + apple-green birefringence + β-pleated sheets = Amyloidosis

## Wrong Options

### Linear IgG deposition on immunofluorescence microscopy

Goodpasture syndrome demonstrates linear IgG deposition along the glomerular basement membrane due to antibodies against type IV collagen. Patients commonly develop rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis and pulmonary hemorrhage.

**Buzzword Combo:** Linear IgG deposition + anti-GBM antibodies + pulmonary hemorrhage = Goodpasture syndrome

### Silver staining demonstrating spike-and-dome appearance

Membranous nephropathy is characterized by subepithelial immune complex deposits producing a spike-and-dome appearance on silver stain. It commonly presents with nephrotic-range proteinuria in adults.

**Buzzword Combo:** Spike-and-dome deposits + subepithelial immune complexes = Membranous nephropathy

### Granular mesangial IgA deposition on immunofluorescence

IgA nephropathy is caused by mesangial IgA immune complex deposition and commonly presents with recurrent hematuria after mucosal infections. It is the most common glomerulonephritis worldwide.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mesangial IgA deposition + recurrent hematuria + mucosal infection = IgA nephropathy
