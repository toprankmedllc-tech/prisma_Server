## Buzzword Question

A physiology student is reviewing skeletal muscle contraction during a laboratory session. The professor explains that repeated power strokes generated by actin-myosin cross-bridge cycling pull thin filaments toward the center of the sarcomere. During active contraction, the student observes that some sarcomere regions decrease in width while others remain unchanged.
Which of the following regions shorten during the power stroke of skeletal muscle contraction?

## Buzzwords Used

shorten during the power stroke of skeletal muscle contraction

## Answer Options

A. A band only
B. H zone and I band
C. A band and H zone
D. Z disc and A band

## Correct Answer

B. H zone and I band

## Explanation

During skeletal muscle contraction, thin filaments slide toward the center of the sarcomere, causing shortening of regions containing only thin filaments (I band) and only thick filaments (H zone). The A band, which corresponds to the length of thick filaments, remains unchanged.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Sliding filament theory + thin filament movement + sarcomere shortening = H zone and I band decrease

## Wrong Options

### A. A band only

The A band represents the length of thick filaments and does not shorten during contraction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thick filament length + constant during contraction + myosin region = A band

### C. A band and H zone

Although the H zone shortens, the A band remains unchanged.

**Buzzword Combo:** Partial correct + thick filament constant + contraction mechanics = A band unchanged

### D. Z disc and A band

Z discs move closer together during contraction, but they are structural boundaries rather than sarcomere zones that shorten; the A band still remains constant.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sarcomere boundaries + move closer + not a shrinking band = Z discs
