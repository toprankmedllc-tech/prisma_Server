## Buzzword Question

A chronic autoimmune disease affects 2,000 individuals in a city. Over the next 5 years, a new therapy substantially improves survival and reduces disease-related mortality. Assuming no outbreaks occur and the rate of new disease cases remains unchanged, which of the following is most likely?

## Buzzwords Used

Improved survival ; Reduced mortality ; Chronic disease ; Stable incidence ; Disease duration

## Answer Options

A) Increased prevalence and increased incidence

B) Increased prevalence and unchanged incidence

C) Decreased prevalence and unchanged incidence

D) Unchanged prevalence and increased incidence

## Correct Answer

B) Increased prevalence and unchanged incidence

## Explanation

Improved survival increases disease duration, causing more individuals to be living with the disease at a given time. Because the rate of new cases remains unchanged, incidence does not change.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Longer survival + stable incidence + increased duration = increased prevalence with unchanged incidence

## Wrong Options

### A) Increased prevalence and increased incidence

Improved survival affects disease duration, not the rate at which new cases occur.

**Buzzword Combo:** Longer duration ≠ more new cases

### C) Decreased prevalence and unchanged incidence

As patients live longer with disease, the total number of affected individuals increases.

**Buzzword Combo:** Improved survival → more people living with disease

### D) Unchanged prevalence and increased incidence

The stem states that the rate of new cases remains unchanged, while prevalence rises due to increased duration.

**Buzzword Combo:** Stable incidence + longer duration ≠ unchanged prevalence
