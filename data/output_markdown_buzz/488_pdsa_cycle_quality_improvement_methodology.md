## Buzzword Question

A hospital's ICU team identifies that central line-associated bloodstream infection (CLABSI) rates are 3× the national benchmark. They hypothesize that a standardized insertion bundle will reduce infections, pilot it in one ICU bay for one month, track CLABSI rates weekly, and meet to review results. Based on findings, they revise the bundle and plan a wider rollout. Which phase of the PDSA cycle does 'revising the bundle based on findings and planning wider rollout' represent?

## Buzzwords Used

CLABSI; insertion bundle; piloted one bay one month; weekly tracking; results reviewed; revise and plan wider rollout

## Answer Options

A) Act — using data from the Study phase to modify the intervention and plan the next cycle or broader implementation 
B) Plan — setting the initial hypothesis and designing the intervention 
C) Do — implementing the pilot intervention and collecting data 
D) Study — analyzing the data collected during implementation

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

The PDSA (Plan-Do-Study-Act) cycle is the core QI improvement methodology: Plan = identify the problem, form a hypothesis, design the intervention and measurement; Do = implement the pilot intervention and collect data; Study = analyze and interpret the collected data (were results as expected?); Act = decide what to do based on findings,  adopt, adapt, or abandon. 'Revising the bundle based on findings' is the Act phase (adapt), and 'planning wider rollout' initiates the next PDSA cycle's Plan phase. Iterative PDSA cycles progressively refine interventions before spread.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

PDSA: Plan = design; Do = pilot + collect; Study = analyze; Act = adopt/adapt/abandon + plan next cycle


## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Plan — setting the initial hypothesis and designing the intervention

The Plan phase occurs at the START of the cycle,  identifying the problem (CLABSI 3× benchmark), forming the hypothesis (bundle will reduce CLABSI), and designing the measurement strategy. Revising based on results comes AFTER the Study phase, in the Act phase.

**Buzzword Combo:** Plan phase = problem identification + hypothesis + intervention design (START of cycle); occurs before Do


### 2: C) Do — implementing the pilot intervention and collecting data

The Do phase is the implementation phase, piloting the bundle in one bay and tracking weekly CLABSI rates. The question describes what happens after this data collection, when the team reviews results and modifies the intervention, this is the Act phase.

**Buzzword Combo:** Do phase = implementation + data collection; occurs after Plan, before Study

### 3: D) Study — analyzing the data collected during implementation

The Study phase involves analyzing and interpreting the collected weekly CLABSI data,  examining whether the bundle achieved the expected results. The revision decision made after this analysis is the Act phase. The question describes what follows the Study phase.

**Buzzword Combo:** Study phase = data analysis + interpretation; Act phase = decision based on Study results (adopt/adapt/abandon)
