## Buzzword Question

A 70-year-old woman with HFrEF (EF 28%) is being optimized on guideline-directed medical therapy. She is currently on carvedilol and lisinopril. Which of the following medications, when added to her regimen, has been shown to reduce both mortality and hospitalizations in patients with HFrEF?
A) Amlodipine
B) Spironolactone 
C) Hydralazine 
D) Digoxin

## Buzzwords Used

HFrEF; Medication reduce mortality

## Answer Options

A) Amlodipine
B) Spironolactone 
C) Hydralazine 
D) Digoxin

## Correct Answer

B) Spironolactone 

## Explanation

Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists (MRAs) such as spironolactone and eplerenone reduce mortality and hospitalizations in HFrEF when added to ACE inhibitor/ARB and beta-blocker therapy. They block aldosterone's effect on sodium retention and myocardial fibrosis. 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

HFrEF + ACEi/ARB + Beta-blocker + Spironolactone = Reduced Mortality 

## Wrong Options

### A) Amlodipine

Amlodipine (dihydropyridine CCB) is neutral in HFrEF, it does not reduce mortality but is safe to use for hypertension or angina if needed. Non-dihydropyridine CCBs (diltiazem, verapamil) are contraindicated in HFrEF due to negative inotropy

**Buzzword Combo:** Amlodipine + HFrEF = Safe but no mortality benefit

Verapamil/diltiazem + HFrEF = Contraindicated

### C) Hydralazine 

Hydralazine alone has not been shown to improve mortality in HFrEF. The hydralazine-isosorbide dinitrate combination reduces mortality in African American patients with HFrEF who cannot tolerate ACEi/ARBs

**Buzzword Combo:** Hydralazine + isosorbide dinitrate + HFrEF + African American patient with ACEi intolerant = Motality benefit

### D) Digoxin

Digoxin reduces hospitalizations and improves symptoms in HFrEF but does NOT reduce mortality

**Buzzword Combo:** Digoxin + HFrEF = Reduces hospitalizations but NO mortality benefit
