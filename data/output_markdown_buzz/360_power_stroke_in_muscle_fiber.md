## Buzzword Question

A physiology student is reviewing the sliding filament mechanism during a skeletal muscle laboratory session. The professor explains that during contraction, thin filaments slide over thick filaments, causing some sarcomere regions to shorten while others remain unchanged. The student is asked to identify which region maintains a constant length throughout the power stroke.
Which of the following sarcomere regions remains constant during skeletal muscle contraction?

## Buzzwords Used

remains constant during skeletal muscle contraction

## Answer Options

A. I band
B. H zone
C. A band
D. Sarcomere length

## Correct Answer

C. A band

## Explanation

The A band corresponds to the entire length of the thick (myosin) filaments. Because the thick filament length does not change during contraction, the A band remains constant while thin filaments slide inward.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Thick filament length + unchanged during contraction + sliding filament theory = A band

## Wrong Options

### A. I band

The I band shortens during contraction because it contains only thin filaments.


**Buzzword Combo:** Thin filaments only + decreases with contraction + peripheral sarcomere region = I band

### B. H zone

The H zone shortens as thin filaments move toward the center of the sarcomere.


**Buzzword Combo:** Thick filaments only + central sarcomere region + decreases during contraction = H zone

### D. Sarcomere length

The entire sarcomere shortens during muscle contraction as Z discs move closer together.

**Buzzword Combo:** Z disc approximation + overall contraction unit + shortening = sarcomere length
