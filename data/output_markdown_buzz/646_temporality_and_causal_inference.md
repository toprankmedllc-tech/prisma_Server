## Buzzword Question

Researchers investigate whether exposure to a newly identified industrial solvent is associated with a rare hematologic disorder. Employee records demonstrate that exposure consistently occurred several years before disease onset in affected workers.

Which of the following conclusions is most appropriate?

## Buzzwords Used

Exposure before disease ; Occupational exposure ; Cause and effect ; Temporality ; Epidemiology

## Answer Options

A) The findings provide support for a causal relationship

B) The findings establish causation

C) The findings demonstrate only correlation and provide no evidence regarding causation

D) The findings are insufficient because exposure should occur after disease onset

## Correct Answer

A) The findings provide support for a causal relationship

## Explanation

Temporality is one of the Bradford Hill criteria that supports causation. Demonstrating that exposure precedes disease onset provides evidence for a causal relationship, although it does not prove causation by itself.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Exposure precedes disease + temporality criterion + evidence supporting causation = causal relationship supported

## Wrong Options

### B) The findings establish causation

No single Bradford Hill criterion is sufficient to prove causation. Temporality supports causation but does not establish it definitively.

**Buzzword Combo:** Single causation criterion present + causation proven = overinterpretation

### C) The findings demonstrate only correlation and provide no evidence regarding causation

Temporality is specifically considered evidence supporting a causal relationship and is more than simple correlation alone.

**Buzzword Combo:** Exposure before outcome + ignore causation evidence = incorrect conclusion

### D) The findings are insufficient because exposure should occur after disease onset

A cause must occur before its effect. Exposure preceding disease onset is exactly what is expected under temporality.

**Buzzword Combo:** Effect before cause + violates temporality = incorrect reasoning
