## Buzzword Question

A highly transmissible respiratory pathogen has a basic reproduction number (R₀) of 4. Public health officials want to determine the minimum vaccination coverage needed to achieve herd immunity, assuming 100% vaccine efficacy. Which of the following correctly calculates the herd immunity threshold?

## Buzzwords Used

R₀ = 4; vaccination; herd immunity threshold; 100% vaccine efficacy; minimum coverage

## Answer Options

A) 75% (HIT = 1 − 1/R₀ = 1 − 1/4 = 0.75) 
B) 25% (HIT = 1/R₀ = 1/4 = 0.25) 
C) 80% (HIT = 1 − 1/R₀ = 1 − 1/5 = 0.80) 
D) 50% (HIT = R₀/2 = 4/2 = 2.0, then halved)

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

The herd immunity threshold (HIT) = 1 − (1/R₀). R₀ is the basic reproduction number — the average number of secondary cases generated by one infectious person in a fully susceptible population. For R₀ = 4: HIT = 1 − (1/4) = 1 − 0.25 = 0.75 = 75%. This means 75% of the population must be immune (through vaccination or prior infection) to prevent sustained transmission. Higher R₀ requires higher vaccination coverage: measles (R₀ ≈ 12–18) requires ~92–95%; COVID-19 original strain (R₀ ≈ 2–3) required ~50–67%; polio (R₀ ≈ 5–7) requires ~80–86%.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

HIT = 1 − (1/R₀); higher R₀ = higher vaccination threshold needed; R₀ = 4 → HIT = 75%

## Wrong Options

### B) 25% (HIT = 1/R₀ = 1/4 = 0.25)

1/R₀ = 0.25 is the fraction of the population that can remain susceptible (unimmunized) while still achieving herd immunity — NOT the proportion that must be immune. The proportion immune = 1 − (1/R₀) = 1 − 0.25 = 0.75 = 75%.

**Buzzword Combo:** 1/R₀ = susceptible fraction tolerable for herd immunity; 1 − (1/R₀) = immune fraction needed (HIT)

###  C) 80% (HIT = 1 − 1/R₀ = 1 − 1/5 = 0.80)

This correctly applies the HIT formula but uses R₀ = 5 instead of R₀ = 4. With R₀ = 4: HIT = 1 − 1/4 = 75%, not 80%. R₀ = 5 would give HIT = 80%. Reading the correct R₀ from the question stem is essential.

**Buzzword Combo:** R₀ = 5 → HIT = 80%; R₀ = 4 → HIT = 75%; always apply correct R₀ from stem

### D) 50% (HIT = R₀/2 = 4/2 = 2.0, then halved)


There is no formula for HIT involving R₀/2. The correct formula is 1 − (1/R₀). R₀/2 has no epidemiological meaning in this context.

**Buzzword Combo:** HIT formula: 1 − (1/R₀) ONLY; no division of R₀ by 2
