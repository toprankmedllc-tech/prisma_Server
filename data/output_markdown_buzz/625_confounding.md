## Buzzword Question

Researchers conduct a randomized controlled trial comparing Drug A and Drug B for prevention of postoperative complications. At the end of the study, postoperative complications occur more frequently among patients receiving Drug A. Review of baseline characteristics reveals that the average age of participants in the Drug A group was 74 years compared with 52 years in the Drug B group.

Which of the following is the most likely explanation for the observed difference in outcomes?

## Buzzwords Used

Randomized trial ; Baseline characteristics ; Age imbalance ; Postoperative complications ; Epidemiology

## Answer Options

A) Confounding

B) Lack of blinding

C) Recall bias

D) Lead-time bias

## Correct Answer

A) Confounding

## Explanation

A baseline characteristic differs substantially between groups and may account for the observed difference in outcomes. This is the classic pattern of confounding.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Unequal baseline characteristic + outcome difference + alternative explanation for association = confounding

## Wrong Options

### B) Lack of blinding

Lack of blinding can influence outcome assessment but does not explain major baseline differences between study groups. There is no clue in the study as to whether patients and investigators were blinded or not.

**Buzzword Combo:** Outcome assessment concern + baseline imbalance present = not primary issue

### C) Recall bias

Recall bias involves inaccurate recollection of exposures and is unrelated to baseline differences between treatment groups.

**Buzzword Combo:** Exposure recall problem + not group imbalance = not recall bias

### D) Lead-time bias

Lead-time bias occurs in screening studies and creates an apparent increase in survival due to earlier diagnosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Screening study concept + treatment trial scenario = not lead-time bias
