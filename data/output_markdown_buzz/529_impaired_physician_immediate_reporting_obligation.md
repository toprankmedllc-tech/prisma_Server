## Buzzword Question

A junior doctor notices that a senior colleague assigned to the acute medical unit is visibly unsteady, confused during handover, and appears intoxicated. The colleague insists they are “just tired” and attempts to continue working.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response?

## Buzzwords Used

Impaired physician ; Intoxication suspicion ; Patient safety risk ; Clinical duty

## Answer Options

A) Allow the colleague to continue working under supervision to avoid embarrassment
B) Immediately report the concern through appropriate hospital channels to ensure patient safety
C) Wait until formal proof of impairment is obtained before taking any action
D) Discuss the concern only with other junior staff to confirm suspicions before escalating

## Correct Answer

B) Immediately report the concern through appropriate hospital channels to ensure patient safety

## Explanation

Suspected impairment with patient safety risk requires immediate escalation through formal institutional reporting mechanisms rather than informal discussion or delay.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Impaired clinician + patient risk + suspicion sufficient = report immediately

## Wrong Options

### A) Allow the colleague to continue working under supervision to avoid embarrassment

Patient safety takes priority over avoiding discomfort or embarrassment.

**Buzzword Combo:** Impairment + continued practice + tolerance = unsafe practice

### C) Wait until formal proof of impairment is obtained before taking any action

Waiting for definitive proof delays necessary protective action; reasonable suspicion is sufficient.

**Buzzword Combo:** Suspected impairment + delay + proof requirement = incorrect threshold

### D) Discuss the concern only with other junior staff to confirm suspicions before escalating

Informal peer discussion delays escalation and does not address immediate safety risk appropriately.

**Buzzword Combo:** Impairment + peer gossip + delay in reporting = inappropriate response
