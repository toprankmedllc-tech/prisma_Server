## Buzzword Question

A 24-year-old pregnant woman at 10 weeks gestation is evaluated for a routine prenatal visit. She has a history of severe cystic acne and mentions she is still using a prescribed topical and oral medication she had leftover from before her pregnancy. An ultrasound performed later in the second trimester reveals a fetus with microtia, thymic hypoplasia, and a transposition of the great vessels. Which of the following mechanisms is responsible for these findings?

## Buzzwords Used

Cystic acne; Microtia; Thymic hypoplasia; Transposition of the great vessels.

## Answer Options

A) Inhibition of folate-dependent DNA synthesis
B) Disruption of HOX gene expression
C) Interference with neural crest cell migration and differentiation
D) Premature closure of the maternal -fetal vascular interface

## Correct Answer

Option c

## Explanation

Isotretinoin is a highly teratogenic vitamin A derivative that interferes with the signaling pathways required for neural crest cell migration. This leads to characteristic "retinoic acid embryopathy," affecting the ears, heart, and thymus (similar to DiGeorge syndrome).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Vitamin A derivative + Neural crest interference = Isotretinoin Embryopathy

## Wrong Options

### A) Inhibition of folate-dependent DNA synthesis

This describes the mechanism of Methotrexate, which typically causes neural tube defects rather than branchial arch and cardiac outflow tract anomalies.

**Buzzword Combo:** Methotrexate + Folate Antagonist = Neural Tube Defects

### B) Disruption of HOX gene expression

HOX gene mutations typically result in limb malformations (e.g., polydactyly or syndactyly) rather than the craniofacial and thymic defects seen here.

**Buzzword Combo:** HOX mutation + Axis patterning = Limb Malformations

### D) Premature closure of the maternal-fetal vascular interface

This is not a recognised mechanism of retinoid teratogenicity; retinoids act via nuclear receptor signaling (RAR/RXR) to alter gene transcription.

**Buzzword Combo:** Vascular disruption + Limb loss = Cocaine/Thalidomide
