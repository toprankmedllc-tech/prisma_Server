## Buzzword Question

A patient with accidental hypothermia develops increased urine output due to peripheral vasoconstriction causing suppression of antidiuretic hormone secretion. What is this phenomenon called?


## Buzzwords Used

 Hypothermia ; increased urine output ; peripheral vasoconstriction ; ADH suppression


## Answer Options

 A) Osmotic diuresis
 B) Cold diuresis
 C) Pressure natriuresis
 D) Diabetes insipidus


## Correct Answer

 B) Cold diuresis


## Explanation

Cold diuresis occurs in hypothermia because peripheral vasoconstriction increases central blood volume and renal perfusion, suppressing ADH release and leading to dilute urine production.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

 Hypothermia + ADH suppression + increased urine output = Cold diuresis


## Wrong Options

###  A) Osmotic diuresis


Osmotic diuresis occurs when substances such as glucose increase urinary water loss, commonly in uncontrolled diabetes mellitus.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Hyperglycemia + glucosuria + polyuria = Osmotic diuresis


###  C) Pressure natriuresis


Pressure natriuresis refers to increased sodium excretion in response to elevated arterial pressure.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Hypertension + sodium excretion + renal compensation = Pressure natriuresis


### D) Diabetes insipidus


Diabetes insipidus causes excessive dilute urine due to impaired ADH secretion or renal response, not cold exposure.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Hypernatremia + polyuria + ADH deficiency = Diabetes insipidus
