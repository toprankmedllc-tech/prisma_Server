## Buzzword Question

A young obese woman with confirmed idiopathic intracranial hypertension is started on acetazolamide. The drug reduces her intracranial pressure primarily by:

## Buzzwords Used

Acetazolamide; idiopathic intracranial hypertension; choroid plexus; CSF production; carbonic anhydrase

## Answer Options

A) Inhibiting carbonic anhydrase in the choroid plexus epithelium, decreasing CSF production
B) Increasing serum osmolality to draw water from the brain into the vasculature
C) Vasoconstricting cerebral arterioles to reduce cerebral blood volume
D) Promoting CSF reabsorption at the arachnoid granulations

## Correct Answer

A) Inhibiting carbonic anhydrase in the choroid plexus epithelium, decreasing CSF production

## Explanation

Acetazolamide inhibits carbonic anhydrase in the choroid plexus epithelial cells, reducing the bicarbonate-driven secretion of CSF. The decrease in CSF production lowers intracranial pressure, improves headache, and protects vision in IIH.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

IIH treatment + reduces CSF production + carbonic anhydrase inhibitor = acetazolamide

## Wrong Options

### Increasing serum osmolality to draw water from the brain into the vasculature

Describes mannitol or hypertonic saline, used acutely for cerebral edema and herniation, not chronic IIH management.

**Buzzword Combo:** Acute cerebral edema + osmotic diuresis = mannitol or hypertonic saline

### Vasoconstricting cerebral arterioles to reduce cerebral blood volume

Hyperventilation transiently reduces ICP via PaCO₂-driven cerebral vasoconstriction, used emergently for impending herniation. It is not the chronic mechanism of acetazolamide.

**Buzzword Combo:** Acute brain herniation + transient ICP reduction = hyperventilation (low PaCO₂ vasoconstriction)

### Promoting CSF reabsorption at the arachnoid granulations

No commonly used pharmacologic agent in IIH works by directly enhancing arachnoid granulation reabsorption; therapeutic LP and CSF shunting physically remove or divert CSF instead.

**Buzzword Combo:** Refractory IIH with vision loss + physical CSF diversion = ventriculoperitoneal or lumboperitoneal shunt; optic nerve sheath fenestration

## Related Concepts

Topiramate (alternative carbonic anhydrase activity with appetite suppression bonus); Furosemide adjunct; CSF shunting and optic nerve sheath fenestration for refractory IIH
