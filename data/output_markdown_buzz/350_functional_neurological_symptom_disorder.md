## Buzzword Question

A woman develops sudden leg paralysis after emotional stress. Examination reveals normal reflexes, inconsistent weakness, and a positive Hoover sign. Brain MRI is normal. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Positive Hoover sign; inconsistent weakness; normal reflexes; emotional stress; normal MRI; sudden paralysis


## Answer Options

 A) Functional neurological symptom disorder
 B) Guillain-Barre syndrome
 C) Multiple sclerosis
 D) Acute ischemic stroke


## Correct Answer

A. Functional neurological symptom disorder

## Explanation


Functional neurological symptom disorder presents with neurological symptoms incompatible with recognized neurologic disease. Positive Hoover sign and inconsistent examination findings strongly support the diagnosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Positive Hoover sign + inconsistent weakness + normal imaging = Functional neurological symptom disorder


## Wrong Options

### B. Guillain-Barre syndrome


GBS causes ascending weakness with decreased or absent reflexes rather than preserved reflexes and inconsistent examination findings.


**Buzzword Combo:** Ascending paralysis + areflexia + recent infection = Guillain-Barre syndrome


### C. Multiple sclerosis


MS causes objective demyelinating CNS lesions and neurologic deficits consistent with neuroanatomy.


**Buzzword Combo:** Optic neuritis + demyelinating lesions + relapsing neurologic deficits = Multiple sclerosis


### D. Acute ischemic stroke


Stroke produces focal neurologic deficits with corresponding vascular or imaging abnormalities.


**Buzzword Combo:** Sudden focal deficit + vascular risk factors + abnormal CT/MRI = Stroke
