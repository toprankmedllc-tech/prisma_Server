## Buzzword Question

A 48-year-old woman with rheumatoid arthritis has fatigue and mild pallor. Laboratory studies show low hemoglobin, low serum iron, low TIBC, and normal-to-increased ferritin. The mechanism most responsible for this patient’s anemia is:

## Buzzwords Used

rheumatoid arthritis; fatigue; mild pallor; low hemoglobin; low serum iron; low TIBC; normal-to-increased ferritin

## Answer Options

A) Increased hepcidin causing decreased ferroportin activity

B) Decreased intrinsic factor causing impaired vitamin B12 absorption

C) Decreased heme synthesis due to impaired protoporphyrin production

D) Increased RBC membrane fragility due to spectrin deficiency


## Correct Answer

A) Increased hepcidin causing decreased ferroportin activity


## Explanation

Anemia of chronic disease is caused by chronic inflammation. IL-6 increases hepatic hepcidin production. Hepcidin inhibits ferroportin, which decreases iron release from macrophages and decreases intestinal iron absorption. Iron is present in storage sites, but it is not available for erythropoiesis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Chronic inflammation + low iron + normal/high ferritin = anemia of chronic disease

## Wrong Options

### B. Decreased intrinsic factor causing impaired vitamin B12 absorption


This describes pernicious anemia, which causes macrocytic anemia due to impaired vitamin B12 absorption.

**Buzzword Combo:** Macrocytic anemia + neurologic symptoms + anti-intrinsic factor antibodies = pernicious anemia

### C. Decreased heme synthesis due to impaired protoporphyrin production


This describes sideroblastic anemia, which involves defective heme synthesis and iron accumulation in mitochondria.

**Buzzword Combo:** Microcytic anemia + ring sideroblasts + iron-loaded mitochondria = sideroblastic anemia

### D. Increased RBC membrane fragility due to spectrin deficiency


This describes hereditary spherocytosis, which causes extravascular hemolysis due to membrane cytoskeleton defects.

**Buzzword Combo:** Spherocytes + splenomegaly + increased osmotic fragility = hereditary spherocytosis
