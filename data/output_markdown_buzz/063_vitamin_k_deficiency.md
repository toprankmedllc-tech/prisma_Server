## Buzzword Question

A newborn has persistent bleeding from the umbilical stump shortly after birth. Laboratory studies show normal platelet count, normal bleeding time, increased PT, and increased PTT. The deficient nutrient normally acts as a cofactor for which of the following processes?

## Buzzwords Used

newborn; persistent bleeding from umbilical stump; shortly after birth

## Answer Options

A) γ-carboxylation of clotting factors II, VII, IX, and X

B) Platelet adhesion to exposed subendothelial collagen

C) Platelet aggregation through glycoprotein IIb/IIIa

D) Cross-linking of fibrin by factor XIII


## Correct Answer

A. γ-carboxylation of clotting factors II, VII, IX, and X


## Explanation

Vitamin K is required as a cofactor for γ-glutamyl carboxylase, which activates clotting factors II, VII, IX, and X, as well as proteins C and S. Vitamin K deficiency causes increased PT and increased PTT, but bleeding time is normal because platelet function is not affected.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Newborn + umbilical stump bleeding + increased PT/PTT + normal bleeding time = vitamin K deficiency

## Wrong Options

### B. Platelet adhesion to exposed subendothelial collagen


This describes von Willebrand factor function, which bridges platelet glycoprotein Ib to exposed collagen.

**Buzzword Combo:** Epistaxis + heavy menses + abnormal ristocetin = von Willebrand disease

### C. Platelet aggregation through glycoprotein IIb/IIIa


This describes Glanzmann thrombasthenia, a platelet aggregation defect.

**Buzzword Combo:** GpIIb/IIIa defect + mucosal bleeding + normal platelet count = Glanzmann thrombasthenia

### D. Cross-linking of fibrin by factor XIII


Factor XIII stabilizes the fibrin clot after the coagulation cascade. Deficiency causes delayed bleeding with normal PT and PTT.

**Buzzword Combo:** Delayed bleeding + normal PT/PTT + unstable clot = factor XIII deficiency
