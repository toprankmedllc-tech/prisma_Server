## Buzzword Question

Researchers investigate whether exposure to Chemical Y is associated with a chronic pulmonary disease. Over the next decade, studies conducted in North America, Europe, Asia, and South America repeatedly demonstrate similar findings despite differences in patient populations and study designs.

Which principle supporting a causal relationship is best illustrated by these findings?

## Buzzwords Used

Multiple studies ; Different countries ; Reproducible findings ; Similar results ; Causation

## Answer Options

A) Consistency

B) Specificity

C) Temporality

D) Analogy

## Correct Answer

A) Consistency

## Explanation

Consistency is demonstrated when similar findings are repeatedly observed across different populations, settings, and study designs.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Repeated observations + different populations + similar findings = consistency

## Wrong Options

### B) Specificity

Specificity refers to a particular exposure being associated with a specific outcome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Single exposure-single outcome relationship = specificity

### C) Temporality

Temporality requires that the exposure occur before the disease develops.

**Buzzword Combo:** Exposure precedes outcome = temporality

### D) Analogy

Analogy relies on similarities to previously established causal relationships.

**Buzzword Combo:** Comparable known relationship = analogy
