## Buzzword Question

A 70-year-old man with a remote history of chickenpox develops a painful unilateral cluster of vesicles on an erythematous base in a band-like distribution on the right side of his chest that ends abruptly at the midline. The most likely causative organism is:

## Buzzwords Used

Older adult; remote history of chickenpox; unilateral painful dermatomal vesicular rash that does not cross the midline

## Answer Options

A) Herpes simplex virus type 1 primary infection
B) Varicella-zoster virus reactivation
C) Cytomegalovirus reactivation
D) Epstein-Barr virus primary infection

## Correct Answer

B) Varicella-zoster virus reactivation

## Explanation

Painful unilateral dermatomal vesicular rash that respects the midline in an older adult with prior chickenpox is reactivation of latent VZV in a dorsal root ganglion (shingles).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Older adult + prior chickenpox + painful unilateral dermatomal vesicular rash + does not cross midline = VZV reactivation (shingles)

## Wrong Options

### Herpes simplex virus type 1 primary infection

HSV-1 typically produces oral or facial vesicular lesions (gingivostomatitis, herpes labialis) or, in adults, encephalitis with temporal lobe involvement, not a band-like dermatomal trunk eruption.

**Buzzword Combo:** Oral or perioral cluster of vesicles with prodromal tingling = HSV-1 reactivation (cold sore)

### Cytomegalovirus reactivation

CMV reactivation in AIDS or post-transplant patients causes retinitis, esophagitis, colitis, or pneumonitis, not a dermatomal vesicular rash.

**Buzzword Combo:** AIDS (CD4 less than 50) + retinitis with cotton-wool spots + owl's eye inclusions = CMV

### Epstein-Barr virus primary infection

EBV primary infection causes infectious mononucleosis with sore throat, lymphadenopathy, splenomegaly, and atypical lymphocytes, not a dermatomal vesicular rash.

**Buzzword Combo:** Adolescent + sore throat + posterior cervical lymphadenopathy + splenomegaly + atypical lymphocytes + positive heterophile = EBV mononucleosis

## Related Concepts

Primary varicella (chickenpox) in children; Disseminated zoster in severely immunocompromised patients (involves more than one dermatome); Recombinant zoster vaccine (Shingrix) for adults age 50 and older
