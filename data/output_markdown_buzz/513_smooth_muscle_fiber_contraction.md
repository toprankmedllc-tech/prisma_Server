## Buzzword Question

67-year-old man with stable angina develops sudden chest discomfort while climbing stairs. In the emergency department, he is given a medication that increases nitric oxide signaling, leading to relaxation of vascular smooth muscle and rapid vasodilation. A medical student asks how smooth muscle relaxation occurs at the molecular level. The attending physician explains that nitric oxide increases intracellular levels of a second messenger that activates myosin light-chain phosphatase (MLCP), promoting smooth muscle relaxation.
Which of the following substances most directly activates myosin light-chain phosphatase (MLCP) in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

substances most directly activates myosin light-chain phosphatase 

## Answer Options

A. cAMP
B. cGMP
C. Calcium
D. Troponin C

## Correct Answer

B. cGMP

## Explanation

Nitric oxide stimulates guanylate cyclase, increasing intracellular cGMP levels. cGMP activates myosin light-chain phosphatase (MLCP), which dephosphorylates myosin light chains and promotes smooth muscle relaxation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Nitric oxide + smooth muscle relaxation + MLCP activation = cGMP

## Wrong Options

### A. cAMP

cAMP can promote smooth muscle relaxation in some tissues, but nitric oxide-mediated activation of MLCP occurs primarily through cGMP signaling.

**Buzzword Combo:** β-adrenergic signaling + protein kinase A + bronchodilation = cAMP

### C. Calcium

Increased intracellular calcium promotes smooth muscle contraction by activating the calmodulin–MLCK pathway.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ca2+–calmodulin + myosin phosphorylation + contraction = MLCK activation

### D. Troponin C

Troponin C is involved in skeletal and cardiac muscle contraction, not smooth muscle regulation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Troponin + thin filament regulation + striated muscle = skeletal/cardiac muscle contraction
