## Buzzword Question

A 29-year-old woman is being treated for Tuberculosis. Two months later, she presents with an unplanned pregnancy despite consistent use of oral contraceptive pills. Which of the following drug is responsible?

## Buzzwords Used

antitubercular therapy+ taking oral contraceptive pills+ unplanned pregnancy

## Answer Options

A. Isoniazid
B. Streptomycin
C. Ethambutol
D. Rifampin

## Correct Answer

D. Rifampin

## Explanation

Rifampin is a potent inducer of the cytochrome P450 enzyme system, which accelerates the metabolism of estrogen and progestin in oral contraceptive pills. This reduction in the plasma concentration of these hormones compromises the efficacy of the birth control, leading to an unintended pregnancy.
