## Buzzword Question

45-year-old woman presents to the neurology clinic with several months of numbness and tingling in her right hand. She reports that the symptoms are worse at night and frequently occur while typing at work. She also notices occasional weakness when gripping small objects and difficulty buttoning her clothes. Physical examination reveals mild weakness with thumb opposition. Tapping over the volar aspect of the wrist reproduces tingling in the affected fingers.
Which of the following sensory distributions is most likely affected in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

Tapping over the volar aspect of the wrist reproduces tingling; weakness with thumb opposition.

## Answer Options

A. Palmar surface of the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of the ring finger
B. Palmar and dorsal surfaces of the little finger and medial half of the ring finger
C. Dorsal surface of the lateral hand and posterior thumb web space
D. Skin over the lateral shoulder and upper arm

## Correct Answer

A. Palmar surface of the thumb, index finger, middle finger, and lateral half of the ring finger

## Explanation

This patient has classic findings of carpal tunnel syndrome. Median nerve compression commonly causes: Weakness of thumb opposition , Positive Tinel sign .The median nerve provides sensory innervation to: The palmar surface of the Thumb ,Index finger ,Middle finger ,Lateral half of the ring finger 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Nighttime hand paresthesias + positive Tinel sign + thumb opposition weakness = median nerve compression

## Wrong Options

### B. Palmar and dorsal surfaces of the little finger and medial half of the ring finger

Incorrect — This sensory distribution is supplied by the ulnar nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fourth and fifth digit numbness + intrinsic hand weakness = ulnar nerve lesion

### C. Dorsal surface of the lateral hand and posterior thumb web space

Incorrect — This area is primarily supplied by the radial nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Wrist drop + dorsal hand sensory loss = radial nerve injury

### D. Skin over the lateral shoulder and upper arm

Incorrect — This sensory region is supplied by the axillary nerve.

**Buzzword Combo:** Shoulder dislocation + impaired arm abduction = axillary nerve injury
