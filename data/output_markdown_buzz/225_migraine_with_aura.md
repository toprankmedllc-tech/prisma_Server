## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old woman has 6 hours of pulsatile right-sided headache with nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia; she prefers to lie in a dark, quiet room. The pain was preceded by 20 minutes of flickering zigzag lines that began in the center of her vision and slowly expanded outward before fading. The most likely diagnosis is: 

## Buzzwords Used

Pulsatile unilateral headache; nausea; photophobia and phonophobia; preference for dark quiet room; preceding visual aura with expanding scintillating scotoma

## Answer Options

A) Cluster headache
B) Migraine with aura
C) Tension-type headache
D) Subarachnoid hemorrhage

## Correct Answer

B) Migraine with aura

## Explanation

Pulsatile unilateral headache with nausea, photophobia, phonophobia, and a preceding expanding scintillating visual aura defines migraine with aura. The aura reflects cortical spreading depression starting in the occipital cortex.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pulsatile unilateral headache + nausea + photophobia and phonophobia + scintillating visual aura = migraine with aura

## Wrong Options

### Cluster headache

Cluster headache produces brief (under 3 hour) strictly unilateral periorbital pain with ipsilateral autonomic features, occurring multiple times daily, with marked restlessness rather than light/sound avoidance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Brief unilateral periorbital pain + ipsilateral autonomic features + circa-annual clusters + pacing = cluster headache

### Tension-type headache

Tension-type headache is bilateral, band-like, mild to moderate, and lacks aura, nausea, photophobia, and phonophobia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Bilateral band-like pressure + mild intensity + no aura or autonomic features = tension-type headache

### Subarachnoid hemorrhage

SAH presents with sudden "worst headache of life" peaking within seconds to minutes, often with brief LOC, meningismus, and risk factors such as ADPKD; it is not preceded by 20 minutes of expanding scintillating aura.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sudden worst-headache-of-life + meningismus + ADPKD = subarachnoid hemorrhage
