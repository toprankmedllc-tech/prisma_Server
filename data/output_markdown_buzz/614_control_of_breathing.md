## Buzzword Question

A patient develops transient apnea when his lungs are suddenly overinflated during mechanical ventilation. Which reflex is responsible?

## Buzzwords Used

Mechanical ventilation; lung overinflation; transient apnea; vagal afferents; inflation reflex; respiratory inhibition

## Answer Options

A) Hering-Breuer reflex
B) Cushing reflex
C) Baroreceptor reflex
D) Bainbridge reflex

## Correct Answer

A) Hering-Breuer reflex

## Explanation

The Hering-Breuer inflation reflex is activated by lung overdistension, which stimulates stretch receptors in the lungs. Vagal afferents send inhibitory signals to the medulla, temporarily suppressing inspiration and causing transient apnea.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Lung overinflation + vagal stretch receptors + respiratory inhibition → Hering-Breuer reflex

## Wrong Options

### B) Cushing reflex

The Cushing reflex is a response to increased intracranial pressure causing hypertension, bradycardia, and irregular respiration, not lung stretch.

**Buzzword Combo:** Increased ICP + systemic hypertension + brainstem pressure response

### C) Baroreceptor reflex

The baroreceptor reflex regulates blood pressure via carotid sinus and aortic arch stretch, not pulmonary stretch or ventilation control.

**Buzzword Combo:** Blood pressure changes + carotid sinus signaling + autonomic modulation

### D) Bainbridge reflex

The Bainbridge reflex is triggered by atrial stretch leading to increased heart rate, unrelated to lung inflation or respiratory control.

**Buzzword Combo:** Atrial stretch + increased venous return + tachycardia response
