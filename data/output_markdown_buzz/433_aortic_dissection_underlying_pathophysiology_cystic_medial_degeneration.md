## Buzzword Question

Microscopic examination of the aortic wall in a patient with chronic hypertension who died of aortic dissection reveals fragmentation of elastic fibers with accumulation of mucoid material within the tunica media. This finding is most consistent with:

## Buzzwords Used

Chronic hypertension; aortic dissection; fragmentation of elastic fibers; mucoid material; tunica media

## Answer Options


A) Cystic medial degeneration
B) Atherosclerotic plaque rupture
C) Granulomatous arteritis with giant cells
D) Fibrinoid necrosis of arterioles

## Correct Answer

A) Cystic medial degeneration

## Explanation

fragmentation of elastic fibers with accumulation of mucoid (proteoglycan) material in the tunica media — is the underlying histologic substrate for aortic dissection. It is driven by chronic hypertension and is accelerated by connective tissue disorders (Marfan, Ehlers-Danlos) that further weaken the medial elastic architecture.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Chronic HTN + fragmented elastic fibers + mucoid medial deposits = cystic medial degeneration (aortic dissection substrate)

## Wrong Options

### B) Atherosclerotic plaque rupture

Atherosclerotic plaque rupture in the coronary arteries drives acute coronary syndromes, and aortic atherosclerosis underlies abdominal aortic aneurysm — not the medial degeneration that causes dissection.

**Buzzword Combo:** Plaque rupture + thrombus formation = acute coronary syndrome

### C) Granulomatous arteritis with giant cells

Granulomatous inflammation with multinucleated giant cells in the vessel wall is the histology of giant cell (temporal) arteritis and Takayasu arteritis, not aortic dissection.

**Buzzword Combo:** Granulomatous arteritis + giant cells + elderly with jaw claudication = GCA

### D) Fibrinoid necrosis of arterioles

Fibrinoid necrosis of small arterioles is the histology of malignant hypertension and polyarteritis nodosa, affecting small/medium vessels — not large-vessel medial degeneration.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fibrinoid necrosis + small arterioles + severe HTN = malignant hypertension

## Related Concepts

Marfan syndrome (fibrillin-1); Atherosclerosis; Polyarteritis nodosa
