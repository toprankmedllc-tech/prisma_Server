## Buzzword Question

22-year-old man presents to the orthopedic clinic with persistent wrist pain 2 months after falling onto an outstretched hand while skateboarding. At the time of injury, he was told that initial wrist radiographs were normal and was discharged with conservative treatment. Since then, he has continued to experience pain and decreased grip strength. Physical examination reveals tenderness in the anatomical snuffbox. Repeat imaging now demonstrates collapse and sclerosis of the proximal portion of the injured carpal bone.
Which of the following complications is most likely responsible for this patient’s persistent symptoms?

## Buzzwords Used

collapse and sclerosis of the proximal portion of the injured carpal bone; tenderness in the anatomical snuffbox

## Answer Options

A. Avascular necrosis due to disruption of retrograde blood supply
B. Compression neuropathy of the median nerve within the carpal tunnel
C. Osteoarthritis caused by autoimmune synovial inflammation
D. Septic arthritis caused by hematogenous bacterial spread

## Correct Answer

A. Avascular necrosis due to disruption of retrograde blood supply

## Explanation

This patient most likely sustained a scaphoid fracture. The scaphoid has a tenuous retrograde blood supply, meaning the proximal portion of the bone depends on blood flow entering distally. Fractures through the waist of the scaphoid can disrupt this circulation, leading to avascular necrosis of the proximal fragment.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

FOOSH injury + snuffbox tenderness + proximal scaphoid collapse = avascular necrosis

## Wrong Options

### B. Compression neuropathy of the median nerve within the carpal tunnel

Incorrect — Carpal tunnel syndrome causes numbness, tingling, and weakness in the median nerve distribution rather than bony collapse after trauma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Nighttime paresthesias + thenar weakness + positive Phalen test = carpal tunnel syndrome

### C. Osteoarthritis caused by autoimmune synovial inflammation

Incorrect — Autoimmune synovial inflammation is characteristic of rheumatoid arthritis, not posttraumatic scaphoid injury.

**Buzzword Combo:** Morning stiffness + symmetric MCP involvement + elevated rheumatoid factor = rheumatoid arthritis

### D. Septic arthritis caused by hematogenous bacterial spread

incorrect — Septic arthritis usually presents with fever, erythema, severe joint pain, and restricted range of motion rather than chronic snuffbox tenderness after trauma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hot swollen joint + fever + elevated inflammatory markers = septic arthritis
