## Buzzword Question

A marathon runner collapses with a temperature of 41°C, confusion, profuse sweating, rhabdomyolysis, and acute kidney injury after strenuous exercise in hot weather. What is the diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Marathon runner ; hyperthermia ; CNS dysfunction ; profuse sweating ; rhabdomyolysis


## Answer Options

A) Heat exhaustion
 B) Exertional heatstroke
 C) Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
 D) Serotonin syndrome

## Correct Answer

 B) Exertional heatstroke


## Explanation

Exertional heatstroke is characterized by core temperature >40°C, severe CNS dysfunction, and strenuous physical activity. Sweating is often still present.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

 Exercise + hyperthermia + altered mental status = Exertional heatstroke

## Wrong Options

###  A) Heat exhaustion


Heat exhaustion causes dehydration and weakness without severe CNS dysfunction or extreme hyperthermia.


**Buzzword Combo:** Heavy sweating + weakness + normal mental status = Heat exhaustion


###  C) Neuroleptic malignant syndrome


Neuroleptic malignant syndrome is associated with dopamine antagonist use and lead-pipe rigidity.


**Buzzword Combo:** Antipsychotic use + rigidity + hyperthermia = Neuroleptic malignant syndrome

###  D) Serotonin syndrome


Serotonin syndrome presents with hyperreflexia and clonus after serotonergic drug exposure.


**Buzzword Combo:** SSRI use + clonus + hyperreflexia = Serotonin syndrome
