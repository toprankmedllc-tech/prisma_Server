## Buzzword Question

A young woman repeatedly checks mirrors and excessively applies makeup because she believes her nose is severely deformed, although others perceive her appearance as normal. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Mirror checking; excessive grooming; perceived physical defect; appearance preoccupation; 


## Answer Options

 A) Body dysmorphic disorder
 B) Anorexia nervosa
 C) Social anxiety disorder
 D) Delusional disorder


## Correct Answer

A. Body dysmorphic disorder


## Explanation

BDD is characterized by excessive preoccupation with perceived appearance flaws accompanied by repetitive behaviors such as mirror checking and grooming. The perceived defect is usually minimal or not observable to others.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Perceived appearance flaw + mirror checking + excessive grooming = BDD


## Wrong Options

### B. Anorexia nervosa


Anorexia nervosa centers on distorted perceptions of weight and body size with restrictive eating behaviors, not isolated concerns about facial appearance.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fear of weight gain + restrictive eating + low BMI = Anorexia nervosa


### C. Social anxiety disorder


Social anxiety disorder involves fear of embarrassment in social settings rather than obsessive appearance-focused behaviors.


**Buzzword Combo:** Fear of public embarrassment + avoidance + performance anxiety = Social anxiety disorder


### D. Delusional disorder


BDD patients often have repetitive compulsive behaviors focused on appearance, unlike isolated fixed delusions without compulsions.


**Buzzword Combo:** Fixed false belief + preserved functioning + nonbizarre delusion = Delusional disorder
