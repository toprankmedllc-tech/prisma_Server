## Buzzword Question

Which portion of the bladder is most likely to rupture during blunt trauma to a distended bladder?

## Buzzwords Used

Bladder; Rupture; Blunt Trauma

## Answer Options

 A) Bladder dome
 B) Trigone
 C) Bladder neck
 D) Ureterovesical junction

## Correct Answer

A) Bladder dome

## Explanation

Intraperitoneal bladder rupture most commonly occurs at the bladder dome because it is the weakest and most distensible portion of the bladder. Blunt trauma to a distended bladder may cause rupture with leakage of urine into the peritoneal cavity.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Distended bladder + blunt trauma + dome rupture = Intraperitoneal bladder injury

## Wrong Options

### Trigone

The trigone is a relatively fixed and structurally supported region of the bladder located between the ureteral openings and urethra.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fixed bladder base + ureteral openings = Trigone

### Bladder neck

The bladder neck is associated with the internal urethral sphincter and outlet of the bladder rather than the most common site of rupture during blunt trauma.


**Buzzword Combo:** Internal sphincter region + bladder outlet = Bladder neck

### Ureterovesical junction

The ureterovesical junction functions as a valve preventing reflux of urine from the bladder into the ureters.

**Buzzword Combo:** Anti-reflux mechanism + ureter insertion = Ureterovesical junction
