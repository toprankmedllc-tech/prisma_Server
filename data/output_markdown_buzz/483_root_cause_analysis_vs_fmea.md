## Buzzword Question

After a patient receives a blood transfusion intended for a different patient and develops an acute hemolytic transfusion reaction, the hospital convenes a structured multidisciplinary team to identify the underlying system failures,  rather than assign individual blame, that allowed the error to reach the patient and to develop corrective actions to prevent recurrence. This process is called:


## Buzzwords Used

After adverse event; system failures identified; no blame; underlying causes; prevent recurrence; retrospective structured investigation

## Answer Options

A) Root cause analysis (RCA) 
B) Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA) 
C) Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycle
 D) Morbidity and mortality (M&M) conference


## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Root cause analysis (RCA) is a structured RETROSPECTIVE investigation conducted AFTER a sentinel event to systematically identify underlying system-level failures,  not individual blame,  that contributed to the harm, and to develop corrective actions to prevent recurrence. Required by The Joint Commission after sentinel events.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Sentinel event occurred + retrospective system failure investigation + no-blame culture + prevent recurrence = Root cause analysis (RCA)

## Wrong Options

### 1: B) Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)

FMEA is PROACTIVE,  performed BEFORE an adverse event to prospectively identify potential failure modes in a high-risk process and prioritize improvements by severity × occurrence × detectability. RCA = after the event; FMEA = before the event. Opposite temporal directions.

**Buzzword Combo:** Before adverse event + prospectively identify potential failure modes + risk prioritization = FMEA

### C) Plan-Do-Study-Act (PDSA) cycle

PDSA is a quality improvement methodology for iteratively testing and refining process changes through small-scale pilots. It is not a retrospective error investigation. PDSA = improvement cycles after a gap is identified; RCA = investigation of a specific adverse event.

**Buzzword Combo:** Plan change → pilot (Do) → measure outcomes (Study) → refine and spread (Act) = PDSA quality improvement cycle


### D) Morbidity and mortality (M&M) conference

M&M conferences are educational case reviews where adverse outcomes are discussed to promote learning. While valuable, they are not the formal structured investigation with documented root causes, corrective action plans, and regulatory reporting required after a sentinel event. RCA is the formal required process.


**Buzzword Combo:** Educational case review of complications + peer discussion + learning from outcomes = M&M conference (educational, not formal investigation)
