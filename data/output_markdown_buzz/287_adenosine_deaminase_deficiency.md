## Buzzword Question

A child with severe recurrent infections is found to have accumulation of deoxyadenosine and dATP in lymphocytes. Which of the following best explains the pathophysiology of this disorder?

## Buzzwords Used

Severe recurrent infections; accumulation of deoxyadenosine and dATP in lymphocytes; pathophysiology of this disorder

## Answer Options

A) Failure of thymic development due to abnormal third and fourth pharyngeal pouch formation
B) Failure of B-cell maturation due to defective tyrosine kinase signaling
C) Toxic purine metabolite accumulation causing lymphocyte death
D) Impaired phagocyte generation of reactive oxygen species

## Correct Answer

C) Toxic purine metabolite accumulation causing lymphocyte death


## Explanation

ADA deficiency prevents normal degradation of adenosine and deoxyadenosine. Accumulated deoxyadenosine and dATP are toxic to developing lymphocytes, causing severe combined immunodeficiency.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Deoxyadenosine/dATP accumulation + lymphocyte toxicity + combined immune dysfunction = ADA deficiency

## Wrong Options

### A) Failure of thymic development due to abnormal third and fourth pharyngeal pouch formation

This describes DiGeorge syndrome, which causes defective thymic development and T-cell deficiency. It does not involve toxic purine metabolite accumulation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Absent thymus + hypocalcemia + conotruncal defects = DiGeorge syndrome

### B) Failure of B-cell maturation due to defective tyrosine kinase signaling

This describes Bruton agammaglobulinemia, which causes failure of B-cell maturation. It does not cause severe combined immunodeficiency through deoxyadenosine and dATP accumulation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Defective BTK + absent mature B cells + low immunoglobulins = Bruton agammaglobulinemia

### D) Impaired phagocyte generation of reactive oxygen species

This describes chronic granulomatous disease due to NADPH oxidase deficiency. It affects phagocyte killing rather than lymphocyte survival.

**Buzzword Combo:** NADPH oxidase defect + impaired respiratory burst + catalase-positive infections = chronic granulomatous disease

## Related Concepts

DiGeorge syndrome; Bruton agammaglobulinemia; chronic granulomatous disease
