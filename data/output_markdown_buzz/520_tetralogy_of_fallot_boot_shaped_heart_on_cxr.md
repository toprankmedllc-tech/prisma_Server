## Buzzword Question

A cyanotic child's chest radiograph demonstrates a boot-shaped cardiac silhouette. This finding is most consistent with:

## Buzzwords Used

 boot-shaped cardiac silhouette

## Answer Options

A) Tetralogy of Fallot
B) Transposition of the great arteries
C) Total anomalous pulmonary venous return
D) Tricuspid atresia

## Correct Answer


A) Tetralogy of Fallot

## Explanation

The boot-shaped heart (coeur en sabot) is produced by right ventricular hypertrophy lifting the cardiac apex upward, combined with a concave pulmonary trunk from pulmonary stenosis. It is the classic chest radiograph finding of Tetralogy of Fallot.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Boot-shaped heart + cyanotic child = Tetralogy of Fallot

## Wrong Options

### B) Transposition of the great arteries

TGA produces an "egg-on-a-string" silhouette from a narrow mediastinum and globular heart.

**Buzzword Combo:** Egg-on-a-string CXR + newborn cyanosis = TGA

### C) Total anomalous pulmonary venous return

TAPVR (supracardiac type) produces a "snowman" silhouette from dilated vertical vein and SVC.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Snowman CXR + infant cyanosis = TAPVR

### 
D) Tricuspid atresia

Tricuspid atresia shows a small RV and left-axis deviation on ECG, without a characteristic boot-shaped silhouette.

**Buzzword Combo:** Left-axis deviation + cyanotic infant + small RV = tricuspid atresia
