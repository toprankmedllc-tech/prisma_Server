## Buzzword Question

A 20-year-old college student joins a recreational boxing club and quickly becomes known for delivering extremely powerful punches during short matches. However, during longer training sessions involving continuous jogging and endurance drills, he becomes fatigued much earlier than his teammates. As part of a sports physiology study, a muscle biopsy is obtained and demonstrates fibers with rapid contraction speed, low mitochondrial density, low myoglobin content, and abundant glycogen stores.
Which of the following muscle fiber types is most likely predominant in this student’s skeletal muscles?

## Buzzwords Used

a muscle biopsy is obtained and demonstrates fibers with rapid contraction speed, low mitochondrial density, low myoglobin content, and abundant glycogen stores

## Answer Options

A. Type I muscle fibers
B. Slow oxidative fibers
C. Type II muscle fibers
D. Red aerobic fibers

## Correct Answer

C. Type II muscle fibers

## Explanation

Type II muscle fibers are fast-twitch fibers specialized for brief, high-intensity activities requiring rapid force generation. These fibers: Contract rapidly ,Rely mainly on anaerobic glycolysis , Contain abundant glycogen ,Have low myoglobin content ,Fatigue quickly 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Powerful bursts + anaerobic metabolism + rapid fatigue + low myoglobin = Type II muscle fibers

## Wrong Options

### A. Type I muscle fibers

Type I fibers are adapted for endurance and prolonged aerobic activity rather than explosive movements.

**Buzzword Combo:** Endurance training + aerobic metabolism + fatigue resistance = Type I fibers

### B. Slow oxidative fibers

Slow oxidative fibers contain many mitochondria and resist fatigue, unlike the fibers described here.

**Buzzword Combo:** High mitochondria + sustained activity + slow contraction = slow oxidative fibers

### D. Red aerobic fibers

Red aerobic fibers correspond to oxidative type I fibers with high myoglobin content and endurance capacity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Red muscle + oxygen-rich + prolonged exercise = red aerobic fibers
