## Buzzword Question

An elderly nursing home resident during a summer heat wave develops temperature of 40.5°C, dry hot skin, confusion, hypotension, and tachycardia. What is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Elderly ; heat wave ; dry skin ; hyperthermia ; altered mental status


## Answer Options

 A) Septic shock
 B) Heat exhaustion
 C) Nonexertional heatstroke
 D) Thyroid storm


## Correct Answer

C) Nonexertional heatstroke


## Explanation

Classic heatstroke occurs in vulnerable individuals exposed to high environmental temperatures and is characterized by hyperthermia and CNS dysfunction, often with dry skin.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

 Heat wave + elderly patient + dry hot skin = Nonexertional heatstroke

## Wrong Options

###  A) Septic shock


Septic shock typically presents with infection, fever, and signs of systemic inflammation rather than environmental heat exposure.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Infection + hypotension + elevated lactate = Septic shock


###  B) Heat exhaustion


Heat exhaustion does not cause severe CNS dysfunction or extreme hyperthermia.


**Buzzword Combo:** Sweating + dizziness + mild dehydration = Heat exhaustion


###  D) Thyroid storm


Thyroid storm presents with hyperthermia and agitation but is associated with thyrotoxicosis findings such as tremor and weight loss.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Hyperthyroidism + fever + atrial fibrillation = Thyroid storm
