## Buzzword Question

A 25-year-old Asian woman presents with fever, night sweats, productive cough, and weight loss. Sputum culture has shown acid-fast bacilli. The organisms survive within macrophages. How will the immune system of this patient act against the disease?

## Buzzwords Used

fever, night sweats, weight loss; acid-fast bacilli;  intracellular survival 

## Answer Options

A. IgG-mediated neutralization
B. CD4+ Th1 cell activation of macrophages via IFN-γ
C. IgE-mediated eosinophil activation
D. Neutrophil oxidative burst via NADPH oxidase

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

Alveolar macrophages containing mycobacteria produce IL-12 and activate cell-mediated immunity i.e., Th1 cells to produce IFN-γ that will activate the macrophages to kill bacteria and form granuloma.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

fever, night sweat, weight loss + acid fast bacilli + survival within macrophages = cell mediated immunity/ CD4+ Th1 cell activation of macrophages via IFN-γ

## Wrong Options

### A. IgG-mediated  neutralization

Works for extracellular toxins/viruses, not intracellular TB

**Buzzword Combo:** Blocks toxins from binding cells + Stops viruses from entering cells + Prevents infection spread in blood/extracellular space = IgG neutralization

### C. IgE-mediated eosinophil activation	

IgE-mediated eosinophil activation is responsible for killing helminth (worm) infections and also contributes to allergic reactions (Type I hypersensitivity).

**Buzzword Combo:** Parasites + allergy (Type I hypersensitivity) = IgE- mediated eosinophil activation

### Neutrophil oxidative burst via NADPH oxidase

Neutrophil oxidative burst is especially important for killing intracellular and catalase-positive organisms.

**Buzzword Combo:** catalase-positive organism (Staph aureus, Serratia, Burkholderia, Aspergillus) = Neutrophil oxidative burst via NADPH oxidase
