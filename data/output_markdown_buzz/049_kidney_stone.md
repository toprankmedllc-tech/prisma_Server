## Buzzword Question

A 43-year-old woman presents with flank pain and fever. CT scan demonstrates a large staghorn calculus occupying the renal pelvis, and urine pH is markedly alkaline.
Which of the following organisms is most likely associated with this patient’s kidney stone?

## Buzzwords Used

Staghorn calculus; Alkaline urine; Flank pain

## Answer Options

A) Proteus mirabilis
B) Escherichia coli
C) Staphylococcus saprophyticus
D) Enterococcus faecalis


## Correct Answer

A) Proteus mirabilis

## Explanation

Struvite nephrolithiasis is caused by urease-positive organisms such as Proteus species. Urease converts urea into ammonia, producing alkaline urine and promoting formation of magnesium ammonium phosphate (struvite) stones, which can form staghorn calculi.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Staghorn calculus + alkaline urine + urease-positive organism = Proteus

## Wrong Options

### Escherichia coli

E. coli is the most common cause of urinary tract infections but does not produce urease and is not classically associated with struvite stones.

**Buzzword Combo:** Most common UTI + lactose fermenter = E. coli

### Staphylococcus saprophyticus

Staphylococcus saprophyticus commonly causes UTIs in sexually active young women

**Buzzword Combo:** Young woman + honeymoon cystitis = S. saprophyticus


### Enterococcus faecalis

Enterococcus may cause urinary tract infections but is not classically associated with urease-mediated staghorn calculi.

**Buzzword Combo:** UTI + bile growth + gram-positive cocci = Enterococcus
