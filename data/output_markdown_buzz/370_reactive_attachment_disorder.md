## Buzzword Question

A child with reactive attachment disorder is undergoing treatment. Which of the following is the most appropriate primary intervention?

## Buzzwords Used

Reactive attachment disorder; neglect; treatment; emotionally available caregiver


## Answer Options

 A) Establishing a stable emotionally available caregiver
 B) Long-term benzodiazepine therapy
 C) Electroconvulsive therapy
 D) Stimulant monotherapy


## Correct Answer

A. Establishing a stable emotionally available caregiver


## Explanation

The cornerstone of treatment for reactive attachment disorder is ensuring consistent, emotionally supportive caregiving to promote healthy attachment development.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Attachment disorder + childhood neglect + treatment = Stable caregiver relationship


## Wrong Options

### B. Long-term benzodiazepine therapy


Benzodiazepines are not first-line treatment and may worsen emotional and behavioral regulation.


**Buzzword Combo:** Acute anxiety + sedation + GABA enhancement = Benzodiazepines


### C. Electroconvulsive therapy


ECT is not indicated for attachment disorders and is mainly used for severe mood disorders.

**Buzzword Combo:** Severe depression + catatonia + refractory psychiatric illness = ECT


### D. Stimulant monotherapy

Stimulants may help comorbid ADHD symptoms but do not address the underlying attachment disturbance.


**Buzzword Combo:** Inattention + hyperactivity + dopamine/norepinephrine enhancement = Stimulants
