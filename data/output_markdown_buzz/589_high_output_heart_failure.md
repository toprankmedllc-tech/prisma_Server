## Buzzword Question


Which of the following is NOT a recognized cause of high-output heart failure?

## Buzzwords Used

High-output heart failure; severe anemia; thyrotoxicosis; arteriovenous fistula; Paget disease; wet beriberi; aortic stenosis

## Answer Options

A) Severe aortic stenosis
B) Severe anemia
C) Thyrotoxicosis
D) Large arteriovenous fistula

## Correct Answer


A) Severe aortic stenosis

## Explanation

Aortic stenosis causes low-output (forward-failure) heart failure, not high-output. The classic causes of high-output heart failure include severe anemia, thyrotoxicosis, arteriovenous fistula, Paget disease of bone, and wet beriberi — all states that lower systemic vascular resistance or increase metabolic demand, driving a compensatory rise in cardiac output that eventually decompensates.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)


Severe aortic stenosis = low-output heart failure (not high-output)

## Wrong Options

### B) Severe anemia

Severe anemia reduces oxygen-carrying capacity, driving compensatory tachycardia and increased cardiac output — a classic cause of high-output failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Severe anemia + tachycardia + ↑ cardiac output = high-output heart failure

### 
C) Thyrotoxicosis


Excess thyroid hormone increases metabolic demand and lowers SVR, driving high-output heart failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thyrotoxicosis + ↑ metabolic rate + ↓ SVR = high-output heart failure

### D) Large arteriovenous fistula


AV fistulas (e.g., dialysis access, traumatic) shunt blood from arterial to venous circulation, lowering SVR and driving high-output failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Large AV fistula + ↓ SVR + ↑ cardiac output = high-output heart failure
