## Buzzword Question

Researchers are evaluating how frequently medical students use evidence-based resources while studying. Students are informed that their online learning activity will be tracked for four weeks as part of an educational research project. During the study period, use of evidence-based resources increases substantially compared with prior semesters. The investigators are concerned that the observed increase may not reflect the students' usual study habits.

Which of the following would be most effective in reducing this source of bias in a future study?

## Buzzwords Used

Educational research ; Monitoring ; Study habits ; Awareness of observation ; Bias reduction

## Answer Options

A) Increase the number of participating students

B) Conceal the behavior being evaluated from participants

C) Match students according to academic performance

D) Exclude students who study fewer than 10 hours per week

## Correct Answer

B) Conceal the behavior being evaluated from participants

## Explanation

The bias arises because participants alter their behavior when they know what is being observed. Concealing the behavior under evaluation reduces this effect.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Awareness of observation + altered behavior + concealment of study focus = Reduce Hawthorne effect

## Wrong Options

### A) Increase the number of participating students

A larger sample size improves precision but does not eliminate behavior changes caused by observation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Observation-related bias + larger sample = Bias remains

### C) Match students according to academic performance

Matching helps control confounding variables but does not address behavior changes caused by awareness of monitoring.

**Buzzword Combo:** Potential confounder + matching = Confounding control

### D) Exclude students who study fewer than 10 hours per week

Changing eligibility criteria does not reduce behavior modification due to observation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Participant restriction + observation awareness remains = Bias remains
