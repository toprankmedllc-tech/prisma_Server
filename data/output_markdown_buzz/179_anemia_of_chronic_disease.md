## Buzzword Question

A 60-year-old man with rheumatoid arthritis, fatigue, normocytic anemia, low serum iron, low transferrin saturation, and elevated ferritin most likely has which laboratory pattern?

## Buzzwords Used

rheumatoid arthritis; chronic inflammation; normocytic anemia; low serum iron; low transferrin saturation; elevated ferritin

## Answer Options

A) Low ferritin, high TIBC
B) High ferritin, low serum iron
C) High TIBC, high serum iron
D) Low ferritin, low serum iron

## Correct Answer

B. High ferritin, low serum iron

## Explanation

Anemia of chronic disease is caused by hepcidin-mediated sequestration of iron within macrophages. This leads to low serum iron with elevated ferritin due to preserved iron stores and inflammation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

High ferritin + low serum iron + chronic inflammation = Anemia of chronic disease

## Wrong Options

### A. Low ferritin, high TIBC

This pattern is characteristic of iron deficiency anemia, where iron stores are depleted and transferrin production increases.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + high TIBC + microcytosis = Iron deficiency anemia

### C. High TIBC, high serum iron

High serum iron and high transferrin saturation are more consistent with iron overload conditions such as hereditary hemochromatosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** High serum iron + high transferrin saturation = Iron overload

### D. Low ferritin, low serum iron

Low ferritin and low serum iron together suggest iron deficiency anemia or mixed iron deficiency with chronic disease rather than isolated anemia of chronic disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + low serum iron = Iron deficiency pattern
