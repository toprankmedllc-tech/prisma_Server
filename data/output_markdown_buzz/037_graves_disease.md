## Buzzword Question

A 52-year-old woman presents with progressive double vision, difficulty driving at night, palpitations, and intermittent diarrhea for the past 4 months. She also reports increased sweating and unintentional weight loss despite normal food intake. Physical examination reveals bilateral conjunctival injection, impaired upward gaze, lid retraction, nail clubbing and a diffusely enlarged nontender thyroid gland. Laboratory studies show suppressed TSH and elevated free T4 levels. Which of the following best explains this patient’s ocular findings?

## Buzzwords Used

Diplopia; Lid retraction; Impaired upward gaze; Diffuse goiter; Weight loss; Palpitations; Nail clubbing (thyroid acropachy) in the setting of hyperthyroidism is specific for Graves disease; Low TSH; High T4


## Answer Options

A) Cytokine-mediated stimulation of orbital fibroblasts causing glycosaminoglycan deposition
B) Compression of the optic chiasm by an enlarging pituitary mass
C) Autoantibody-mediated destruction of postsynaptic acetylcholine receptors
D) Spread of bacterial sinus infection into orbital tissues

## Correct Answer

A) Cytokine-mediated stimulation of orbital fibroblasts causing glycosaminoglycan deposition


## Explanation

Graves ophthalmopathy occurs due to T-cell activation and cytokine-mediated stimulation of retro-orbital fibroblasts, leading to glycosaminoglycan deposition, edema, and enlargement of extraocular muscles. This causes diplopia, lid retraction, impaired gaze, and exophthalmos.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hyperthyroidism + Lid retraction + Diplopia = Graves ophthalmopathy

## Wrong Options

### B) Compression of the optic chiasm by an enlarging pituitary mass

Pituitary adenomas can cause visual field defects such as bitemporal hemianopia but do not cause hyperthyroidism with diffuse goiter and ophthalmopathy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Headache + Bitemporal hemianopia + Pituitary enlargement = Pituitary adenoma


### C) Autoantibody-mediated destruction of postsynaptic acetylcholine receptors

Myasthenia gravis causes fluctuating diplopia and ptosis due to fatigable weakness, but it does not cause lid retraction, thyroid enlargement, or hyperthyroidism.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ptosis + Fatigable weakness + Worse at end of day = Myasthenia gravis


### D) Spread of bacterial sinus infection into orbital tissues

Orbital cellulitis presents with fever, painful eye movements, and unilateral orbital swelling due to infection. Chronic hyperthyroid symptoms are absent.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fever + Painful eye movement + Sinus infection = Orbital cellulitis
