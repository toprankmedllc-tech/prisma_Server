## Buzzword Question

A 15-year-old boy is brought to the neurology clinic due to progressive difficulty in walking for the past 2 years. He frequently trips while running and has become clumsy in school sports. His parents report slurred speech and increasing difficulty with handwriting.

On examination, he has a wide-based gait, absent patellar reflexes, and impaired vibration and position sense in both lower limbs. He also has a high-arched foot (pes cavus) and mild scoliosis.

Cardiac examination reveals a systolic murmur, and ECG shows left ventricular hypertrophy.

Genetic testing reveals an expanded GAA repeat in a gene responsible for mitochondrial iron regulation.

Which of the following best describes the inheritance pattern of this disease?

## Buzzwords Used

Adolescent onset ; progressive ataxia ; loss of proprioception ; absent reflexes ; pes cavus ; scoliosis ; cardiomyopathy ; GAA repeat expansion ; mitochondrial dysfunction ; dysarthria

## Answer Options

A) Autosomal dominant inheritance with anticipation
B) X-linked recessive inheritance with variable expressivity
C) Autosomal recessive inheritance with trinucleotide repeat expansion
D) Maternal mitochondrial inheritance

## Correct Answer

C) Autosomal recessive inheritance with trinucleotide repeat expansion

## Explanation

Friedreich Ataxia is caused by a GAA trinucleotide repeat expansion in the FXN gene, leading to decreased frataxin and mitochondrial dysfunction. It follows an autosomal recessive inheritance pattern and presents with progressive ataxia, loss of proprioception, and cardiomyopathy.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

GAA repeat expansion + ataxia + cardiomyopathy = Friedreich ataxia (autosomal recessive neurodegeneration)

## Wrong Options

### A) Autosomal dominant inheritance with anticipation

Autosomal dominant anticipation is seen in disorders like Huntington disease, not Friedreich ataxia, which is autosomal recessive.

**Buzzword Combo:** CAG expansion + chorea + psychiatric symptoms = Huntington disease

### B) X-linked recessive inheritance with variable expressivity

X-linked disorders typically affect males more severely but do not explain GAA repeat expansion or cardiomyopathy with sensory ataxia.

**Buzzword Combo:** X-linked mutation + macroorchidism + intellectual disability = Fragile X syndrome

### D) Maternal mitochondrial inheritance

Mitochondrial diseases show maternal inheritance and multisystem involvement but Friedreich ataxia is nuclear gene–based autosomal recessive disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Maternal inheritance + lactic acidosis + muscle weakness = mitochondrial disease
