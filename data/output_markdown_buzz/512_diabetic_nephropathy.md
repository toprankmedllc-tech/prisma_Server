## Buzzword Question

A 52-year-old man with long-standing poorly controlled hypertension and diabetes mellitus undergoes routine follow-up. Physical examination is unremarkable, and serum creatinine is normal. Which of the following findings is most suggestive of early diabetic nephropathy?

## Buzzwords Used

Diabetes mellitus; Long standing; Normal creatinine

## Answer Options

A) Massive hematuria with red blood cell casts
B) Markedly decreased serum complement levels
C) Microalbuminuria
D) Bence Jones proteinuria

## Correct Answer

C) Microalbuminuria

## Explanation

Diabetic nephropathy initially presents with microalbuminuria due to early glomerular damage from chronic hyperglycemia. Persistent microalbuminuria is an important early marker of diabetic kidney disease and may progress to nephrotic-range proteinuria over time.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Diabetes mellitus + early glomerular injury + microalbuminuria = Diabetic nephropathy

## Wrong Options

### Massive hematuria with red blood cell casts

Glomerulonephritis is characterized by red blood cell casts and significant hematuria due to glomerular inflammation. These findings are more consistent with nephritic syndromes than early diabetic nephropathy

**Buzzword Combo:** RBC casts + glomerular inflammation + nephritic syndrome = Glomerulonephritis

### Markedly decreased serum complement levels

Immune complex glomerulonephritis commonly produces low complement levels due to immune complex activation of the complement cascade. Examples include poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis and lupus nephritis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Immune complex deposition + complement consumption +RBC cast= Lupus nephritis

### Bence Jones proteinuria

Multiple myeloma is associated with Bence Jones proteins, which are monoclonal light chains filtered into the urine. Patients may also develop lytic bone lesions, hypercalcemia, and renal failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Monoclonal light chains + plasma cell proliferation + Bence Jones proteinuria = Multiple myeloma
