## Buzzword Question

A physician begins a clinic visit with a patient who has a strong accent and limited eye contact. Before discussing symptoms, the physician asks which language the patient prefers for medical communication. The patient requests Mandarin interpretation services.

Which of the following is the most appropriate next step?

## Buzzwords Used

Preferred language ; Interpreter request ; Avoiding assumptions ; Clinical communication

## Answer Options

A) Continue the visit in English because the patient answered the initial greeting appropriately

B) Ask a bilingual office receptionist to interpret during the visit

C) Arrange professional interpretation services for the encounter

D) Request that the patient return another day with a family member to translate

## Correct Answer

C) Arrange professional interpretation services for the encounter

## Explanation

Professional interpreters should be used when requested or needed to ensure accurate communication and informed medical decision-making.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Patient language preference + interpreter request + medical discussion = professional interpretation

## Wrong Options

### A) Continue the visit in English because the patient answered the initial greeting appropriately

Basic conversational ability does not guarantee adequate understanding of medical discussions.

**Buzzword Combo:** Conversational English + medical complexity + false fluency assumption = communication risk

### B) Ask a bilingual office receptionist to interpret during the visit

Non-certified bilingual staff may lack appropriate medical interpretation training.

**Buzzword Combo:** Untrained translator + clinical discussion + interpretation inaccuracy = inappropriate substitution

### D) Request that the patient return another day with a family member to translate

Professional interpretation should be provided directly rather than delaying care or relying on relatives.

**Buzzword Combo:** Interpreter need + delayed care + family reliance = inappropriate management
