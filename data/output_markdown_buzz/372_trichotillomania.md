## Buzzword Question

A patient with trichotillomania continues to have distressing hair-pulling behavior despite education and reassurance. Which of the following is considered first-line therapy?


## Buzzwords Used

 Hair-pulling disorder; treatment; CBT; habit reversal therapy


## Answer Options

 A) Habit reversal therapy
 B) Electroconvulsive therapy
 C) Lithium
 D) Benzodiazepines


## Correct Answer

A. Habit reversal therapy

## Explanation

Cognitive behavioral therapy with habit reversal training is the first-line treatment for trichotillomania. SSRIs have limited evidence of benefit.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Trichotillomania + CBT + behavioral treatment = Habit reversal therapy


## Wrong Options

### B. Electroconvulsive therapy


ECT is mainly used for severe mood disorders and catatonia, not trichotillomania.


**Buzzword Combo:** Severe depression + catatonia + refractory psychiatric illness = ECT


### C. Lithium


Lithium is primarily used as a mood stabilizer in bipolar disorder.


**Buzzword Combo:** Mania + mood stabilization + suicide risk reduction = Lithium

### D. Benzodiazepines


Benzodiazepines are not first-line therapy for compulsive hair-pulling disorders.


**Buzzword Combo:** Acute anxiety + sedation + GABA enhancement = Benzodiazepines
