## Buzzword Question

A 38-year-old patient with fatigue, easy bruising, spontaneous mucosal bleeding, promyelocytes with numerous Auer rods, and DIC most likely has which genetic abnormality?

## Buzzwords Used

38-year-old; fatigue; easy bruising; mucosal bleeding; promyelocytes; Auer rods; DIC; genetic abnormality

## Answer Options

A) t(9;22)
B) t(15;17)
C) t(8;14)
D) t(14;18)

## Correct Answer

B. t(15;17)

## Explanation

t(15;17) creates the PML-RARA fusion gene, which blocks differentiation at the promyelocyte stage. This leads to acute promyelocytic leukemia, which is strongly associated with life-threatening DIC.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

t(15;17) + PML-RARA + promyelocytes + DIC = Acute promyelocytic leukemia

## Wrong Options

### A) t(9;22)

t(9;22) produces BCR-ABL tyrosine kinase in chronic myeloid leukemia, characterized by granulocytosis and basophilia. It is not associated with promyelocyte arrest or DIC.

**Buzzword Combo:** BCR-ABL + basophilia + low LAP = CML

### C) t(8;14)

t(8;14) leads to c-MYC overexpression in Burkitt lymphoma, which presents with a starry-sky pattern and rapidly growing tumors. It is unrelated to AML or DIC.

**Buzzword Combo:** c-MYC + starry-sky + jaw mass = Burkitt lymphoma

### D) t(14;18)

t(14;18) causes BCL2 overexpression in follicular lymphoma, leading to inhibition of apoptosis. It presents as an indolent lymphoid malignancy, not an acute myeloid leukemia.

**Buzzword Combo:** BCL2 + follicular pattern + indolent course = Follicular lymphoma
