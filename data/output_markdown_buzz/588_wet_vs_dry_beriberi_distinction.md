## Buzzword Question

A patient with thiamine deficiency presents with high-output heart failure characterized by warm extremities, bounding pulses, peripheral edema, and an S3 gallop. This presentation is best described as:

## Buzzwords Used

Thiamine deficiency; high-output heart failure; warm extremities; bounding pulses; peripheral edema; S3 gallop; wet beriberi

## Answer Options

A) Wet beriberi
B) Dry beriberi
C) Wernicke encephalopathy
D) Korsakoff syndrome

## Correct Answer

A) Wet beriberi

## Explanation

Wet beriberi is the cardiac manifestation of thiamine deficiency, characterized by high-output heart failure with warm extremities, bounding pulses, wide pulse pressure, peripheral edema, and an S3 gallop. Dry beriberi is the neurologic manifestation, presenting as symmetric peripheral polyneuropathy.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)


Thiamine deficiency + high-output cardiac failure + S3 gallop = wet beriberi

## Wrong Options

### 
B) Dry beriberi


Dry beriberi is the neurologic form of thiamine deficiency — symmetric peripheral polyneuropathy with muscle wasting — not cardiac failure.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thiamine deficiency + symmetric peripheral polyneuropathy + muscle wasting = dry beriberi

### C) Wernicke encephalopathy

Wernicke encephalopathy is the acute CNS manifestation of thiamine deficiency: the triad of confusion + ataxia + ophthalmoplegia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thiamine deficiency + confusion + ataxia + ophthalmoplegia = Wernicke encephalopathy

### D) Korsakoff syndrome

Korsakoff syndrome is the chronic CNS manifestation: amnesia + confabulation following untreated Wernicke encephalopathy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thiamine deficiency + amnesia + confabulation = Korsakoff syndrome
