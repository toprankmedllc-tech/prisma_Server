## Buzzword Question

A 32-year-old woman is brought to the clinic by her husband who reports she has been “a completely different person” for the past 8 months. She has been making large unauthorized purchases, started multiple new business ventures simultaneously, and has been sleeping only 2–3 hours per night without feeling tired. She denies any mood problems and states she “has never felt better.” Her husband notes she had a 3-month episode of profound sadness, hypersomnia, and worthlessness 2 years ago that resolved without treatment. She drinks 2 glasses of wine nightly and takes no medications. Urine toxicology is negative. Which of the following best explains her current presentation?

## Buzzwords Used

Grandiosity; decreased need for sleep; impulsivity/spending; multiple projects; denial of illness (anosognosia); prior depressive episode; alcohol use; tox negative

## Answer Options

A) Bipolar I disorder, current manic episode
B) Bipolar II disorder, current hypomanic episode
C) Antisocial personality disorder
D) Substance-induced mood disorder

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Bipolar I requires at least one manic episode (≥7 days OR any duration if hospitalized) with grandiosity, decreased sleep, impulsivity, and goal-directed activity causing marked functional impairment — her 8-month history of unauthorized spending, business ventures, and disrupted sleep with a prior depressive episode confirms Bipolar I, current manic episode. Anosognosia (lack of insight) is a hallmark feature. Alcohol use alone does not account for 8 months of sustained symptoms with negative toxicology.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

8 months grandiosity + decreased sleep need + impulsive spending + prior depressive episode + tox negative + no insight = Bipolar I, Manic episode

## Wrong Options

###  B) Bipolar II disorder, current hypomanic episode

Bipolar II requires that the patient has NEVER had a full manic episode — hypomania lasts ≥4 days and causes noticeable change but NOT marked impairment or psychosis. This patient’s unauthorized spending, multiple failed ventures, and 8-month duration represent marked functional impairment, which by definition rules out hypomania and confirms mania.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prior depression + elevated mood → Bipolar II (ignores marked impairment + 8-month duration = crosses threshold into mania)

### C) Antisocial personality disorder

Antisocial PD requires a conduct disorder history before age 15 and a pervasive pattern of disregard for others’ rights — not episodic onset with a clear prior depressive episode. The acute 8-month behavioral change from a previously normal baseline rules against a lifelong personality pattern.

**Buzzword Combo:** Impulsive spending + disregard for consequences → antisocial PD (ignores episodic onset + prior depressive episode + no conduct disorder history = not a personality pattern)

###  D) Substance-induced mood disorder

Substance-induced mood disorder requires that symptoms are directly attributable to intoxication or withdrawal from a substance. Two glasses of wine nightly does not produce sustained 8-month grandiosity with decreased sleep need, and urine tox is negative — symptoms clearly exceed what alcohol alone would cause.

**Buzzword Combo:** Alcohol use + behavioral change → substance-induced (ignores 8-month sustained course + negative tox + symptoms exceed substance effect)
