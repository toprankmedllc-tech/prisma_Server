## Buzzword Question

A 15-year-old boy requests contraception counseling and asks that the discussion remain private from his parents. Which of the following is the best physician response?

## Buzzwords Used

Minor confidentiality ; Contraception counseling ; Adolescent reproductive health

## Answer Options

A) “I must obtain parental permission before discussing contraception.”
B) “I will keep this discussion confidential.”
C) “Your parents are entitled to know about all reproductive health visits.”
D) “Confidential counseling is only available after age 18.”

## Correct Answer

B) “I will keep this discussion confidential.”

## Explanation

Confidential reproductive health counseling encourages adolescents to seek appropriate medical care and is generally protected unless safety concerns are present.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Minor patient + Reproductive counseling + No safety concern = Confidential care

## Wrong Options

### A) “I must obtain parental permission before discussing contraception.”

Parental permission is not generally required for confidential contraceptive counseling in adolescents.

**Buzzword Combo:** Teenager + Contraception request + False parental requirement = Incorrect restriction

### C) “Your parents are entitled to know about all reproductive health visits.”

Confidential reproductive healthcare is commonly protected for adolescent patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Minor patient + Confidential counseling + Automatic disclosure misconception = Ethical breach

### D) “Confidential counseling is only available after age 18.”

Adolescents may receive confidential reproductive counseling before adulthood.

**Buzzword Combo:** Age under 18 + Reproductive counseling + Denial of confidentiality = Incorrect practice
