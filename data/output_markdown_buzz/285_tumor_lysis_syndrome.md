## Buzzword Question

A patient with high-grade lymphoma develops acute kidney injury after starting chemotherapy. Serum uric acid is markedly increased. Which of the following best explains the renal injury in this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

High-grade lymphoma after starting chemotherapy; acute kidney injury; markedly increased serum uric acid

## Answer Options

A) Immune complex deposition in glomeruli
B) Uric acid crystal precipitation in renal tubules
C) Myoglobin-induced tubular epithelial injury
D) Calcium oxalate crystal deposition in renal tubules

## Correct Answer

B) Uric acid crystal precipitation in renal tubules


## Explanation

Tumor lysis syndrome causes nucleic acid release from destroyed tumor cells. These nucleic acids are degraded into purines and then uric acid, which can precipitate in renal tubules and cause acute kidney injury.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Tumor cell lysis + purine degradation + uric acid crystals in renal tubules = tumor lysis syndrome

## Wrong Options

### A) Immune complex deposition in glomeruli

Immune complex deposition causes glomerular injury in diseases such as lupus nephritis and poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis. It is not the mechanism of kidney injury from rapid tumor cell breakdown.

**Buzzword Combo:** Immune complexes + complement activation + glomerular inflammation = immune complex glomerulonephritis

### C) Myoglobin-induced tubular epithelial injury

Myoglobin-induced tubular injury occurs in rhabdomyolysis. The classic clues are muscle injury, dark urine, and heme-positive urine with few RBCs.

**Buzzword Combo:** Muscle breakdown + dark urine + heme-positive urine with few RBCs = rhabdomyolysis

### D) Calcium oxalate crystal deposition in renal tubules

Calcium oxalate crystal deposition is associated with ethylene glycol poisoning. It causes acute kidney injury through toxic alcohol metabolism, not purine degradation after tumor cell lysis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Antifreeze ingestion + anion gap metabolic acidosis + calcium oxalate crystals = ethylene glycol poisoning
