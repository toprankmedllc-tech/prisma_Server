## Buzzword Question

An 8-year-old boy with intellectual disability and a long history of seizures has multiple oval hypopigmented skin macules over the trunk that fluoresce under Wood lamp examination, and clusters of small reddish papules on the cheeks and around the nose. MRI shows cortical tubers and small calcified nodules along the ventricular surfaces. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Intellectual disability; seizures; ash-leaf hypopigmented macules visible under Wood lamp; facial angiofibromas; cortical tubers and calcified subependymal nodules on MRI

## Answer Options

A) Neurofibromatosis type 1
B) Sturge-Weber syndrome
C) Tuberous sclerosis complex
D) Von Hippel-Lindau disease

## Correct Answer

Tuberous sclerosis complex

## Explanation

Ash-leaf hypopigmented macules, facial angiofibromas, cortical tubers, and subependymal nodules with seizures and intellectual disability are diagnostic for tuberous sclerosis complex (autosomal-dominant TSC1 or TSC2 mutation, mTOR pathway disinhibition).

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Ash-leaf spots + facial angiofibromas + cortical tubers + subependymal nodules + seizures + intellectual disability = tuberous sclerosis complex

## Wrong Options

### Neurofibromatosis type 1

NF1 (chromosome 17, neurofibromin) features cafe-au-lait macules, axillary and inguinal freckling, Lisch nodules of the iris, cutaneous neurofibromas, and optic gliomas; it does not produce ash-leaf macules, angiofibromas, or cortical tubers.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cafe-au-lait macules + axillary freckling + Lisch nodules + optic glioma + chromosome 17 = neurofibromatosis type 1

### Sturge-Weber syndrome

Sturge-Weber features a facial port-wine stain in the trigeminal V1 distribution, ipsilateral leptomeningeal angiomatosis, seizures, and glaucoma; it does not produce ash-leaf macules or cortical tubers.

**Buzzword Combo:** Port-wine stain in V1 distribution + leptomeningeal angiomatosis + seizures + glaucoma = Sturge-Weber syndrome

### Von Hippel-Lindau disease

VHL (chromosome 3) features cerebellar and retinal hemangioblastomas, clear cell renal cell carcinoma, and pheochromocytoma; it has no skin findings or cortical tubers.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cerebellar hemangioblastoma + clear cell RCC + pheochromocytoma + chromosome 3 = von Hippel-Lindau disease

## Related Concepts

Cardiac rhabdomyoma; Renal angiomyolipoma in adults with TSC; Pulmonary lymphangioleiomyomatosis (LAM) in adult women with TSC
