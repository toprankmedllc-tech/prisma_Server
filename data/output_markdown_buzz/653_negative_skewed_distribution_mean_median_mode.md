## Buzzword Question

A pharmacology department is analyzing serum drug concentrations in patients receiving a fixed dose of a new medication. Most patients show similar drug levels within a narrow therapeutic range. However, a small subset of patients demonstrate unexpectedly very low drug concentrations despite identical dosing, likely due to variability in metabolism. When summarizing the dataset, investigators note that a few unusually low values appear to influence the average concentration more than the value that appears most frequently in the dataset.

Which of the following best describes the distribution of this dataset?

## Buzzwords Used

Drug concentrations ; Fixed dosing ; Low outliers ; Central tendency ; Population variability

## Answer Options

A) Mean > median > mode
B) Mean = median = mode
C) Median > mean > mode
D) Mode > median > mean

## Correct Answer

D) Mode > median > mean

## Explanation

A small number of very low outliers pull the mean downward more than the median, while most values remain clustered at higher concentrations. This produces a negatively skewed distribution where the mode is highest and the mean is lowest.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

low outliers + left tail influence + clustering at higher values = mode > median > mean

## Wrong Options

### A) Mean > median > mode

This pattern reflects a positively skewed distribution caused by high-value outliers, which is the opposite of what is described.

**Buzzword Combo:** high outliers + right tail = mean > median > mode

### B) Mean = median = mode

A symmetric distribution would not be influenced by extreme outliers, so all measures of central tendency would remain similar.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
symmetry + no skew + no outliers = mean ≈ median ≈ mode

### C) Median > mean > mode

This ordering does not represent a valid skew pattern for continuous biological data and reflects a misinterpretation of how outliers influence central tendency. In real distributions, mean shifts more than median, and mode remains anchored to the most frequent cluster.

**Buzzword Combo:** incorrect central tendency ordering + misapplied summary statistics = invalid distribution interpretation
