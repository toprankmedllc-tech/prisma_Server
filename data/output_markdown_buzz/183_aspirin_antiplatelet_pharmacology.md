## Buzzword Question

A patient with ischemic heart disease is prescribed a daily medication to reduce platelet-mediated arterial thrombosis. The drug decreases platelet production of a mediator that normally promotes platelet aggregation and vasoconstriction. Which of the following best describes this drug’s antiplatelet mechanism?

## Buzzwords Used

Ischemic heart disease; platelet-mediated arterial thrombosis; decreased platelet aggregation mediator

## Answer Options

A) Irreversible inhibition of cyclooxygenase-1
B) Reversible blockade of ADP receptors
C) Activation of antithrombin III
D) Inhibition of vitamin K recycling

## Correct Answer

A) Irreversible inhibition of cyclooxygenase-1


## Explanation

Aspirin irreversibly inhibits COX-1 in platelets, decreasing thromboxane A2 synthesis. This reduces platelet aggregation and prolongs bleeding time.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Ischemic heart disease prevention + decreased platelet aggregation + decreased thromboxane A2 = aspirin

## Wrong Options

### B) Reversible blockade of ADP receptors

This describes P2Y12 inhibitors such as ticagrelor. These drugs inhibit ADP-mediated platelet activation, not COX-1-mediated thromboxane A2 synthesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** ADP receptor blockade + decreased platelet aggregation + antiplatelet therapy = P2Y12 inhibitor

### C) Activation of antithrombin III

This describes heparin. Heparin acts on the coagulation cascade through antithrombin III rather than directly inhibiting platelet thromboxane production.

**Buzzword Combo:** Injectable anticoagulant + antithrombin III activation + inhibition of thrombin/Xa = heparin

### D) Inhibition of vitamin K recycling

This describes warfarin. Warfarin reduces activation of vitamin K–dependent clotting factors and is monitored with PT/INR.

**Buzzword Combo:** Oral anticoagulant + increased PT/INR first + decreased activation of II, VII, IX, X = warfarin
