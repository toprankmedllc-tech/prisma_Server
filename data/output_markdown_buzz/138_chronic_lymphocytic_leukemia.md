## Buzzword Question

A 72-year-old man with fatigue, painless lymphadenopathy, lymphocytosis, and smudge cells on peripheral smear undergoes flow cytometry. Which immunophenotype is most consistent with CLL?

## Buzzwords Used

72-year-old man; fatigue; painless lymphadenopathy; lymphocytosis; smudge cells; flow cytometry

## Answer Options

A) CD10+ CD19+
B) CD5+ CD20+
C) CD15+ CD30+
D) CD3+ CD4+

## Correct Answer

B. CD5+ CD20+

## Explanation

CLL is a mature B-cell malignancy that aberrantly expresses CD5 along with CD19 and CD20 (dim) and CD23 positivity. This immunophenotype helps distinguish it from other B-cell lymphomas.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CD5+ CD23+ B cells + smudge cells = CLL

## Wrong Options

### A. CD10+ CD19+

CD10 positivity is seen in follicular lymphoma and some B-ALL cases, not CLL.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD10+ BCL2+ = Follicular lymphoma

### C. CD15+ CD30+

CD15 and CD30 positivity is characteristic of Reed-Sternberg cells in Hodgkin lymphoma.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD15+ CD30+ owl-eye cells = Hodgkin lymphoma

### D. CD3+ CD4+

CD3 and CD4 are T-cell markers, while CLL is a B-cell malignancy.

**Buzzword Combo:** CD3+ CD4+ = Helper T cells
