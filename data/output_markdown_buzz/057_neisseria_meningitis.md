## Buzzword Question

A previously healthy 18-year-old college dormitory resident develops fever, neck stiffness, and a non-blanching purpuric rash. CSF Gram stain shows gram-negative diplococci. The disease is driven primarily by:

## Buzzwords Used

18-year old; college dormitory resident; gram-negative diplococci

## Answer Options

A) IgA protease and lipooligosaccharide (LOS) endotoxin
B) Polysaccharide capsule alone
C) An exotoxin that increases intracellular cAMP
D)Lipoteichoic acid in the cell wall

## Correct Answer

A) IgA protease and lipooligosaccharide (LOS) endotoxin

## Explanation

N. meningitidis uses IgA protease for mucosal colonization and LOS endotoxin to drive DIC, septic shock, and the classic petechial rash.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Gram-negative diplococci + purpura + dorm = N. meningitidis

## Wrong Options

### Polysaccharide capsule alone

The capsule resists phagocytosis and is the basis of the meningococcal vaccine, but capsule alone does not explain the petechial rash and DIC seen here.

**Buzzword Combo:** Capsule = anti-phagocytic, not the cause of the rash

### An exotoxin that increases intracellular cAMP

Describes cholera toxin (Vibrio cholerae) or pertussis toxin — secretory diarrhea or whooping cough, not meningitis.

**Buzzword Combo:** ↑cAMP exotoxin = cholera / pertussis

### Lipoteichoic acid in the cell wall

Lipoteichoic acid is found in gram-positive cell walls (e.g., S. pneumoniae) — not in gram-negative N. meningitidis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Lipoteichoic acid = gram-positive bug
