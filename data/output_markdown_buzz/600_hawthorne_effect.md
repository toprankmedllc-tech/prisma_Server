## Buzzword Question

A company introduces wearable fitness trackers to monitor employee physical activity as part of a workplace wellness study. During the six-month study period, average daily step counts increase substantially. Three months after monitoring ends, step counts return to previous levels.

Which bias most likely explains these findings?

## Buzzwords Used

Fitness trackers ; Monitoring ; Increased activity ; Behavior change ; Observation

## Answer Options

A) Recall bias

B) Hawthorne effect

C) Lead-time bias

D) Sampling bias

## Correct Answer

B) Hawthorne effect

## Explanation

Employees increased their activity because they knew they were being monitored. This change in behavior due to awareness of observation is characteristic of the Hawthorne effect.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Awareness of monitoring + temporary behavior change + return to baseline after observation = Hawthorne effect

## Wrong Options

### A) Recall bias

Recall bias involves inaccurate recollection of past events or exposures and is not related to behavior changes caused by observation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Retrospective self-reporting + inaccurate memory = Recall bias

### C) Lead-time bias

Lead-time bias occurs when earlier detection falsely appears to improve survival without changing disease outcome.

**Buzzword Combo:** Earlier diagnosis + unchanged mortality + apparent survival benefit = Lead-time bias

### D) Sampling bias

Sampling bias occurs when the study sample does not adequately represent the target population.

**Buzzword Combo:** Nonrepresentative sample + recruitment issue = Sampling bias
