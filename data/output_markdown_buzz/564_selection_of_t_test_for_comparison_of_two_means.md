## Buzzword Question

Researchers compare the mean serum LDL cholesterol level between patients receiving Drug A and patients receiving Drug B. LDL cholesterol is reported as a continuous variable, and the investigators wish to determine whether the group means differ significantly.

Which statistical test is most appropriate?

## Buzzwords Used

Two groups ; Continuous variable ; Mean comparison ; LDL cholesterol

## Answer Options

A) Chi-square test

B) Pearson correlation coefficient

C) Independent samples t-test

D) ANOVA

## Correct Answer

C) Independent samples t-test

## Explanation

An independent samples t-test is used to compare the means of a continuous variable between two independent groups.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Continuous outcome + two independent groups + comparison of means = Independent samples t-test

## Wrong Options

### A) Chi-square test

Chi-square analysis is used for categorical variables rather than continuous outcomes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Categorical outcome + categorical groups + association testing = Chi-square test

### B) Pearson correlation coefficient

Pearson correlation assesses the relationship between two continuous variables rather than differences between group means.

**Buzzword Combo:** Two continuous variables + strength of association + linear relationship = Pearson correlation

### D) ANOVA

ANOVA is generally used when comparing means across three or more independent groups.

**Buzzword Combo:** Continuous outcome + three or more groups + comparison of means = ANOVA
