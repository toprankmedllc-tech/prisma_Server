## Buzzword Question

Autopsy of a patient with longstanding hypertension reveals a left ventricle with wall thickening at the expense of cavity size, without chamber dilation. Histology shows uniform enlargement of cardiomyocytes arranged in their normal parallel pattern. This finding is most consistent with:

## Buzzwords Used

Longstanding hypertension; wall thickening; no chamber dilation; uniform myocyte enlargement; concentric LVH; sarcomeres added in parallel; pressure-overload hypertrophy

## Answer Options


A) Concentric left ventricular hypertrophy (sarcomeres added in parallel)
B) Eccentric left ventricular hypertrophy (sarcomeres added in series)
C) Myocyte disarray with interstitial fibrosis
D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining

## Correct Answer

A) Concentric left ventricular hypertrophy (sarcomeres added in parallel)

## Explanation

wall thickening at the expense of cavity size with uniformly enlarged cardiomyocytes — is the structural pattern produced by chronic pressure overload (hypertension, aortic stenosis). At the cellular level, sarcomeres are added in parallel, widening each myocyte without lengthening it.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Chronic HTN / AS + wall thickening + uniform myocyte enlargement + sarcomeres in parallel = concentric LVH

## Wrong Options

### 
B) Eccentric left ventricular hypertrophy (sarcomeres added in series)

Volume overload (aortic or mitral regurgitation, dilated cardiomyopathy) produces eccentric hypertrophy with chamber dilation; sarcomeres are added in series, lengthening myocytes.

**Buzzword Combo:** Volume overload + chamber dilation + sarcomeres in series = eccentric hypertrophy

### C) Myocyte disarray with interstitial fibrosis


The hallmark of hypertrophic cardiomyopathy — a sarcomeric protein disorder, not pressure-overload remodeling.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asymmetric septal hypertrophy + myocyte disarray + fibrosis = HCM

### 
D) Apple-green birefringence with Congo red staining

Hallmark of cardiac amyloidosis — extracellular amyloid deposition, not pressure-overload hypertrophy.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Congo red + apple-green birefringence = amyloidosis
