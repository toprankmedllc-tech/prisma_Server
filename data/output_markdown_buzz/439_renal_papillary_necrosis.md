## Buzzword Question

A patient develops hematuria and sloughed necrotic tissue in the urine due to ischemic injury of the renal medulla. Which of the following underlying conditions most likely predisposes to this complication?

## Buzzwords Used

Medullary ischemia; Hematuria; Sloughed papillae

## Answer Options

A) Hyperthyroidism
B) Diabetes mellitus
C) Myasthenia gravis
D) Ulcerative colitis

## Correct Answer

B) Diabetes mellitus

## Explanation

Renal papillary necrosis commonly occurs in conditions that impair blood supply to the renal medulla, including diabetes mellitus, sickle cell disease, severe pyelonephritis, and chronic analgesic use.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Diabetes mellitus + medullary ischemia + Hematuria = Renal papillary necrosis

## Wrong Options

### Hyperthyroidism

Hyperthyroidism commonly presents with heat intolerance, weight loss, tachycardia, and tremor rather than ischemic renal injury. Increased sympathetic activity and metabolic rate are characteristic features

**Buzzword Combo:** Weight loss + tremor + tachycardia = Hyperthyroidism

### Myasthenia gravis

Myasthenia gravis causes fluctuating muscle weakness due to antibodies against acetylcholine receptors. Symptoms commonly worsen with repeated muscle use and improve with rest.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fatigable weakness + ptosis + acetylcholine receptor antibodies = Myasthenia gravis

### Ulcerative colitis

Ulcerative colitis causes continuous colonic inflammation with bloody diarrhea and abdominal pain. The disease primarily involves the rectum and colon mucosa.

**Buzzword Combo:** Bloody diarrhea + colonic inflammation = Ulcerative colitis
