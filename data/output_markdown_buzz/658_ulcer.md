## Buzzword Question

A patient presents with epigastric pain and upper GI bleeding after chronic use of over-the-counter pain medications. Endoscopy shows erythematous gastric mucosa with shallow ulcers.

## Buzzwords Used

epigastic pain; GI bleeding 

## Answer Options

A. Mucus barrier formation
B. Gastrin secretion
C. Intrinsic factor production
D. Pepsin activation

## Correct Answer

A. Mucus barrier formation

## Explanation

↓ Prostaglandins → ↓ mucus protection

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pain meds + decrease PG = mucus protection

## Wrong Options

### B. Gastrin secretion

Gastrin is a gastrointestinal peptide hormone secreted by enteroendocrine G-cells primarily in the antrum of the stomach. It stimulates gastric acid HCL secretion by parietal cells and promotes the growth of the gastric mucosa.

**Buzzword Combo:** Gastrin + HCL from parietal cells = growth of the gastric mucosa.

### C. Intrinsic factor production

Intrinsic factor (IF) is a glycoprotein produced by the parietal cells in the stomach's body and fundus. It is absolutely vital for the absorption of Vitamin B12 (cobalamin) in the small intestine, which is required for healthy red blood cell formation and neurological function.

**Buzzword Combo:** decrease IF and low Vitamin B12 = pernicous anemia 

### D. Pepsin activation

Pepsin activation is the process by which the inactive precursor pepsinogen is converted into the active digestive enzyme pepsin in the stomach.

**Buzzword Combo:** Increase Pepsin + Cheif cells = increase acid
