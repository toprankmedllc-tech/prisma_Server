## Buzzword Question

A boy with Tourette syndrome and ADHD continues to have functionally impairing tics despite behavioral therapy. Which medication class is commonly used for treatment and may also improve ADHD symptoms?


## Buzzwords Used

Tourette syndrome; ADHD; functionally impairing tics; pharmacologic treatment

## Answer Options

 A) Alpha-2 receptor agonists
 B) SSRIs
 C) Benzodiazepines
 D) Cholinesterase inhibitors


## Correct Answer

A. Alpha-2 receptor agonists


## Explanation

Alpha-2 receptor agonists such as clonidine and guanfacine are commonly used in Tourette syndrome and may also improve comorbid ADHD symptoms.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Tourette syndrome + ADHD + tic suppression = Alpha-2 receptor agonists


## Wrong Options

### B. SSRIs


SSRIs are mainly used for anxiety and OCD symptoms rather than primary tic suppression.


**Buzzword Combo:** Obsessions + compulsions + serotonin reuptake inhibition = SSRIs


### C. Benzodiazepines

Benzodiazepines are not first-line therapy for chronic tic disorders.

**Buzzword Combo:** Acute anxiety + panic symptoms + GABA enhancement = Benzodiazepines


### D. Cholinesterase inhibitors


Cholinesterase inhibitors are used in dementia disorders such as Alzheimer disease, not Tourette syndrome.


**Buzzword Combo:** Memory loss + dementia + acetylcholine enhancement = Cholinesterase inhibitors
