## Buzzword Question

An oral anticoagulant used for long-term prevention of embolic stroke in atrial fibrillation causes the PT/INR to increase before other coagulation tests. This drug most likely acts by which of the following mechanisms?

## Buzzwords Used

Oral anticoagulant for long-term stroke prevention; PT/INR increases first; atrial fibrillation

## Answer Options

A) Direct inhibition of thrombin and factor Xa
B) Inhibition of vitamin K recycling in the liver
C) Activation of antithrombin III
D) Direct inhibition of platelet ADP receptors

## Correct Answer

B) Inhibition of vitamin K recycling in the liver


## Explanation

Warfarin inhibits vitamin K epoxide reductase, preventing recycling of vitamin K into its active form. This decreases γ-glutamyl carboxylation and functional activation of factors II, VII, IX, X and proteins C/S.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Oral long-term anticoagulation + increased PT/INR first + decreased activation of II, VII, IX, X = warfarin

## Wrong Options

### A) Direct inhibition of thrombin and factor Xa

This describes direct oral anticoagulants, not warfarin. These drugs directly inhibit thrombin or factor Xa rather than vitamin K recycling.

**Buzzword Combo:** Direct thrombin or factor Xa inhibition + no vitamin K effect + oral anticoagulation = direct oral anticoagulants

### C) Activation of antithrombin III

This describes heparin. Heparin acts through antithrombin III and is not the classic oral drug that increases PT/INR by reducing vitamin K–dependent factor activation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Injectable anticoagulant + antithrombin III activation + inhibition of thrombin/Xa = heparin

### D) Direct inhibition of platelet ADP receptors

This describes clopidogrel. It blocks platelet aggregation, not vitamin K–dependent coagulation factor activation.

**Buzzword Combo:** ADP receptor blockade + decreased platelet aggregation + arterial thrombosis prevention = clopidogrel

## Related Concepts

Heparin mechanism; direct oral anticoagulants; vitamin K deficiency
