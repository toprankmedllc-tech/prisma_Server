## Buzzword Question

A 66-year-old woman undergoes a scheduled colonoscopy. Shortly after the procedure, the endoscopy unit discovers that due to an identification mismatch, the procedure was performed on a different patient with a similar name. The patient who received the procedure is stable but confused about why it was done. The attending physician is asked how to address the situation with the patient.

Which of the following is the most appropriate response?

## Buzzwords Used

Wrong patient procedure ; Identification error ; Patient safety event ; Post-procedure confusion

## Answer Options

A) “The procedure was part of your routine care and there is nothing unusual to report.”
B) “A mistake occurred and I will explain everything in detail and take full personal responsibility.”
C) “There was an identification issue that led to the procedure being performed on the wrong patient. I will explain what is known and discuss what happens next.”
D) “This type of event is expected in hospital settings and does not require further discussion.”

## Correct Answer

C) “There was an identification issue that led to the procedure being performed on the wrong patient. I will explain what is known and discuss what happens next.”

## Explanation

Ethical disclosure requires honesty about medical errors, clear communication of known facts, and discussion of next steps without withholding information or prematurely assigning personal blame.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Wrong patient event + system identification failure + need for transparency = disclose and explain

## Wrong Options

### A) “The procedure was part of your routine care and there is nothing unusual to report.”

This is a deliberate misrepresentation of a medical error and violates informed disclosure principles.

**Buzzword Combo:** Procedure error + concealment + false reassurance = unethical nondisclosure

### B) “A mistake occurred and I will explain everything in detail and take full personal responsibility.”

While disclosure is correct, taking full personal blame is inappropriate before understanding system-level contributors.

**Buzzword Combo:** Medical error + over-personalisation + premature blame = incorrect framing

### D) “This type of event is expected in hospital settings and does not require further discussion.”

Minimizing or normalizing a wrong-patient procedure is unethical and undermines patient trust and autonomy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Serious error + minimisation + avoidance of disclosure = incorrect response
