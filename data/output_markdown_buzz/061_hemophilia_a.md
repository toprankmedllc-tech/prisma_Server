## Buzzword Question

A 7-year-old boy is brought to the clinic after recurrent episodes of painful knee swelling. His mother reports that her brother died after minor head trauma during childhood. Laboratory studies show normal platelet count, normal bleeding time, normal PT, and increased PTT. The most likely defect involves:

## Buzzwords Used

Boy + hemarthrosis + maternal uncle + isolated increased PTT = hemophilia A

## Answer Options

A. Factor VIII deficiency
B. Platelet glycoprotein Ib deficiency
C. von Willebrand factor deficiency
D. Factor VII deficiency

## Correct Answer

A. Factor VIII deficiency


## Explanation

Hemophilia A is caused by factor VIII deficiency and is inherited in an X-linked recessive pattern. Factor VIII is part of the intrinsic coagulation pathway, so PTT is increased. PT and bleeding time are normal because the extrinsic pathway and platelet function are unaffected.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Boy + hemarthrosis + maternal uncle + isolated increased PTT = hemophilia A

## Wrong Options

### B. Platelet glycoprotein Ib deficiency


This describes Bernard-Soulier syndrome, which causes defective platelet adhesion and usually presents with mucocutaneous bleeding and abnormal bleeding time.

**Buzzword Combo:** GpIb defect + giant platelets + impaired adhesion = Bernard-Soulier syndrome

### C. von Willebrand factor deficiency


von Willebrand disease causes impaired platelet adhesion and decreased factor VIII stabilization, leading to mucosal bleeding with increased bleeding time and sometimes increased PTT.

**Buzzword Combo:** Epistaxis + heavy menses + abnormal ristocetin = von Willebrand disease

### D. Factor VII deficiency


Factor VII is part of the extrinsic pathway, so deficiency would primarily increase PT rather than PTT.

**Buzzword Combo:** Factor VII problem + increased PT = extrinsic pathway defect
