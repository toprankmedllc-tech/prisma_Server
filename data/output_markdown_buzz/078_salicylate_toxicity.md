## Buzzword Question

A 27-year-old man is brought to the emergency department after ingesting a large quantity of aspirin. Arterial blood gas demonstrates decreased PaCO₂ and decreased bicarbonate.
Which of the following acid-base disturbances is most likely present?

## Buzzwords Used

Aspirin; Low PaCO₂; Low bicarbonate

## Answer Options

A) Non-anion gap metabolic acidosis
B) Isolated metabolic alkalosis
C) Mixed respiratory acidosis and metabolic alkalosis
D) Mixed respiratory alkalosis and metabolic acidosis

## Correct Answer

D) Mixed respiratory alkalosis and metabolic acidosis

## Explanation

Salicylate toxicity classically causes a mixed acid-base disturbance. Salicylates directly stimulate the respiratory center, producing respiratory alkalosis, while accumulation of organic acids causes an anion gap metabolic acidosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Aspirin overdose + low CO₂ + low bicarbonate = Mixed respiratory alkalosis/metabolic acidosis

## Wrong Options

###  Isolated metabolic alkalosis

Metabolic alkalosis is usually caused by vomiting or diuretic use and is associated with elevated bicarbonate.

**Buzzword Combo:** Vomiting + elevated bicarbonate = Metabolic alkalosis

### Mixed respiratory acidosis and metabolic alkalosis

This mixed disorder may occur in severe COPD with chronic vomiting or diuretic use.

**Buzzword Combo:** COPD + vomiting + hypoventilation = Respiratory acidosis with metabolic alkalosis

### Non-anion gap metabolic acidosis

Normal anion gap metabolic acidosis commonly results from diarrhea or renal tubular acidosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Diarrhea + bicarbonate loss + chloride retention = Normal anion gap metabolic acidosis
