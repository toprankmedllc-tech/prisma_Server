## Buzzword Question

A 36-year-old pregnant woman undergoes routine second-trimester prenatal screening. The results show an abnormal serum pattern suggestive of a fetal chromosomal condition. Which of the following maternal serum marker patterns is most consistent with the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Advanced maternal age; second-trimester screening; chromosomal abnormality; 


## Answer Options

 A) Increased AFP and decreased hCG
 B) Decreased hCG and decreased inhibin A
 C) Increased hCG and increased inhibin A with decreased AFP and estriol
 D) Increased estriol with decreased AFP


## Correct Answer

C) Increased hCG and increased inhibin A with decreased AFP and estriol


## Explanation

The most likely diagnosis is Down syndrome (Trisomy 21), classically associated with ↑ β-hCG and ↑ inhibin A along with ↓ AFP and ↓ estriol on second-trimester quad screening.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

↑ hCG + ↑ inhibin A + ↓ AFP + ↓ estriol = Down syndrome


## Wrong Options

### A) Increased AFP and decreased hCG


Elevated AFP is most strongly associated with open neural tube defects, not chromosomal aneuploidy.


**Buzzword Combo:** ↑ AFP = neural tube defect (spina bifida, anencephaly)


### B) Decreased hCG and decreased inhibin A


 This pattern is not consistent with any classic trisomy screening profile.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Low hCG + low inhibin A ≠ Down syndrome

### D) Increased estriol with decreased AFP


Down syndrome is associated with decreased estriol, not increased estriol.


**Buzzword Combo:** ↓ estriol + ↑ hCG + ↓ AFP = Down syndrome
