## Buzzword Question

A young boy with recurrent hemarthrosis and easy bruising after minor injuries undergoes coagulation testing. Which laboratory finding is most characteristic of hemophilia A?

## Buzzwords Used

young boy; recurrent hemarthrosis; easy bruising; coagulation testing; factor VIII deficiency suspicion

## Answer Options

A) Prolonged PT only
B) Prolonged aPTT only
C) Prolonged PT and aPTT
D) Normal PT and aPTT

## Correct Answer

B. Prolonged aPTT only

## Explanation

Hemophilia A is due to factor VIII deficiency affecting the intrinsic coagulation pathway, leading to isolated prolongation of aPTT. PT and platelet count remain normal because the extrinsic pathway and primary hemostasis are intact.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Intrinsic pathway defect + normal PT + hemarthrosis = Hemophilia A

## Wrong Options

### A) Prolonged PT only

Isolated PT prolongation indicates factor VII deficiency or vitamin K antagonist use, which affects the extrinsic pathway. It is not consistent with hemophilia A.

**Buzzword Combo:** Factor VII deficiency + isolated PT prolongation = Extrinsic pathway defect

### C) Prolonged PT and aPTT

Combined PT and aPTT prolongation suggests a common pathway defect, liver disease, DIC, or severe vitamin K deficiency. Hemophilia A affects only aPTT.

**Buzzword Combo:** Both PT and aPTT prolonged + bleeding = Common pathway defect

### D) Normal PT and aPTT

Normal PT and aPTT with bleeding suggests platelet dysfunction, von Willebrand disease, or factor XIII deficiency. Hemophilia A specifically prolongs aPTT.

**Buzzword Combo:** Normal PT/aPTT + bleeding = Platelet disorder or vWD
