## Buzzword Question

A young adult has several weeks of generalized lymph node enlargement, fever, loose stools, hepatosplenomegaly, anemia, leukopenia, and elevated IgM and IgG levels. Which of the following diagnoses best explains this pattern?

## Buzzwords Used

Several weeks of generalized lymph node enlargement; fever and loose stools; elevated IgM and IgG levels

## Answer Options

A) HIV infection/AIDS
B) Common variable immunodeficiency
C) Selective IgA deficiency
D) Bruton agammaglobulinemia

## Correct Answer

A) HIV infection/AIDS


## Explanation

HIV infection/AIDS causes progressive loss of CD4+ T-cell–mediated immunity, leading to impaired immune coordination and susceptibility to opportunistic infections and malignancies. Elevated immunoglobulins argue against a primary antibody-production defect.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Several weeks of lymphadenopathy + fever/loose stools + elevated IgM and IgG = HIV infection/AIDS

## Wrong Options

### B) Common variable immunodeficiency

Common variable immunodeficiency usually causes low IgG and low IgA with recurrent sinopulmonary infections, not elevated IgM and IgG.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recurrent sinopulmonary infections + low IgG + low IgA = common variable immunodeficiency

### C) Selective IgA deficiency

Selective IgA deficiency causes isolated mucosal antibody deficiency with recurrent mucosal infections, allergies, or autoimmune disease. It does not explain global systemic symptoms with elevated IgM and IgG.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recurrent mucosal infections + normal IgG/IgM + low IgA = selective IgA deficiency

### D) Bruton agammaglobulinemia

Bruton agammaglobulinemia causes absent mature B cells and very low immunoglobulins, typically presenting in male infants after maternal IgG declines.

**Buzzword Combo:** Male infant after 6 months + absent mature B cells + very low immunoglobulins = Bruton agammaglobulinemia
