## Buzzword Question

A 25-year-old man presents with infertility. He reports a history of recurrent respiratory infections in childhood and chronic malabsorption with bulky, foul-smelling stools. Examination reveals signs of chronic lung disease, and he is found to have thickened secretions affecting multiple exocrine glands. Which of the following genetic mutations is most likely responsible for this patient’s condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Infertility; Childhood recurrent respiratory infections; Chronic malabsorption; Bulky foul-smelling stools; Thick secretions; Pseudomonas infections; Exocrine gland dysfunction

## Answer Options

A) Trinucleotide repeat expansion on chromosome 19
B) ΔF508 deletion affecting ATP-binding cassette chloride channel
C) Mutation in dystrophin gene on Xp21
D) Mutation in α-globin gene deletion

## Correct Answer

B) ΔF508 deletion affecting ATP-binding cassette chloride channel

## Explanation

The patient has classic cystic fibrosis with infertility (due to congenital absence of vas deferens), recurrent childhood respiratory infections, and malabsorption. CF is caused by a ΔF508 deletion in the CFTR gene, an ATP-binding cassette chloride channel leading to thick secretions.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Infertility + recurrent Pseudomonas respiratory infections + malabsorption + thick secretions = CFTR ΔF508 mutation

## Wrong Options

### A) Trinucleotide repeat expansion on chromosome 19

Chromosome 19 trinucleotide repeat expansion suggests myotonic dystrophy, which causes cataracts, myotonia, and testicular atrophy but not malabsorption or chronic pulmonary infections.

**Buzzword Combo:** Infertility + muscle weakness + cataracts + myotonia = Myotonic dystrophy

### C) Mutation in dystrophin gene on Xp21

Dystrophin gene mutation causes Duchenne muscular dystrophy, characterized by progressive muscle weakness, Gower sign, and cardiomyopathy, not respiratory infections with malabsorption.

**Buzzword Combo:** Male child + proximal muscle weakness + Gower sign + calf pseudohypertrophy = Duchenne muscular dystrophy

### D) Mutation in α-globin gene deletion

α-globin gene deletion causes alpha-thalassemia, leading to microcytic anemia and possible hydrops fetalis, not chronic lung disease or pancreatic insufficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Microcytic anemia + target cells + hemolysis + Hb Bart’s = Alpha-thalassemia
