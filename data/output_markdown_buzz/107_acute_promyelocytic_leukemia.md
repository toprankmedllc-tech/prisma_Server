## Buzzword Question

An adult with acute myeloid leukemia develops spontaneous bleeding from IV sites, prolonged PT and aPTT, low fibrinogen, and elevated D-dimer. Which AML subtype is most associated with this complication?

## Buzzwords Used

spontaneous bleeding; prolonged PT; prolonged aPTT; low fibrinogen; elevated D-dimer; DIC

## Answer Options

A) Acute promyelocytic leukemia
B) Acute myeloid leukemia with maturation
C) Acute monocytic leukemia
D) Acute megakaryoblastic leukemia

## Correct Answer

A. Acute promyelocytic leukemia

## Explanation

Acute promyelocytic leukemia (AML-M3) with t(15;17) produces promyelocytes that release procoagulant substances, leading to severe disseminated intravascular coagulation (DIC). It is a hematologic emergency due to high risk of fatal hemorrhage.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

t(15;17) + promyelocytes + DIC + bleeding = Acute promyelocytic leukemia

## Wrong Options

### B. Acute myeloid leukemia with maturation

AML with maturation (often t(8;21)) shows myeloblasts with some differentiation and Auer rods but is not strongly associated with DIC like APL.

**Buzzword Combo:** Myeloblasts + Auer rods + t(8;21) = AML-M2

### C. Acute monocytic leukemia

AML-M5 presents with gingival hypertrophy, skin infiltration, and CNS involvement due to monocytic lineage. It is not classically associated with severe DIC.

**Buzzword Combo:** Gingival hyperplasia + tissue infiltration = AML-M5

### D. Acute megakaryoblastic leukemia

AML-M7 is associated with Down syndrome and marrow fibrosis. It does not typically present with fulminant DIC like APL.

**Buzzword Combo:** Down syndrome + marrow fibrosis + megakaryoblasts = AML-M7
