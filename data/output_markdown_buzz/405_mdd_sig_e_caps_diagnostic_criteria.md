## Buzzword Question

A 38-year-old man presents with depressed mood, loss of interest in hobbies, insomnia, fatigue, feelings of worthlessness, difficulty concentrating, and recurrent thoughts of death for 3 weeks. TSH is normal. He has no history of mania. The minimum symptom count and duration required to diagnose his condition are:


## Buzzwords Used

Depressed mood; anhedonia; insomnia; fatigue; worthlessness; concentration difficulty; death thoughts; 3 weeks; no mania; normal TSH

## Answer Options

A) ≥5 symptoms including depressed mood or anhedonia for ≥2 weeks 
B) ≥3 symptoms for ≥1 month 
C) ≥5 symptoms for ≥1 month regardless of specific symptom type 
D) ≥4 symptoms including suicidal ideation for ≥2 weeks

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

MDD requires ≥5 of 9 DSM-5 depressive symptoms (SIG E CAPS + depressed mood) for ≥2 weeks, with at least one being depressed mood or anhedonia. This patient has 7 qualifying symptoms over 3 weeks — well above the threshold. Normal TSH excludes hypothyroidism as a medical cause.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

≥5/9 SIG E CAPS symptoms including depressed mood or anhedonia for ≥2 weeks + functional impairment = MDD

## Wrong Options

### 1: B) ≥3 symptoms for ≥1 month

 Persistent depressive disorder (dysthymia) requires depressed mood plus ≥2 additional symptoms for ≥2 years (not 1 month). Neither option B's symptom count nor duration matches any DSM-5 mood disorder criterion correctly

**Buzzword Combo:**  Depressed mood + ≥2 symptoms for ≥2 years = Persistent depressive disorder (dysthymia)


### 2: C) ≥5 symptoms for ≥1 month regardless of specific symptom type

The 5 symptoms must specifically include depressed mood or anhedonia (one of these two anchor symptoms is required). Duration threshold is 2 weeks, not 1 month. The symptom type matters, not just the count.

**Buzzword Combo:** Depressed mood or anhedonia is required anchor symptom  cannot diagnose MDD from peripheral symptoms alone


### 3: D) ≥4 symptoms including suicidal ideation for ≥2 weeks

MDD requires ≥5 symptoms, not 4. Suicidal ideation is one of the 9 possible symptoms but is not required. Four symptoms with SI would represent subthreshold depression, not MDD.

**Buzzword Combo:** Suicidal ideation + depressed mood + 3 other symptoms = Subthreshold — does not meet MDD full criteria
