## Buzzword Question

A young boy with recurrent painful joint swelling and knee bleeding after minor trauma has prolonged aPTT, normal bleeding time, and normal platelet count. What is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

young boy; recurrent hemarthrosis; knee bleeding; prolonged aPTT; normal bleeding time; normal platelet count; minor trauma

## Answer Options

A) Hemophilia A
B) von Willebrand disease
C) Immune thrombocytopenic purpura
D) Disseminated intravascular coagulation

## Correct Answer

A. Hemophilia A

## Explanation

Hemophilia A is an X-linked recessive deficiency of factor VIII causing intrinsic pathway dysfunction. It presents with deep tissue bleeding such as hemarthrosis and an isolated prolonged aPTT with normal platelet count and bleeding time.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hemarthrosis + isolated prolonged aPTT + X-linked = Hemophilia A

## Wrong Options

### B. von Willebrand disease

vWD causes mucocutaneous bleeding due to defective platelet adhesion and typically prolongs bleeding time with possible mild aPTT prolongation. Hemarthrosis is not a classic feature.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mucosal bleeding + prolonged bleeding time + vWF defect = vWD

### C. Immune thrombocytopenic purpura

ITP causes isolated thrombocytopenia with normal PT and aPTT and presents with petechiae and mucosal bleeding. It does not cause hemarthrosis or coagulation factor deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isolated thrombocytopenia + petechiae + normal PT/aPTT = ITP

### D. Disseminated intravascular coagulation

DIC is a consumptive coagulopathy with prolonged PT and aPTT, low fibrinogen, elevated D-dimer, and thrombocytopenia. It presents with systemic bleeding rather than isolated hemarthrosis in a child.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sepsis + low fibrinogen + ↑D-dimer = DIC
