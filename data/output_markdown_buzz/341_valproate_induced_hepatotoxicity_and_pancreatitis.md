## Buzzword Question

Generalized tonic-clonic seizures, weight gain, hair loss, easy bruising, elevated liver enzymes, pancreatitis, teratogenic anticonvulsant that blocks Na⁺ channels, T-type Ca²⁺ channels, and GABA transaminase most strongly indicates toxicity from which medication?

## Buzzwords Used

Generalized tonic-clonic seizures; weight gain; hair loss; easy bruising; pancreatitis; hepatotoxicity; teratogenicity; GABA transaminase inhibition

## Answer Options

A) Ethosuximide
B) Valproate
C) Topiramate
D) Levetiracetam

## Correct Answer

B) Valproate

## Explanation

Valproate is a broad-spectrum anticonvulsant that blocks Na⁺ channels, T-type Ca²⁺ channels, and GABA transaminase. Classic adverse effects include hepatotoxicity, pancreatitis, weight gain, hair loss, thrombocytopenia/easy bruising, and teratogenicity.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Pancreatitis + weight gain + hair loss = Valproate

## Wrong Options

### A) Ethosuximide

Ethosuximide is used only for absence seizures and commonly causes fatigue, sedation, and GI upset. It is not associated with pancreatitis or major hepatotoxicity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Absence seizures + T-type Ca²⁺ blockade + GI distress = Ethosuximide

### C) Topiramate

Topiramate commonly causes kidney stones, paresthesias, cognitive slowing, and weight loss. Weight gain and pancreatitis are not classic features.

**Buzzword Combo:** Kidney stones + weight loss + angle-closure glaucoma = Topiramate

### D) Levetiracetam

Levetiracetam acts through SV2A synaptic vesicle protein modulation and is notable for mood and behavioral disturbances rather than hepatotoxicity and pancreatitis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Psychosis + irritability + SV2A binding = Levetiracetam
