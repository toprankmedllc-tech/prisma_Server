## Buzzword Question

A 6-year-old boy from a malaria-endemic region has recurrent episodes of severe right lower abdominal pain with anemia and mild scleral icterus. Which of the following findings would best support the suspected diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Malaria-endemic region; recurrent severe right lower abdominal pain; anemia and mild scleral icterus

## Answer Options

A) Crescent-shaped erythrocytes on peripheral blood smear
B) Spherocytes with a positive direct Coombs test
C) Bite cells after exposure to oxidant stress
D) Decreased β-globin chain synthesis

## Correct Answer

A) Crescent-shaped erythrocytes on peripheral blood smear


## Explanation

Sickle cell disease causes RBC deformation, reduced RBC flexibility, and microvascular obstruction. Blood smear can show crescent-shaped erythrocytes, and hemoglobin electrophoresis confirms HbS.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Recurrent abdominal pain + anemia/jaundice + crescent-shaped erythrocytes = sickle cell disease

## Wrong Options

### B) Spherocytes with a positive direct Coombs test

This describes warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia due to splenic removal of IgG-coated RBCs. It does not cause recurrent vaso-occlusive pain from HbS polymerization.

**Buzzword Combo:** Recent infection/drug exposure + spherocytes + positive Coombs test = warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia

### C) Bite cells after exposure to oxidant stress

This describes G6PD deficiency, in which oxidative stress damages hemoglobin. It is not the classic smear finding for HbS-related microvascular obstruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Oxidant drug/fava beans + episodic hemolysis + bite cells = G6PD deficiency

### D) Decreased β-globin chain synthesis

This describes β-thalassemia, which causes reduced β-globin production. Sickle cell disease is caused by a structural β-globin mutation, not decreased β-globin synthesis.

**Buzzword Combo:** Microcytic anemia + target cells + decreased β-globin synthesis = β-thalassemia

## Related Concepts

Hemoglobin electrophoresis; β-thalassemia; warm autoimmune hemolytic anemia
