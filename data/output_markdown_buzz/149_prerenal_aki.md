## Buzzword Question

Which of the following laboratory findings is most consistent with prerenal acute kidney injury caused by volume depletion?

## Buzzwords Used

Volume depletion; Lab Finding; Acute Kidney Injury

## Answer Options

A) Fractional excretion of sodium >2%
B) Fractional excretion of sodium <1% 
C) Bilateral hydronephrosis on renal ultrasound
D) Red blood cell casts in urine sediment

## Correct Answer

B) Fractional excretion of sodium <1% 

## Explanation

Prerenal acute kidney injury occurs when renal hypoperfusion decreases glomerular filtration. The kidneys compensate by increasing sodium and water reabsorption, resulting in a low fractional excretion of sodium (FeNa <1%)

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Volume depletion + sodium retention + FeNa <1% = Prerenal AKI

## Wrong Options

### Fractional excretion of sodium >2%

FeNa >2% is more consistent with intrinsic renal injury such as acute tubular necrosis due to impaired tubular sodium reabsorption. Damaged tubular epithelial cells lose the ability to appropriately conserve sodium.

**Buzzword Combo:** Tubular injury + impaired sodium reabsorption + FeNa >2% = ATN

### Bilateral hydronephrosis on renal ultrasound

Bilateral hydronephrosis suggests postrenal acute kidney injury due to urinary tract obstruction. Obstruction increases hydrostatic pressure proximal to the blockage, impairing glomerular filtration

**Buzzword Combo:** Urinary obstruction + hydronephrosis + impaired urine flow = Postrenal AKI

### Red blood cell casts in urine sediment

Red blood cell casts are characteristic of nephritic syndromes and indicate glomerular inflammation or injury. They are commonly seen in glomerulonephritis rather than prerenal azotemia

**Buzzword Combo:** RBC casts + glomerular inflammation + hematuria = Nephritic syndrome
