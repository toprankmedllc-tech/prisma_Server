## Buzzword Question

A 29-year-old woman with fatigue, glossitis, difficulty concentrating, normal neurologic exam and anemia with hypersegmented neutrophils most likely has elevation of which marker?

## Buzzwords Used

fatigue; glossitis; hypersegmented neutrophils; normal neurologic exam; macrocytic anemia

## Answer Options

A. Methylmalonic acid
B. Homocysteine
C. Lactate dehydrogenase only
D. Ferritin

## Correct Answer

B. Homocysteine

## Explanation

Folate deficiency causes impaired conversion of homocysteine to methionine, leading to elevated homocysteine with normal methylmalonic acid. Neurologic symptoms are absent, unlike vitamin B12 deficiency.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Normal MMA + elevated homocysteine + macrocytosis = Folate deficiency

## Wrong Options

### A. Methylmalonic acid

Methylmalonic acid is elevated only in vitamin B12 deficiency due to impaired methylmalonyl-CoA mutase activity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Elevated MMA + neurologic deficits = B12 deficiency

### C. Lactate dehydrogenase only

LDH is elevated in both B12 and folate deficiency due to ineffective erythropoiesis and is nonspecific.

**Buzzword Combo:** Elevated LDH + macrocytosis = Nonspecific megaloblastic anemia

### D. Ferritin

Ferritin reflects iron stores and is unrelated to folate metabolism; it is typically normal in folate deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Low ferritin + microcytosis = Iron deficiency anemia
