## Buzzword Question

A 57-year-old man with advanced chronic kidney disease develops easy bruising and recurrent nosebleeds despite a normal platelet count. Which of the following best explains this patient’s bleeding tendency?

## Buzzwords Used

CKD; Epistaxis; Normal platelet count

## Answer Options

A) Factor VIII deficiency
B) Vitamin K deficiency
C) Uremic platelet dysfunction
D) Autoimmune platelet destruction

## Correct Answer

C) Uremic platelet dysfunction

## Explanation

Uremia impairs platelet adhesion and aggregation, causing a qualitative platelet dysfunction despite a normal platelet count. Patients commonly develop mucosal bleeding, epistaxis, and easy bruising.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

CKD + normal platelet count + mucosal bleeding = Uremic platelet dysfunction

## Wrong Options

### Vitamin K deficiency

Vitamin K deficiency impairs synthesis of clotting factors II, VII, IX, and X, leading to prolonged PT/INR and abnormal coagulation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fat malabsorption + elevated PT + Low clotting factors = Vitamin K deficiency

### Factor VIII deficiency

Factor VIII deficiency causes hemophilia A, which classically presents with hemarthroses and deep tissue bleeding

**Buzzword Combo:** Factor VIII deficiency + hemarthrosis + Male = Hemophilia A

### Autoimmune platelet destruction

Immune thrombocytopenic purpura causes autoimmune destruction of platelets, resulting in thrombocytopenia and mucosal bleeding

**Buzzword Combo:** Low platelets + Normal Coagulation + mucosal bleeding = ITP
