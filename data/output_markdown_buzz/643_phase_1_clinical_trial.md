## Buzzword Question

A new medication is administered to a small number of healthy volunteers. Investigators gradually increase the dose while monitoring for adverse effects and determining the highest dose that can be safely tolerated.

This study is most consistent with which phase of clinical testing?

## Buzzwords Used

Healthy volunteers ; Dose escalation ; Safety assessment ; Maximum tolerated dose ; Clinical trials

## Answer Options

A) Phase 1

B) Phase 2

C) Phase 3

D) Phase 4

## Correct Answer

A) Phase 1

## Explanation

Phase 1 trials primarily assess safety and determine the maximum tolerated dose through dose-escalation studies in a small number of participants.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Healthy volunteers + dose escalation + safety assessment = Phase 1

## Wrong Options

### B) Phase 2

Phase 2 trials focus on evaluating efficacy in patients with the disease of interest.

**Buzzword Combo:** Disease efficacy testing = Phase 2

### C) Phase 3

Phase 3 trials compare treatments with placebo or standard therapy in large patient populations.

**Buzzword Combo:** Large randomized comparison study = Phase 3

### D) Phase 4

Phase 4 trials occur after approval and monitor long-term or rare adverse effects.

**Buzzword Combo:** Postmarketing surveillance = Phase 4
