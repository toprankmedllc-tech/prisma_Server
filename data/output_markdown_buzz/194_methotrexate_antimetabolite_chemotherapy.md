## Buzzword Question

A patient receiving an antimetabolite for systemic psoriasis develops painful oral ulcers, low blood cell counts, liver injury, and progressive lung scarring. Which agent can reduce toxicity from this drug?

## Buzzwords Used

Antimetabolite for systemic psoriasis; painful oral ulcers and low blood cell counts; liver injury and progressive lung scarring

## Answer Options

A) Folinic acid
B) Pyridoxine
C) Mesna
D) Dexrazoxane

## Correct Answer

A) Folinic acid


## Explanation

Methotrexate toxicity includes mucositis, bone marrow suppression, hepatotoxicity, and pulmonary fibrosis. Folinic acid rescue reduces toxicity by bypassing the blocked dihydrofolate reductase step.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Antimetabolite for psoriasis + mucositis and marrow suppression + folinic acid rescue = methotrexate toxicity

## Wrong Options

### B) Pyridoxine

Pyridoxine prevents isoniazid-induced peripheral neuropathy. It does not reverse folate pathway inhibition from methotrexate.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isoniazid use + peripheral neuropathy prevention + vitamin B6 = pyridoxine

### C) Mesna

Mesna prevents hemorrhagic cystitis caused by cyclophosphamide or ifosfamide. It does not protect against methotrexate toxicity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Alkylating agent + hemorrhagic cystitis + acrolein neutralization = mesna

### D) Dexrazoxane

Dexrazoxane reduces doxorubicin cardiotoxicity. It is unrelated to dihydrofolate reductase inhibition.

**Buzzword Combo:** Anthracycline exposure + dilated cardiomyopathy risk + free radical protection = dexrazoxane
