## Buzzword Question

A woman spends hours each day washing her hands because of intrusive fears of contamination. She recognizes that these thoughts are irrational but feels intense anxiety if she does not perform the behavior. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?


## Buzzwords Used

Intrusive thoughts; contamination fears; repetitive hand washing; 

## Answer Options

 A) Obsessive-compulsive disorder
 B) Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder
 C) Generalized anxiety disorder
 D) Schizophrenia


## Correct Answer

A. Obsessive-compulsive disorder


## Explanation

OCD is characterized by egodystonic obsessions and compulsions performed to reduce anxiety. Patients recognize that their thoughts and behaviors are irrational but feel unable to resist them.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Contamination fears + repetitive hand washing + egodystonic thoughts = OCD

## Wrong Options

### B. Obsessive-compulsive personality disorder


OCPD involves perfectionism and rigid behavior patterns that are egosyntonic, meaning patients view them as appropriate and desirable.


**Buzzword Combo:** Perfectionism + rigid control + egosyntonic behavior = OCPD


### C. Generalized anxiety disorder


GAD involves excessive anxiety about real-life concerns rather than intrusive irrational obsessions with compulsive rituals.


**Buzzword Combo:** Excessive worry + daily life stressors + muscle tension = GAD


### D. Schizophrenia


Schizophrenia involves psychosis, hallucinations, and delusions rather than preserved insight with compulsive rituals.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hallucinations + delusions + disorganized speech = Schizophrenia
