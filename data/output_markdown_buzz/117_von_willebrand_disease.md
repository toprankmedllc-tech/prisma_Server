## Buzzword Question

A young woman with easy bruising, recurrent nosebleeds, normal platelet count, prolonged bleeding time, normal PT, and mildly prolonged aPTT is suspected of having von Willebrand disease. Which laboratory pattern is most consistent with this condition?

## Buzzwords Used

easy bruising; epistaxis; normal platelet count; prolonged bleeding time; normal PT; mildly prolonged aPTT

## Answer Options

A) Normal bleeding time, prolonged PT, prolonged aPTT
B) Prolonged bleeding time, normal PT, normal or mildly prolonged aPTT
C) Normal bleeding time, normal PT, prolonged aPTT only
D) Prolonged bleeding time, prolonged PT, normal aPTT

## Correct Answer

B. Prolonged bleeding time, normal PT, normal or mildly prolonged aPTT

## Explanation

von Willebrand disease causes impaired platelet adhesion, leading to prolonged bleeding time. Because vWF stabilizes factor VIII, aPTT may be normal or mildly prolonged, while PT remains normal.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Mucosal bleeding + prolonged bleeding time + normal PT + mild ↑aPTT = vWD

## Wrong Options

### A. Normal bleeding time, prolonged PT, prolonged aPTT

This pattern suggests a defect in the common pathway or severe liver disease/DIC rather than vWD. Bleeding time would not be normal in vWD.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prolonged PT + prolonged aPTT = common pathway defect

### C. Normal bleeding time, normal PT, prolonged aPTT only

Isolated prolonged aPTT suggests intrinsic pathway defects such as hemophilia A or B. vWD typically affects bleeding time due to platelet adhesion defects.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isolated prolonged aPTT + hemarthrosis = Hemophilia A/B

### D. Prolonged bleeding time, prolonged PT, normal aPTT

Isolated prolonged PT suggests factor VII deficiency or warfarin effect, not vWD. vWD does not typically prolong PT.

**Buzzword Combo:** Isolated prolonged PT + normal aPTT = factor VII deficiency / warfarin effect

Hemophilia A; DIC; factor VII deficiency
