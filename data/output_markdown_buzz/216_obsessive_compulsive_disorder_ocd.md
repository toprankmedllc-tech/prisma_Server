## Buzzword Question

A patient with obsessive-compulsive disorder is started on pharmacologic therapy after failing cognitive behavioral therapy alone. Which of the following medications is considered first-line pharmacotherapy for this condition?


## Buzzwords Used

Obsessive-compulsive disorder; first-line pharmacotherapy; 


## Answer Options

 A) Fluoxetine
 B) Haloperidol
 C) Lithium
 D) Alprazolam


## Correct Answer

A. Fluoxetine


## Explanation

SSRIs such as fluoxetine are first-line pharmacologic treatments for OCD. They help reduce both obsessions and compulsions by increasing serotonergic neurotransmission.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

OCD + first-line medication + serotonin = SSRI


## Wrong Options

### B. Haloperidol


Haloperidol is an antipsychotic primarily used for psychotic disorders. It is not first-line monotherapy for OCD.


**Buzzword Combo:** Psychosis + dopamine blockade + hallucinations = Haloperidol use


### C. Lithium


Lithium is primarily used for bipolar disorder and mood stabilization, not OCD treatment.


**Buzzword Combo:** Mania + mood stabilization + bipolar disorder = Lithium


### D. Alprazolam


Benzodiazepines may reduce anxiety transiently but do not effectively treat the obsessions and compulsions of OCD.


**Buzzword Combo:** Acute anxiety + panic attack + rapid sedation = Benzodiazepines
