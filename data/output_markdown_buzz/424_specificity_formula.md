## Buzzword Question

In a screening study for colorectal cancer, 500 patients undergo both a fecal immunochemical test (FIT) and confirmatory colonoscopy. Of the 50 patients with colonoscopy-confirmed cancer, 45 have a positive FIT and 5 have a negative FIT. Of the 450 patients without cancer, 27 have a positive FIT and 423 have a negative FIT. The specificity of this FIT test is:


## Buzzwords Used

50 disease-positive; 5 false negatives; 450 disease-negative; 27 false positives; 423 true negatives

## Answer Options

 A) 94% 
B) 90% 
C) 63% 
D) 97%

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Specificity = TN/(TN+FP) = 423/(423+27) = 423/450 = 0.94 = 94%. Specificity measures the proportion of truly disease-free patients correctly identified as negative (true negative rate). It reads down the disease-negative column of the 2×2 table. The 50 disease-positive patients and their results are not used in the specificity calculation. A specificity of 94% means that 94% of patients without colorectal cancer will correctly test negative with this FIT.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

TN/(TN+FP) × 100 = Specificity = proportion of disease-free patients correctly identified as negative


## Wrong Options

### B) 90%

90% represents the sensitivity of this test, not the specificity. Sensitivity = TP/(TP+FN) = 45/(45+5) = 45/50 = 90%. Sensitivity reads down the disease-positive column; specificity reads down the disease-negative column. Confusing sensitivity and specificity formulas is the most common calculation error.

**Buzzword Combo:** TP/(TP+FN) = Sensitivity (disease-positive column) — not specificity; specificity uses disease-negative column

### C) 63%

63% approximates the positive predictive value (PPV) = TP/(TP+FP) = 45/(45+27) = 45/72 ≈ 62.5%. PPV uses the test-positive row and is a prevalence-dependent measure of clinical utility. Specificity uses the disease-negative column and is an intrinsic test property unaffected by prevalence.

**Buzzword Combo:** TP/(TP+FP) = PPV (test-positive row, prevalence-dependent)   not specificity

### D) 97%

97% approximates the negative predictive value (NPV) = TN/(TN+FN) = 423/(423+5) = 423/428 ≈ 98.8%. NPV uses the test-negative row and changes with disease prevalence. Specificity = TN/(TN+FP) = 423/450 = 94%, which uses the disease-negative column, not the test-negative row. 

**Buzzword Combo:** TN/(TN+FN) = NPV (test-negative row, prevalence-dependent) — not specificity; specificity = TN/(TN+FP)
