## Buzzword Question

A 25-year-old man started haloperidol 5 days ago for new-onset psychosis develops T 40.2°C (104.4°F), marked uniform resistance to passive movement of all four limbs through their entire range without any ratchet quality, altered mental status, BP 188/108 mm Hg, and HR 130/min. CK is 8,200 U/L. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Recent antipsychotic (D2 antagonist) initiation; high fever; lead-pipe rigidity (uniform throughout range, no ratchet); autonomic instability; markedly elevated CK

## Answer Options

A) Serotonin syndrome
B) Anticholinergic toxicity
C) Malignant hyperthermia
D) Neuroleptic malignant syndrome

## Correct Answer

D) Neuroleptic malignant syndrome

## Explanation

NMS develops days to weeks after starting a dopamine D2 antagonist (antipsychotic, prochlorperazine, metoclopramide) and produces LEAD-PIPE RIGIDITY, hyperthermia, autonomic instability, altered mental status, and markedly elevated CK from sustained muscle contraction.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Antipsychotic exposure + lead-pipe rigidity + fever + autonomic instability + CK greater than 1000 = neuroleptic malignant syndrome

## Wrong Options

### Serotonin syndrome

Serotonin syndrome develops within hours after serotonergic drugs and produces HYPERREFLEXIA WITH CLONUS and lower greater than upper hypertonia, not uniform lead-pipe rigidity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Serotonergic drug combination + clonus + hyperreflexia + diaphoresis = serotonin syndrome

### Anticholinergic toxicity

Anticholinergic poisoning produces dry skin, dry mucous membranes, urinary retention, and ileus, not the rigidity and very high CK seen here.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hot + dry + red + mad + blind + urinary retention = anticholinergic toxicity

### Malignant hyperthermia

MH occurs in patients with RyR1 mutations exposed to volatile anesthetics or succinylcholine. There is no anesthetic exposure here.

**Buzzword Combo:** Volatile anesthetic or succinylcholine + intraoperative hyperthermia + RyR1 mutation = malignant hyperthermia

## Related Concepts

Serotonin syndrome (hyperreflexia, clonus); Atypical antipsychotic NMS risk (lower than typical, but still possible); Catatonia (related condition, also responds to lorazepam)
