## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old male with 3 days of fever and confusion undergoes an atraumatic lumbar puncture. CSF shows 240 WBC/µL (predominantly lymphocytes), 80 RBC/µL, protein 110 mg/dL, and glucose 60 mg/dL (serum 95 mg/dL). The most likely etiology is:

## Buzzwords Used

Lymphocytic CSF pleocytosis; RED BLOOD CELLS in CSF despite non-traumatic tap; elevated protein; normal glucose; acute febrile encephalopathy in an immunocompetent adult

## Answer Options

A) Acute bacterial meningitis
B) Enteroviral (aseptic) meningitis
C) Herpes simplex virus type 1 encephalitis
D) Subarachnoid hemorrhage from a ruptured saccular aneurysm

## Correct Answer

C) Herpes simplex virus type 1 encephalitis

## Explanation

The combination of lymphocytic pleocytosis, RBCs in atraumatic CSF, elevated protein, and normal glucose in a febrile adult with encephalopathy is the classic HSV-1 encephalitis profile. The RBCs reflect the underlying HEMORRHAGIC NECROSIS of the temporal lobes.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Febrile encephalopathy + lymphocytic CSF + RBCs in atraumatic tap + normal glucose = HSV-1 hemorrhagic encephalitis

## Wrong Options

### Acute bacterial meningitis

Bacterial meningitis shows neutrophilic pleocytosis with MARKEDLY LOW glucose and very high protein, not lymphocytic with normal glucose.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neck stiffness + neutrophilic CSF + low glucose + Gram stain organism = bacterial meningitis

### Enteroviral (aseptic) meningitis

Enteroviral meningitis produces lymphocytic pleocytosis with normal glucose but is typically a benign meningitis WITHOUT RBCs, encephalopathy, or focal deficits.

**Buzzword Combo:** Summer outbreak + lymphocytic CSF + normal glucose + no encephalopathy = enteroviral aseptic meningitis

### Subarachnoid hemorrhage from a ruptured saccular aneurysm

SAH produces RBCs in CSF and yellow-tinged supernatant, but it presents with sudden thunderclap headache, NOT a 3-day febrile course, and the CSF pleocytosis is not lymphocyte-predominant.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sudden worst-headache-of-life + meningismus + yellow CSF supernatant + RBCs = subarachnoid hemorrhage
