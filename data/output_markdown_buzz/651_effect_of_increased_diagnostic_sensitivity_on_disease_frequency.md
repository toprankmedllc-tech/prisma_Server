## Buzzword Question

Public health officials reviewing records from a city over the past 5 years notice that the total number of residents carrying a diagnosis of hypertension has increased substantially. During the same period, the number of newly diagnosed cases each year has remained relatively stable.

Which of the following is the most likely explanation for this observation?

## Buzzwords Used

Hypertension ; Total diagnosed cases increase ; Stable new cases ; Population health ; Disease frequency

## Answer Options

A) Increased diagnostic sensitivity

B) Extensive vaccine administration

C) Faster recovery time

D) Increased contact between affected and unaffected individuals

## Correct Answer

A) Increased diagnostic sensitivity

## Explanation

Improved diagnostic sensitivity identifies more existing cases that previously went undetected. This increases the total number of recognized cases in the population without necessarily changing the rate at which new cases develop.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

More diagnosed individuals + stable new case rate + improved detection = increased diagnostic sensitivity

## Wrong Options

### B) Extensive vaccine administration

Vaccination reduces the development of new disease and would be expected to decrease disease occurrence rather than increase the number of diagnosed individuals.

**Buzzword Combo:** Disease prevention = fewer new cases

### C) Faster recovery time

Faster recovery shortens disease duration and would reduce the number of individuals living with disease.

**Buzzword Combo:** Shorter disease duration = fewer affected individuals

### D) Increased contact between affected and unaffected individuals

Greater contact may increase transmission of infectious diseases, but hypertension is not transmitted between individuals.

**Buzzword Combo:** Increased transmission applies to infectious diseases, not hypertension
