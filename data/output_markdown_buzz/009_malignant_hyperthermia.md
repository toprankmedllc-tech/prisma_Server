## Buzzword Question

A patient undergoing surgery suddenly develops high fever, generalized muscle rigidity, tachycardia, and rapidly increasing end-tidal CO₂ shortly after administration of Succinylcholine. The anesthesiologist immediately administers emergency treatment.
Which of the following medications is most appropriate for this patient’s condition?

## Buzzwords Used

high fever ;generalized muscle rigidity ; tachycardia ;and rapidly increasing end-tidal CO₂; Succinylcholine

## Answer Options

A. Bromocriptine
B. Dantrolene
C. Naloxone
D. Physostigmine

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

Dantrolene is the treatment of choice for malignant hyperthermia. It works by inhibiting calcium release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum, thereby reducing muscle contraction and heat production.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Malignant hyperthermia + succinylcholine = dantrolene treatment

## Wrong Options

### A. Bromocriptine


Incorrect — Bromocriptine is used in Neuroleptic malignant syndrome due to dopamine blockade.


**Buzzword Combo:** Antipsychotic use + lead-pipe rigidity = neuroleptic malignant syndrome

### C. Naloxone


Incorrect — Naloxone reverses opioid overdose.


**Buzzword Combo:** Respiratory depression + pinpoint pupils = opioid overdose

### D. Physostigmine

Incorrect — Physostigmine may be used for anticholinergic toxicity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hot + dry + delirium = anticholinergic toxicity
