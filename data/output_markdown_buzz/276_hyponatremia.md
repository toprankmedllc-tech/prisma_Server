## Buzzword Question

A smoker develops weight loss, cough, and hemoptysis. Laboratory studies show severe hyponatremia with low serum osmolality and inappropriately concentrated urine. Which of the following malignancies is most likely associated with this patient’s electrolyte abnormality?

## Buzzwords Used

Smoker; Hyponatremia; Concentrated urine

## Answer Options

A) Adenocarcinoma of the lung
B) Squamous cell carcinoma of the lung
C) Small cell carcinoma of the lung
D) Large cell carcinoma of the lung

## Correct Answer

C) Small cell carcinoma of the lung

## Explanation

Small cell lung carcinoma is strongly associated with syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion due to ectopic ADH production. Excess ADH causes water retention, euvolemic hyponatremia, low serum osmolality, and inappropriately concentrated urine

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Small cell carcinoma + ectopic ADH + hyponatremia = SIADH

## Wrong Options

### Adenocarcinoma of the lung

is less strongly associated with paraneoplastic endocrine syndromes such as SIADH.

**Buzzword Combo:** Peripheral lung mass + nonsmoker = Adenocarcinoma

### Squamous cell carcinoma of the lung

Squamous cell lung carcinoma is strongly associated with smoking and may produce parathyroid hormone–related peptide causing hypercalcemia. It is not classically associated with ectopic ADH secretion

**Buzzword Combo:** Smoking + PTHrP secretion + hypercalcemia = Squamous cell carcinoma

### Large cell carcinoma of the lung

Large cell lung carcinoma is a poorly differentiated malignant epithelial tumor that may arise centrally or peripherally. It is less commonly associated with classic paraneoplastic syndromes compared with small cell carcinoma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Poor differentiation + aggressive growth = Large cell carcinoma
