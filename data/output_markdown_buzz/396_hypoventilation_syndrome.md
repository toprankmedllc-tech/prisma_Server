## Buzzword Question

A newborn develops shallow breathing and episodes of apnea during sleep due to failure of automatic central respiratory control. What is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Newborn ; apnea during sleep ; absent automatic breathing ; central respiratory control dysfunction


## Answer Options

 A) Obstructive sleep apnea
 B) Congenital central hypoventilation syndrome
 C) Respiratory distress syndrome
 D) Laryngomalacia


## Correct Answer

B) Congenital central hypoventilation syndrome

## Explanation

Congenital central hypoventilation syndrome (Ondine’s curse) is characterized by impaired autonomic control of breathing, especially during sleep, leading to shallow breathing or apnea in newborns.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Newborn + absent automatic breathing + sleep apnea = Congenital central hypoventilation syndrome


## Wrong Options

###  A) Obstructive sleep apnea


Obstructive sleep apnea results from upper airway collapse rather than defective central respiratory drive.

**Buzzword Combo:** Snoring + obesity + airway collapse = Obstructive sleep apnea


### C) Respiratory distress syndrome


Respiratory distress syndrome in premature infants results from surfactant deficiency causing alveolar collapse.


**Buzzword Combo:** Prematurity + surfactant deficiency + ground-glass lungs = Respiratory distress syndrome


###  D) Laryngomalacia


Laryngomalacia causes inspiratory stridor due to floppy supraglottic structures, not failure of automatic breathing control.


**Buzzword Combo:**  Inspiratory stridor + floppy airway + infant = Laryngomalacia
