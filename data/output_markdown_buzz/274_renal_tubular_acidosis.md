## Buzzword Question

Which of the following electrolyte abnormalities is most commonly associated with type 4 renal tubular acidosis caused by hypoaldosteronism?

## Buzzwords Used

Hypoaldosteronism; Tubular acidosis; Type 4

## Answer Options

A) Hypophosphatemia
B) Hypokalemia
C) Hypercalcemia
D) Hyperkalemia 

## Correct Answer

D) Hyperkalemia 

## Explanation

Type 4 renal tubular acidosis results from decreased aldosterone activity, leading to impaired potassium and hydrogen ion secretion in the distal nephron. Patients develop hyperkalemia and normal anion gap metabolic acidosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hypoaldosteronism + impaired distal secretion + hyperkalemia = Type 4 RTA

## Wrong Options

### Hypokalemia

Hypokalemia is more commonly associated with type 1 and type 2 renal tubular acidosis due to increased renal potassium wasting. These disorders differ from type 4 RTA, which is characterized by potassium retention

**Buzzword Combo:** renal potassium wasting + hypokalemia = Type 1/2 RTA

### Hypercalcemia

Hypercalcemia is not a defining feature of type 4 renal tubular acidosis. Elevated calcium levels are more commonly associated with hyperparathyroidism, malignancy, or vitamin D excess

**Buzzword Combo:** bone resorption + hypercalcemia = Calcium disorders

### Hypophosphatemia

Hypophosphatemia may occur in proximal tubular dysfunction such as Fanconi syndrome due to phosphate wasting. It is not a characteristic finding in hypoaldosteronism-induced type 4 RTA

**Buzzword Combo:** glucosuria + phosphate wasting + Proximal tubular dysfunction= Fanconi Syndrome
