## Buzzword Question

A patient with resistant hypertension, hypokalemia, metabolic alkalosis, elevated plasma aldosterone concentration, and suppressed plasma renin activity most likely has which condition?


## Buzzwords Used

 Resistant hypertension ; hypokalemia ; metabolic alkalosis ; high aldosterone ; low renin


## Answer Options

 A) Primary hyperaldosteronism
 B) Secondary hyperaldosteronism
 C) SIADH
 D) Diabetes insipidus


## Correct Answer

A) Primary hyperaldosteronism


## Explanation

Primary hyperaldosteronism is characterized by autonomous aldosterone secretion causing sodium retention, hypertension, hypokalemia, and metabolic alkalosis with suppressed renin activity.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Hypertension + hypokalemia + low renin = Primary hyperaldosteronism


## Wrong Options

### B) Secondary hyperaldosteronism

Secondary hyperaldosteronism causes elevated renin levels because aldosterone secretion is driven by RAAS activation.


**Buzzword Combo:** Renal artery stenosis + high renin + high aldosterone = Secondary hyperaldosteronism


### C) SIADH


SIADH causes euvolemic hyponatremia with concentrated urine rather than hypokalemic hypertension.


**Buzzword Combo:** Hyponatremia + concentrated urine + euvolemia = SIADH


### D) Diabetes insipidus


Diabetes insipidus causes polyuria and hypernatremia due to impaired ADH activity.

**Buzzword Combo:** Polyuria + hypernatremia + dilute urine = Diabetes insipidus
