## Buzzword Question


A young patient with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and dynamic LVOT obstruction is being counseled on medication safety. Which of the following drug classes should be avoided because it worsens his obstruction?

## Buzzwords Used

Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy; dynamic LVOT obstruction; preload reduction; nitrates

## Answer Options

A) Beta-blockers
B) Non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers (verapamil)
C) Nitrates
D) Disopyramide

## Correct Answer

C) Nitrates

## Explanation

Nitrates decrease preload by venodilation, which reduces venous return to the heart, in turn reducing left ventricular cavity size and worsening dynamic LVOT obstruction in HCM. The same principle applies to other preload- and afterload-reducing agents (ACE inhibitors, dihydropyridine CCBs, diuretics) and to positive inotropes (digoxin), all of which are avoided.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

HCM + ↓ preload (nitrates, ACEi, diuretics, dihydropyridine CCBs) = worsened LVOT obstruction

## Wrong Options

### A) Beta-blockers


First-line therapy in HCM; slow heart rate, prolong diastolic filling, and reduce dynamic obstruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** β-blockers = first-line for HCM

### B) Non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers (verapamil)

Used in HCM as an alternative to β-blockers; slow heart rate and improve diastolic filling.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Verapamil = β-blocker alternative in HCM

### D) Disopyramide

A negative inotrope used specifically in HCM to reduce contractility and relieve LVOT obstruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Disopyramide = negative inotrope used in HCM
