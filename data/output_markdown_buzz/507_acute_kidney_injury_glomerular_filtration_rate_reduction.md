## Buzzword Question

A 45-year-old man with a history of chronic kidney disease is evaluated during a routine follow-up. Laboratory studies show a serum creatinine of 4.0 mg/dL. The physician estimates the patient's glomerular filtration rate (GFR) using a 24-hour urine creatinine clearance test. The calculated creatinine clearance is determined to be 30 mL/min. However, the true GFR measured via inulin clearance is actually 24 mL/min. Which of the following transport mechanisms best explains why the calculated clearance of creatinine overestimates the true GFR in this patient?

## Buzzwords Used

Serum creatinine of 4.0 mg/dL; 24-hour urine creatinine clearance; Inulin clearance; Overestimates the true GFR

## Answer Options

A) Passive reabsorption in the collecting duct
B) Carrier-mediated secretion in the proximal tubule
C) Facilitated diffusion in the thick ascending limb
D) Paracellular reabsorption in the distal tubule

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

Creatinine is freely filtered at the glomerulus, but it is also moderately secreted by the proximal renal tubules via organic anion/cation transporters. Because the total excreted amount in urine includes both the filtered load and the secreted amount (Ucr ×V increases), the mathematical calculation for clearance ( UCr × V /Pcr ) slightly overestimates the true GFR.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Creatinine clearance + Overestimates true GFR + Proximal renal tubules = Carrier-mediated secretion in the proximal tubule

## Wrong Options

### A) Passive reabsorption in the collecting duct

If a substance undergoes significant reabsorption, its renal clearance value would drop below the true GFR. Urea exhibits this behavior, but creatinine does not undergo passive reabsorption along the collecting duct.

**Buzzword Combo:** Serum creatinine + Clearance lower than GFR = Passive reabsorption in the collecting duct

### C) Facilitated diffusion in the thick ascending limb

The thick ascending limb is highly involved in active ion transport (Na+/K +/2Cl− ) and is impermeable to water, but it plays no role in the active secretion or modification of creatinine levels.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chronic kidney disease + Medullary concentration gradient = Facilitated diffusion in the thick ascending limb

### D) Paracellular reabsorption in the distal tubule

Paracellular reabsorption in the distal segments handles ions like calcium and magnesium under hormonal control. This choice is wrong as the specific physiology of creatinine overestimation is confined strictly to active secretion.

**Buzzword Combo:** Electrolyte handling + Distal nephron segment = Paracellular reabsorption in the distal tubule
