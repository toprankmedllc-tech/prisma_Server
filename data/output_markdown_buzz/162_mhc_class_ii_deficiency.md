## Buzzword Question

A 10-month-old infant has impaired activation of naive T cells despite antigen recognition through the TCR. The missing second signal normally comes from an antigen-presenting cell and is required for full T-cell activation. Which of the following interactions provides this signal?

## Buzzwords Used

10-month-old infant; impaired naive T-cell activation; missing second signal from an antigen-presenting cell

## Answer Options

A) B7 on an APC binding CD28 on a naive T cell
B) MHC II on an APC binding CD4 on a helper T cell
C) Fas ligand on a T cell binding Fas on a target cell
D) IL-2 binding the high-affinity IL-2 receptor on activated T cells

## Correct Answer

A) B7 on an APC binding CD28 on a naive T cell


## Explanation

The secondary co-stimulatory signal for naive T-cell activation is B7-1/B7-2 on the APC binding CD28 on the T cell. This signal is required after MHC-TCR/CD antigen recognition.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Naive T cell + second activation signal + B7 binding CD28 = T-cell co-stimulation

## Wrong Options

### B) MHC II on an APC binding CD4 on a helper T cell

This describes the primary MHC-restricted antigen recognition signal for CD4+ helper T cells. It is not the separate co-stimulatory signal required for full activation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Extracellular antigen + MHC II + CD4+ helper T cell = helper T-cell antigen recognition

### C) Fas ligand on a T cell binding Fas on a target cell

This interaction triggers extrinsic apoptosis. It does not provide the co-stimulatory signal for naive T-cell activation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Fas-FasL interaction + caspase activation + apoptosis = extrinsic apoptosis pathway

### D) IL-2 binding the high-affinity IL-2 receptor on activated T cells

IL-2 signaling promotes expansion of T cells after activation. The APC-derived co-stimulatory signal is B7 binding CD28.

**Buzzword Combo:** Activated T cell + IL-2 signaling + clonal expansion = T-cell proliferation
