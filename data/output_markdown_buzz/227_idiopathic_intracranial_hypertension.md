## Buzzword Question

A 28-year-old obese woman has 3 weeks of daily headaches and brief grayouts of vision when bending over. Examination shows bilateral swelling of the optic discs and incomplete abduction of the left eye. MRI brain and MR venography are unremarkable; lumbar puncture shows opening pressure 38 cm H₂O with normal CSF composition. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Obese young woman; positional headache; transient visual obscurations; bilateral papilledema; sixth nerve palsy; opening pressure greater than 25 cm H₂O; normal CSF composition; normal MRI and MRV

## Answer Options

A) Migraine without aura
B) Idiopathic intracranial hypertension
C) Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis
D) Subarachnoid hemorrhage

## Correct Answer

B) Idiopathic intracranial hypertension

## Explanation

Obese woman of reproductive age plus papilledema plus opening pressure greater than 25 cm H₂O with normal CSF composition and normal neuroimaging including MRV defines idiopathic intracranial hypertension. The sixth nerve palsy is a false localizing sign of raised ICP.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Obese young woman + papilledema + opening pressure greater than 25 cm H₂O + normal CSF + normal MRI/MRV = idiopathic intracranial hypertension

## Wrong Options

### Migraine without aura

Migraine lacks papilledema, sixth nerve palsy, and elevated opening pressure. Visual phenomena in migraine reflect cortical spreading depression, not raised ICP.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pulsatile unilateral headache + nausea + photophobia + dark room preference = migraine

### Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis

CVT produces an identical raised-ICP syndrome and is the principal mimic, but normal MR venography in this patient effectively excludes it.

**Buzzword Combo:** Postpartum or OCP use + headache + papilledema + filling defect on MR venography = cerebral venous sinus thrombosis

### Subarachnoid hemorrhage

SAH presents with sudden worst-headache-of-life and blood in the basal cisterns or sulci, not a 3-week course of positional headache with papilledema.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sudden worst-headache-of-life + meningismus + ADPKD = subarachnoid hemorrhage

## Related Concepts

Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis (principal mimic); Minocycline / vitamin A / tetracycline as IIH contributors; Empty sella sign on MRI in IIH
