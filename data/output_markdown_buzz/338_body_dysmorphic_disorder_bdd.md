## Buzzword Question

A patient with body dysmorphic disorder asks for repeated cosmetic procedures to “fix” a minor perceived defect. Which of the following is the most appropriate treatment?

## Buzzwords Used

Repeated Cosmetic procedures; perceived defect; 


## Answer Options

 A) Cosmetic surgery
 B) Cognitive behavioral therapy and SSRIs
 C) Benzodiazepine monotherapy
 D) Electroconvulsive therapy


## Correct Answer

B. Cognitive behavioral therapy and SSRIs


## Explanation

First-line treatment for BDD includes cognitive behavioral therapy and SSRIs. Cosmetic interventions are generally not recommended because they rarely improve the underlying psychiatric condition.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

BDD + repetitive appearance concerns + first-line treatment = CBT + SSRIs

## Wrong Options

### A. Cosmetic surgery


Cosmetic procedures typically do not relieve the obsessive preoccupation in BDD and may worsen symptoms.


**Buzzword Combo:** Perceived cosmetic flaw + repeated procedures + persistent dissatisfaction = BDD cosmetic-seeking behavior


### C. Benzodiazepine monotherapy


Benzodiazepines may reduce anxiety temporarily but are not effective primary treatments for BDD.


**Buzzword Combo:** Acute anxiety + rapid sedation + panic symptoms = Benzodiazepines

### D. Electroconvulsive therapy


ECT is primarily used for severe mood disorders and catatonia, not uncomplicated BDD.


**Buzzword Combo:** Severe depression + suicidality + catatonia = ECT indication
