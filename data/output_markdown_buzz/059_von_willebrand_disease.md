## Buzzword Question

A 17-year-old girl has recurrent nosebleeds, easy bruising, heavy menstrual bleeding, and prolonged bleeding after dental extraction. Laboratory studies show normal platelet count, normal PT, increased bleeding time, and mildly increased PTT. The defective step in this patient’s disease is most likely:

## Buzzwords Used

Epistaxis; heavy menses; normal platelet count; increased bleeding time; von Willebrand disease

## Answer Options

A. Platelet adhesion to exposed collagen
B. Platelet aggregation via fibrinogen
C. Platelet destruction by autoantibodies
D. Fibrin cross-linking by factor XIII

## Correct Answer

A. Platelet adhesion to exposed collagen


## Explanation

von Willebrand disease is caused by decreased or defective von Willebrand factor. vWF normally helps platelets adhere by bridging platelet glycoprotein Ib to exposed collagen/endothelium. Because platelet adhesion is impaired, bleeding time increases even though platelet count is normal.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Epistaxis + heavy menses + normal platelet count + increased bleeding time = von Willebrand disease

## Wrong Options

### B. Platelet aggregation via fibrinogen


This describes Glanzmann thrombasthenia, which is due to defective glycoprotein IIb/IIIa and impaired platelet aggregation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mucosal bleeding + defective GpIIb/IIIa + impaired aggregation = Glanzmann thrombasthenia

### C. Platelet destruction by autoantibodies


This describes immune thrombocytopenic purpura, which causes low platelet count due to immune-mediated platelet destruction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Petechiae + isolated thrombocytopenia + antiplatelet antibodies = ITP

### D. Fibrin cross-linking by factor XIII


Factor XIII deficiency causes poor clot stabilization and delayed bleeding, but PT and PTT are usually normal because factor XIII acts after fibrin formation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Delayed bleeding + normal PT/PTT + poor clot stability = factor XIII deficiency
