## Buzzword Question

A 22-year-old man dies of fulminant meningococcal sepsis. At autopsy, both adrenal glands are grossly hemorrhagic and necrotic. This finding is best described as:

## Buzzwords Used

meningococcal sepsis; hemorrhagic adrenal glands

## Answer Options

A) Sheehan syndrome
B) Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome
C) Addison disease
D) Conn syndrome

## Correct Answer

B) Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome

## Explanation

Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome is caused by bilateral adrenal hemorrhage from DIC during fulminant meningococcemia, leading to acute adrenal insufficiency.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Meningococcemia + bilateral adrenal hemorrhage = Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome

## Wrong Options

### Sheehan syndrome

Postpartum pituitary necrosis from hemorrhage/hypotension during delivery.

**Buzzword Combo:** Postpartum + can't lactate = Sheehan

### Addison disease

Chronic primary adrenal insufficiency, most often autoimmune in developed countries.

**Buzzword Combo:** Hyperpigmentation + ↓Na + ↑K = Addison

### Conn syndrome

Primary hyperaldosteronism from an adrenal adenoma or hypertrophy

**Buzzword Combo:** HTN + ↓K + ↑aldosterone, ↓renin = Conn
