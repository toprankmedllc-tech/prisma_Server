## Buzzword Question

An 82-year-old woman dies after an 8-year course of progressive memory loss, word-finding difficulty, and dependence in all activities of daily living. Which of the following findings is most likely to be most prominent on autopsy of her brain?

## Buzzwords Used

Insidious progressive amnestic dementia; loss of instrumental ADLs; elderly woman

## Answer Options

A) Extracellular β-amyloid plaques and intracellular neurofibrillary tangles of hyperphosphorylated tau
B) Eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions of alpha-synuclein in pigmented brainstem neurons
C) Caudate nucleus atrophy with loss of GABAergic medium spiny neurons
D) Spongiform vacuolation with deposition of misfolded prion protein

## Correct Answer

A) Extracellular β-amyloid plaques and intracellular neurofibrillary tangles of hyperphosphorylated tau

## Explanation

Alzheimer disease is characterized by extracellular β-amyloid (Aβ) plaques (from cleavage of amyloid precursor protein by β- and γ-secretase) and intraneuronal neurofibrillary tangles composed of hyperphosphorylated tau. Cortical atrophy is most marked in the medial temporal lobe (hippocampus) and parietal lobes.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Insidious progressive amnestic dementia + β-amyloid plaques + tau neurofibrillary tangles + hippocampal atrophy = Alzheimer disease

## Wrong Options

### Eosinophilic cytoplasmic inclusions of alpha-synuclein in pigmented brainstem neurons

Describes Lewy bodies in Parkinson disease or dementia with Lewy bodies, which present with parkinsonism (± early dementia and visual hallucinations), not pure amnestic dementia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asymmetric resting tremor + cogwheel rigidity + Lewy bodies (alpha-synuclein) = Parkinson disease

### Caudate nucleus atrophy with loss of GABAergic medium spiny neurons

Describes Huntington disease, autosomal dominant CAG repeat expansion in HTT presenting in midlife with chorea, psychiatric symptoms, and dementia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Chorea + caudate atrophy + autosomal dominant family history + CAG repeats = Huntington disease

### Spongiform vacuolation with deposition of misfolded prion protein

Describes Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease, a rapidly progressive dementia over weeks to months with myoclonus and periodic sharp waves on EEG, not an 8-year course.

**Buzzword Combo:** Rapid dementia + myoclonus + 14-3-3 protein in CSF + periodic sharp waves on EEG = Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease
