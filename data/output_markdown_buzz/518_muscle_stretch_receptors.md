## Buzzword Question

A 32-year-old man visits a neurology clinic because of progressive numbness in his feet and difficulty maintaining balance. During the physical examination, the physician tests the patient’s patellar reflex by tapping the patellar tendon with a reflex hammer. The quadriceps muscle rapidly contracts, producing extension of the leg. A medical student asks which specialized sensory receptor detects sudden muscle stretch and initiates this reflex response.
The physician explains that a specific proprioceptive receptor located within skeletal muscle is primarily responsible for mediating deep tendon reflexes.
Which of the following structures is most responsible for deep tendon reflexes?

## Buzzwords Used

deep tendon reflexes

## Answer Options

A. Golgi tendon organ
B. Pacinian corpuscle
C. Muscle spindle
D. Free nerve ending

## Correct Answer

C. Muscle spindle

## Explanation

Muscle spindles are stretch-sensitive proprioceptors located within skeletal muscle. They detect rapid muscle stretch and initiate deep tendon reflexes through Ia afferent fibers that synapse directly with α motor neurons in the spinal cord.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Muscle stretch + patellar reflex + proprioceptor = muscle spindle

## Wrong Options

### A. Golgi tendon organ

Golgi tendon organs detect muscle tension rather than muscle stretch.

**Buzzword Combo:** Muscle tension + tendon receptor + autogenic inhibition = Golgi tendon organ

### B. Pacinian corpuscle.

Pacinian corpuscles detect vibration and deep pressure

**Buzzword Combo:** Vibration sensation + rapidly adapting receptor = Pacinian corpuscle

### D. Free nerve ending

Free nerve endings primarily detect pain and temperature.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pain sensation + temperature detection = free nerve ending
