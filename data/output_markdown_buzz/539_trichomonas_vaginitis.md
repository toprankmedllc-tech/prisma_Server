## Buzzword Question

A 22-year-old sexually active woman presents with vaginal itching, dysuria, and foul-smelling discharge. Pelvic examination reveals a friable cervix with punctate hemorrhages. Wet mount microscopy shows motile flagellated organisms. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Strawberry cervix; Motile flagellated organisms; Frothy foul-smelling discharge; Vaginal itching; Vaginal pH >4.5; Inflammation

## Answer Options

A) Bacterial vaginosis
B) Candida vulvovaginitis
C) Trichomonas vaginitis
D) Herpes simplex virus infection

## Correct Answer

C) Trichomonas vaginitis

## Explanation

Trichomonas vaginalis is a sexually transmitted protozoan infection characterized by frothy yellow-green discharge, vaginal inflammation, strawberry cervix, and motile trichomonads on wet mount microscopy. Treatment is metronidazole for both the patient and sexual partners.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Strawberry cervix + Motile trichomonads + Frothy discharge = Trichomonas vaginitis

## Wrong Options

### A) Bacterial vaginosis

Bacterial vaginosis causes thin gray-white discharge with fishy odor and clue cells but usually lacks inflammation or strawberry cervix.

**Buzzword Combo:** Clue cells + Fishy odor + Thin discharge = Bacterial vaginosis

### B) Candida vulvovaginitis

Candida infection presents with intense pruritus and thick white cottage-cheese discharge with pseudohyphae on KOH prep. Vaginal pH remains normal.

**Buzzword Combo:** Pseudohyphae + Cottage-cheese discharge + Normal pH = Candida vulvovaginitis

### D) Herpes simplex virus infection

Genital herpes presents with painful vesicular lesions and multinucleated giant cells rather than frothy discharge or motile organisms.

**Buzzword Combo:** vesicles + Multinucleated giant cells + Recurrent ulcers = Herpes simplex virus infection
