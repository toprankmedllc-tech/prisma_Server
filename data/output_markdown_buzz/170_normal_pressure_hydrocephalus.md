## Buzzword Question

A 75-year-old man presents with a 6-month history of slow, magnetic-feeling shuffling gait, urinary incontinence, and mild cognitive impairment. MRI shows enlargement of the ventricles out of proportion to cortical atrophy. The most likely diagnosis is:

## Buzzwords Used

Gait disturbance with "magnetic"/shuffling quality; urinary incontinence; cognitive impairment; ventriculomegaly out of proportion to atrophy

## Answer Options

A) Alzheimer disease
B) Normal pressure hydrocephalus
C) Vascular dementia
D) Dementia with Lewy bodies

## Correct Answer

B) Normal pressure hydrocephalus

## Explanation

NPH presents with the classic triad of GAIT disturbance ("wobbly"), URINARY incontinence ("wet"), and cognitive impairment ("wacky"). Imaging shows ventriculomegaly disproportionate to cortical atrophy. Lumbar puncture often produces transient symptomatic improvement and supports the diagnosis; ventriculoperitoneal shunting can be curative.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Wet + wobbly + wacky + enlarged ventricles out of proportion to atrophy = normal pressure hydrocephalus

## Wrong Options

### Alzheimer disease

Alzheimer disease begins with amnestic decline; gait and continence are preserved until late in the course. Cortical atrophy on imaging is proportionate to ventricular enlargement.

**Buzzword Combo:** Insidious amnestic decline + β-amyloid plaques + tau tangles + hippocampal atrophy = Alzheimer disease

### Vascular dementia

Vascular dementia produces a STEPWISE cognitive decline with focal neurologic signs reflecting prior strokes and confluent white matter changes on imaging, not a triad with magnetic gait and ventriculomegaly.

**Buzzword Combo:** Stepwise decline + focal neurologic deficits + confluent white matter lesions = vascular dementia

### Dementia with Lewy bodies

DLB features parkinsonism, fluctuating cognition, REM sleep behavior disorder, and well-formed recurrent visual hallucinations.

**Buzzword Combo:** Parkinsonism + visual hallucinations + fluctuating cognition + REM sleep behavior disorder = dementia with Lewy bodies

## Related Concepts

Alzheimer disease (amnestic decline + hippocampal atrophy); Vascular dementia (stepwise + focal deficits); Communicating hydrocephalus (impaired CSF absorption at arachnoid granulations)
