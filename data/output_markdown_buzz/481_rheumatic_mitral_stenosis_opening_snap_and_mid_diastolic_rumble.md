## Buzzword Question

A patient with rheumatic mitral stenosis is examined. Which of the following auscultatory findings is most characteristic of her condition?

## Buzzwords Used

Rheumatic mitral stenosis; opening snap; mid-diastolic rumble; apex

## Answer Options


A) Opening snap followed by a mid-diastolic rumble at the apex
B) Mid-systolic click followed by a late systolic murmur
C) Harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur at the right upper sternal border
D) Holosystolic murmur at the apex radiating to the axilla

## Correct Answer

A) Opening snap followed by a mid-diastolic rumble at the apex

## Explanation

The auscultatory hallmark of mitral stenosis is a high-pitched opening snap in early diastole followed by a low-pitched, mid-diastolic rumble at the apex, heard best in the left lateral decubitus position.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)


Opening snap + mid-diastolic apical rumble = mitral stenosis

## Wrong Options

### 
B) Mid-systolic click followed by a late systolic murmur


Characteristic of mitral valve prolapse.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mid-systolic click + late systolic murmur = mitral valve prolapse

### C) Harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur at the right upper sternal border

Characteristic of aortic stenosis.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur + RUSB = aortic stenosis

### D) Holosystolic murmur at the apex radiating to the axilla

Characteristic of mitral regurgitation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Holosystolic apical murmur + axillary radiation = mitral regurgitation
