## Buzzword Question

A physiology student is reviewing the mechanisms of skeletal muscle relaxation during a laboratory session. During the lecture, the professor explains that after muscle contraction occurs, calcium ions must be transported from the cytoplasm back into the sarcoplasmic reticulum by the SERCA pump to allow relaxation of the muscle fiber. The professor emphasizes that this transport process is an example of active transport and requires a specific energy source to function properly.
Which of the following is directly required for SERCA pump activity in skeletal muscle?

## Buzzwords Used

SERCA pump activity 

## Answer Options

A. ATP
B. Sodium ions
C. Acetylcholine
D. Troponin

## Correct Answer

A. ATP

## Explanation

SERCA (Sarcoplasmic/Endoplasmic Reticulum Ca2+-ATPase) is an ATP-dependent transport protein located in the membrane of the sarcoplasmic reticulum. Its function is to actively pump calcium ions from the cytoplasm back into the sarcoplasmic reticulum after muscle contraction, allowing muscle relaxation to occur.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Calcium reuptake + active transport + sarcoplasmic reticulum = SERCA pump

## Wrong Options

### B. Sodium ions

Sodium ions are important in membrane depolarization and action potential generation, not direct SERCA pump function.

**Buzzword Combo:** Membrane depolarization + action potentials + extracellular cation = sodium ions


### C. Acetylcholine

Acetylcholine is the neurotransmitter released at the neuromuscular junction to initiate muscle contraction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neuromuscular junction + nicotinic receptors + muscle stimulation = acetylcholine

### D. Troponin

Troponin binds calcium during muscle contraction and regulates tropomyosin movement.


**Buzzword Combo:** Calcium binding + thin filament regulation + contraction initiation = troponin
