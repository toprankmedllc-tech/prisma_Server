## Buzzword Question

A kidney transplant recipient is treated with an immunosuppressive drug that binds FK506-binding protein and reduces T-cell activation. Several weeks later, the patient develops elevated serum creatinine, tremor, hypertension, and new hyperglycemia. Which adverse effect is especially associated with this drug?

## Buzzwords Used

Kidney transplant recipient; binds FK506-binding protein; elevated creatinine, tremor, hypertension, and new hyperglycemia

## Answer Options

A) Hyperglycemia and diabetes
B) Gingival hyperplasia and hirsutism
C) Hemorrhagic cystitis
D) Bone marrow suppression

## Correct Answer

A) Hyperglycemia and diabetes


## Explanation

Tacrolimus inhibits calcineurin after binding FK506-binding protein. Its major adverse effects include nephrotoxicity, neurotoxicity, hypertension, and hyperglycemia/diabetes.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

FK506-binding protein + transplant immunosuppression + hyperglycemia/diabetes = tacrolimus toxicity

## Wrong Options

### B) Gingival hyperplasia and hirsutism

This is classically associated with cyclosporine. Cyclosporine binds cyclophilin, whereas tacrolimus binds FK506-binding protein.

**Buzzword Combo:** Cyclophilin binding + calcineurin inhibition + gingival hyperplasia/hirsutism = cyclosporine

### C) Hemorrhagic cystitis

This is associated with cyclophosphamide or ifosfamide due to acrolein toxicity. It is not a classic adverse effect of tacrolimus.

**Buzzword Combo:** Alkylating agent + bladder irritation and blood in urine + acrolein = cyclophosphamide toxicity

### D) Bone marrow suppression

Bone marrow suppression is more characteristic of antimetabolites that impair nucleotide synthesis. Tacrolimus toxicity is more strongly associated with nephrotoxicity, neurotoxicity, hypertension, and hyperglycemia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Antimetabolite immunosuppression + low blood cell counts + impaired nucleotide synthesis = bone marrow suppression
