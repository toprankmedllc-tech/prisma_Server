## Buzzword Question

A 26-year-old man with auditory hallucinations, thought insertion, flat affect, and social withdrawal has had symptoms for 7 months continuously with no identifiable mood episode and negative toxicology. The minimum diagnostic requirement that distinguishes his diagnosis from schizophreniform disorder is:

## Buzzwords Used

Auditory hallucinations; thought insertion; flat affect; social withdrawal; 7 months; no mood episode

## Answer Options

A) Total illness duration ≥6 months including prodromal/residual phases 
B) Psychosis persisting ≥2 weeks outside a mood episode 
C) Prodromal symptoms alone for ≥6 months without active-phase symptoms 
D) Active-phase symptoms for ≥1 month followed by complete remission

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Schizophreniform disorder and schizophrenia share identical symptom criteria. The sole distinguishing factor is duration: schizophreniform = 1–6 months total; schizophrenia = ≥6 months total (including prodromal and residual phases). At 7 months, this patient meets schizophrenia's duration threshold.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Active-phase psychosis ≥1 month + total illness ≥6 months + no mood episode = Schizophrenia

## Wrong Options

### 1 B) Psychosis persisting ≥2 weeks outside a mood episode


1 Explanation: This is the defining criterion for schizoaffective disorder — psychosis must be present for ≥2 weeks without a concurrent mood episode. It does not distinguish schizophrenia from schizophreniform disorder.


**Buzzword Combo:** Psychosis ≥2 weeks outside mood episode + mood episode majority of illness = Schizoaffective disorder


### 2 C) Prodromal symptoms alone for ≥6 months without active-phase symptoms


2 Explanation: Prodromal symptoms alone cannot establish a schizophrenia diagnosis. At least 1 month of active-phase symptoms must occur within the 6-month window.

**Buzzword Combo:** Social withdrawal + attenuated psychosis + functional decline without full psychosis = Schizophrenia prodrome (not yet diagnosable)

### 3: D) Active-phase symptoms for ≥1 month followed by complete remission

3 Explanation: Complete remission after 1–6 months of active-phase symptoms fits schizophreniform disorder. Full recovery to baseline after a brief episode may also suggest brief psychotic disorder if duration is under 1 month.

**Buzzword Combo:** Full psychosis 1–6 months + full recovery = Schizophreniform disorder
