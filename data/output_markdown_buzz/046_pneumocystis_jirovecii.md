## Buzzword Question

A 36-year-old man with untreated HIV presents with progressive dyspnea and diffuse bilateral interstitial infiltrates. Bronchoalveolar lavage demonstrates organisms staining dark with silver stain.
Which of the following organisms is the most likely cause?

## Buzzwords Used

HIV; Interstitial infiltrates; Silver stain

## Answer Options

A) Toxoplasma gondii
B) Cytomegalovirus
C) Pneumocystis jirovecii
D) Mycobacterium avium complex

## Correct Answer

C) Pneumocystis jirovecii

## Explanation

Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia commonly causes pneumonia in patients with advanced HIV/AIDS. It classically presents with progressive dyspnea, diffuse bilateral interstitial infiltrates, and organisms visible on silver stain.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

HIV + silver stain + diffuse infiltrates = Pneumocystis jirovecii

## Wrong Options

### Toxoplasma gondii

Toxoplasma commonly causes brain abscesses in AIDS patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Ring-enhancing brain lesions + AIDS = Toxoplasma

### Cytomegalovirus

CMV commonly causes retinitis and colitis in immunocompromised patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Owl-eye inclusions + retinitis + AIDS = CMV

### Mycobacterium avium complex

MAC causes disseminated infection in advanced AIDS patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Disseminated infection + CD4 <50 + AIDS = MAC
