## Buzzword Question

A patient with acute mania is started on a medication that decreases suicide risk and requires serum level monitoring because of a narrow therapeutic index. Which medication was most likely prescribed?

## Buzzwords Used

Acute mania; mood stabilizer; suicide risk reduction; narrow therapeutic index; serum monitoring

## Answer Options

A) Lithium
B) Fluoxetine
C) Haloperidol
D) Alprazolam

## Correct Answer

A. Lithium

## Explanation

Lithium is a first-line mood stabilizer for bipolar disorder and is the only maintenance medication proven to reduce suicide risk. It has a narrow therapeutic index and requires serum level monitoring.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Mood stabilizer + narrow therapeutic index + suicide prevention = Lithium

## Wrong Options

### B. Fluoxetine

Fluoxetine is an SSRI antidepressant that may precipitate mania if given without a mood stabilizer in bipolar disorder.

**Buzzword Combo:** Depression + serotonin reuptake inhibition + antidepressant = Fluoxetine

### C. Haloperidol

Haloperidol is an antipsychotic that may be used acutely for agitation but is not the classic mood stabilizer associated with suicide risk reduction.

**Buzzword Combo:** Psychosis + dopamine blockade + extrapyramidal symptoms = Haloperidol

### D. Alprazolam

Alprazolam is a benzodiazepine used for short-term anxiety management and does not function as a mood stabilizer

**Buzzword Combo:** Panic attacks + rapid anxiolysis + benzodiazepine = Alprazolam
