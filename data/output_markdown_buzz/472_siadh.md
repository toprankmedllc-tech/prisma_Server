## Buzzword Question

Which of the following medications acts as an ADH receptor antagonist and may be used in the treatment of severe syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion?

## Buzzwords Used

ADH receptor; antagonist; SIADH

## Answer Options

 A. Conivaptan
 B. Desmopressin
 C. Hydrochlorothiazide
 D. Fludrocortisone 

## Correct Answer

 A. Conivaptan

## Explanation

Conivaptan is an ADH receptor antagonist used in the treatment of severe Syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion. Blocking vasopressin receptors decreases water reabsorption in the collecting ducts, helping correct dilutional hyponatremia

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

ADH receptor + antagonist+ water excretion = Conivaptan

## Wrong Options

###  Desmopressin

Desmopressin mimics antidiuretic hormone activity and is used to treat central diabetes insipidus rather than SIADH.

**Buzzword Combo:** Synthetic ADH + increased urine concentration + Central DI = Desmopressin

### Hydrochlorothiazide

Hydrochlorothiazide inhibits sodium-chloride reabsorption in the distal convoluted tubule and may itself contribute to hyponatremia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Thiazide diuretic + Distal Convoluted tubule + hyponatremia risk = Hydrochlorothiazide

### Fludrocortisone

Fludrocortisone is used to increase sodium retention in conditions such as adrenal insufficiency and orthostatic hypotension.

**Buzzword Combo:** Mineralocorticoid activity + sodium retention = Fludrocortisone
