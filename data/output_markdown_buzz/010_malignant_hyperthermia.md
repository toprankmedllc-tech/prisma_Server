## Buzzword Question

a boy undergoes emergency surgery after a motor vehicle accident. Shortly after induction of anesthesia, he develops generalized muscle rigidity, tachycardia, hyperthermia, and dark-colored urine. Laboratory studies reveal markedly elevated creatine kinase and hyperkalemia.
Which of the following complications is this patient most at risk of developing?

## Buzzwords Used

generalized muscle rigidity; tachycardia; hyperthermia; dark-colored urine

## Answer Options

A. Acute kidney injury due to myoglobinuria
B. Megaloblastic anemia due to folate deficiency
C. Pulmonary fibrosis due to chronic inflammation
D. Hypoglycemia due to excess insulin secretion

## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Malignant hyperthermia causes severe skeletal muscle breakdown (rhabdomyolysis), leading to release of myoglobin into the bloodstream. Myoglobin can precipitate within renal tubules and cause acute kidney injury.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Muscle rigidity + dark urine + hyperthermia after anesthesia = rhabdomyolysis causing AKI

## Wrong Options

### B. Megaloblastic anemia due to folate deficiency

Incorrect — Megaloblastic anemia is associated with impaired DNA synthesis due to folate or vitamin B12 deficiency.

**Buzzword Combo:** Macrocytosis + hypersegmented neutrophils = megaloblastic anemia

### C. Pulmonary fibrosis due to chronic inflammation

Incorrect — Pulmonary fibrosis is commonly associated with medications such as amiodarone or bleomycin.

**Buzzword Combo:** Amiodarone + restrictive lung disease = pulmonary fibrosis

### D. Hypoglycemia due to excess insulin secretion

Incorrect — Excess insulin secretion causes hypoglycemia but is unrelated to malignant hyperthermia.

**Buzzword Combo:** Insulin excess + diaphoresis + confusion = hypoglycemia
