## Buzzword Question

A 19-year-old woman presents for her first prenatal visit at 18 weeks gestation. She has not been taking any prenatal vitamins. Maternal serum alpha-fetoprotein (MSAFP) is significantly elevated. An ultrasound reveals a defect in the lumbosacral region with protrusion of both the meninges and spinal cord through a vertebral arch defect. This condition most likely resulted from a failure of which of the following processes?

## Buzzwords Used

No prenatal vitamins; Elevated MSAFP; Meninges and spinal cord protrusion; Lumbosacral defect.

## Answer Options

A) Failure of the rostral neuropore to close
B) Failure of the caudal neuropore to close
C) Inadequate proliferation of the paraxial mesoderm
D) Failure of the neural crest cells to migrate into the branchial arches

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

The clinical description of meninges and spinal cord protruding defines a myelomeningocele. This occurs due to the failure of the caudal neuropore to fuse during the 4th week of development, often associated with low folate intake.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Caudal neuropore failure + Myelomeningocele = Lower spinal defect

## Wrong Options

### A) Failure of the rostral neuropore to close

Failure of the rostral (cephalic) neuropore to close results in anencephaly, which presents as the absence of the forebrain and skull vault, not a lumbosacral defect.

**Buzzword Combo:** Rostral neuropore failure + Anencephaly = Absence of cranial vault

### C) Inadequate proliferation of the paraxial mesoderm

While mesoderm forms the vertebral arches, the primary defect in neural tube defects is the failure of the neuroectoderm itself to fuse.

**Buzzword Combo:** Paraxial mesoderm + Sclerotome = Vertebral column formation

### D) Failure of the neural crest cells to migrate into the branchial arches

Neural crest migration issues lead to craniofacial defects (like CATCH-22 or Treacher Collins), not spinal neural tube defects.

**Buzzword Combo:** Neural crest + Branchial arches = Craniofacial abnormalities
