## Buzzword Question

A 32-year-old woman presents with weight loss, palpitations, anxiety, and heat intolerance. Physical examination reveals diffuse enlargement of the thyroid gland and bilateral exophthalmos. Laboratory studies show decreased TSH and elevated free T4. A radioactive iodine uptake scan demonstrates diffuse increased uptake throughout the entire thyroid gland. Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

Heat intolerance; Weight loss; Exophthalmos; Diffuse goiter; Low TSH; High T4; Diffuse hot uptake on radioactive iodine scan


## Answer Options

A) Graves disease
B) Toxic adenoma
C) Subacute granulomatous thyroiditis
D) Toxic multinodular goiter


## Correct Answer

A) Graves disease


## Explanation

Graves disease causes diffuse stimulation of TSH receptors by autoantibodies, leading to increased iodine uptake throughout the entire thyroid gland. The radioactive iodine scan classically shows a diffuse “hot” thyroid.


## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Exophthalmos + Diffuse hot thyroid scan + Hyperthyroidism = Graves disease


## Wrong Options

### B) Toxic adenoma

Toxic adenoma presents as a single hyperfunctioning “hot” nodule with suppression of the remaining thyroid tissue. It does not cause diffuse uptake or ophthalmopathy.

**Buzzword Combo:** Single hot nodule + Hyperthyroidism + Suppressed surrounding thyroid = Toxic adenoma

### C) Subacute granulomatous thyroiditis

Subacute thyroiditis causes release of preformed thyroid hormone due to gland inflammation, leading to low radioactive iodine uptake. Patients usually have a painful tender thyroid after a viral illness.

**Buzzword Combo:** Painful thyroid + Viral illness + Low iodine uptake = Subacute thyroiditis

### D) Toxic multinodular goiter

Toxic multinodular goiter demonstrates multiple focal areas of increased uptake rather than diffuse uptake throughout the gland. It is more common in older patients.

**Buzzword Combo:** Older patient + Multiple hot nodules + Hyperthyroidism = Toxic multinodular goiter
