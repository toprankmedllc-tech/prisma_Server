## Buzzword Question

A newborn is evaluated after a difficult vaginal delivery complicated by shoulder dystocia. Physical examination reveals that the affected arm is adducted and internally rotated with the elbow extended and forearm pronated. The Moro reflex is absent on the affected side, but grip strength is preserved.
Which of the following is the most likely diagnosis?

## Buzzwords Used

shoulder dystocia; arm is adducted and internally rotated with the elbow extended and forearm pronated; The Moro reflex is absent on the affected side

## Answer Options

A. Carpal tunnel syndrome
B. Klumpke palsy
C. Erb palsy
D. Radial nerve injury

## Correct Answer

C. Erb palsy

## Explanation

This infant has Erb palsy, which results from injury to the upper trunk (C5–C6) of the brachial plexus, commonly due to shoulder dystocia during delivery. Classic findings include: “Waiter’s tip” posture ,Adducted arm ,Internally rotated shoulder ,Extended elbow ,Pronated forearm ,Absent Moro reflex with preserved grasp reflex. 

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Shoulder dystocia + waiter’s tip posture = Erb palsy

## Wrong Options

### A. Carpal tunnel syndrome

it Causes median nerve compression with numbness in the lateral hand.


**Buzzword Combo:** Thenar weakness + nocturnal hand numbness + median nerve compression=carpal tunnel syndrome

### B.Klumpke palsy

it Causes claw hand due to lower trunk (C8–T1) injury.


**Buzzword Combo:** Excessive arm traction + claw hand + weak grip= klumpke palsy

### D. Radial nerve injury

it Causes wrist drop due to impaired wrist extension.

**Buzzword Combo:** Midshaft humeral fracture + wrist drop + impaired extension= radial nerve injury
