## Buzzword Question

An elderly patient is found to have a harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur at the right upper sternal border radiating to the carotids, with a delayed and diminished carotid upstroke. This combination is most characteristic of:

## Buzzwords Used

Harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur; right upper sternal border; radiation to carotids; pulsus parvus et tardus; delayed diminished carotid upstroke; aortic stenosis

## Answer Options

A) Aortic stenosis
B) Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy
C) Mitral regurgitation
D) Aortic regurgitation

## Correct Answer

A) Aortic stenosis

## Explanation

The combination of a harsh crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur at the right upper sternal border radiating to the carotids with pulsus parvus et tardus (delayed, diminished carotid upstroke) is pathognomonic for severe aortic stenosis.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Crescendo-decrescendo systolic murmur + RUSB radiating to carotids + pulsus parvus et tardus = aortic stenosis

## Wrong Options

### B) Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy

HCM also produces a harsh systolic murmur, but at the LLSB (not RUSB), and the carotid upstroke is brisk and bifid, not pulsus parvus et tardus.

**Buzzword Combo:** LLSB systolic murmur + bifid carotid pulse + louder with Valsalva = HCM

### C) Mitral regurgitation

MR produces a holosystolic apical murmur radiating to the axilla, not a crescendo-decrescendo murmur at RUSB.

**Buzzword Combo:** 
Holosystolic apical murmur + axillary radiation = mitral regurgitation

### 
D) Aortic regurgitation

AR produces a diastolic decrescendo murmur at the left sternal border with a wide pulse pressure and bounding pulses — not a systolic murmur with diminished pulses.

**Buzzword Combo:** Diastolic decrescendo murmur + wide pulse pressure + bounding pulses = aortic regurgitation
