## Buzzword Question

A 4-year-old boy is brought to the pediatrician for evaluation of developmental delay and unusual behavior. The mother reports that he is "always happy," laughs frequently without cause, and has a jerky, unstable gait. Physical exam reveals a wide-mouthed expression and microcephaly. Genetic testing confirms a deletion of a chromosome .The missing genetic material in this patient is normally expressed from which of the following?

## Buzzwords Used

Always happy; Jerky gait; Wide-mouthed; Chromosome 15 deletion.

## Answer Options

A) The paternal allele, due to maternal imprinting of the UBE3A gene
B) The maternal allele, due to paternal imprinting of the UBE3A gene
C) The paternal allele, due to maternal imprinting of the SNRPN gene
D) The maternal allele, due to paternal imprinting of the SNRPN gene

## Correct Answer

B

## Explanation

Angelman syndrome ("Happy Puppet") is caused by the loss of the maternally inherited UBE3A gene on chromosome 15. In normal individuals, the paternal UBE3A gene is silenced (imprinted), so the individual relies solely on the maternal copy.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Angelman + Happy Puppet = Maternal Deletion (UBE3A)

## Wrong Options

### A) The paternal allele, due to maternal imprinting of the UBE3A gene

This is the reverse of the actual imprinting pattern; UBE3A is silenced on the paternal chromosome in the brain.

**Buzzword Combo:** Paternal deletion + UBE3A = Non-pathological (as it was already silenced)

### C) The paternal allele, due to maternal imprinting of the SNRPN gene

Loss of the paternal SNRPN gene (and maternal imprinting) results in Prader-Willi syndrome, characterized by hyperphagia, obesity, and hypogonadism.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prader-Willi + Obesity = Paternal Deletion (SNRPN)

### D) The maternal allele, due to paternal imprinting of the SNRPN gene

SNRPN is normally silenced on the maternal chromosome, so a maternal deletion of this gene would not result in the Angelman phenotype.

**Buzzword Combo:** Maternal deletion + SNRPN = Non-pathological
