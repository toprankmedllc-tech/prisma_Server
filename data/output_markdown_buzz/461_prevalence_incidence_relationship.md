## Buzzword Question

A public health researcher compares type 2 diabetes (chronic, low mortality, 20-year average disease duration) with Ebola virus disease (acute, >50% case fatality rate, 2-3 week illness course) in a stable population. Compared to type 2 diabetes, which of the following best describes the relationship between prevalence and incidence for Ebola?

## Buzzwords Used

Chronic vs acute; high mortality; short duration Ebola vs long duration DM2; prevalence-incidence relationship

## Answer Options

A) Prevalence ≈ Incidence for Ebola because short duration and high mortality keep cases from accumulating 
B) Prevalence >> Incidence for Ebola because high mortality attracts more reporting 
C) Prevalence is always greater than incidence for both diseases 
D) Incidence cannot be calculated for acute diseases


## Correct Answer

A

## Explanation

Prevalence = Incidence × Duration. For Ebola: very short duration (2-3 weeks) + high case fatality rate → cases resolve quickly (by death or recovery) → cases do not accumulate → prevalence ≈ incidence (point-in-time). For DM2: very long duration (20 years) + low mortality → cases accumulate over decades → prevalence >> incidence.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Short duration + high mortality = Prevalence ≈ Incidence (cases don't accumulate); Long duration + low mortality = Prevalence >> Incidence

## Wrong Options

### B) Prevalence >> Incidence for Ebola because high mortality attracts more reporting

High mortality causes cases to leave the prevalence pool quickly (through death), reducing accumulation. It does not increase reporting in a way that inflates prevalence beyond incidence. High mortality + short duration = prevalence ≈ or < incidence.

**Buzzword Combo:** High case fatality rate + short illness = Cases exit prevalence pool rapidly → prevalence remains low relative to incidence

### C) Prevalence is always greater than incidence for both diseases

Prevalence = Incidence × Duration. When disease duration is very short (days to weeks) and mortality is high, the prevalence pool cannot accumulate, prevalence can approximate or equal incidence. Prevalence > incidence only holds reliably for chronic diseases with long duration.

**Buzzword Combo:** Prevalence > Incidence only when: long duration AND low mortality AND stable population (chronic disease pattern)

### D) Incidence cannot be calculated for acute diseases

Incidence (new cases per population per unit time) can and should be calculated for acute diseases. Incidence is particularly important for acute diseases and outbreak investigations. Incidence cannot be directly calculated from case-control studies (only odds ratios).

**Buzzword Combo:** Case-control study → cannot calculate incidence → use odds ratio; Cohort study → can calculate incidence → use relative risk
