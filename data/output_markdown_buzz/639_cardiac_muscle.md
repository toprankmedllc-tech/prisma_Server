## Buzzword Question

Cardiac myocytes depolarize in a synchronized fashion because they are connected to one another by which of the following structures?

## Buzzwords Used

Synchronized depolarization

## Answer Options

A) Gap junctions (connexin channels) 
B) Desmosomes within the sarcolemma
C) T-tubules within the sarcoplasmic reticulum
D) Tight junctions between adjacent cells

## Correct Answer

A) Gap junctions (connexin channels)

## Explanation

Cardiac myocytes are electrically coupled via gap junctions (connexin channels) located within intercalated discs. These gap junctions allow ions to flow directly between adjacent cells, enabling the heart to depolarize in a synchronized fashion.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Synchronized depolarization + connexin gap junctions = electrical coupling

## Wrong Options

### B) Desmosomes within the sarcolemma

Desmosomes provide mechanical coupling between cardiac myocytes (resisting shear forces during contraction) but do not allow electrical coupling.

**Buzzword Combo:** Desmosomes + mechanical anchoring + intercalated discs = structural cell-cell adhesion

### C) T-tubules within the sarcoplasmic reticulum

T-tubules are invaginations of the sarcolemma that bring depolarization close to the sarcoplasmic reticulum within a single cell — they do not couple adjacent cells.

**Buzzword Combo:** T-tubules + dyad / triad + SR coupling = excitation-contraction coupling (within a single cell)

### D) Tight junctions between adjacent cells

Tight junctions form impermeable seals between epithelial cells (e.g., blood-brain barrier, gut epithelium) and are not the basis of cardiac electrical coupling.

**Buzzword Combo:** Tight junctions + impermeable seals + blood-brain barrier = epithelial barrier function
