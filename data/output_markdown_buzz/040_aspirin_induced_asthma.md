## Buzzword Question

A 19-year-old man develops wheezing after taking aspirin and is prescribed a medication that blocks leukotriene receptors. Which of the following drugs was most likely prescribed?

## Buzzwords Used

Aspirin; Wheezing; Leukotriene Receptors

## Answer Options

 A) Cromolyn
 B) Omalizumab
 C) Albuterol
 D) Montelukast 

## Correct Answer

 D) Montelukast 

## Explanation

Montelukast is a leukotriene receptor antagonist used in asthma, especially aspirin-exacerbated asthma. Leukotrienes promote bronchoconstriction, mucus production, and airway inflammation.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Asthma + aspirin sensitivity + leukotriene blockade = Montelukast

## Wrong Options

### Omalizumab

Omalizumab is an anti-IgE monoclonal antibody used for severe allergic asthma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Severe allergic asthma + anti-IgE = Omalizumab

### Albuterol

Albuterol is a short-acting β2-agonist that increases cAMP to produce rapid bronchodilation.

**Buzzword Combo:** Acute asthma relief + ↑cAMP = Albuterol

### Cromolyn

Cromolyn stabilizes mast cells and prevents degranulation; it is used prophylactically in asthma.

**Buzzword Combo:** Asthma prophylaxis + mast cell stabilization = Cromolyn
