## Buzzword Question

A patient using amphetamines reports intense pleasure, increased motivation, and craving behavior due to increased dopamine activity in a pathway projecting to the nucleus accumbens. Which of the following is the primary function of this dopaminergic pathway?

A. Motivation and reward
B. Motor control
C. Prolactin inhibition
D. Regulation of circadian rhythm

## Buzzwords Used

Amphetamines ; Dopamine ; Nucleus accumbens ; Pleasure ; Addiction ; Craving ; Reward pathway

## Answer Options

A) Motivation and reward
B) Motor control
C) Prolactin inhibition
D) Regulation of circadian rhythm

## Correct Answer

A. Motivation and reward

## Explanation

The Mesolimbic Pathway is responsible for reward, motivation, pleasure, and addiction-related behaviors. Increased dopamine activity in this pathway produces euphoria and reinforcement seen with substance use.

## Buzzword Combination (Correct)

Nucleus accumbens + dopamine + addiction = Motivation and reward

## Wrong Options

### B. Motor control

Motor control is primarily mediated by the nigrostriatal dopaminergic pathway. Dysfunction here causes extrapyramidal symptoms and parkinsonism.

**Buzzword Combo:** Rigidity + tremor + bradykinesia = Motor control

### C. Prolactin inhibition

Prolactin inhibition is mediated by the tuberoinfundibular pathway, where dopamine suppresses prolactin release from the anterior pituitary.

**Buzzword Combo:** Galactorrhea + amenorrhea + hyperprolactinemia = Prolactin inhibition

### D. Regulation of circadian rhythm

Circadian rhythm regulation is controlled primarily by the suprachiasmatic nucleus of the hypothalamus and is not the main function of the mesolimbic dopamine pathway.

**Buzzword Combo:** Sleep-wake cycle + light exposure + hypothalamus = Circadian rhythm regulation
